# Fixed-budget timing diagnostic: offline evidence

Run `python -I -B -S verify.py` from the extracted archive.
No libraries, credentials, network, trajectories or checkpoints are needed.

The verifier adds the opportunity and choice counts across six anonymous
development city groups. It recomputes held release qualification from the
stored candidate risk ratios, motion-floor gains and conservative pair and
trajectory gains, plus the fixed incumbent's stored eligibility indicator.
It does not reconstruct raw forecasts, verify the indicator from labels,
rerun training or certify independent confirmation.

The figure is a compact view of these same development counts. Timing
substitutions keep the original two recipients and their peak displacements.
The original harm tolerance is 2%. Nothing in this archive establishes
physical safety or a deployable policy.
