\paragraph{Separating nomination and chronological budgets.}
On the same exposed OpenDD locations, a $2\times2$ label-aware audit
keeps the per-query and aggregate count caps and changes the menu and
chronological budget separately. If $b_t$ is retained credit, $a_t$ is
new credit, $k_t$ is the count spent, and $C$ is storage, the inequalities
$b_t\geq0$, $b_t+k_t\leq C$, and $b_t+k_t\leq b_{t-1}+a_t$ are exact for
feasibility: the greedy balance from zero dominates any feasible
retained balance by induction, and the actual balance is itself
feasible. Rescored witnesses passing original risk and beating both
references number \StreamingTeacherFullTotal{}/\StreamingTeacherViews{}
for the full menu and total quota, \StreamingTeacherFullToken{} with
added chronology, \StreamingTeacherNestedTotal{} for the nested menu
with total quota, and \StreamingTeacherNestedToken{} with both
restrictions. One strict case is unresolved. The 202 distinct recordings
are not 606 independent observations; full-menu results are shared across
seeds. This measures feasibility with labels, not causal identifiability.
