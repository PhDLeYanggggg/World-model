Dataset: previously admitted MovingDroneCrowd++ SOURCE and already-exposed own-B
only. Native observation8,prediction12,annotation stride1; image coordinates,
not verified raw-video timing,seconds or metric units. No new images admitted.

Own cohort:62,466eligible agent-window rows,374,796legal nonfloor candidate rows,
eight conservative source components and24unique recordings. Overlapping agent

seeds;72required recording views are not72independent recordings. The fixed

Producer-A donor pool:125,540agent-window rows,753,240nonfloor candidate rows.
Its per-job pool is disjoint from queried own-B sources and keeps established
producer-held ancestry. Row joins use exact original agent-window IDs; action
IDs retain the original7-action namespace. No matching on future labels.

Source caches are cached_verified by existing hash/ancestry receipts. New
causal48joins and model training are fresh_run. The encoder accepts current/past
positions,masks and IDs only; its existing regression test verifies unchanged
inputs under altered/truncated future annotations. These pipeline checks do not
verify the causal provenance of annotations generated upstream.

Global48summarizes currently visible agents' past motion and history support,
including partially observed past agents excluded from the complete-history
neighbor budget. It is richer inventory than the frozen forecaster's nearest
eight complete-past neighbors,not a pure architecture-only forecaster comparison.
It is neither a camera-motion measurement nor visual scene understanding.

All learned normalization uses known fitting rows from nonquery sources. Targets
and future validity are loss/evaluation only. Partial-label ADE and wholly
unknown-label completion bounds remain distinct from complete12ADE and endpoint
FDE. Zero/empty reference remains not estimable under the original contract.

No raw images,trajectories,feature arrays or checkpoints are published to Git.
Only source,configuration,scalar diagnostics,hashes and reports are synchronized.
Claims are exploratory development,no independent confirmation or deployment.
No future endpoints as input,no central velocity,no test endpoint goals.
