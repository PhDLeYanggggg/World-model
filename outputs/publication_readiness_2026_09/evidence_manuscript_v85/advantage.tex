\section{Candidate-Conditioned Gain With Double Exclusion}

For a held site $h$, a selector training row from site $s$
uses a comoving EqMotion trajectory from a producer fitted
without either $s$ or $h$. One producer per unordered pair and
seed generates forecasts for both sites, giving
\AdvantageProducers{} producer fits. The evaluation trajectory
uses the frozen producer trained without $h$. We fit
\AdvantageHeads{} causal heads to predict missing-step lower gain
against CV, all/easy/signed-easy risk excesses, and easy
support. The gain readout sorts by predicted gain; the risk
readout subtracts positive predicted excesses with fixed
weights $2,4,1$. Neither readout fits a threshold on held sites.

The hardness rule sets each query's count, and every readout
uses the same raw trajectory bank and exact count in that
query (\AdvantageEdits{} edits). The motion control ranks by
observed CV/OLS4 disagreement. A label-aware utility rank
optimizes gain alone at the same counts. Recipients and total
displacement may differ; this is not a matched-radius test.

Direct paired lower effects versus hardness are
\AdvantageGainEffect{} for gain and \AdvantageRiskEffect{} for
the risk readout (3,000 site bootstrap draws after averaging
seeds). The effects versus CV are \AdvantageGainCV{} and
\AdvantageRiskCV{}, respectively. This separates ranking
relative advantage from predicting baseline difficulty; the
matched count itself is not selected by either new policy.

With the same path bank and counts, an outcome-aware
integer program maximizes the lesser of the two conservative
gains subject to the original recording risk constraints.
For the binary choice $z_i$, the fixed query count gives
$\sum_{i\in q}z_i=k_q$. Positive selected easy reference
also implies positive selected all-reference mass, so the
two ratio constraints can be multiplied by their
denominators and written as linear excess inequalities.
The signed easy inequality uses the unchanged full-recording
easy mass. Subtracting the unedited CV prediction's lower
gain from each OLS4 term leaves an additive change plus a
constant. Thus one common margin variable upper-bounded by
both gains gives the maximin objective without relaxing
either risk denominator.
It finds \AdvantageCapacity{} qualifying views,
with \AdvantageNoEdit{} no-edit views, \AdvantageNoEasy{} lacking
eligible easy support, \AdvantageInfeasible{} risk-infeasible,
\AdvantageNoDual{} without positive dual gain, and
\AdvantageUnresolved{} unresolved. Positive witnesses reproduce
in the original scorer. This is not a bound for other
counts, query clocks, or forecast banks and is not evidence
of external calibration or independent generalization.
