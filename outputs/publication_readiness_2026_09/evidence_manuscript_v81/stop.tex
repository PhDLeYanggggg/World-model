\paragraph{Donor-selected online stopping.}
We fit \StopFits{} heads, including double-excluded fits that produce
out-of-fold donor predictions for each held location. The threshold and
fixed-delay alternatives use only the donor readouts. All policies use
the same candidate bank and may make at most one edit. The learned stop
passes \StopLearned{} of \StopViews{} joint checks versus
\StopImmediate{} for immediate and \StopFixed{} for fixed delay, but
its conservative forecast effect against immediate is \StopFull{}.
On the \StopMatched{} views where both actually edit once, it is
\StopEffect{}. On \StopFixedMatched{} views matched with fixed delay,
it is \StopFixedEffect{}. Matching here is retrospective and utility-only.

In a secondary diagnostic, a threshold must preserve both donor
joint-pass rate and conservative CV gain. It returns to immediate
in \StopFallback{} of seven folds. Held joint passes stay at
\StopImmediate{} and the conservative effect is \StopGuard{}.
No policy qualifies. The large exact timing ceiling is not evidence
that these past-only scores identify the later feasible actions.
