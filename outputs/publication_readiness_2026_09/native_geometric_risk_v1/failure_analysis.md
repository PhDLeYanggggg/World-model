# Geometric-Risk Failure Taxonomy

**Support is thin, but not the whole explanation.** Training contains only2-7
moving zero-CV rows from1-3tracks per view. This limits learning on that type of
exact-reference trajectory. However, the three remaining harmful strict-rule
instances are all currently stopped, not moving. The new objective fails even
on a group with much broader aggregate training support.

**A better cost parameterization is not a better decision.** Protected harm's
geometric identity holds; all48new endpoints improve its global MSE versus the
previous direct regressor. Yet every new strict head leaves a false negative in
each seed and does not outperform the simple causal-stop guard. This supports
keeping global fit and conditional intervention reliability separate.

**The new features do not earn a policy claim.** Explicit acceleration, velocity
consistency and backcast residual features improve some cost scores but yield
negligible/negative matched policy contrasts. Their four extra slots were zero
in the base control, so parameter count, draws and budget match. More features
or training loss terms are not established contributions.

**The simple control is informative.** Existing MSE-strict plus a past-stop veto
retains1.29190%sourceADE improvement and zero observed complete zero-CV harms
in allseeds. It was registered before this readout, not chosen by a new sweep.
It still has283unknown-ADE/3,064incomplete-risk selected instances and only
1.73956%hard gain. A complete-label empirical result is not a population,
physical-safety or independent-calibration guarantee.

The probability model is fitted on complete-label supervision. Missing-at-random
or transport of that conditional risk to incomplete-future queries has not been
established. The geometric identity itself does not repair that observation
process or identify unobserved full-population risk.

**Matched counts remove another false explanation.** The new comparison keeps
all4,437requested interventions, so its negative effect is not solely the prior
eight-decision collapse. Even ordinary MSE strict net ranking has zero observed
zero-CV harms at this count. Additional learned-risk filtering brings no practical
ADE advantage. This matched rule is an offline control, not streaming deployment.

**No runtime or label-input failure was found.** All48endpoints, normalizers,
sampler exposure, checkpoint probabilities, independent target identities and
selected-row hashes verify. Unknown labels are excluded from risk supervision,
not silently made safe. No bound model/config file was changed after registration.
Future zero-CV membership is a target only, never an inference input.

## Research Consequence

Do not present factorized risk learning as the successful method or call this
CCF-A-ready. Freeze these negative factorial results. Carry the simple stop/strict
control into the next properly scoped scene-level comparison rather than fit
more similar heads without a discriminating hypothesis. Before any calibration
claim, check training/calibration producer exclusion and independent event/scene
support. Preserve unknown outcomes and allseeds, not just apparent safe averages.
The explored sources cannot be relabeled as fresh confirmation data. No change
to the scientific metric or risk tolerance is authorized by these results.
