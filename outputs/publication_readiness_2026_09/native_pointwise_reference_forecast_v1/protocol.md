# Does pointwise positive-harm supervision help a frozen recipient policy?

The earlier two-reference loss only changed the learning gradient through fully unobserved targets: subtracting the error of either reference on observed rows adds a constant. Here the stronger reference changes the gradient directly. From each of the three completed CV-only checkpoints, continue both arms for 300 more AdamW updates at 0.0001, using identical optimizer and sampler initial state, and 128 rows per update from the same nine fitting sources. No early stopping or development selection. The model, known signed CV regret, CV disagreement on wholly missing rows, and the CV positive-easy penalty are identical. In the treatment only, replace the global 0.5-times positive CV harm term on observed rows with 0.5-times the maximum of positive CV and positive OLS4 harm on each row. This stronger reference is a training target only. No future label or comparator winner enters prediction.

Score the complete exposed-development roster after both predictions freeze. The primary endpoint is the completion-lower individual ADE improvement of pointwise versus CV-only continuation, retaining the original 9,675 switch recipients. Also compare each to the warm start, original gain path, CV and OLS4; full-roster arms are secondary. A substitution occurs only where the warm and both continued forecasts remain a genuine switch; otherwise all matched arms retain the original gain path. Bootstrap 3,000 paired source draws after averaging the three matched seeds within each source. Keep the original 2% recording support and risk rule. Do not use an observed-label gain to justify a safety, independent-confirmation or deployment claim.

The experiment does not touch the running DroneCrowd calibration, confirmation role or official scores. Image coordinates and 8/12 annotation steps are not verified physical distance or seconds. No Stage5C or SMC.
