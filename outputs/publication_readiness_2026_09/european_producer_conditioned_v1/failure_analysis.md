# Failure Taxonomy

## 1. The New Floor Is Not An Immutable Safety Guarantee

Matching producer size required evaluating two-source producer bundles. Their
floor quality differs from the unchanged four-source anchor. Aggregate gain
against these weaker floors is not proof of overall progress. All seven easy
violations for the producer-tagged policy originate in already-bad branch floors,
and six remain violations even after a beneficial neural correction in decision
space. The worst case improves10.04054% degradation to8.02939%, still unsafe by
the observed-error criterion. No branch is deployed.

## 2. Producer Tags Also Identify Fitting Cohorts

Each producer scores only the opposite two-source cohort during head fitting.
The tag therefore encodes a joint producer/cohort condition. In the all-event
configuration, bundle0 gains versus global range-0.04113% to+0.93933%; bundle1
ranges-0.50256% to+0.08704%. Wrong-tag inference can outperform the true tag.
This supports sensitivity to the tag, not a robust understanding of producer
transport. No causal attribution to producer identity is established.

## 3. Predicted Low Risk Is Not Realized Low Risk

The rule constrains predicted moments. Of288 selected locality/views,102 exceed
a2% realized positive-harm ratio and233 underpredict their ratio. This remains
true even though most fixed training-batch risk losses decrease. Error on a
changing source distribution, selective use of predictions and sparse difficult
events remain unresolved. No independent calibration role was opened, so this
experiment does not claim a calibration guarantee.

## 4. The Candidate Forecasts Can Be Worse Before Selection

Two-source neural forecasts versus the existing four-source models range from
-10.8544% to+3.4851% all-ADE gain. There are no positive intervals and16 negative
intervals across36 event-duplicated views. The earlier transport study already
identified this tradeoff. Extra tags cannot manufacture useful trajectory
information where the candidate itself is poor.

## 5. Runtime And Optimization Did Not Block The Study

All108 Torch controllers completed2,000 updates with finite logged losses and
gradients, saved checkpoints, matching supervised draws and exact prediction
replays. Risk gradient logs are pre-clipping; the registered clip remains5.
The pilot resumed within the final budget. This is not a NumPy fallback or a
truncated run. It is also not evidence of new trajectory-dynamics learning:
the trajectory producers were reused, not retrained.

## 6. A Positive Mean Cannot Erase A Locality Failure

Producer-policy equal-locality easy gain versus CV is positive in every view,
yet the worst locality degrades8.02939%. Its worst hard locality loses15.12363%
relative to the old stop controller. Reporting only the mean would change the
safety question. The seven easy failures are repeated views of one locality,
not seven independent failures or seven independent datasets.

## Repair Supported For The Next Study

First preserve the stronger four-source producing bundle rather than replacing
it with a weaker two-source floor. Assign distinct opened-source groups to
producer fitting, controller supervision and evaluation, so the same producer
can generate both controller-training and evaluation forecasts without training
on their source labels. Compare against the existing cross-fitted supervision
on identical final forecasts. Register the comparison before any new fit/readout.

This is an unexecuted hypothesis, not a promised cure. It must retain zero-CV,
positive-easy, partial-label, worst-locality and realized-harm checks. It must
not choose a better branch using the present results or open reserved roles.

All evidence remains source-development, image-pixel raw-frame obs8/pred12,
with detector labels and no metric, seconds, human-gold, physical-safety,
true3D or foundation claim. Stage5C and SMC stay off.
