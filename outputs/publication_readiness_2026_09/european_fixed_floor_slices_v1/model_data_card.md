# Frozen Source-Diagnostic Card

## Material Passport

`fresh_run`: causal-slice computation, fitting/held score evaluation and
locality reductions. `cached_verified`:108signed-excess heads,108MSE controls,
the complete forecasting/floor/utility producer chain, source manifests,
geometry and frozen actions. `not_run`: new training, deployment, independent
confirmation, full legacy suite and cold raw-data reconstruction.

The inputs are318,969indexed queries from12already-exposed European source
localities. Predictions use eight observed steps and twelve requested future
steps at raw-frame stride12, with partial supervision masked only in the loss
and evaluation. Records are detector-derived silver, not human gold. Invalid
neighbor padding is ignored and no future timestamp is admitted by the new
causal diagnostic API.

For each frozen group, two localities fit causal quantiles with equal source
weight and two other localities receive those fixed cutpoints. The forecaster's
four localities and the floor controller's four localities are separate from
both. Future completeness and actual reference error only stratify outcome
analysis; they do not enter features or actions. Future-unknown rows remain in
the inference population.4,458selected unknown row-views have no measurable
outcome; neither zero loss nor safety is inferred for them.

The held readout contains5,741,442dependent row-views, not that many independent
examples. Locality aggregation precedes bootstrap. The12fixed localities are
never dropped to rescue an undefined slice. CIs are descriptive and unadjusted
across the many subgroup comparisons. Teacher/producer independence is checked
within the approved development protocol; no historical contaminated benchmark
is rehabilitated as confirmation.

No model weights or thresholds change. The direct-loss output components are
unidentified score bases, not calibrated probabilities or separately identified
cost moments. Observed easy net preservation remains distinct from positive
harm control. No deployment, Stage5C, SMC, metric/seconds, physical-safety,
true3D or foundation-model claim is supported.
