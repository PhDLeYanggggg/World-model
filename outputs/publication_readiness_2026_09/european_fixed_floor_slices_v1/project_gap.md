# Remaining Research Gap

## Material Passport

Fresh diagnostic associations from frozen development models, not new training
or independent confirmation. Image-local detector silver, obs8/pred12 rawstride12.

The experiment is reproducible but the learned intervention is not certified
or deployable. Average ADE relief does not satisfy a selected positive-harm
budget, and an oracle's opportunity is not proof of a learnable rule.

The next repair is a controlled causal-descriptor augmentation: encode observed
motion and predicted rollout disagreement explicitly, while keeping the parent
loss, floor, utility, training samples and source exclusions fixed. Match the
shared initialization and start additional feature weights at zero so initial
predictions are identical. Use fitting-only preprocessing and keep future
completeness/reference-error strata evaluation-only. Prespecify same-query
count ranking, harm, nonempty coverage and easy checks before training.

This feature hypothesis is still untested; no evidence here supports promoting
a subgroup discovered after readout, changing the2%budget, or opening reserved
data to make it succeed. It is a targeted learnability experiment, not yet a
paper contribution. Scene-joint allocation, independent-source calibration,
confirmation, public strong baselines and complete paper evidence remain open.

There is no reason to enlarge the neural forecaster until intervention learning
and risk transfer are better supported. Local runtime is adequate; disk space
needs a fresh preflight before another full checkpoint bank. HPC is an option
under the existing approved scheduler/access contract, not a reason to change
authentication, run on login nodes, or start duplicate jobs.

Current verdict: negative risk-transfer result localized; useful conditional
signals identified, no safe learned repair demonstrated. Keep deployment
unchanged. Not yet a submission-ready main method, true3D or foundation model.
Stage5C and SMC remain disabled.
