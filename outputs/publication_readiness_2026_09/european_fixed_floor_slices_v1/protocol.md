## Prespecified Diagnostics

Eight causal axes: mean/last observed displacement divided by inherited past
extent, path nonlinearity, mean absolute turn over nonzero consecutive steps,
valid neighbor-history occupancy, mean neural/floor rollout disagreement
divided by extent, unclipped standardized-feature RMS divided by the frozen
99% support limit, and feature clipping fraction. Extent is max(past ego and
valid neighbor norm,1); the clamp is inherited, not metric calibration.
Invalid neighbor padding is excluded. No future timestamps are accepted.

Each axis uses25th/75th weighted fitting-source quantiles, giving low/middle/high
bins. Fitting sources contribute equal total weight, including observed rows
with unknown future labels. Boundaries are never learned on held rows. Exact
boundary ties go to the higher bin; ties can leave empty bins, reported as such.
Also report the existing inside/outside99% support guard.

Separately, **evaluation-only** strata are unknown/partial/complete future
labels, and fitting-known-source quartiles of normalized floor error (plus
unknown). These outcomes cannot change inference or select a deployment rule.
No cross-product search, feature selection or best-slice promotion is allowed.

For every bin, role, locality and policy, retain additive normalized reference,
benefit, harm, score error/bias, row/known/selected counts, unknown interventions,
oracle and eligible oracle benefit. Both heads use their frozen causal guards.
Compare in-sample fitting diagnostics with held development diagnostics;
in-sample values are not generalization evidence. Predictions, decisions and
checkpoint hashes are checked against the previous sealed study.
