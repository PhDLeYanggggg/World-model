# A Small Accuracy Recovery, Not a Risk Repair

## Decision

Do not deploy the risk-priority arm. The registered predictive contrast is
positive, but the risk screen fails and the original raw-risk control remains
more accurate. Independent selection, calibration and confirmation stay closed.
This is a completed risk-head experiment over fixed forecasts, not a new neural
world-dynamics training result or independent generalization claim.

All108 causal action groups and the full numerical evaluation replay exactly.
The separate verification report records the independent arithmetic scope.
The action manifest was committed in `b0d319cc` before first readout. No new
weight, threshold, eligible subset or primary was selected from these results.

## What the Controlled Change Achieved

The only changed training factor caps the aggregate auxiliary gradient norm
at half the direct-risk norm. Both arms retain108 heads, identical initialization,
sample chains, architecture, AdamW and2,000 updates. All216 heads completed;
all uncapped states reproduce the old supervised controls. Only the first
repaired pair was fully retrained for exact training replay, not all108 pairs.

At the same intervention count in every query, risk-priority versus uncapped
ADE improves by **0.00374642%**, with a nominal3,000-draw locality-bootstrap
95% interval **[0.00128515%, 0.00680774%]**. Eleven of twelve locality point
contrasts are positive. The small effect is not a0.375% improvement and is not
independent confirmation after repeated development on these localities.

The frozen-error decomposition attributes the net change to more retained
benefit, not less harm. On the common full-floor diagnostic denominator,
benefit increases by0.00448299 percentage points and positive harm increases
by0.00073821 points; net gain is0.00374478 points. That denominator differs
from relative ADE and from selected-risk assessment.

## What Did Not Improve

- Relative to the original raw-risk matched control, the repaired arm still
  loses0.01435295% ADE, interval[-0.02330288%, -0.00611054%]. Recovering some
  performance from a worse control is not beating the existing method.
- Matched selected-risk violations increase from51/216 to54/216 dependent
  views. Each arm has25 undefined selected-risk views. Abstention is not zero
  selected risk; all views remain in the report.
- Occurrence Brier worsens from0.15813114 to0.19938745. Signed-risk MSE worsens
  from0.00444302 to0.00461287. Lower mean signed bias does not imply better
  conditional costs, allocation or calibration.
- Only46/108 repaired heads improve final direct-risk fitting loss. The cap
  activates on215,041/216,000 updates, with mean alpha0.03656; this is a large
  change in effective auxiliary updates, not proof of better optimization.
- At least19 undefined views are forced by unchanged raw/uncapped anchors,
  established before new outcomes. An auxiliary-gradient repair cannot make
  the current every-view-defined-risk screen pass. This structural limit is
  reported separately from the54 observed violations; it does not excuse them.

Easy preservation and zero-CV protection pass their developmental screens, but
these do not override failed risk or establish a physical-safety guarantee.

## Interpretation and Next Research Boundary

The prior diagnostic established gradient-norm imbalance. This experiment tests
one fixed response and shows that limiting that imbalance is insufficient.
The Euclidean pre-optimizer projection bound does not guarantee finite AdamW
descent or transport of conditional risk to a held locality. It would be wrong
to call this universal evidence against all multi-task weighting methods.

The next study should distinguish conditional risk-model error, useful-switch
ranking and insufficient source support, with a fitting-source-only design
before another action readout. Do not sweep cap weights on these outcomes.
Do not repeat global bias centering or claim selection optimism as an established
cause: those explanations already have controlled negative or incomplete
evidence. A learned probability cannot itself change an individual signed-risk
sign when it is strictly positive; its role here is query weighting.

Independent source calibration remains a separate unresolved requirement.
Existing finite-locality feasibility notes cannot be converted into a2% relative
ADE certificate by changing the denominator, clipping loss or counting overlapping
windows as independent scenes. No closed source role is opened by this result.
The current study can support a transparent optimization ablation/limitation,
not the main claim of a safe world model or submission readiness.

## Reproducibility and Limits

Sources are fresh_run paired Torch risk-head fitting, actions and readout;
upstream inputs, forecasts, floor and utility are cached_verified. A disk guard
interrupted the first action replay after28 exact groups; recovery reran all108
groups after free space recovered, without deleting data, lowering the10GiB
reserve or retraining. Completed action replay took922.81s and evaluation replay
76.21s. The intermediate record is retained rather than rewritten as final.

Only12 already-opened development localities, with repeated4/4/2/2 source roles
and three forecast seeds, are used. Nominal intervals do not account for the
entire accumulated research search. Full legacy integration and a cold raw-data
rebuild remain not_run. Observation8/prediction12 at raw stride12, image-local
detector-silver labels. No metric, seconds, human-gold, true3D, foundation,
calibration-certificate or deployment claim. Stage5C and SMC remain disabled.
