\section{Optional edits and the fixed-nominee bottleneck}
\label{supp:cv-admission}
We rotate three disjoint groups within the original fitting sources through
training, calibration and held scoring. Training and calibration use only
analytic proposals so no forecaster trained on a held source can provide
their supervision or selection data. The held menu includes frozen neural
predictions made by producers excluding those sources. A factorized head
predicts positive CV reference and harm masses, their easy shares, and
support or wholly missing probabilities. The expected missing debit is the
missing probability times causal peak displacement. These estimates do not
certify risk. An edge head shares the fixed nomination bank and can alter
which recipients are selected, not how many are admitted.

After \AdmissionUpdates{} updates, \AdmissionQualified{}/\AdmissionCalibration{}
calibration fits qualify. With no edits, selected support is undefined;
the calibrated policy simply returns CV. A separate fixed diagnostic at
\AdmissionEdits{} edits gives \AdmissionIndependent{}/72 original risk passes
for screening, \AdmissionGain{}/72 for same-count gain-only ranking, and
\AdmissionJoint{}/72 for joint screening. The paired lower utility contrasts
are \AdmissionRiskContrast{} CV-normalized points for risk versus gain-only
and \AdmissionJointContrast{} for joint versus independent. Intervals
resample nine sources after averaging seeds. These results cannot be used
to select a new calibration setting.

Hindsight withholding from the exact forced nominees verifies
\AdmissionCV{}/72 against CV alone but only \AdmissionDual{}/72 against
both CV and OLS4. Without early rank pruning there are \AdmissionUnpruned{}/72
dual-reference witnesses; making OLS4 directly available again gives
\AdmissionAddBack{}/72. These are solver witnesses, not causal policies.
No A or independent confirmation labels enter this study. The original
risk threshold, support requirement and coordinate interpretation remain.
