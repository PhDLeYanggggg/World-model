\section{Noisy donor training and full-path capacity}
We trained the same OLS4-anchored spline on clean or synthetically
perturbed OpenDD histories, with paired donor batches, initialization
and \CamNoiseUpdates{} total updates over both arms and three seeds.
We added AR(1) noise to the eight observations and kept each
absolute target unchanged. The perturbation does not simulate
missing detections, identity errors or camera calibration.
The three camera inputs below were previously exposed development
clips. The 13 reserved WEPDTOF sources were not scored again.

The noise-trained spline gains \CamNoiseWepOlsLower{} pp against
OLS4 on WEPDTOF after the missing-step debit. It is not consistently
better than median pair slope (\CamNoiseWepMedianLower{} pp).
Its easy positive-harm bound is \CamNoiseEasy\%, not a pass at 2\%.
Even before the missing-step debit, the OLS4 contrast is negative
at GMU (\CamNoiseGmuOlsKnown{} pp) and CMU
(\CamNoiseCmuOlsKnown{} pp). Both cameras have only eight matched
histories. We do not infer that unmatched histories are false
detections or fit camera-specific risk cutoffs. These ranges are
across donor seeds, not confidence intervals across sources.

On the WEPDTOF pilot, hold each query's eligibility and count cap
fixed. With the original radius, the expanded menu's risk-constrained
upper bound is negative in \CamNoiseCapped{}/3 views. With each
action at its full size, exact search verifies a plan meeting the
unchanged risk and two-reference utility checks in
\CamNoiseAnalytic{}/3 views for OLS4 and median slope alone and
\CamNoiseSpline{}/3 after adding the two splines. The larger menu
increases verified conservative gain against OLS4 by
\CamNoiseExtra{} pp. These plans use future outcomes and only
establish capacity under the changed action set. No causal
selector for these larger actions has been evaluated.
