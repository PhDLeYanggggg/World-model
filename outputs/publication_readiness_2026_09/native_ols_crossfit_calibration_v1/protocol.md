# Fitting-source calibration of selective OLS4 replacement

The previous fixed-zero threshold was active in almost all permitted intervention slots. Test whether a positive predicted score tracks positive realized conservative utility on unseen *fitting* sources. For each of nine original fitting groups and three seeds, fit the direct and neighbor-factorized architectures on the other eight groups. Constant support uses the held-fold factorized costs with the equal-source prevalence of its eight training groups. This yields 54 matched fits. No development, DroneCrowd calibration or independent confirmation outcomes enter threshold selection.

Use the frozen twelve-action OLS4-relative bank and same input normalization. Reconstruct query membership from past masks and verify the original 69,746 fit IDs. Train 600 steps, batch 128, with matched seed and draw sequences per fold. Freeze every held-source score before calculating thresholds. Score the fixed ladder 0, .0025, .005, .01, .02, .04, .08 for the dimensionless gain-minus-harm score. Use the same maximum of two replacements per query and keep the ordinary observed-query weighting. Report conservative utility (unknown rows debited by maximal forecast disagreement), positive harm, switch counts and empirical predicted-versus-realized net scores in fixed nomination bins.

For each arm, only select a threshold with nonzero switches, positive lower endpoint of a paired 3,000-source bootstrap on fitting folds, and positive source mean in at least seven of nine groups. Pick maximum mean, then fewer switches, then the more conservative threshold. This is an exploratory selection criterion, not a formal interval after threshold search, not the original CV safety gate, and not external calibration. If no policy qualifies, report fallback and do not reopen exposed development labels. If any qualifies, apply frozen thresholds to the existing full-fit models, freeze development paths before targets, and evaluate once alongside zero-threshold controls and CV/OLS4. Do not retune on development. Primary external risk, role assignments, and confirmation access are unchanged. No metric/time claims, deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
