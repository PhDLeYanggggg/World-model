# Acquiring Easy Reference Without Losing Paired Utility

Use the frozen direct policy and bank on the same 102 exposed
Moving A recording views. Focus on all 35 views whose direct easy
upper bound is undefined: 18 with no switches, 17 with active
switches but no selected easy reference. This is a retrospective
stratum, not an inference input. Keep the original observed 2%
and reference-support rules, 8/12 annotation steps, native image
coordinates, and original at-most-two per-query cap (also fewer
than the query population).

Require exactly one insertion from the existing bank on a
previously unswitched agent row. It must supply positive observed
easy reference. For this zero-selected-easy stratum, an insertion
with positive easy excess cannot meet the easy budget, even if
all removable original easy excess is deleted: all such original
excesses are nonnegative. This allows exact filtering. Within a
query, lower easy excess and higher paired gain dominate; the
insertion is known easy, so its all excess equals easy excess and
its signed easy cost is negative unweighted gain.

Minimize deletions under the same risk conditions and nonnegative
paired completion-lower utility versus direct. Keep the missing
outcome penalty on each deletion. Permit at most one deletion of
an originally selected action per query. Compare:

1. Insertion anywhere, no deletion.
2. Joint edit with insertion strictly before the first direct
   switch (all queries eligible if the direct stream is empty).
3. Joint edit with insertion anywhere, no deletion before it.
   Insertion and deletion in the same query are allowed.
4. Joint edit with the chronology restriction relaxed.
5. The ordered joint edit restricted to anchors in the frozen
   original eight-slot family-diverse candidate shortlist.

Postcheck every witness against the exact scorer and query cap.
Report optimal feasible, other checked feasible, infeasible, no
eligible anchor and unresolved separately; time limits do not
prove infeasibility. Report total deletions only over checked
witnesses, and which views are rescued by candidate, timing, or
combined edit restrictions. Save only aggregate receipts; do not
export row choices. A chronology constraint is not proof of
causal inference: the oracle still sees development outcomes.
No fitted model or independent evaluation, rule change, Stage5C,
SMC, metric, seconds or physical safety claim.
