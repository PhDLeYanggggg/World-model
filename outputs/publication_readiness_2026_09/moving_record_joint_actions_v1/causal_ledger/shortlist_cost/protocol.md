# Risk Selection Within A Fixed Eight-Action Shortlist

Use the previously exposed Moving A 8/12 annotation-step image-space
task, same 14 sources and three forecaster seeds. Preserve the bank,
direct policy, original 2% risk/support rules, and 7 fitting / 6
calibration / 1 held source per cell. No independent outcome access.

Build the same eight-slot family-diverse shortlist in every comparison,
from the original joint easy-support score. Keep the highest scoring
candidate from each available family before filling any remaining slots
in score order. The first candidate is exactly the old joint winner.
Build fitting shortlists using donor-excluded cost and ranking heads.
Use all eligible fitting queries and weight those before the first
direct switch fourfold; weight recordings and sources equally after
normalization. Fit one multi-target ridge with shortlist descriptors
for lower utility, all and easy risk excess, signed easy degradation,
and the positive-supported event. No labels or future positions enter
the inference features. Calibration and held features use the full
seven-source heads.

Compare predicted lower utility alone with predicted lower utility
minus positive parts of the three predicted risk costs. Both select
at most one opening before direct's first intervention. Calibrate
only the selected action's predicted positive-event score on the
same fixed donor grid and preservation criteria. The unthresholded
first-query comparisons share the identical queries, count and
shortlist, including the original joint winner control. Do not
change any direct action or certify a risk pass from a prediction.

Report same-query paired completion-lower utility, full-policy
observed risk, counts and zero-direct support. Separately compare
shortlist and final-candidate recall within the first two eligible
queries of the registered 18 zero-direct views. Recall scoring uses
outcomes only after decisions freeze; it is not a policy result.
No forecast retraining, deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
