# Deliberate Online Waiting Changed Actions But Lost Utility

On the same 102 exposed Moving A recording views, the causal reservation
policy holds 137 earned slots and later releases 70. It leaves 67 unused.
Thirty-five views finish at exactly the direct total, including zero-total
views. The policy receives neither future labels nor the eventual total
and does not change earlier outputs. The candidate forecasts and estimated
risk costs stay fixed.

| Policy | Switches | Full original passes | Undefined easy | Signed easy net >2% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Direct | 6,952 | 2 / 102 | 35 | 8 |
| One-slot reservation | 6,885 | 3 / 102 | 34 | 9 |

The paired completion-lower ADE gain relative to direct is **-0.00505 pp**
(95% whole-source bootstrap interval **[-0.01073, -0.00031]**). Thus,
explicit waiting does activate the mechanism, but thresholding on the
estimated marginal gain does not produce a reliable improvement or a
pass of the unchanged observed risk contract. This is not a matched
volume comparison; the separate hindsight experiment matches volume.

All causal decisions were frozen before this scoring run. The direct
metrics match the previous recorded results exactly. Development only;
no policy promotion, independent confirmation, Stage5C or SMC.
