# Replace Pooled Reopening Costs With Source-Stable Costs

On exposed Moving A, preserve the seven fitting sources, six unused
sources and one held source of each calibration-size fold. Reuse the
same past-only bank, donor-excluded fitting proposals, candidate masks
and normalized two-channel veto targets. Fit the full residual model and
seven leave-one-fitting-source-out residual models. For each predicted
downward risk revision add one jackknife standard error, capped at zero
revision. Leave upward revisions unchanged. This is a sensitivity penalty
on learned costs, not a confidence bound, threshold grid or safety claim.

Compare against the pooled residual without threshold and the same
no-reopening control. All policies match switch counts query by query;
original witnesses, budget arithmetic and evaluation are unchanged.
Primary: stable minus pooled paired completion-lower utility with 3,000
whole-source bootstrap draws after averaging the same three frozen
forecaster seeds. Also report full original passes, undefined easy risk,
signed easy violations and matched action differences. No calibration or
held labels fit the residual or choose a margin; the margin is fixed here.
No new independent evaluation, deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
