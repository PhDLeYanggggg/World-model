# Bounded Substitution In A Diverse Forecast Bank

Earlier Moving amplitude-head experiments changed the score shape for
one forecast family without meeting the recording-risk requirements. Here
the scientific question is different: can a ledger with multiple forecast
families spend its fixed intervention count more safely if each original
forecast also has half and quarter displacement from the CV floor?

Fixed exposed-A population, eight observed and twelve future native
annotation steps, original image coordinates, three actual forecast-bank
seeds, source-excluded fitting, and original 2% risk/support contract.
Keep full actions and their learned costs exactly. For each new level fit
the original actionwise ridge to the actual blended paths, with identical
descriptor, normalization and fitting weights. Blending adds no future
feature, label-dependent action or learned residual.

Primary: bounded bank vs full bank, at the original exact per-query
counts. Fixed half and quarter versions of the same full-bank selections
distinguish smaller displacement from learned selection. Report both
controls, not only the better one. Secondary: same free chronological
allocator with the two banks and unconstrained counts. No choice among
arms using exposed A to claim confirmation. Require a positive lower
interval and recording-level safety under the existing rules for an
improvement claim. Count each nonidentical path as one switch regardless
of amplitude. Zero or undefined support is never certified.

By convexity of the norm, for a fixed selected row and the same observed
mask, positive ADE harm of the blend is bounded by its amplitude times
the full action's positive harm. Its maximum path distance from CV also
scales by amplitude. This is a per-action envelope, not evidence that the
learner ranks well, meets the fixed 2% limit, or preserves utility. New
allocations need not obey that comparison to the old policy because the
selected rows can change. Read all 102 recording/seed views and 3,000
paired source bootstrap draws. External roles and all outcome access
boundaries are unchanged; no deployment, Stage5C, or SMC.
