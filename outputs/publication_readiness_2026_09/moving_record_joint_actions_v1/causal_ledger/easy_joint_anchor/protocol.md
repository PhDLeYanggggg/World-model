# Joint Easy-Support Opening Action

Retiming cannot help the 18 zero-intervention views. Test at most one
additional action before the original causal policy's first intervention
(or earlier than recording end when it never intervenes). This changes
the controller and may increase coverage; it does not change the official
2% realized-risk rule, reference support or original controls.

On the same Moving A 8/12 annotation-step image-coordinate task, learn
four source-balanced ridge outputs from the same action descriptors:
observed easy and candidate's standalone all-harm excess nonpositive
jointly; observed easy marginally; candidate's all-harm excess nonpositive
marginally; and positive all-harm excess. Missing labels contribute their
unchanged disagreement bound in the excess. Score joint minus harm
versus the product of the two marginals minus harm. Use the same model,
regularization, frozen trajectories, and fit sources. The product is an
independence baseline, not a probability identity.

Reuse the seven fitting and six calibration sources from the fixed-fit
study for each held source, separately for three forecaster seeds.
Recompute direct calibration actions with the held fold's original
coefficients (do not use their separately fitted out-of-fold controls).
Choose the threshold from the fixed grid maximizing restored complete
risk passes on calibration recordings without losing prior complete passes,
creating signed-easy violations, or negative summed paired lower utility.
Break ties by fewer failed standalone anchors, utility, count and threshold.
Otherwise abstain. Report all 42 threshold cells. Test outcomes do not
select thresholds. Direct acts as before; the one extra action is not
assumed to satisfy the old predicted prefix model. Evaluate using the
unchanged observed scorer, not the learned classifier.

Primary: calibrated joint versus calibrated marginal and the unchanged
direct policy on all 102 exposed views, including actual switch counts,
whole-source paired completion lower, complete risk passes, and the
registered 18 zero-direct-switch views. Secondary: unconditional first
opening with joint score versus original utility in exactly the same
queries and counts, to separate score choice from calibration/coverage.
This is explicitly diagnostic, not a deployment claim. No independent
labels, teacher retraining, Stage5C or SMC.
