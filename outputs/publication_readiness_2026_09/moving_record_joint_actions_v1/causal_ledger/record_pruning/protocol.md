# Deletion-Only Repair Of The Direct Recording Policy

Test whether removing actions is sufficient when a good early action
cannot repair full-record risk. Use the exposed Moving A native
8/12 annotation-step development task and the exact frozen direct
stream. At most one already selected action may be removed from any
query. A removal restores the causal floor; no action is added,
replaced, or moved. The original all/easy and signed easy 2% and
reference-support requirements apply without changes.

**Capacity.** On exposed labels only, maximize the retained direct
count under the actual risk/support rules and the removal cap, first
without a utility constraint and then requiring nonnegative paired
completion-lower utility versus direct. The latter retains the
unknown-label penalty of a deletion, including when deleting it
improves the bound versus the floor. Check every returned witness
with the unchanged original scorer. Report unsupported, infeasible
and unresolved separately. Keep only aggregate receipts, not choices.

**Causal comparison.** For each of the original 42 source/seed cells,
form training candidates by regenerating direct choices on each
fitting source with its other six fitting sources, excluding the
outer held and calibration sources. Fit a single ridge head from
past descriptors, family identity and direct predicted costs to the
normalized paired deletion gain, all/easy excesses, signed easy cost
and easy reference. Weight queries within records and records within
sources. Rank current selected actions by predicted deletion gain
plus the two signed excesses and positive signed easy degradation,
minus nonnegative easy reference. The rule uses no outcome input,
future queries or completed recording totals. On six calibration
sources, select from the fixed threshold grid only if it adds a full
pass, loses none, adds no signed easy violation, and has nonnegative
paired lower utility in aggregate. Otherwise do not prune. Compare
to direct and a matched-query control that removes the largest
original predicted normalized excess at the same queries and counts.

Freeze learned decisions before evaluating. A learned improvement
requires more complete passes, no additional signed easy violations,
and a strictly positive paired 95% lower-utility interval versus
direct (3,000 resamples of 14 sources with three seeds averaged within
source). The matched comparator and label-aware capacity diagnose
mechanisms, not new risk certificates or independent evidence.
No reserve access, Stage5C, SMC, metric or seconds claim.
