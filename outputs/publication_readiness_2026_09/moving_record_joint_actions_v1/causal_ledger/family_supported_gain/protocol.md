# Supported Gain Ranking Within Fixed Forecast Families

The previous cost portfolio dropped useful families. This experiment
preserves the exact ordered family slots and legal-action budget of
the family-diverse control. It changes only the row that represents
a family; repeated family slots, if any, still use distinct actions.
Keep the frozen forecaster bank, original direct actions, past-only
descriptors, and the final shortlist risk-aware head.

For each family fit two ridge outputs, using seven sources from the
original source-role cell. The reward is positive completion-lower
utility divided by the causal scale on actions with observed easy
reference support and all-harm within the original 2% bound; reward
is zero otherwise. The second output is the sum of positive all/easy
risk excess normalized by the same scale. Rank legal actions within
each family by nonnegative predicted reward minus nonnegative
predicted excess, breaking ties by stable row order. Use the original
per-source fitting weights and ridge penalty. The original feature
coordinates are recalculated for the selected rows; no targets enter
held-source inference.

Retain the old final choice head and recalibrate only the opening
threshold on the original six donors, with the unchanged grid.
Repeat for fourteen held sources and three forecaster seeds. Freeze
all choices before scoring. Measure first-two eligible query recall
before direct's first intervention on the 102 exposed development
views and the original 18 zero-direct views. Record retained/added/
lost view counts and useful action counts by family. Report matched
query/count and calibrated effects separately, with 3,000 paired
source resamples and the three seeds averaged within source.

Policy improvement requires more complete passes, no extra signed
easy violations, and a strictly positive paired 95% interval versus
the family-diverse calibrated control. Retrieval recall alone is
not policy evidence. No change to 2% rules, reference support, or
undefined cases. Native image coordinates and 8/12 annotation steps;
no reserved outcome access, Stage5C, SMC or deployment change.
