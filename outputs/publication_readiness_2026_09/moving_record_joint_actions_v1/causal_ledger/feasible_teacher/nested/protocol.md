# Risk Correction Of Mistaken Donor Proposals

The first constrained teacher and gain-only teacher gave identical labels
in every active query. The label-aware gain maximum already satisfied the
two budgets, so that experiment cannot identify a teaching benefit.

Here the objective is the causal utility estimated by a model fitted
without either the outer evaluation source or the demonstration donor.
For each ordered held/donor pair, the two teachers use that **same**
estimated utility. One imposes the original observed all/easy excess
constraints at zero starting credit; the other keeps the resulting
query count and ignores those constraints. The donor's outcomes appear
only in the constrained teacher, not in the utility proposal or features.
The resulting discrepancy targets actual predicted-utility mistakes.

The two students, frozen input bank, evaluation counts, inference costs,
source bootstrap, original 2% rules and support handling are unchanged
from the parent study. No development selection of a threshold or donor
roster. Each held-source fit excludes it from teacher generation and
student fitting. The three seeds are the existing independently trained
forecasters, not newly trained trajectory models. Decisions freeze
before scoring; no donor example actions are exported.

Report the number of differing teacher queries, primary paired completion
lower and observed recording risk. If the teachers or their resulting
policies are identical, do not claim evidence about the mechanism.
This is exposed development evidence, no new outcome role, deployment,
Stage5C or SMC.
