# Support-Specific Retrieval And Ordered Joint Repair

The prior hindsight study finds 33/35 missing-support views repairable
by one ordered insertion and optional deletions, versus 18/35 with
the old shortlist. This experiment tests past-only retrieval and
operating policies, not new forecast training. Preserve the frozen
direct stream, 8/12 annotation-step exposed Moving A cohort, native
image coordinates, and original 2% and support requirements.

Before retrieval, mask already-switched rows in both arms. The control
uses family diversity on this eligible pool. The alternative fills
the same eight slots by alternating estimated easy credit, predicted
easy reference mass, joint easy/safe score, and predicted gain,
without duplicating actions. At evaluation, measure each new list's
checked ordered hindsight capacity in the original 35 missing-support
views. Do not route on that retrospective stratum.

Use the same frozen causal heads from each original seven-source
fit and the same six separate donors for thresholds. At each query,
test insertion only before accumulating more than a fixed amount
of predicted easy reference from current/past direct actions.
Require positive estimated inserted easy mass, a predicted joint
easy/safe score above threshold, positive paired predicted gain,
and nonpositive incremental all/easy excess. For a joint arm, allow
one concurrent deletion to satisfy the query cap or improve the
increment; after insertion, allow at most one deletion per query
with nonnegative predicted paired gain and excess and at most
.05 normalized predicted easy reference. No deletion before
insertion, no prediction-family replacement of an existing action,
and no final recording quantities enter the decision.

Compare family retrieval plus joint edits, support retrieval plus
insertion only, and support retrieval plus joint edits. Use identical
threshold grids (prefix reference .25 or 1, confidence 0/.1/.2/.35).
Select independently per arm on donors, requiring more complete
passes, no lost passes or extra signed easy violations and
nonnegative aggregate paired lower gain. If unavailable, retain
direct. Score held sources only after decision freeze. Report
coverage differences, not a matched-count ranking contrast.

An operating improvement requires more passes, no additional
signed easy violations and a positive paired 95% lower utility
interval versus direct over 3,000 source resamples with three seeds
averaged within source. Compare retrieval capacity separately,
with original-scorer checks of label-aware witnesses. No new heads
fit, no oracle choices exported, independent outcomes, deployment,
metric or seconds claim, Stage5C, or SMC.
