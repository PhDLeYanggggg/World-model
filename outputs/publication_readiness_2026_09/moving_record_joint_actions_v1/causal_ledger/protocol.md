# Causal Recording Ledger On A Frozen Full Candidate Bank

The screened matched oracle supports only 30 of 102 views, while the full
bank at identical counts has at least 72 feasible views. The next question
is whether useful candidates can survive a past-only allocator. Keep the
eight alternatives, CV floor, source-excluded predicted costs, A rows and
original observed 2% scoring fixed. No new weights or threshold search.

**Chronology.** Reset two balances to zero at the first frame of each
recording. At each query choose zero, one or two distinct interventions,
at most one candidate per row and at most n-1 rows. Costs are the frozen
predicted all/easy completion harm minus .02 times the corresponding
predicted reference. Spend only balances from earlier choices. No later
query, future label, actual elapsed outcome or future length is used to
update a balance. This differs from offline optimization that can inspect
later query inputs. Negative-cost actions may fund other actions; reject
only exact CV-equivalent trajectories, not pointwise-risk failures.

**Matched arms.** Original filter-first. Full-bank query optimization
with no carry. Full-bank chronological ledger with carry. All three use
exactly the original causal count in each query, including zeros. The two
optimized arms maximize the same current normalized utility as the point
ranking; no claim of globally optimal temporal scheduling. Exact within
query for counts up to two; dominated actions on the same row may be removed
without changing the predicted problem. Report any witness fallback.

**Secondary arms.** Free-count uncalibrated ledger; original joint-calibrated
pointwise filter; free-count ledger with those same source-nested harm and
reference offsets. Free-count arms maximize predicted utility over zero,
one and two, so may abstain. These are not matched-count results. A
predicted-feasible prefix is not the observed recording-risk test; empty
or undefined observed reference remains unsupported. No identity fallback
certification is added.

**Evaluation.** Freeze all six arms before reading outcomes. Primary:
ledger matched versus query matched, direct paired completion-lower ADE.
Also compare with filter-first at identical query counts, and the
calibrated free pair. Replay both frozen pointwise controls exactly. Track
observed pair displacement, all/easy/hard utility, ADE/FDE availability,
tails, count changes and all 102 observed recording-risk views. 3,000
source bootstrap draws after averaging three actual forecast seeds.
A is exposed development. No independent outcomes, deployment change,
metric/seconds or physical safety claim, Stage5C or SMC.
