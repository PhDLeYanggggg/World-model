# Learn Which Current Agent To Switch

The fixed-row amplitude experiment leaves little decision leverage.
This experiment lets the allocator change the agent and forecast family,
holding every current-query switch count fixed. Population: exposed
Moving A, 125,540 rows, 1,613 queries, 34 recordings, 14 source groups,
eight observed and twelve future annotation steps in image coordinates.
Use the frozen full-amplitude eight-candidate bank from the ledger, with
three actual forecaster seeds and no future inference input.

Target the same normalized scalar in both new ranking models: conservative
gain minus the all and easy positive-harm budget excesses. Wholly unknown
outcomes use the same existing maximum-path radius in all three terms;
this makes the training target conservative and does not redefine observed
utility, support or risk. Fit separate slopes per forecast family using
the original descriptors and ridge 0.002 on all coefficients. Pointwise
MSE and within-current-query centered MSE differ only in their objective.
Centering removes a common query offset and is algebraically equivalent
to weighted least squares on all legal within-query action differences.
Use equal donor-source and query weights; share each query weight
equally across legal actions. Every fit excludes the evaluated source.
No other source's fitted choice, calibration, or outcome enters its
action inputs. The compared original and learned objectives all use the
same frozen predicted costs and legal candidates in the chronological
ledger, with its original exact count witness.

Primary: centered vs pointwise with identical counts and costs. Secondary:
each vs original; change in selected agents versus just forecast family;
all/easy completion upper, signed easy degradation, hard gain and
undefined support. Use 3,000 paired source bootstrap draws after averaging
the three forecast banks and keep all 102 recording views. This is a
development test, not a new independent result. No modification to the
2% safety rules or deployment. No new trajectory model, Stage5C or SMC.
