\paragraph{Calibration after action selection.}
On the same seven exposed OpenDD locations, we fit and replay 147 native
Torch heads over three seeds. Each inner donor is excluded from its fit
and CV-derived easy cutoff; the outer location is excluded from model
and policy selection. The policy maximizes predicted minimum gain against
CV and OLS4 over an analytic bank of three bridge amplitudes, with at most
two edits and at least one unedited agent per query. Adding a learned
easy-reference support hurdle to signed safety admission yields
\SelectedBridgeHurdle{}/\SelectedBridgeViews{} risk passes with positive
gain against both references, compared with \SelectedBridgeSafety{} for
safety-only and \SelectedBridgeIndependent{} for independent utility at
the same query counts. The paired direct gain over matched independent is
\SelectedBridgePoint{} pp (95\% location-bootstrap interval
[\SelectedBridgeLow{}, \SelectedBridgeHigh{}] after seed averaging); gain
against CV is \SelectedBridgeCV{} pp. With future labels and the identical
bank and query cap, an exact diagnostic verifies feasible, positive
actions for \SelectedBridgeCapacity{}/\SelectedBridgeRecordings{} distinct
recordings; \SelectedBridgeAbsent{} lack easy support. The learned ranking
does not recover this capacity. The original risk criterion is unchanged,
and this development result neither qualifies the policy nor opens
DroneCrowd confirmation.
