# Research Gap After the Selected-Set Control

The implementation and numerical-reproduction gap for this72-head experiment
is closed. The method gap is not: actual-selected-set empirical calibration did
not improve complete source-OOF support and lost conservative utility. Neither
another iteration nor choosing the secondary reference-only arm is justified by
the registered result.

Priority1 is conditional decision reliability: separate source-recording
instability from existing causal-support/cross-head effects before proposing a
new risk head. Priority2 is outcome support: three remaining completion failures
are driven by unknown-label envelope mass, while one is genuinely observed
harm. They require different evidence; no future-availability gate is allowed.
Priority3 remains independent evaluation: the selection/calibration/confirmation
roles stay closed, and these exposed development localities cannot become a
fresh test by relabeling them.

The full target-transfer diagnosis from european_selected_pool_v1 remains valid
and negative. This source-only experiment does not overwrite its216 transfer
views, re-evaluate targets or establish cross-domain safety. A new neural fit is
not yet justified by this particular calibration hypothesis. Existing frozen
forecasts are preserved; no deployment is promoted.

What can be used in a draft: a reproducible negative calibration control and a
concrete distinction between observed harm and unknown-completion uncertainty.
What cannot be a main claim: independent generalization, a safe world model,
new neural dynamics, or an accepted-set calibration guarantee. Source selection
and denominator-shift observations connect to prior calibration literature;
they are not novelty by themselves. The CVPR-ready method/evidence package is
still incomplete. No metric/seconds, human-gold, true3D or foundation claim;
Stage5C and SMC off.
