# Why This Is Not Another Pointwise Deviance Trial

The completed[historical easy-harm deviance study](../european_easy_harm_deviance_v1/readout_verified_v3/conclusions.md)
retains its negative result:full paired lower utility versus quadratic
-0.902716pp,59/72known easy-risk violations and1/72finite full-policy support.
Those72views,old32-wide heads and binary producer contracts are not the present
multi-action experiment,and are not silently renamed or counted as fresh.
Changing a sparse pointwise loss again is not the selected next action.

The[current frozen diagnosis](../european_selected_cost_audit_v1/readout.md)
finds selected-region EH bias despite an approximately calibrated all-legal
EH mass median. The new trial changes the training estimand to fixed policy-
region mean costs,using the full398-input scene cost network. It does not fit
posthoc scalar multipliers. Existing[iterative selected-set calibration](../european_selected_set_calibration_v1/conclusions.md)
and[scalar moment matching](../european_cost_mass_v1/conclusions.md) remain
negative controls,not proof this neural variant works.

## Primary Research Context

[Zhao et al.,NeurIPS2021](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/bbc92a647199b832ec90d7cf57074e9e-Abstract.html)
already study calibration relative to downstream decisions rather than only
global probability accuracy. Their multiclass decision-calibration algorithm
is not implemented or theoretically reproduced by this continuous-cost loss.
Calibration for decisions is not claimed as a new M3W concept.

[Sahoo et al.,NeurIPS2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/0e65972dce68dad4d52d063967f0a705-Paper.pdf)
show that average calibration can fail to predict threshold-decision losses
and develop a stronger CDF-based notion. Our empirical fixed-region moment
regularizer is not their CDF recalibration algorithm or a threshold guarantee.

[Kiyani et al.,ICLR2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/b8b6df58745740e54f00d84534bd182d-Paper-Conference.pdf)
derive robust decision rules from specified partial-calibration guarantees.
Their H-calibration residual tests and minimax formulation motivate separating
cost estimation from trust in a plug-in policy. We have not established those
conditions,bounded-outcome assumptions or a minimax guarantee for these costs.

This is a measured empirical repair,not yet a main-method novelty or submission
claim. Formal independent calibration,successful multi-agent joint selection,
strong matched forecasting controls and confirmation still remain required.
