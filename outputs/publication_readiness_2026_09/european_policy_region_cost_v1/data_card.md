# Region-Moment Comparison Data

No new dataset or trajectory predictor is introduced. Source identity,adapter
coordinates and cohort boundaries are inherited unchanged from the
[EuropeanSquares data card](../european_shared_reference_cost_v1/data_card.md)
and the matched static-scene experiment.

The public-webcam release is used here as detector-track image box midpoints
with8observed/12requested steps and raw stride12. It is not SDD official gold,
metric coordinates or seconds-calibrated trajectories. Static image-region
polygons are unverified region priors,not walkable maps. No test endpoint goal
construction,central velocity or future input is permitted.

The6contexts and3controller seeds repeat views over74unique fitting recordings
and31unique exposed inner-held recordings. The1,332fitting record/head/bank
views are mechanism diagnostics,not1,332independent recordings. Independent
selection,calibration,confirmation and DroneCrowd roles remain unopened.

New supervised statistics are means of existing known fitting labels inside
fixed old-teacher selected regions. They are not held statistics or independent
calibration. Unknown teacher-selected outcomes are not assigned zero cost.
They remain represented in later choice counts and completion bounds. Empty
selected risk remains not_estimable;count-matched comparisons are utility-only.

The aggregate archive contains numeric summaries and hashes,not trajectories,
images,weights or a redistributable data cache. Actual model inference requires
private source assets and checkpoints. The study cannot establish independent
world-model generalization,true3D,foundation,metric/seconds or deployment claims.
Stage5C andSMC remain off.
