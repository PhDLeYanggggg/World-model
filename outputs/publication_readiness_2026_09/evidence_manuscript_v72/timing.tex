\paragraph{Chronological feasibility at unchanged displacement budgets.}
Holding the exact edit count and slot radii per recording fixed, allowing
an edit to wait two queries raises label-aware joint witnesses from
\TimingFixed{} to \TimingShort{}; eight queries raise this to \TimingLong{},
and allowing advance as well gives \TimingBoth{}. All solutions satisfy the
original streaming credit constraints. Of \TimingEmpty{} zero-edit views,
\TimingQuota{} cannot earn even one edit credit. For such recordings,
every policy using that ledger has zero selected easy reference mass.
This is an identifiability limit of the specified budget and evaluation,
not evidence that zero edits pass the risk check.

The causal greedy wait rules lose to the frozen reference at the same
offered budget. A second experiment trains an explicit value difference
between emitting the original edit and waiting up to two queries.
At inference, unmodified edits retain priority, and waiting edits cannot
consume their token reserve. The conservative learned variant moves
\TimingMoved{} slots and drops \TimingDropped{}. It clears \TimingLearned{}
joint checks versus \TimingAnchor{} for the frozen policy, with direct
gain \TimingGain{}. We do not infer controller superiority or independent
generalization from the hindsight capacity.
