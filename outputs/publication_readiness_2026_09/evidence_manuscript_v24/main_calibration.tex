\section{Frozen Transfer to DroneCrowd Calibration}
On the separate release of Wen et al.~\cite{wen}, we freeze the
development controller and all forecasts for \DrCalQueries\ queries
in 40 calibration recordings (15 exclusion groups) before
reading outcomes. The prediction bank, CV thresholds, and original
2\% rule are unchanged. Intervals use 3,000 paired source draws after
averaging the three seeds. These are calibration groups, not
verified independent physical sites.

At the same \DrCalJointSwitch\ switches per arm, ungated joint selection
has a conservative individual gain of $\DrCalJointIndependentGain$
percentage points over independent selection, but only
$\DrCalJointAdditiveGain$ over the additive pair cost. The
full-grid lower relative-path gain is $\DrCalPairIndependent$ pixels
versus independent and $\DrCalPairAdditive$ versus additive.
Table~\ref{tab:dr-cal} separates those matched controls from gating.
Joint is substantially worse than OLS4: $\DrCalJointOLSGain$ points
on the observed CV denominator. OLS4 also fails the full risk rule.

All four registered thresholds make \DrCalGateSwitch\ switches and pass
\DrCalGatePass/120 checks. Easy risk is undefined in \DrCalGateUndefined\
views. Their paired conservative CV gain is $\DrCalGateGain$ points,
whose lower interval endpoint is exactly zero. No threshold qualifies,
and all 72 confirmation recordings remain unopened. The matched
benefit over independent selection therefore does not establish
deployment or generalization.
