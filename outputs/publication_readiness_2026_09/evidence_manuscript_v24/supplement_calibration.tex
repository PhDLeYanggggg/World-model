\paragraph{Calibration accuracy and support.}
The frozen calibration roster contains \DrCalRows\ agent-window
requests across \DrCalQueries\ queries, not independent samples.
We average each agent's error on observed steps for ADE and use
only observed step-12 endpoints for FDE. Query means are merged
within source and seed before seed and source averaging.
The CV, OLS4 and ungated joint ADEs are \DrCalAdeCV, \DrCalAdeOLS,
and \DrCalAdeJoint\ pixels; their FDEs are \DrCalFdeCV, \DrCalFdeOLS,
and \DrCalFdeJoint. Pair-additive ADE/FDE are \DrCalAdeAdditive/
\DrCalFdeAdditive\ pixels. Only \DrCalHardRows\ rows meet the fixed
hard criterion, and \DrCalHardMissing/45 source-seed cells have no
hard ADE support. We do not report a macro hard result or retune
the difficulty cut. Equal-count joint versus additive gives a
full-grid lower pair gain of $\DrCalPairAdditive$ pixels; against
independent it is $\DrCalPairIndependent$. Unobserved pair errors
are bounded by disagreement, not replaced by zero. This is
relative geometry under matched proposal counts, not physical
safety. A calibration failure does not justify a second threshold
search or access to confirmation.
