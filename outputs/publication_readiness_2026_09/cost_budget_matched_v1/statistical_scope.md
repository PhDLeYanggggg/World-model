# Statistical Scope

## Material Passport

Interpretation of the fixed equal-capacity/equal-budget objective study.
All11 risk types below were considered; these are not11 passed scientific
gates. Numerical verification and independent confirmation are different.

| Risk checked | Finding |
|---|---|
|Aggregation reversal|Aggregate candidate easy improvement coexists with deathCircle degradation. Both are reported; this is heterogeneity, not proof of a strict Simpson paradox.|
|Ecological inference|Four-site means do not establish individual-agent protection. Exact-CV harms, scene/seed failures and unobserved outcomes remain explicit.|
|Selection bias|Selected-row mean harm is conditional on each policy. It describes intervention error, not a population causal effect.|
|Collider conditioning|Future-defined easy/hard/complete labels are evaluation-only. Conditional analyses are not causal explanations and cannot become inference features.|
|Base-rate neglect|Benefit/harm regressors are continuous costs, not calibrated probabilities. Unknown counts and all denominators remain visible.|
|Regression to the mean|All arms, seeds and sites are retained. Prior favorable reference results motivated this control; the comparison is not independent replication.|
|Survivorship/missing labels|Available-waypoint ADE, endpoint-valid FDE and complete-only cost diagnostics are distinct. Missing outcomes are neither zero error nor safe; full-grid bounds remain reported.|
|Multiple comparisons|The primary requires both fixed paired contrast lower bounds to be positive. It fails. Secondary policy intervals are descriptive and unadjusted, not replacement primary tests.|
|Forking paths|Registration preceded the new fits; thresholds and the primary candidate were fixed. The larger research history still exposed all four sites.|
|Causal interpretation|Matching capacity, budget and row draws removes that particular objective-comparison confound. It does not prove the mechanism of conditional harm or establish population generalization.|
|Reverse causality/leakage|Teacher/source exclusions and causal feature lineage are verified; futures are supervision/evaluation only. Scores and decisions precede aggregate readout. No new roles or test-dependent goals are introduced.|
