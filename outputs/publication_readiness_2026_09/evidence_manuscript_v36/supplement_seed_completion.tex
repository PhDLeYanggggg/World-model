\section{One Unknown Label Shared by Training Seeds}
We revisit the frozen OpenDD scene-context comparison without refitting.
For each missing target-step, let $a_s$ and $b_s$ be the reference and
alternative forecasts under seed $s$, respectively. All three seeds
refer to the same unknown coordinate $y$. For any permutation $\pi$,
\[
\left|\frac{1}{3}\sum_s
  \left(\|a_s-y\|_2-\|b_s-y\|_2\right)\right|
\leq \frac{1}{3}\sum_s\|a_s-b_{\pi(s)}\|_2 .
\]
Taking the least of six assignments yields an outer bound no wider
than the diagonal pairing; it need not be sharp for distance-to-point
functions. The observed term and fixed observed-CV normalization
are unchanged. We verify each original per-seed lower score first.

For an attainable sensitivity check, each incomplete target row takes
its missing coordinates from a single entire template: CV, or one
of the three seed paths of either compared policy. All released
coordinates are preserved. The resulting completions are shared by
seeds and score only the two policies in that comparison; the
extremizing completions need not agree across comparisons. These
templates are not inferred labels or physical trajectory constraints.
