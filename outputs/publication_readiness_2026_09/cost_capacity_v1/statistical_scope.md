# Statistical Interpretation Audit

## Material Passport

Validation of the frozen capacity-duration development experiment. Numerical
replay and separate arithmetic pass; independent research confirmation does
not exist. This audit introduces no test, threshold or new analysis population.
All11 statistical/methodological risk types were checked; this is not11pass
gates or a certification of generalization.

| Risk checked | Finding and interpretation |
|---|---|
|Simpson-type aggregation reversal|Aggregate easy improvement coexists with deathCircleeasy degradation. This is observed heterogeneity, not proof of a strict Simpson paradox. Report both; do not infer all-site preservation.|
|Ecological inference|Four-site mean gains do not imply individual-agent safety. Exact-zero query harm, per-site and per-seed outcomes remain explicit.|
|Berkson-type selection bias|Selected and new-only groups are policy-conditioned populations. Their post-readout score biases describe those groups; they do not identify a general causal relationship.|
|Collider conditioning|Easy status depends on future baseline error and is diagnostic only. Neither it nor complete-future status enters the decision rule. Conditioning on these labels does not establish causal mechanism or general safety.|
|Base-rate neglect|Continuous cost heads are not event probabilities. Selected counts,398unknown-ADE and3,917incomplete outcomes are reported; zero observed complete-CV harms do not establish zero deployment risk.|
|Regression to the mean|All fixed arms/seeds/sites are retained, not just the prior worst slice. The matrix was frozen before outcomes, but its motivation arose on explored sources. Improvement cannot be called independent replication.|
|Survivorship/missing outcomes|Scored ADE/FDE use available labels; cost means require complete labels. Unknowns remain unknown and incomplete-grid bounds permit harm. No complete-case safety extrapolation.|
|Look-elsewhere effect|One primary contrast was registered. Secondary factorial/policy intervals are unadjusted descriptive results, not multiple independent confirmations or a winner search.|
|Forking paths|Configuration, budgets, seeds, rules and primary comparison were frozen before training; no new threshold choice. The overall multi-experiment development history remains exposed and cannot be retrospectively made confirmatory.|
|Causal interpretation|Width/duration are controlled computational changes within this matrix. Same-population post-readout correlations do not prove a mechanism. The old fraction reference has a different capacity/budget, so that contrast does not isolate objective merit.|
|Reverse causality/leakage|Training uses legal labels; inference consumes archived causal inputs and forecasts only. Decisions were frozen before aggregate outcome readout. No future labels, outcome support or retrospective goals define inference. This relies on the verified input/teacher lineage, not merely temporal ordering of report files.|

The3000 bootstrap resamples use four physical sites after averaging seed errors.
They are conditional development uncertainty, not3000independent scenes or an
independent held-out guarantee. Multi-seed positive averages do not repair data
role exposure, incomplete labels, conditional harm underprediction or the
remaining budget mismatch of the objective comparator.
