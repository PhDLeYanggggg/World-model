# Interpretation Rules Fixed Before Producer Readout

This note does not contain a forecast result or change a gate. It records how
to distinguish the possible mechanisms without selecting a secondary result
after scoring. The primary is single-fold history, and all 72 trained arms
remain required. SOURCE outcomes are development-exposed.

1. History versus disabled asks whether the bundled new causal measurements
   help this fixed trajectory model. A positive training-loss difference alone
   does not answer it.
2. History versus current-only asks whether temporal detector context adds
   information beyond the latest box. Equal parameter counts do not equalize
   information rank, so this is not a pure parameter-count explanation.
3. History versus identity-key noise controls an equally sized additional
   branch. Noise is generated from causal identifiers, not shuffled future
   outcomes. If history does not beat it, useful observation semantics are not
   established by a disabled-only difference.
4. Known-label improvement and completion-lower improvement are separate.
   Partial labels still do not identify full-grid ADE; wholly missing rows
   cannot be removed just because the bound is unfavorable.
5. Complement models are secondary evidence, not substitutes for a failed
   single-fold primary. A strong individual seed or locality does not replace
   the fixed three-seed, all-locality result.
6. The inherited zero-motion budget makes stationary-to-moving misses
   structurally unfixable here. Their counts are retained. If this slice is
   important, an output-budget repair is a new registered experiment, not a
   reason to alter this run midway.
7. Better average proposals can coexist with harmful tails or easy cases.
   Per-recording mean and p95 errors include all six physical references.
   They describe raw proposals, not the complete 2% learned-policy risk rule.
8. Physical-plus-neural oracle opportunity uses future labels and is diagnostic
   only. It neither implements a causal selector nor proves learnable safe
   intervention. No routing threshold, alpha, ensemble or checkpoint is chosen
   in this readout.

The new input is not itself a complete novelty claim. Existing work already
studies noisy trajectory observations and attention over their histories;
the [primary-source reading scope](literature_position.md) records the portions
actually read. The paper still needs useful learned neural intervention,
appropriate joint-decision comparisons and independent scene risk evidence.
No result in this producer experiment alone restores those missing claims.
