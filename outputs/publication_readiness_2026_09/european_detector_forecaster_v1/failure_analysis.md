# What The Detector-Token Producer Study Establishes And Does Not

The complete readout is frozen. Separate PID55783 redoes all 72 groups exactly
in 1,181.451332 seconds, with peak RSS 2,947,039,232 bytes. verification.json
and evidence.json bind the numerical recovery. No model, threshold, primary arm
or dataset role is changed after readout.

## Positive Result: The New Information Reaches The Predictor

All 72 real Transformer producers complete the original 4,000 updates. The
primary history model's known masked-ADE gain over its own-fit-selected causal
reference is 8.811113%, versus disabled 4.922961%, current-only 5.961379% and
identity-noise 4.840526%. The history-minus-disabled paired change is
+3.888152 percentage points, locality-bootstrap CI [+2.245557, +5.655328].
History also beats current-only by +2.849733pp, CI [+1.869917, +3.784299], and
noise by +3.970587pp, CI [+2.403991, +5.668633]. These are conditional,
development-data intervals, not independent confirmation or correction for the
entire history of experiments.

The primary history-minus-disabled known change is positive in 11 of 12
localities; locality124 changes by -0.476506pp. The three producer-seed mean
changes are +4.198934, +4.298951 and +3.166570pp. This supports a useful
bundled past detector-history signal in these actual trajectory outputs,
not merely better classification of baseline choices. It does not isolate
confidence, shape, motion or IoU as the causal component.

## Failure 1: Unknown Outcomes Still Prevent The Required Claim

The 149,510 unique allowed readout queries contain 91,609 complete futures,
54,738 partial futures and 3,163 wholly missing futures. Primary views repeat
each query across two producer folds and three seeds: 897,060 row views, not
897,060 independent samples. The labels are supplied detector-silver midpoints.

The primary history-minus-disabled completion-lower contrast is -11.355480pp,
CI [-27.808700, -0.343903]. The corresponding upper endpoint is +19.131784pp,
CI [+9.639000, +34.365542]. The denominator is fixed known reference-error
mass. This is an unidentified-outcome envelope: a negative lower endpoint
does not prove observed harm or identify the full-population percentage gain.
It means the required positive completion-lower evidence is absent.

Eight locality-level lower endpoints are negative. This is not solely the
effect of one small locality. Unknown rows are retained, rather than removed
or assigned the known-label average. Partial masked ADE does not identify the
missing steps of a complete 12-step path.

## Failure 2: The Final-Step Metric Is Not Identified Everywhere

Within the allowed readout, locality082 has 78 unique queries: 62 have some
future labels, 16 have none, and zero have the final requested step or a
complete future. Consequently the required 12-locality FDE and complete-future
summary are not_estimable. Other locality-specific values remain available.
Dropping locality082 after seeing this result would change the registered
population and is not used to manufacture an overall endpoint result.

The current receipts establish missing support, not why each raw label is
missing. Clip boundaries, agent disappearance and import-rule provenance
still require a scoped raw-label audit. This document does not invent a cause
or claim that the whole SOURCE dataset lacks such labels.

## Failure 3: Positive Mean Accuracy Is Not Easy Preservation

Primary history easy-ADE gain is +0.551800%, CI [-4.154079, +6.630546].
Locality124 easy gain is -14.804979%; localities067,112,126 also have mean
easy degradation above 2%. Across 444 repeated recording/fold/seed views,
six known overall-mean views worsen by more than 2%, and 18 p95 views do so.
The worst known recording-mean gain is -3.869042%; worst p95 gain -13.580234%.
Those are descriptive proposal diagnostics, not the approved positive-harm
policy-risk estimator and not 444 independent recordings (there are 74).

This experiment trains no new routing policy. It does not test or pass the
complete 2% all/easy per-required-recording learned-policy rule. A positive
overall gain cannot cancel harm in easy cases, hide unknown outcomes, turn
empty selected risk into zero or authorize deployment.

## Failure 4: The Larger Complement Design Is Not A Substitute Winner

Secondary complement history known ADE gain is +5.744188%, CI
[-2.655877, +11.877062]. Its change from disabled is +1.231403pp, CI
[-3.004550, +4.786436]. This does not establish a stable complement-design
benefit. The fixed single-fold primary is retained, but this negative control
is not hidden or relabelled as success from more training localities.
Complement recording tails can also be poor: among 222 repeated views, three
known means and four p95 views degrade by more than 2%. Worst mean gain is
-39.572380%, and worst p95 gain -167.188063%. These are reference-relative
descriptive tail ratios, not a calibrated or complete policy-risk certificate.

## Explanations Ruled Out Or Not Yet Established

A supplementary comparison added after the complete readout retains all six
fixed physical methods, rather than choosing a new deployment reference.
Damped velocity0.97 has known gain +6.436269% against the same own-fit reference.
History-minus-this-fixed-baseline is +2.374844pp, paired locality CI
[-2.730947,+6.709627]. The analogous causal-CV difference is +6.059798pp,
CI [-1.074082,+12.028285]. These differences retain the original reference
mass denominator; they are not newly normalized percentage gains against each
physical method and do not change the primary gate. Stable superiority over
all strong fixed physical controls is not established.

- Runtime truncation is not the explanation: all 72 models finish, all 18
  disabled states and fresh predictions match their original models, and the
  first complete history refit is exact. No training budget was reduced.
- The inherited zero-motion constraint is real, but there are zero exactly
  stationary observed rows in this readout. It cannot explain this empirical
  result. That structural limit must not be presented as a measured root cause.
- Only 512 producer parameters are added, with current/noise controls, but the
  input information ranks differ. These results do not identify a pure
  parameter-count effect or a confidence-only mechanism.
- No RGB/scene-image encoder, JEPA contribution, new joint-routing method,
  cross-dataset transfer or physical safety theorem is demonstrated here.
- SOURCE was previously development-exposed. Locality exclusion and exact
  replay do not make it an untouched confirmation set.
