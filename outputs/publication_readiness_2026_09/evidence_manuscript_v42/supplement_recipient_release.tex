positive-harm and signed easy constraints, and positive selected
easy reference mass. It maximizes the smaller direct conservative
gain against CV and OLS4. Every positive allocation is re-scored
using the original paths and masks. We retain the solver's negative
upper bounds separately from risk infeasibility. The fixed-total
control gives \ReleaseCurvedTotal/24, and the free-count half bank
gives \ReleaseHalfFree/24. Even the expanded free bank cannot pass

This is a path-availability and allocation diagnostic, not evidence
of a learned policy or physical safety. No new cohort is opened.
