# Research Gap And Priority

The full goal remains unfinished. A diagnostic counterexample and reproducible
error decomposition are supporting evidence, not the main method contribution.

1. Learnable relative risk: distinguish rare producer-relative cap exceedance
   from unpredictable realized variation, then test a justified joint-cost
   repair against strong controls. Do not keep widening a failed residual.
2. Intervention utility: demonstrate useful scene-level joint decisions over
   independent-agent and whole-scene controls at the fixed protocol, rates or
   budgets, with accuracy, easy preservation, tail risk and interaction checks.
3. Generalization: independent selection, calibration and final confirmation
   remain unopened. Development reuse cannot become independent by renaming.
4. Publication package: main experiments, method positioning, manuscript,
   supplementary evidence and anonymous reproduction are still incomplete.
   See venue_check_20260926.md for the current official dates and unavailable
   2027 formatting detail. A deadline does not lower the scientific standard.

Prior tail/hurdle, factorization, auxiliary, nested-residual and risk-conditioned
failures remain part of the evidence. No false claim that the current model
has improved simply because more checks pass. No deployment change, Stage5C or
SMC. Native annotation steps, detector pixels and automatic labels only.
