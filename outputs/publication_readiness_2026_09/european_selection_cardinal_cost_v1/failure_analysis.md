# Better Cardinal Utility, Still Unsafe Intervention

## What Worked

The registered mean_absolute contrast improves paired completion-lower utility
over the original context independent controller by **+0.799527pp**
**[95% locality-bootstrap CI:+0.175107,+1.333576]**. The known component is
+1.344268pp[+0.717840,+1.895003]. Known gain versus the same fixed SOURCE floor
increases from0.159478%to1.503747%. All six locality known effects are positive.
Mean primary effects by actual seed17/29/43 are+.955754/+.429803/+1.013023pp.
These are development results after outcome exposure,not final confirmation.

The two single-factor completion-lower comparisons remain inconclusive:
mean_centered+.045522pp[-.188159,+.270377];weighted_absolute+.229374pp
[-.072069,+.553307]. The combined contrast passes its registered primary but
does not prove either factor is individually necessary or a formal statistical
interaction. Lower regression loss is not the evidence; frozen-policy utility is.

## What Failed

Full-policy recording risk fails for every arm. The combined model supports
only6/252repeated recording views,versus23/252original. Its worst known easy
net degradation is8.495468%,not<=2%. Known all/easy positive-risk violations
are199/211;14easy-risk views remain undefined. Known risk violations cannot be
explained only by conservative unknown-future completion bounds.

Mean intervention rate rises from5.521367%to22.495891%,averaged across the same
locality/producer views. The repair recovers more useful interventions but also
admits harmful ones. This rate change is a descriptive mechanism,not proof of
the unique cause. We did not run a same-count comparison for this loss-repair
experiment and do not relabel its primary as a matched-intervention contribution.

The weighted_absolute factor has lower worst easy net3.417284%than original
3.772416%,but still fails2% and has179known easy-risk violations. mean_centered
has9.231882%worst easy net. No favorable secondary arm is deployed.

## Diagnosis Boundaries

The preceding fixed-cost audit showed selected EH underprediction and R
inflation on SOURCE fitting data and new selection scenes. That audit applies
to the old critics. This experiment establishes that repairing cardinal utility
alone does not repair recording risk; it does not remeasure those five-moment
error components for the new critics or uniquely attribute them to a decoder.
The current critic already shares R/ER across actions. Clipped-field fractions
in the old audit are small; this does not eliminate representation or domain gaps.

Historical hurdle/conditional,decision-boundary,deviance,tail-ranking and
source-crossfit studies are retained. In particular,a high all-row harm AUROC
previously collapsed near chance on positive-disagreement intervention support.
Another generic classifier,global cost multiplier or threshold scan is not
justified simply by these results. Neither is increasing model size blindly.

## Next Minimal Repair

Keep the now-supported cardinal-utility arm as a development comparator,not a
deployment. Test a new causal uncertainty signal:disagreement among the three
actual same-fold forecasters trained with seeds17/29/43. For SOURCE cost rows,
all three forecasters must exclude the same eight critic-fitting localities;
for selection,all are frozen SOURCE-trained models. Row alignment and lineage
must be checked before feature creation. No target residual may create these
features. Compare disabled,real and fitting-SOURCE-matched noise controls with
matched parameters and budgets. This follow-up is **not_run**,not a claimed cure.

The purpose is to test whether the current point-forecast critic lacks usable
epistemic-disagreement information exactly where switching can hurt. This is
not a calibrated confidence interval or proof that ensemble spread estimates
all uncertainty. It must improve policy risk as well as utility. Independent
calibration/confirmation remain closed; do not spend them tuning a failed policy.
