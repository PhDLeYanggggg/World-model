# What This Repair Can And Cannot Show

The intervention problem needs the sign and magnitude of expected improvement,
not merely an action ordering. The original within-row centered loss removes
the common signed-gain offset. Its moment regression still supervises that
offset,so centering is not a proof of complete nonidentifiability. The absolute
gain factor tests whether additional anchoring helps the actual safe policy.

With target Y in{0,1},P(Y=1)=0.1,ordinary squared loss is minimized at0.1.
The original4xunderprediction square is minimized at4p/(1-p+4p)=0.307692.
This simple calculation does not model the full clipped/composed controller;
it shows why an asymmetric score should not be described as estimating a mean.
Removing the weight is not guaranteed to fix selected-action calibration.

Choosing a score matched to its target functional is established practice,not
new M3W theory. See Gneiting,[Making and Evaluating Point Forecasts](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0902),
and Banerjee,Guo,Wang,[On the Optimality of Conditional Expectation as a Bregman Predictor](https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/huiwang/research/bgw.pdf).
The latter link is an author-hosted technical report,not a claim to introduce
Bregman scores here. No count-likelihood assumption is needed or asserted.

The current model already shares R/ER across candidates. Selected R inflation
is therefore a prediction/selection error,not proof that references are
candidate-specific. Marginal harm overprediction can coexist with selected
harm underprediction. Global scaling from exposed selection outcomes is not a
legitimate calibration repair and is not part of this experiment.

An earlier144-fit easy-deviance study improved some known easy-risk summaries
but failed its registered paired-utility and complete-risk advancement rule.
It is not being repeated as a new idea. Both factors here are tested on the
new fixed SOURCE-to-selection bank with matched architecture and full budgets.
This modest loss experiment does not itself establish a paper contribution,
pairwise scene-composition optimization,calibrated risk or better dynamics.

Successful output would justify further controlled study and independent
risk calibration. Failure must retain all arms and motivate a different data,
representation or risk-estimation intervention,not another test-threshold scan.
