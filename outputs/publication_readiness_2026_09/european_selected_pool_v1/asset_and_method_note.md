arXiv2208.12084v2, introduction and sections3-4.1, accessed1 October2026.
The work distinguishes accuracy from calibration on accepted predictions and
uses a learned selector with a coverage constraint. Its setting is classification;
our positive-harm/reference ratio and partially observed trajectories require
different accounting. Source-to-target robustness is not supplied by simply
applying a percentile margin. This diagnostic is not a reproduction of their
method and makes no conformal claim. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.12084v2).
