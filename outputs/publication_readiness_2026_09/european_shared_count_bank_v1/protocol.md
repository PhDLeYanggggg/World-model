## Common count and fixed factorial controls

Compute the physical-only independent rule in every source/recording/current
raw-frame query. Its number of admitted actions,k,sets the common budget.
The physical assignment is feasible in both banks. Keep every query,including
k=0;do not manufacture an identical-floor pseudo-action to fill support.

Four controls have exactly k actions in EACH query:

1. Physical independent:original pointwise rule restricted to actions0-5.
2. Neural trimmed independent:original seven-action pointwise choices,trimmed
   to their top k predicted gains,with canonical-ID tie order.
3. Physical joint:reoptimize the six physical actions under both predicted2%
   all/easy signed budgets at count k.
4. Neural joint:reoptimize all seven actions under the same rules and count k.

The expanded bank uses the higher-predicted-gain feasible solution from physical
joint and neural trimmed independent as its warm fallback. The common inherited
solver then cannot return lower predicted utility than this feasible anchor.
This is a predicted-objective nesting property,not observed-error protection.
In particular,the physical control remains a full reoptimization,not neural deletion.

Use the same SciPy/HiGHS implementation:relative gap0,2s/query,raw signed-budget
postchecks,separate constraint scaling. Nonoptimal/invalid output retains the
feasible anchor and is reported,not called exact. No original numerical
tolerance,missing-label rule or full-policy risk condition is relaxed.

The previous seven-action independent and joint-matched strategies are retained
as contextual controls. Fresh prediction hashes and full scores must agree;
the old joint choices are cached_verified,not newly solved. The no-intervention
floor is also retained. These controls have different counts from the new four
and cannot substitute for the primary matched-bank comparison.

## Outcomes fixed before any new score

Primary:neural joint minus physical joint full-policy known ADE utility and
wholly-unknown completion-lower utility. Secondary:neural trimmed minus physical
independent,joint versus independent within each bank,and a four-policy
interaction contrast. The latter has its own unknown-disagreement bound,using
the smaller of two valid pair matchings before summing. Do not subtract lower
bounds or add interval endpoints. It tests whether the neural-bank increment
is larger under joint than independent allocation,not a causal real-world effect.

Retain original complete-policy all/easy2% recording-risk and easy-degradation
checks,unknown outcomes,hard/easy slices,tails,regret and predicted-path geometry
proxies. Count-trimmed controls are utility-only;empty is not estimable,not a
safety pass. Full-roster FDE stays undefined if required endpoints are absent.
Use3,000 locality bootstrap draws after context/head-seed averaging. These
remain exposed SOURCE fitting/resubstitution results,not whole-pipeline OOF,
independent generalization or a new deployment repair merely from a smaller k.
