# What the stronger bank comparison does and does not establish

## Neural value survives the stronger comparator, but is modest

At identical physical-independent counts in every query, optimized physical
gain is2.322960% known ADE over the frozen floor; optimized physical+neural
gain is2.544091%. The primary increment is+0.221131 percentage points,
conditional95%CI[+0.065452,+0.430560]. Its wholly-unknown completion-lower
increment is+0.154613pp[+0.005466,+0.362079]. All three cost-head seed means
are positive, but there is one frozen forecaster seed, not three producers.
Seed29's individual completion-lower interval crosses zero; do not claim that
every seed has a positive lower interval.

This is stronger than deleting an actual neural use to floor. It still does
not show that the learned forecast beats every possible physical model, an
unseen-domain policy or an independent test. The completion-lower interval
barely excludes zero on12 exposed localities; it is not a search-adjusted or
simultaneous guarantee. Ten locality known point contrasts are positive, one
is negative and one is zero. Completion-lower contrasts are positive in eight,
negative in three and zero in one. Do not hide those source-dependent failures.

## Joint allocation is not demonstrated neural-specific synergy

Physical joint minus physical independent:+0.612804 known pp. Neural joint
minus neural trimmed independent:+0.643873pp. The extra four-policy contrast
is only+0.031068pp[-0.003349,+0.071656]; its own completion-lower estimate
is-0.012419pp[-0.061671,+0.027880]. Neither interval supports the stronger
claim that joint allocation specifically amplifies the neural bank.

The defensible fitting statements are narrower: allocation helps both banks,
and a neural candidate retains some marginal value after physical reoptimization.
The optimizer couples predicted unary cost budgets. It does not learn coupled
future trajectories, social interactions or a full neural world transition.

## Risk is the actual deployment failure

All four primary controls select262,284 repeated row views at the same counts.
Neural joint supports only47/444 recording/context/seed risk views. There are
229 known all-risk violations,322 known easy-risk violations and43 undefined
easy-support views. The overlapping complete-policy failures are336 all-risk
upper,387 easy-risk upper and272 easy-degradation upper conditions.

These are not merely missing-label pessimism: known easy violations already
exist. Shrinking the unknown envelope alone cannot fix them. Predicted2%
feasibility and larger predicted gain therefore cannot certify observed safety.
The43 undefined easy views are not certified safe abstentions. Nor may risk
be replaced with a full-floor denominator or canceled with beneficial switches.

The preceding frozen-choice diagnosis, reused as cached_verified evidence,
finds joint-selected easy harm severely underpredicted and predominantly
numerator-side excess on fully known selected queries. That accounting is not
freshly remeasured for the present four new policies and is not a uniquely
identified causal explanation. It motivates a selected-action/query-conditional
cost repair, not a new arbitrary threshold or global loss multiplier.

## Numerical fallback is preserved, not concealed

Physical joint rejects4 solver outputs and neural joint rejects7 at raw
postcheck. Every rejection retains a feasible warm anchor; no timeout occurs.
The expanded bank preserves predicted utility nesting even in those cases.
The all-optimal gate therefore correctly fails. A numerical fix, if needed,
must use a new experiment identity rather than silently overwrite these results.
It would not explain hundreds of observed easy-risk failures by itself.

## Next discriminating repair

The next model should learn the actual selected-action/query harm, keeping the
successful utility/forecast components frozen. A fair test needs a matched
row-local recalibrator and a selected-set-conditioned critic, supervised on
the same permitted fitting queries with identical budgets and actual labels.
Unknown-selected queries must still be present at inference and in full-policy
evaluation; a future known mask is not a feature. Compare selective retention
and count-matched utility, not only a lower violation count after abstaining.

This repair has not been registered, trained or evaluated in the present study.
Old easy-hurdle, peer-mean context, conditional calibration and deviance studies
already failed their own screens; repeating one under a new name is not enough.
A new protocol must distinguish the selected action set from the old all-peer
mean, and fit/calibration/selection roles from independent confirmation.

No deployment change or independent admission follows. The goal remains active;
the current scientific blocker is a useful, reliably safe intervention method,
not local compute capacity. No metric/seconds/human-gold/true3D/foundation claim,
Stage5C execution or SMC.
