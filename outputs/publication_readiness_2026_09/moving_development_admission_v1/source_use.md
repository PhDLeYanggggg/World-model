# MovingDroneCrowd++ Development Use

This is a scoped local research-use decision under the author's delegated
independent-source acquisition and evidence-based role authority. It applies
only after the source-screen receipts and admission manifest are actually
complete. It is not a model result or a general data-ownership certification.

The author-linked Hugging Face release at revision
0867fef0a8885fceb4253bbeab394f3d4dd85f5c declares CC-BY-NC-4.0 in both its
pinned README and structured dataset card. The raw downloaded annotations are
verified against that pinned release. I accept eligible publisher-train clips
for attributed, local, non-commercial development fitting. No raw annotation,
source image, video, large derived cache or checkpoint is redistributed through
Git. Dataset attribution and the license must accompany any later permitted
derived release; this decision does not settle every underlying internet-video
right or authorize a commercial product.

Only the explicit publisher-train set, minus complete components linked to
reserved sources, is eligible. All DUT and DroneCrowd reservations remain;
source overlap does not justify moving a protected recording into fitting.
The other MovingDroneCrowd clips remain unadmitted for predictive use in this
track, not newly invented independent confirmation. Extended val/test share
publisher labels and cannot be used as scene-independent calibration/test.

The permitted role is fit: supervised or representation fitting inside an
explicit future producer/fold protocol. Preprocessing, baselines selected from
labels, goal prototypes, teachers, predictors, costs and policy selection must
obey its complete source-group exclusions. The source admission alone does not
authorize a learned artifact or open risk-calibration/final-confirmation arrays.

The task remains eight observed native annotation steps and twelve requested
native steps, with native +50 only a separate supplement. These units do not
establish equal physical duration to SDD raw frames. Head-box centers are image
pixels; no verified scale, effective seconds, ground homography, depth or true3D
claim follows. All currently visible agents remain in the causal input inventory;
future targets/availability masks stay in the supervision/evaluation path.

DUT GSOC backgrounds have unverified aggregation time and must not be used as
past-only neural inputs. Moving source-audit first/middle/last images are not
blanket predictive context: a later image experiment must register exact query-
aligned current/past extraction, independently of this source-grouping audit.

Negative image matches do not prove geographical or sampling independence.
Publisher labels and conservative components are exclusion units, not certified
physical-site counts. The original full 2% risk contract, historical failures,
Stage5C-off and SMC-off conditions are unchanged. New data access is not a
submission-ready method or a deployment improvement.
