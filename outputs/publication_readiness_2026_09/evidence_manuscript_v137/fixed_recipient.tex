\section{Support for equal-peak changes on frozen recipients}
We keep the earlier policy's recipients, halve its per-row peak
displacement, and either shrink uniformly or choose another
direction at the same peak. The pooled model and three separate
train-source models estimate known benefit, missing disagreement,
dual-reference increments, risk changes, and annotation support.
All use random analytic training anchors. Source consensus changes
direction only when every train-source model supports the change;
this is a selection rule, not a formal risk guarantee.

Across 36 fits (\FixedSupportUpdates{} updates), each held arm
keeps \FixedSupportEdits{} recipients. Pooled and consensus
change at least one path coordinate by more than $10^{-6}$ pixels
on \FixedSupportPooled{} and \FixedSupportConsensus{} rows,
respectively. The original
risk pass counts (frozen, uniform, pooled, consensus) are
\FixedSupportPasses{} out of 72. Relative to uniform shrinkage,
the paired full-grid lower contrasts are \FixedSupportPoolEffect{}
and \FixedSupportSupportEffect{} pp. The corresponding pair-distance
lower contrasts are \FixedSupportPoolPair{} and
\FixedSupportSupportPair{} pixels. Calibration qualifies
\FixedSupportQual{} of nine cells. Intervals use paired
source resampling.

The exact menu's separable upper bound excludes OLS4 gains in
\FixedSupportMenuExcluded{} views. For a wider bound, allow any
point inside each selected row's CV-centered radius $r_i$ at every
step, set $r_i=0$ on unedited rows, and ignore the risk constraints.
For known steps the smallest error is
$[\lVert y_{it}-c_{it}\rVert-r_i]_+$; for missing steps the
smallest OLS4 disagreement is $[\lVert o_{it}-c_{it}\rVert-r_i]_+$.
Summing with the same query weights yields an upper bound for the
conservative OLS4 comparison. Even this wider envelope excludes
\FixedSupportHalfExcluded{} views at half peak, \FixedSupportFullExcluded{}
at full peak, and \FixedSupportFreeExcluded{} when selected rows
are unconstrained. Positive values do not establish feasibility;
they can use impossible paths or violate risk. This diagnoses
recipient coverage and displacement, not independent confirmation.
