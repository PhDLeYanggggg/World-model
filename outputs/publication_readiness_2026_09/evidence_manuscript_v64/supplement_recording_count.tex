\paragraph{Recording-aware count supervision.}
We hold the additive recipient scorer fixed at evaluation and compare
two heads for zero, one or two edits. The first learns a query-local
proxy, the second a verified integer plan from an entire donor
recording under the original risk constraints and an edit quota.
Training menus and cutoffs exclude both the outer and donor locations.

eligible query and store at most two. Across seven previously exposed
OpenDD locations and three seeds, the local and recording heads make
\RecordingCountLocalN{} and \RecordingCountRecordN{} edits, with
\RecordingCountLocalPass{} and \RecordingCountRecordPass{} of
\RecordingCountViews{} risk passes with positive gain against both
references. The recording head leaves \RecordingCountUndefined{} views

\RecordingCountGap{} pp (95\% location-bootstrap interval
[\RecordingCountGapLow{}, \RecordingCountGapHigh{}]). We also match

label-blind score allocation with nonzero counts in
