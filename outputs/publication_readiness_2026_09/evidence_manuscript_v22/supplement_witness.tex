\paragraph{Constructive sign check.}
An outer envelope spanning zero need not contain attainable
contrasts of both signs. We fix every released coordinate and
use a single position for each missing recording-agent-step,
shared across all windows and all three seeds. An
interpolation with constant endpoint extension gives
$\WitnessInterpolation$ pixels. We add one bounded direction
per missing gap, scaled by median consecutive observed
displacement (floor 0.25 pixels) and distance to the nearest
observed step (cap twelve). Two feasible optimized
completions give $\WitnessLow$ and $\WitnessHigh$ pixels.
These are witnesses within this coordinate completion class,
not extrema, imputed labels, or physically validated paths.
They do not change the registered score or recording rule.
