\paragraph{The pair ranking can reverse under coherent completions.}
Keeping every released coordinate and one shared position
per missing agent-step across all windows and seeds, two
bounded completions give joint-minus-additive gains of
$\WitnessLow$ and $\WitnessHigh$ pixels. These are feasible
counterexamples in the specified image-coordinate
completion class, not recovered labels or a new test.
