# Cross-Fitted Candidate Costs: What the New Labels Permit

## Material Passport

Protocol interpretation written during the registered candidate training run.
No result selection, new optimization or change to the frozen analysis.
This document distinguishes candidate cross-fitting from validation of a later
learned risk head. The latter has not been run.

## Supervision Construction

Let S be the four admitted training sites, with bookstore excluded everywhere.
For each s in S and seed r, fit candidate f[-s,r] only on S without s, including
its normalizer and loss scale c[-s]. For a query i belonging to s, save

```text
candidate_i = f[-s,r](observed_input_i)
gain_i = (ADE(CV_i, target_i) - ADE(candidate_i, target_i)) / c[-s]
harm_i = max(-gain_i, 0)
```

The future trajectory is a supervised target only. It does not enter the
observed input or candidate construction. Each row has exactly one candidate
per seed; seed-mean error is a reporting device, not a forecast ensemble.
Candidate producers, their excluded site and training/normalizer hashes are
bound in the trial receipts. The OOF label archive is not self-authorizing:
downstream consumers must verify those receipts and row alignment.

## A Second-Level Leakage Trap

These labels can train a fixed risk head on all four source-training sites
for a predictor whose outer evaluation remains bookstore-excluded. They do
**not** automatically support an inner validation split for that risk head.

Example: suppose coupa is held out to validate the risk head. A label for a
deathCircle training query was generated by a candidate fitted on coupa, gates
and hyang. That candidate has seen the risk head's validation site's labels.
The final risk-head parameters can therefore inherit validation exposure
through their supervision, even though the deathCircle query itself is OOF.

For honest site-held risk-head validation, every candidate producing its
training labels must also exclude that risk-held site. With four sites this
requires a nested producer construction or a separately fixed training/selection
partition. Any new material selection/calibration/confirmation role remains
subject to the approved main protocol. Do not relabel the current archives
as independently calibrated or validation-safe for an arbitrary downstream
split. No such risk head is trained in this experiment.

## Interpretation Limits

An in-sample versus OOF contrast here changes training population size,
physical-site exposure and fitted normalizers. It is not an isolated causal
estimate of optimizer overfitting. It measures whether this candidate-training
recipe produces useful out-of-site forecasts and whether its cost labels
differ materially from in-sample labels on the same query population.

Neither cross-fitting nor a mean positive gain proves easy preservation,
independent scene calibration, joint-agent compatibility or deployment safety.
All four sites are explored training material; overlapping windows and shared
fold training invalidate treating their count as independent replications.
The registered bootstrap is descriptive and conditional, not a new confirmation
claim. The current stationary-history subset is not the complete world-model
benchmark, and mask-only inputs do not establish a multimodal contribution.

## Prior-Work Boundary

Regression deferral already includes joint and two-stage predictor/rejector
learning with bounded regression costs. This label/provenance repair is not
a new deferral framework. [Mao, Mohri and Zhong, ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html).

Learning a predictor without rejection before fitting a rejector also has
existing theoretical support under specified realizability and loss assumptions.
Those results do not directly supply a guarantee for our correlated-window ADE
and baseline-dependent costs. [Li et al., AISTATS 2024, Sections 2-3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/li24g/li24g.pdf).

The unproved M3W contribution remains useful baseline-relative forecasts,
scene-level joint intervention, independently calibrated risk and support-aware
fallback under matched comparisons. A clean OOF archive is a prerequisite,
not evidence that this contribution has succeeded.
