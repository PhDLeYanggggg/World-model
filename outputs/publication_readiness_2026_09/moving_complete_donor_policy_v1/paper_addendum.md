# Complete-Policy Readout Addendum

The fixed seed-mean and unanimity policies were reconstructed on all 62,466
past-eligible own-B rows, including 1,765 wholly censored rows, rather than only
the 48,061 known proposals. No model or threshold was refitted. All known causal
features and policy decisions reproduced; a retained batch-tail verification
amendment changes only numerical parity checking, not inference or risk limits.

Available-label source-macro ADE gains over the parent training-defined floor
are 1.3130% for mean and 0.3359% for unanimity. Their completion lower gains are
0.8238% and 0.3056%. Paired completion-lower differences versus forest are
0.8206 pp [0.1266, 1.7240] and 0.3011 pp [0.0306, 0.6443], using 3,000 paired
bootstrap resamples of eight exposed source components. These exploratory
intervals are trajectory contrasts, not safety guarantees or independent tests.

The original complete risk contract fails: mean supports only 1/24 required
recording views, unanimity 2/24. Mean has 15 known-easy positive-harm violations,
unanimity 9; their undefined easy-reference views number 6 and 12. Including
censored-label envelopes increases completion risk violations. Benefits cannot
cancel positive harm, and absence of selected reference remains not estimable.
All 24 recordings have observed easy events before selection.

Thus seed replication and improved component information do not establish a
safe intervention method. The next model test should incorporate complete-risk
supervision and reference support, not relax the original evaluator. No new
C/H readout, deployment upgrade, independent confirmation, Stage5C or SMC.
The compiled main paper is unchanged and not submission-ready. The original
24-group primary remains NA. Results use native obs8/pred12 image coordinates,
silver annotations and available labels, not verified metric/seconds or true 3D.
