\section{CV-Tangent Forecasting at a Matched Radius}
For observed residual $e=C-y$ and edit $d$, the one-sided
derivative of Euclidean error at CV is $e^\top d/\|e\|$ for
$e\ne0$, and $\|d\|$ at zero. This derivative is a lower
bound on the finite error change along the edit, not a safety
guarantee. Adding it to finite observed ADE and positive harm
loss changes held OpenDD utility by \TangentLoss{} across
\TangentEdits{} matched edits. Against the earlier shape at
the same recipients and per-row peak, the contrast is
\TangentOld{}, but passes fall from \TangentPrevious/606
to \TangentTangent/606.

A fixed probability guard recovers exact CV at rejected rows
but passes only \TangentGuard/606 and leaves \TangentGuardUndefined{}
undefined easy views. Redistributing a smaller budget while
keeping every recipient active and each query's radius sum fixed
yields \TangentRedistribute{} against uniform allocation of the
same model and still passes only \TangentAdaptive/606.
Of these views, \TangentUndefined{} lack easy support anywhere
in the roster. Intervals resample locations after averaging the
three seeds. No gate, deployment or confirmation status changes.
