# OpenDD: where the exact exchange opportunity is lost

Replay the frozen aligned-context model and enumerate the complete legal
exchange menu, including views where the model keeps the original plan.
Use the original 606 development views, 780 edits, movement caps, causal
proposal features, chronological credits and three-part 2% rule.
This is offline analysis, not independent confirmation.

Partition exact possible rescues into views outside and inside the frozen
intervention mask. Within that mask, compute the forced one-exchange
ceiling, subtracting already-passing plans that no legal exchange can
preserve. For missed opportunities outside the mask, distinguish no
positive predicted gain anywhere from no surviving transition score
above the original margin. For missed opportunities and destroyed
incumbent passes, distinguish undefined risk, measured risk failure and
failure to beat both utility references, in that order. These are
exclusive diagnostic categories, not a changed release rule.

At the same frozen mask compare ranking by the original transition
score, the same head's predicted gain alone, least total movement, and
uniform sampling over all legal choices (exact expectation). Select all
indices before outcomes are scored. Report counts and paired 3,000-draw
location intervals after seed averaging. Do not choose deployment
thresholds from these development outcomes. No new fits or external
labels are involved.
