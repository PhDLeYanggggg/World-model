# Complete-Risk Donor Supervision, Original Evaluation Unchanged

## Hypothesis And Scope

Known-only donor fitting omits wholly censored rows that remain in the deployed
causal population. Adding their existing conservative path-envelope targets
may reduce false-safe intervention. This is one model-side treatment, not
threshold tuning or a change to the original risk/support contract.

Use only original source-held producer-A donors and the eight already-exposed
own-B sources. No new C/H, reserved Moving20, DUT or DroneCrowd readout.
Forecasters, proposer, 120 causal features, six amplitude actions, 64/32 MLP,
4,096 updates, 50/50 pool mass and zero decision threshold remain fixed.
Unknown status is a training-label role, never an inference feature or gate.

## Treatment And Controls

Add 1,274 unknown active own-B rows and the 16,944 legal unknown producer-A
candidate rows omitted by prior fitting. Known features and labels must match
their frozen caches exactly. Bound targets use normalized maximum path
disagreement in both all/easy excess channels, with zero observed-reference
contribution. They are upper bounds, not fabricated future ADE or human labels.
Rebalance source/query weights on the complete pool and fit normalization only
on fitting sources; these changes are part of the treatment, not isolated effects.

Train all eight source-excluded folds at risk seeds 17/29/43: 24 new heads and
24 complete exact refits. Query sources cannot enter their own fitting or
preprocessing. Frozen known-only augmented heads are cached-verified controls.
Keep all negative seeds and source slices. The seed-mean and unanimity rules
are fixed before fitting. No validation/test threshold optimization.

## Readout

Freeze all fits and verify them before inference. Then compute seed-specific,
mean and unanimity choices on all 62,466 past-eligible rows without using labels
or future masks. Evaluate all 24 recording views using the same original
2% all/easy positive harm, signed easy-net and support rules. Empty support
stays NA, with no identity exception. Same-query counts remain utility-only.

Report complete-policy gain, unknown interventions, all risk violations and
support, plus observed-versus-bound component errors. Use 3,000 paired bootstrap
resamples of eight source components for exploratory trajectory contrasts,
not window-wise independence or safety certificates. A model-side safety
success requires all original recording views to pass with positive completion
lower gain. Otherwise no promotion, even when observed ADE improves.

## Execution And Limits

Commit definitions before real preparation/fitting. Native arm64 Torch CPU4,
interop1, workers0, atomic checkpoints, heartbeat, explicit resume and 12-hour
limit. Reuse known arrays; save only compact unknown supplements and new fits.
Preserve 5 GiB free and cap new private artifacts at 1 GiB. Replay inference
and metrics without refitting after reporting is stable.

This is exposed-development research, not independent confirmation or a new
forecaster-seed result. Complete-risk targets may not solve selected-reference
support; report that limitation. Original 24-group main NA and all old failures
remain. Native obs8/pred12 image-coordinate silver labels, not metric, seconds,
true 3D or foundation. Source annotation-generation causality unverified.
Stage5C and SMC remain false.
