# Complete-Risk Donor Treatment: Negative Model-Side Follow-Up

The complete-policy audit exposed unknown-label admissions absent from known-only
risk-head fitting. We therefore restored the existing conservative upper-bound
contributions for 16,944 producer-A candidate rows and 1,274 own-B proposals.
The same causal features, network, proposer, zero threshold and 4,096-update
budget were retained. All 24 new heads (three risk seeds, eight held sources)
and 24 complete exact refits finished before full-policy readout. Source/query
reweighting and fitted normalization change with the pool and are not isolated.

The treatment does not repair safety. Mean unknown admissions fall from 81 to
68, but known-easy positive-harm violations rise from 15 to 19; supported views
remain 1/24. Unanimity support falls from 2/24 to 1/24. Individual-seed policies
pass 0/24. Known-target MSE worsens on all 24 source/seed views while bound-target
MSE improves on 21/24; fitting RMS inflation is substantial. The results motivate
factorizing observability/envelope prediction from observed-risk learning and
explicitly modeling reference support, not relaxing evaluation rules.

Mean available-label source-macro gain falls from 1.3130% to 1.1343%. Its separate
completion lower gain rises from 0.8238% to 0.9214%, but the paired lower contrast
is -0.7833 pp [-2.0853, +0.0954], using 3,000 resamples of eight exposed source
components. Differencing separately pessimistic bounds is not a valid paired
improvement claim. The original 2% positive-harm, signed easy-net and support
conditions remain; empty support is NA. No identity exception or deployment.

This is exposed-development evidence with one fixed forecaster seed, not an
independent test. The original 24-group primary remains NA and earlier failures
are retained. Available-label image-coordinate obs8/pred12 silver results do
not establish metric/seconds, true 3D, foundation or submission readiness.
No Stage5C or SMC execution. The compiled main manuscript is unchanged.
