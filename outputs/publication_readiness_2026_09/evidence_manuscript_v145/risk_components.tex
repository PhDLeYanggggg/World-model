\section{Separate observed harm, missing debit, and support}
We hold gains and causal features fixed. Both heads learn the
same risk moments and support classes over \RiskPartsUpdates{}
updates. The component head combines conditional observed
moments and adds the probability of a wholly unknown future
times the exact causal peak displacement. The direct head predicts
the marginal moments without this factorization.

At \RiskPartsEdits{} matched edits, the component-minus-direct
conservative contrast is \RiskPartsHigh{} pp; risk passes are
\RiskPartsPasses{} out of 72. Swapping only the positive-harm
moments gives \RiskPartsHarmSwap{} pp.
At the component portfolio's lower counts (\RiskPartsLowEdits{}
edits), direct independent/component independent/component joint
pass \RiskPartsLowPasses{} out of 72. At those exact query counts,
component minus direct is \RiskPartsLow{} pp, and joint minus
component independent is \RiskPartsJoint{} pp (95\% paired source
resamples). These are exposed development comparisons.
Calibration qualifies \RiskPartsQual{}/9 cells.
