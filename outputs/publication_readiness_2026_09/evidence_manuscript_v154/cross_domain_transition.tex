\section{Transport of pass-transition prediction}
We test transfer of a decision head rather than adaptation of the
trajectory predictor itself~\cite{xu2022transfer}. The action moves
one existing edit to another person in the same query. The incoming
path follows OLS4 at the outgoing peak radius; the count and the
multiset of peaks are unchanged. A recording receives at most one
exchange. A shared head predicts conservative gain and three outcomes:
an added pass, a destroyed pass, or neither. It acts at the first
query meeting the fixed predicted-gain and transition-margin rule.

We fit \CrossDomainFits{} heads (\CrossDomainUpdates{} updates), all on
OpenDD, with three seeds. Held OpenDD models exclude the evaluated
source. The three pooled models use no MovingDroneCrowd++ outcomes
for fitting or selection. Their target is the prior 24-recording,
nine-source fitting-development roster under its frozen half-peak
anchor, not Moving A or independent confirmation. Each domain retains
its own conservative utility aggregation and recording evaluator.

The 606 OpenDD views contain \CrossDomainOpenRescues{} qualifying
exchanges on previously failing views, but the learned head's nine
rescues are offset by nine destroyed passes. The 72 Moving views
offer no such exchange. The model makes \CrossDomainMovingModelSwaps{}
exchanges but adds no pass. Its direct lower contrast against gain at
the same query times is \CrossDomainMovingMatched{} pp (95\% paired
source bootstrap); the interval describes development data only.

\paragraph{Direction-free upper bound.}
For a query with common weight $w_q$ and total CV-relative peak
displacement $R_q$, replacing all trajectories in the query with
arbitrary paths at the same total peak can change the OLS4-relative
conservative gain by at most $2T w_q R_q$, with $T=12$ annotation
steps. This follows from the reverse triangle inequality for the
observed errors and the missing-step disagreement debit, then the
sum of old and new peak displacements. Ignoring the risk constraints,
maximizing the bound over all queries still excludes OLS4 gain in
\CrossDomainExcluded{}/\CrossDomainViews{} Moving views. The median
upper gain is \CrossDomainUpperMedian{} CV-normalized points. This
does not rule out changing the forecast bank, more than one query,
or a different budget. It does rule out explaining the observed
failure solely by this particular shortlist or a bad transfer head.
