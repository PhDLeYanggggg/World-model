## Target And Weight Matching

For each legal observed row/action, five costs are B,H,R,ER,EH, divided by the
original causal row scale. Let r be the frozen original SOURCE RMS vector.
Augment the targets with (B-H)/norm(r_B,r_H). Multiply the first five normalized
channels by sqrt(6/5), and the gain channel by sqrt(3). Mean six-channel squared
error is exactly mean five-cost squared error plus0.5absolute gain squared error
before projection, matching mean_absolute rather than the old centered objective.

Action weight is original query visits divided by supported agents in that
query and by legal actions for that agent. Unknown rows get no supervision,
but all causal visible rows remain eligible neighbor context. The common global
weight rescaling does not change tree fitting. Unit tests check objective and
query-weight equivalence. All original queries require at least four visits.

At inference, average predicted R/ER across action codes to preserve candidate-
invariant references, then apply the existing physical cost projection:
B,H>=0, B+H<=causal envelope, B<=R,ER<=R,EH<=H. The neural head uses its coupled
decoder during optimization; the forest applies projection after fitting.
Differences cannot uniquely identify architecture, optimizer or irreducible risk.

## Interpretation And Literature

ExtraTrees is a conventional estimator control, not a new method or evidence
that the stronger estimator necessarily wins. Standard squared-error and
warm-start behavior are documented by the
[official implementation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.ExtraTreesRegressor.html).
No novelty or general finite-network calibration theorem is claimed.

Available-label ADE is not full12ADE. Current completion bounds cover wholly
unknown rows, not missing steps inside partially labeled future trajectories.
EuropeanSquares detector/tracker silver is not human gold. Obs8/pred12 with
raw stride12 and local image pixels are not seconds/meters/physical safety.

from program completion. Stage5C and SMC remain off.
