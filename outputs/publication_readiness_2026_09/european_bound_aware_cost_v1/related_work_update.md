# Positioning Check, 3 November 2026

[Regression with Multi-Expert Deferral](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html)
already treats single-/two-stage regression deferral with multiple experts and
instance-/label-dependent costs. Cost-aware expert routing itself is not new.

[Conformal Risk Control for Non-Monotonic Losses](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.20151v1)
extends risk guarantees using algorithmic stability and exchangeability. Its
general theorem does not rely on boundedness in the proof; specific usable
stability bounds introduce further conditions. Non-monotonicity alone therefore
does not establish novelty or rule out every conformal approach. Our current
experiment establishes no stability bound and no expected-risk certificate;
its complete per-record empirical contract is a distinct requirement.

The original2024CRC discussion in the working paper should be accompanied by
this2026extension. The test here is narrower: whether explicit missing-label
moment contributions improve forecast admission on matched real data. Neither
the new target decomposition nor the classical conditional-mean argument is
claimed as a new theorem. Method-level novelty and independent benefit remain
unproved. The published Royal Society DOI endpoint returned403 in this check,
so the citation uses the inspected author arXiv version,not an inferred journal
bibliography. No third-party article text or code is redistributed.
