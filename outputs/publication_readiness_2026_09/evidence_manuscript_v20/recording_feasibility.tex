\paragraph{Local pair gain does not imply recording feasibility.}
At fixed recipients, a negative one-sided derivative
characterizes query-local gain under convex affine blending.
Shared and separate weights yield verified gains in
\DirectionShared{} and \DirectionSeparate{} of \DirectionQueries{}
active query-seed views, respectively. Yet the fixed
recording risk numerators are monotone in each row's
masked ADE or, if wholly unobserved, maximum CV
disagreement. Their minima are attained simultaneously
by independent scalar minimization. Of \RiskViews{} views,
\RiskUndefined{} lack selected easy mass, \RiskExcluded{}
exceed the original 2\% budget even at the lower bound,
and \RiskFeasible{} admit a checked witness. In \RiskUnknown{}
views, minimum unobserved disagreement alone exceeds
the easy allowance. No checked safe ray beats OLS4 on the
conservative pair score (\RiskOlsPositive{}/\RiskFeasible{}).
This excludes the fixed-recipient affine family, not new
recipients or new path directions.
