\section{Query Gain and Recording Feasibility}
\label{sec:affine-feasibility}
The two frozen paths for each intervention recipient are an
individual-motion forecast and a forecast trained with a
relative-motion objective. Both differ from CV at the original
switch recipients. Write their affine mixture as
$p_i(\alpha_i)=a_i+\alpha_i(b_i-a_i)$, with $0\leq\alpha_i\leq1$.
All other paths are fixed. A separate learned mixture adds
debits for missing-label disagreement to the fitting objective;
its median weight on the relative-motion path drops from
\AlphaObserved{} to \AlphaCompletion{}, with no new recording
passes. This is a fitted shrinkage result, not a feasibility
bound.

\paragraph{Query-local direction.}
For each requested pair-step, let $D\alpha$ denote the
relative-vector change from the individual path. With a
co-observed residual $e$, the increase in pair error is
$\|e+D\alpha\|-\|e\|$. With missing labels, charge
$\|D\alpha\|$ instead. Their sum is convex in $\alpha$ and
starts at zero. If a nonzero mixture improves this score,
the one-sided derivative along its direction is negative
by the secant inequality. Conversely, a negative derivative
gives a finite improving mixture sufficiently close to zero.
The original nonidentity recipients remain unchanged for
sufficiently small steps. For at most two recipients, a
nonnegative direction with unit coefficient sum has at
most one free coordinate. We bracket its convex minimum
and verify each reported positive witness by the original
pair scorer, rather than count derivative signs alone.
Shared weights yield \PairShared{} positive witnesses and
separate weights \PairSeparate{} of \PairActive{} active
query-seed views. The latter are repeated queries, not
independent observations.

\paragraph{A simultaneous recording lower bound.}
With recipients fixed, the three recording reference masses
are constants. On any selected row with future support let
$c_i$ be its masked ADE; on a wholly unobserved selected row
let $c_i$ be its maximum disagreement from CV. Each original
risk numerator is a coordinatewise nondecreasing function
of $c=(c_i)$. In particular, clipping the known increments
at zero preserves monotonicity, as does the signed easy sum.
Thus minimizing each convex scalar cost $c_i(\alpha_i)$
separately simultaneously minimizes all three numerators
in the relaxed box. If a resulting lower bound exceeds
any allowance, the exact-nonidentity problem is infeasible.
The converse requires a witness with the exact nonidentity
mask and all three original checks re-scored. A missing
positive denominator cannot be repaired by changing weights.

Of \FeasibilityViews{} views, \FeasibilityUndefined{} lack
selected easy reference, \FeasibilityExcluded{} exceed an
allowance at the lower bound, and \FeasibilitySupported{}
admit checked witnesses. In \FeasibilityUnknown{} views,
the minimum disagreement of unobserved switches alone
exceeds the easy allowance. A checked ray toward the
query-local pair witnesses improves the conservative pair
score relative to the individual minimum-risk anchor in
\FeasibilityPair{} of the feasible views; \FeasibilityOLS{}
beat OLS4. This is not a global pair optimum. It excludes
this affine family at this recipient roster as a complete
solution, not a different roster or different trajectories.

\section{Past-Only Velocity Adaptation}
\label{sec:prefix-motion}
A second forecast basis uses anchored velocity slopes
from the last two, four and six observed positions. We
compare fixed mixture weights with two ways to adapt them
using only the observation window. The adaptation fits each
slope on an earlier prefix and measures its one- and
two-step error against later positions that are already
observed at the actual forecast origin. We use four
pseudo-origins, favor recent one-step errors, divide by
observed RMS displacement (with a pixel-scale floor),
and multiply the prior weights by exponentiated negative
errors. The agent arm uses its own weights; the query
arm uses the mean across observed query members. Neither
variant is tuned on future labels.

The matched analysis replaces paths only at the original
switch recipients and keeps the original switch if a new
path would equal CV. The fixed, query and agent weights
pass \PrefixPassFixed{}, \PrefixPassQuery{} and
\PrefixPassAgent{} of \FeasibilityViews{} original risk
checks; gain ranking passes \PrefixPassGain{}. The
available-label gain over fixed weights does not persist
under the conservative lower contrast, and the agent arm
does not establish a gain over query-shared weighting.

evaluate standalone forecasts, not intervention policies.
