\section{Direct causal velocity forecast as a candidate generator}
We train a bounded quadratic velocity profile and integrate it in a
past-motion frame to produce twelve positions. At initialization the
forecast equals OLS4. A seven-difference ego encoder is matched to the
same architecture with up to four causal neighbors. A third arm adds
the per-missing-step disagreement with OLS4 to the observed-error
objective. The study uses \VelSplineFitCount{} fits and
\VelSplineUpdates{} updates over three source-exclusive rotations
and three seeds. No future positions, masks or scene identities enter
inference. Selecting by the calibration-role observed gain chooses
ego/peer/completion in \VelSplineSelectedChoices{} of nine cells.

Gains are CV-normalized points against OLS4, with 95\% paired
source-group intervals. The frozen neural comparators use the same
held rows but six fitting sources versus three for the spline; this
is not a matched training-budget comparison or the published benchmark.
The observed-step
gain of the completion arm does not establish a positive lower bound:
partially observed tracks debit \VelSplinePartialDebit{} points
and wholly unobserved tracks debit \VelSplineMissingDebit{}.
Matched peer inputs change observed accuracy by \VelSplinePeerAblation{}
points; the calibration-selected arm changes observed pair-displacement
error by \VelSplinePairSelected{} pixels relative to OLS4 (positive
is better). We do not claim a causal benefit from neighbor context.

Adding frozen spline paths at three amplitudes to the preceding menu,
with \VelSplineCapacityEdits{} exact query-matched edits, changes verified
outcome-aware capacity from \VelSplineOldCapacity{} to
\VelSplineNewCapacity{} of \VelSplineViews{} views
(\VelSplineCapacityDelta{} source-balanced pp). There are
\VelSplineLearnedEdits{} learned-path actions among verified plans.
This comparison measures candidate availability, not whether a
past-only selector can recover those plans. No deployment rule changes,
and no independent outcomes were opened.

After the raw development readout, we also explore a smooth return to
OLS4 in the final forecast steps. At identical per-trajectory total
displacement from OLS4, the taper's direct paired conservative contrast
against uniform shrinkage is \VelSplineTailMatched{} pp. Selecting on
the calibration roles and allowing OLS4 fallback gives
\VelSplineTailSelected{} pp versus OLS4 on held development and
\VelSplineTailPasses{} original risk passes. The repair does not
establish a gain or qualify for deployment.
