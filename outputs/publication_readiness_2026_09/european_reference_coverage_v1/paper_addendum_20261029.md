# Reference Coverage Does Not Resolve Safe Intervention

English evidence addendum, 29 October 2026. This is not a completed anonymous
conference paper. Earlier manuscript exports and their negative results remain
unchanged; this addendum does not pool SDD and European results.

## Controlled Parameterization Study

We test whether a demonstrated output-support restriction contributes to
unsafe neural forecasting. The existing motion-bounded decoder is centered
on a training-selected causal baseline, which need not place constant velocity
inside its output ball. We expand only that radius to include causal CV and
damping .97 with a fixed 5% interior margin. The backbone, input schema, source
folds, seeds, normalizers, loss and optimization budget are unchanged. Future
labels are used for supervision and evaluation, not the output-bound construction.

All nine 4,000-update fits complete. Six radius-unchanged fits reproduce their
control's full training state and predictions exactly. On the twelve explored
European source localities, the equal-locality ADE gain over CV changes from
7.531916% to 7.595475%, a paired improvement of 0.063559 percentage points. Its
conditional 3,000-draw locality-bootstrap interval is [0.029923, 0.096635]. All
three seeds are positive. Nevertheless, easy degradation remains 8.345904%, and
the original ADE-only forecaster remains stronger overall. This establishes
a small development mechanism effect, not sufficient predictive or safety
evidence for the proposed method.

## Support-Limited Binary Decisions

The exact fixed-floor TRAIN feasibility check remains positive in 147 of 148
recording/context views, over 74 unique recordings. The residual failure contains
three known eligible easy observations and is produced by an unchanged model.
Reference coverage therefore does not repair that obstruction.

A subsequent registered diagnostic considers all six existing motion baselines
and a frozen neural forecast, with at most one real action per row. Exact
analytic copies of the floor are excluded. The physical-only bank has useful
label-aware feasibility in all 148 views. Adding the original neural action
provides additional oracle utility in all 148 views. These are offline witnesses
computed with future labels, not decisions available to a deployed model.

The diagnostic distinguishes two questions: whether useful safe actions exist
in the declared bank, and whether past-only context allows a learner to find
them. The present experiments support the former for the expanded TRAIN bank,
not the latter. A learned multi-action cost policy must beat a physical-only
learner and the old binary policy under matched utility and complete-policy
risk evaluation. A favorable oracle cannot establish that contribution.

## Claim and Reproducibility Boundary

All source localities have informed development. Overlapping windows and
repeated producer/seed predictions are not independent calibration units.
Unknown outcomes remain unknown. Independent selection, calibration and
confirmation are unopened. The original 2% requirements, including per-recording
coverage, are not relaxed; count matching evaluates utility only.

The output construction's algebraic coverage property is elementary and is
not claimed as a new safety theorem. The action-bank oracle is not a scene-
joint interaction model. Neither the current predictor nor this diagnostic
establishes a JEPA, multimodal, foundation or true-3D contribution. Data remain
image-local detector tracks and raw-frame steps, not metres or seconds.
Code, configuration, endpoint hashes, exact readout replays and operating commands
are preserved; raw data, caches and weights are not redistributed.

This addendum supports the next controlled learning experiment, not a claim
that the project is ready for CVPR submission. Stage5C and SMC remain off.
