# Prospective TRAIN Action-Bank Feasibility Probe

Defined before the full148-view bank readout, after the fixed remaining-case
diagnosis. This is an offline label-aware diagnostic, not a new selector,
policy gate, trained model or independent generalization experiment.

Keep the same24cached-verified SOURCE TRAIN packets, six frozen seed43producer/
controller contexts,74unique recordings and148record/context views. Preserve
the old floor, causal eligibility, recording roster, masks, reference/event
definitions and2%budgets. Exact equality to the floor is not an intervention;
remove such analytic no-ops before the new bank oracle. Do not add tiny
artificial perturbations, adjust thresholds or credit an empty risk as safe.

Fixed banks:

1. All six existing causal baselines: position,CV,damping.90,damping.97,OLS4,OLS8.
2. The physical bank plus the original ADE-only neural candidate.
3. The physical bank plus the latest reference-coverage candidate, secondary.

The original ADE-only producer is retained as the strong neural comparator:
the just-completed development contrast shows the new harm-aware/coverage
models are worse overall. This is a development design choice, not selection
on independent test. Reproduce original-neural and preceding harm-aware
single-action controls before computing new bank results.

At most one real action is allowed per row. Maximize known completion-lower
benefit with the same all/easy ratios and signed easy-degradation rule.
Choose the largest positive benefit per row; if no selected easy reference
exists, add the minimum-harm eligible easy anchor within2%. Unknown outcomes
cannot improve this lower-utility objective and remain unselected/unknown.
Prove the small oracle against exhaustive synthetic enumeration. This is not
a scene-joint conflict optimizer or a causal action rule: labels choose the
offline witness. No inference input or actual deployment action is created.

Report feasibility for every view, physical-only utility, incremental neural
oracle utility and new/lost support, along with fixed-roster full reference
mass counted once per row. Do not multiply that denominator by bank size.
No new hypothesis test or claim of a learned gain follows from this probe.
Its purpose is to decide whether a prospective multi-action cost policy has
useful support and whether the neural candidate adds potential over physical
choices. A favorable oracle still needs new causal training/validation and
independent calibration/confirmation; all legacy failures remain failures.

Everything runs locally from verified cached packets; no new remote fetch or
HPC job is required. No independent roles, Stage5C, SMC, metric/seconds,
human-gold, true3D or foundation claims are opened.
