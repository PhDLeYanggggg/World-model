# Project Gap After Reference-Population Repair

The project now has a controlled result separating reference coverage from
reference population and magnitude. Restricting donor labels to the frozen
policy's selected population halves easy-risk error without changing query
selection or normalization. This is useful mechanism evidence, not a deployable
world-model improvement: the original head remains better in the registered
selected-cohort MSE, and policy utility and risk certification were not rerun.

Average easy-harm probability is close to observed event frequency but does not
recover the harm allocation. This motivates an objective-aligned local cost
fit, after checking earlier Newton/conditional-risk results. Uniform envelope
fractions and source-normalized signed-risk MSE are not generally the same
regression objective. The follow-up is not_run and must retain the frozen roster,
reference exclusions and reserved roles. No more k/threshold/global-rescale
sweeps are justified by these tables.

Still missing for the full goal: a risk learner that enables useful safe
interventions, independent calibration/confirmation, matched-count joint scene
selection evidence, appropriate public strong baselines, three genuine
forecaster seeds with recording-level uncertainty, and a complete reproducible
paper package. Internal checks, lower fitting error and a collection of negative
controls do not establish CVPR readiness or a strong neural world model.

The goal remains active. Ordinary in-scope audits, repairs and safe Git sync
need no repeated user approval; role boundaries and the2% risk definition stay
fixed. Formal submission remains the user's decision. No Stage5C/SMC. All current
results remain detector-silver, pixel/local obs8/pred12 rawstride12, without
metric/seconds/gold/physical-safety/true3D/foundation claims.
