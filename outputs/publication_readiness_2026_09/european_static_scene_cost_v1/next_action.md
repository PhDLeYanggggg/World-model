# Next Decisive Test

The scene ablation gives a positive known-label component effect but still
fails every-record safety and the mixed-minus-physical comparison. Do not
deploy it,choose the best harm multiplier,or open independent roles.

Audit selection-conditioned calibration on frozen TRAIN predictions. Keep
the existing action rule and fitting roster;no new optimizer updates. For
every required recording/head view,compare predicted and observed B/H/R/ER/EH
on all legal actions versus the selected action. Separate unknown selections,
near-zero predicted harm,reference inflation,and candidate-choice composition.
Use labels only as diagnostic outcomes,not model inputs. This can distinguish
inaccurate means,an ill-conditioned easy denominator,and selection concentration;
it cannot independently establish a causal source of generalization failure.

Only then register a single learning/calibration change. Do not infer safety
from lower overall MSE or claim a generic conformal theorem covers this task.
[Conformal Risk Control,ICLR2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/f3549ef9b5ff520a7e41ff3cc306ab2b-Paper-Conference.pdf)
controls an expected bounded monotone loss under its stated exchangeability
conditions. Our selected-risk ratios can be nonmonotone when membership changes;
empty selected risk is undefined,and every-record support is a separate demand.
Those task-specific conditions must be checked before importing a guarantee.

The source-development-only knowledge limit remains:old inner-held cannot
become fresh confirmation through another seed or renaming. A successful
prospectively frozen policy still needs independently reserved selection,
calibration and confirmation,public strong forecasting comparisons,and a
same-predictor joint-decision gain with enough genuinely nonadditive queries.
Existing joint,partial-neighbor,track-encoding,loss and support failures stay
visible. No Stage5C execution orSMC.
