# Failure Analysis

## Tested Explanations

1. **Easy-definition drift alone.** Not supported as a uniform repair.
   With row regime fixed, both raw full-input comparisons have1 positive,
   1 negative and4 overlapping intervals. Changing to the secondary labels
   does not authorize replacing the primary endpoint.
2. **Larger fitting regime alone.** Conditional, not general. Fixedcut2
   gives4 favorable full-input intervals; fixedcut3 only1. The latter also
   harms the coverage guard in one assignment. Composition, preprocessing
   and balanced sampling co-vary with regime; pure sample count is not isolated.
3. **A consistent cut-by-regime interaction.** Unresolved. Rawfull1/0/5;
   scaledfull0/0/6. Do not equate overlapping intervals with proof of no effect.
4. **Magnitude mismatch disappears at matched producer size.** False as a
   sufficient repair on these measurements. Native two-site scaled/raw primary
   has3 favorable intervals, but coverage-log error worsens in all6 full-input
   assignments. A matching site count is not a matching site population.
5. **A training/runtime or data-role error.** The real native bridge reproduces
   parameters exactly;864 draw/preprocessing checks pass; cached native3
   predictions and held metrics match. All label-definition producers are
   declared. These checks support the comparison, not universal correctness
   of every historical dataset or the scientific hypothesis.

## The Remaining Problem

Frozen scaling can reduce average squared cost while suppressing predicted
harm mass. The post-hoc summary shows full-input median mass ratios falling
from0.631 to0.097 for the native two-site cell and0.663 to0.109 for the
native three-site cell. The mass quantities use the same unchanged target.
This is a measured calibration tradeoff, not evidence that the model became
safer. The fitting-only objective and held cost distribution may differ;
rare high-severity events and scene composition remain plausible mechanisms.
Those mechanisms have not been isolated by this study.

Do not attribute the original true-auxiliary failure to any single factor:
this experiment trains cost-only heads, not crossed true/shuffled auxiliaries.
No new auxiliary comparison, scene-world dynamics lift or deployment gain
has been measured. Retraining more auxiliary heads before diagnosing the
mass-versus-MSE discrepancy is not justified by the present results.

## Evidence Limits and Negative Results

All144 views and432 dependent replicas remain; no missing cell was dropped.
Minimum training easy-harm support is only5 in one cell. Four held localities
per assignment and overlapping source assignments limit statistical power.
Large negative motion-only relative effects and broad intervals remain in
the complete tables/figures. They are not hidden by reporting only full inputs.
Source-held histories have prior exposure. Independent confirmation stays
closed, and a fresh computation cannot make exposed data independent again.

No trajectory policy was evaluated. There is no fresh t50/FDE/easy-degradation
claim here. Stage5C and SMC remain off; units stay native-step detector pixels.
