# Remaining World-Model and Paper Gap

## What This Study Adds

A registered crossed comparison now separates two previously confounded
training changes while holding the outer scoring target fixed.864 new heads,
exact native-control reproduction and complete locality intervals prevent
explaining a failure by an uncontrolled two-site/three-site comparison.
The comparison does not support a universal repair. It narrows the diagnosis:
producer size alone cannot account for the persistent coverage tradeoff.

## Shortest Next Discriminating Step

Use only the already exposed source data and frozen fitting records to
decompose the squared-error slope objective into zero-harm, positive-harm
severity and locality contributions. Compare the fitting-only optimum,
the identity and an explicitly diagnostic mass-preserving reference, with
the same frozen predictions and target definition. If constrained fitting
is subsequently proposed, preregister it and keep its tuning inside honest
inner folds; do not fit any constant on outer held outcomes.

First answer whether the discrepancy already exists on fitting-only OOF
records or appears mainly after transport. That distinguishes objective
misspecification from scene-dependent transport more directly than another
architecture sweep. Keep identity, cost-only and shuffled controls. Any
new objective must preserve the original primary and guards, not replace
them after observing a favorable secondary endpoint.

## What Not to Repeat

Do not repeat common-event residual relabelling, attribute failure to a
prior alone, or run another undifferentiated size/cut sweep. Those specific
repairs or explanations have already failed their registered screens.
Do not open independent selection, calibration or confirmation to choose
a slope, threshold, event definition or favorable assignment.

## What Still Blocks the Main Goal

No stable cost improvement with all guards, no new joint-scene deployment
gain, no independently confirmed neural dynamics lift, and no new physical
calibration are demonstrated here. A full paper claim still needs a useful
method under matched predictors, independent scene-level evaluation,
strong baselines, meaningful module ablations and reproducible improvements.
This is not yet a submission-ready evidence package or a foundation model.

Obs8/pred12 native annotation steps in detector pixels remain the protocol.
No metric/seconds,human-gold,true3D or physical-safety claim. Stage5C and SMC
remain disabled. The next diagnostic is not an execution of either.
