# Approved WEPDTOF source roles and next comparison

The approved assignment in [external_roles.json](external_roles.json)
keeps Convenience Store development-only and reserves the other
13 source groups for one frozen external evaluation. None of these
13 has been used for forecast scoring. Of the reserved groups, 12 have
structurally eligible multi-agent queries at stride four. Large Office
and Jewelry Store each keep their two clips together. Structural label
presence does not establish camera history coverage.

The [image-only schedule](blind_camera_plan.json) fixes 115 disjoint
windows without using any reference track. It pins the past JPEG
members and checksums, not future labels or a risk threshold.

The useful next comparison is a frozen transport experiment: bind
one detector and tracker from the pilot, select observation windows
using archive frame numbers alone, and freeze outputs for all candidate
sites before opening the outcome positions. Compare CV and OLS4 first,
then a single previously fixed learned candidate and the independent,
scene-uniform and joint rules with the same candidate bank and matched
edit allowance. Choose those policies using only already exposed
development, or a separately agreed calibration subset. Do not choose
them after the new-source baseline readout. Abstention does not turn
undefined support into a pass.

Before a formal risk result, fix the released-step stride, the
forecaster and policy, and the past-only normalization
for transporting the easy reference cutoff; the pilot does not set
these. The original 2% recording rule is not negotiable by this
experiment. Reporting must retain unmatched camera histories and
missing future label support, distinguish separate from common
detector populations, and resample original sources rather than
individual windows. No WEPDTOF outcome substitutes for the sealed
DroneCrowd groups or makes pixel errors metric.
