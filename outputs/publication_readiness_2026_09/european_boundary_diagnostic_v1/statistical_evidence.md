# Interpretation Boundaries

This is a descriptive decomposition of already frozen decisions, not a new
significance test or risk certificate. Eleven interpretation risks are checked
below; this does not mean eleven empirical assumptions have been established.

1. Simpson: preserve locality, phase, arm, event and decision-scope breakdowns.
2. Ecological: a mean selected-harm ratio does not certify an individual agent.
3. Selection: all 12 development localities were previously opened; no new
   population-representative or independent-confirmation claim.
4. Collider: selected and easy subsets are descriptive, not causal effects of
   conditioning on those subsets.
5. Base rates: selected known/unknown counts and all/easy reference denominators
   remain explicit. Unknown losses are not zero; empty denominators are undefined.
6. Regression to the mean: report every registered fitting and transfer group,
   not only groups with the worst prior error.
7. Survivorship: all 288 groups and full numerical replay are required before
   calling the diagnostic complete. Conditional summaries identify missing views.
8. Look elsewhere: fixed bins and training-defined tails are descriptive. No
   p-value, adjusted significance or population confidence bound is claimed.
9. Forking paths: the committed protocol, packet manifest and frozen predictions
   precede this readout. Earlier research exposure remains part of the limitation.
10. Causation: harm/reference error decomposition is an arithmetic identity. It
    locates observed prediction error but cannot identify its unique causal origin.
11. Reverse causality: outcomes are labels used after frozen actions. Future label
    availability and tail labels never determine inference eligibility.

The aggregation first averages views within locality and then localities equally.
Repeated rows, overlapping trajectories and shared producers are dependent.
The 72 fitting and 216 internal-transfer views are not 288 independent samples.
No new bootstrap is computed for this descriptive diagnostic; the parent
experiment's nominal intervals are not transferred to these quantities.

The original 2% positive-harm budget and selected-reference denominator are
unchanged. Independent model selection, risk calibration and confirmation remain
closed. Raw-frame image-local detector-silver evidence is not metric, seconds,
human-gold, true-3D, foundation or physical-safety evidence. Stage5C and SMC remain off.
