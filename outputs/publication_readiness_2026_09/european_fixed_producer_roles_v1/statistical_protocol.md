# Statistical Interpretation

All36 development groups and all5 policies are retained. A group is an ordered
producer/controller/readout rotation, one seed and one all/easy training event.
Three groups of four already-opened source localities provide the six rotations.
This is not a new independent primary split.

Each view has four expected readout localities. Compute paired model/reference
ADE and actual endpoint FDE on identical supported rows in native image pixels;
average the four locality gains equally. Bootstrap those four paired locality
gains3,000 times with fixed seed39271. Report95% percentile intervals, worst
locality, p95/p99 within-locality errors, complete-future ADE and unknown-label
counts. Partial observations do not become complete trajectories by averaging.

The same recordings, agents and overlapping windows recur across views. The
number of positive/negative intervals is descriptive, not a binomial sample or
an independently replicated success rate. These conditional intervals are not
adjusted for the full research history or multiple comparisons. Four localities
per interval provide limited generalization evidence even when row counts are
large. Never pool all indexed windows as independent samples or claim new scenes
were independently confirmed.

The original producer-A easy/hard cutoffs are fixed before new cost-head
training. They apply to both arms and the common readout. Preprocessors and all
new supervision come from B. Both forecasts at inference come from A. The OOF
control's B labels use source-excluded opposite-half producers within B; this
training-producer difference is deliberate, not shared-target equivalence.

No readout chooses a model, threshold, event, checkpoint or subset. No statement
of deployment readiness follows solely from a positive average. Easy degradation,
observed zero-reference harm, adverse localities and risk underprediction remain
explicit. Reserved model-selection, calibration and confirmation roles stay closed.
