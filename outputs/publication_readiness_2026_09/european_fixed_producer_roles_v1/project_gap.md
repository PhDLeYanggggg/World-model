# Project Gap After The Fixed-Producer Experiment

Current evidence: real new cost-head training, exact frozen-policy readout,
source-level bootstrap and a useful easy-preservation repair. Not a deployment
upgrade, independent confirmation, cross-dataset world-model success or
submission-ready method.

The immediate gap is now narrower than in the preceding experiment: the matched
head no longer inherits the weak two-source floor's easy violations. It still
loses some incumbent gains, and its predicted risk is miscalibrated. Train-only
incremental learning relative to the incumbent is the next specific hypothesis,
not another arbitrary threshold search.

Broader missing evidence remains: a stable advantage over strong same-forecaster
controls; meaningful scene-joint/interaction benefit; independent source-level
calibration with adequate support; untouched confirmation; public strong-method
comparisons under one valid-label/8-to12 protocol; and a complete anonymous
reproduction package. Formal2027 formatting/policy checks are not finished.

The method-positioning note distinguishes this diagnostic from established
stacking and conformal-risk control. A neural cost head plus a role split alone
is not the project's final method contribution. Neither physical units nor
complete multi-agent visibility have been established. Raw data, caches and
checkpoints stay local. Stage5C and SMC remain disabled.
