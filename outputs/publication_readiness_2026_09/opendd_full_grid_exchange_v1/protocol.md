# OpenDD: is the single-exchange headroom robust to missing positions?

Use the frozen OpenDD development roster, 202 recordings at seven
locations and three training seeds. Replay the incumbent and all legal
one-exchange options, including those the aligned ranker does not take.
The 780 realized edits, movement ceilings, forecasts, original donor
cutoffs, ranker and its 101-view intervention mask do not change.
Do not use new external outcomes or train or select a replacement policy.

Compare the original dual test with the intersection of the original
test and the complete twelve-step risk certificate. The original utility
already subtracts maximum missing-step disagreement to each reference;
strict positive lower gains therefore survive every free completion on
the original observed reference scale. The [full-grid risk certificate]
(../songdo_full_grid_easy_risk_v1/protocol.md) handles possible changes in
easy membership. These are hindsight certificates of individual plans,
not a pre-outcome guarantee on a new recording.

Count passing views for the incumbent, aligned ranker, exact uniform
choice on the same mask, best legal option on that mask, and best legal
option or keep across the roster. Report separately the number whose
best passing option beats both references by more than 0.10 percentage
points (the earlier descriptive materiality cutoff). Record why an
original passing option loses the full-grid certificate. Bootstrap
paired rates over seven locations after averaging seeds; the 606 views
are not independent replicates. Keep every original result unchanged.
