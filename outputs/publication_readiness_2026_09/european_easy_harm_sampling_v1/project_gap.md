# Remaining Project Gap

The experiment completes a concrete registered training test, not the M3W
research objective. It is not yet submission-ready or a certified deployment.

Established in source development: structured query allocation can improve
accuracy over independent vetoes and count-only controls. The current sampler
does not make that allocation reliably safe, and the old raw-neural policy is
not uniformly beaten. The positive comparisons are not new trajectory-dynamics
evidence or a novel importance-sampling method.

Still missing: robust conditional risk estimation under scene shift, reliable
accuracy at the same realized risk, a contribution beyond strong simple scoring
controls, independent calibration/confirmation, and a coherent final paper
claim supported by those results. Source-C roles overlap and have historical
development exposure; more windows or more overlapping role views cannot
replace new independent localities.

Shortest next diagnostic: protect reference-moment fitting and test harm-only
optimization with a matched continuation control, choosing any budget only on
training-source held-locality evidence. If that does not transport, move to
causal event-support and detector-label quality diagnosis, not another C-tuned
threshold or another generic oversampling variant. This next intervention is
not_run and has no claimed result.

The six opened selection localities are not evaluated in this experiment. The
12 reserved calibration and six confirmation localities remain closed. Legacy
Stage35/37/43/44 results affected by exposure or selection remain exploratory;
they are not restored to formal status by this study. No change to deployment.

All present measurements use obs8/pred12 annotation steps, raw stride12,
image pixels and detector-derived labels. No metric, seconds-level, human-gold,
physical-safety, true3D or foundation claim. Stage5C and SMC remain off.
