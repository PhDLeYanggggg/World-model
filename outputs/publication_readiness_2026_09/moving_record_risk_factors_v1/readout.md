## Why The Next Target Is Support-Aware Reference Estimation

All51shared active/unsupported easy views have positive predicted reference
mass on their known rows, but zero observed selected easy-reference mass.
Only4include selected rows without any future label. Thus this pattern is not
solely unknown-label credit. A positive conditional mean does not guarantee
that a particular finite selection contains an observed supporting event.

The upward harm refiners deliberately freeze R/ER. They can reduce predicted
gain and the number of interventions, but cannot learn a different reference
support atom. The active/no-support count rises from34to51despite fewer active
views. This does not prove a causal effect of the features or an impossibility
theorem; selection populations differ. It exposes a target the refiner cannot
change and makes another scalar harm-multiplier sweep poorly motivated.

Next: test a separate hurdle reference model, with an event head for ER>0 and
a conditional positive-reference head. Use raw causal descriptors and the
existing producer-excluded forecasts, NOT a critic trained on the inner-held
source. Fit internal source-excluded C predictions, select only on those
predictions, and retain the old policies during the first prediction audit.
Do not assume independence of agents to convert event means into record-level
confidence. A positive support-model result alone would not certify deployment.
