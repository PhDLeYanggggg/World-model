# Gap After the Source-Separated Calibration Test

The research goal remains active and not CVPR-ready. A new empirical calibration
test was implemented and completed; it did not recover the neural method claim.
The complete static guards and source calibration preserve observed easy cases,
but the protected neural policy still loses to equally protected damping.

## Next Priority

Measure the incremental opportunity of neural forecasts over the stronger
protected damping floor on already opened development sources. Separate missing
headroom from unlearnable or misrepresented conditional gain/harm. Future-based
oracle selection is diagnostic only. Do not open independent roles to find a
more favorable answer. Register any subsequent relative-floor learning before
its outcomes, with strict exclusion of learned-floor producers and normalizers.

## Still Missing

- Safe neural contribution over matched strong controls.
- Useful scene-joint contribution at matching intervention rate or budget.
- Sufficient independent-source calibration evidence and confirmation.
- Strong public-method comparison and complete retrained ablations.
- Submission-ready anonymous reproduction and a full checked paper package.

The positive damping and easy-preservation results can support an honest
development study and motivate the next relative-floor experiment. They cannot
serve as proof of a superior neural world model or independent external success.
No deployment, Stage5C or SMC change. Dataset-local/raw-frame limitations remain.
