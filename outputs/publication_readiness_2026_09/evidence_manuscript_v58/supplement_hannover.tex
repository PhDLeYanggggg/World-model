\section{Frozen Transport to Hannover Shared Space}
We score three previously unscored clips from the Hannover
release~\cite{hannoverrelease}, treated as one provisional acquisition
group. Of \HannoverTransferTargets{} native 8-to-12 targets,
\HannoverTransferComplete{} have all future labels. We use the
published scalar for input conversion and report errors in image
pixels. Native sampling differs from OpenDD. All models and rules
are frozen without Hannover fitting or selection.

At half radius, tangent's direct gain relative to matched hindcast is
\HannoverTransferTangent{} points. The six-path utility oracle reaches
\HannoverTransferOracle{} points over CV. By the reverse triangle
inequality, each observed point can gain at most the smaller of its
CV error and its row's radius. Even allowing no missing-step debit,
the arbitrary-direction ceiling is \HannoverTransferCap{} points at
half radius and \HannoverTransferOriginal{} at the original radius,
below OLS4's aggregate conservative gain of \HannoverTransferOLS{}.
This utility gap is driven by the first clip. Even at quarter radius,
wholly unknown edited rows alone exceed the original easy-risk budget
in \HannoverTransferRisk/\HannoverTransferViews{} views. This is a
budget and support limitation, not solely a ranking failure. It is
not a new official test split or independent confirmation of success.
