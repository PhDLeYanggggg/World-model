# Interpretation: Schedule Repair Does Not Establish Safe Transfer

## What Was Actually Tested

Six real Torch risk heads continued identical warm weights for 2,000 updates
per model. The only changed training component is a fixed cosine learning-rate
schedule, .001 to .00001, versus constant .001. Capacity, original unmasked
causal features, labels, MSE, query sampling and decision thresholds are matched.
All six fits froze before the same already exposed nested-C development readout.
The three fresh constant controls exactly reproduce prior checkpoints. The first
full treatment refit and resumed pilots are exact. No new H was read.

This is intervention-risk learning, not newly trained trajectory dynamics or
representation pretraining. It does not replace any deployed policy.

## Measured Outcome

- Equal-source known ADE gain over floor: 3.6006% to 3.7549%.
- Hard-subset gain: 4.6257% to 4.7483%.
- Intervention: 15.0144% to 16.5645%.
- Paired known improvement: +0.1543 percentage points.
- Missing-label completion bounds: [+0.0275, +0.2811] pp, NOT a sampling CI.
- Relative to the affine risk control: -0.5685 pp known difference, completion
  bounds [-1.4704, +0.3335] pp.
- Known all-risk violations: 14 to 14 of 17 recording views.
- Known easy-risk violations: 13 to 15.
- Worst defined full-recording easy-net degradation: 38.9046% to 44.6540%.
- Full risk/support passes: 0/17 for both arms.

Only one of three prospective development expansion conditions passes; the
aggregate expansion screen fails. Even that one passing condition is not a
safety certificate: its worst easy-net degradation rises. The original 24-group
primary remains not estimable, not passed. No threshold or identity rule changes.

## Failure Taxonomy

**All/easy gradient interference is not supported at the inspected checkpoints.**
The preceding complete-B diagnostic has positive shared task cosines in all
nine frozen views. Projecting the all gradient changes nothing. This only rejects
the local explanation; it does not prove absence of conflict along every update.

**Annealing has not repaired fitting.** Full-fitting easy MSE rises 28.25%,
13.82% and 4.50% in contexts0/1/2; easy sign regret rises 8.49%, 24.91% and 3.77%.
Both all and easy MSE are higher in all three contexts. Reduced step size at
this budget is consistent with less effective fitting, but does not establish
irreducible feature error, or prove that longer training would be successful.
Counts alone mislead: context0 false-safe easy channels fall from 76 to 58 while their
cost-weighted decision error rises. The local saved-AdamW diagnostic cannot be
treated as a causal explanation of the entire training trajectory.

**Held utility and easy harm remain different objectives.** Source 078 gains
1.4822 pp versus constant, but source 066 loses 0.5155 pp. Four sources improve
and three worsen. In source 077/record 46, intervention increases from 1,110 to
1,225 of 3,193 rows and worst easy-net degradation rises from 38.9046% to
44.6540%. In source 070, intervention increases from 79 to 129 of 2,496 rows
and easy-net degradation becomes 2.6057%.
These are observed associations, not evidence that intervention count alone
causes the harm or that another threshold would solve it.

**More measurable support is not safety.** Undefined selected-easy views fall
from 2 to 0, but the newly measurable risks still fail. For example, source 075
selects 29 rather than 8 rows and has known easy-risk ratio 0.5118, above .02.
No empty reference is converted to zero and no identity certificate is applied.

**Generalization remains unproven.** These seven SOURCE units and one producer
seed are repeatedly exposed development data. Own-B contexts contain only two
or three source groups. Overlapping windows do not add independent scenes.
Neither a small mean gain nor exact numerical replay is independent confirmation.

## Next Model Work

Before another schedule or loss-weight sweep, test convergence of the unchanged
full-fitting B objective with the fixed model and source roles. Distinguish
remaining optimizable fitting error from held-source failure after improved
fitting. Such an experiment is not yet run. Do not use a new V/H readout to
choose optimizer stopping rules, and do not interpret finite-model failure as
proof that causal history has no predictive information.

The present hypothesis test is complete and negative for safe transfer. The
research goal remains open. Deployment unchanged; not submission-ready.
Native obs8/pred12 image pixels, not metric/seconds/true3D/foundation/human gold.
Stage5C and SMC stay disabled.
