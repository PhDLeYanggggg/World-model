# Support-Preserving Motion Exchange

Can reallocating a small amount of motion improve the fixed-half
control without losing its easy reference? Use the same exposed OpenDD
8/12 development roster, seven held sites, three seeds and pair-excluded
incumbent producers. No new selection on held data or external readout.

Begin with half the incumbent departure for every nonidentity target.
Within each query, allow one ordered two-target exchange: transfer a
radius of one quarter of the smaller incumbent radius from one target
to the other. Both remain at least quarter strength. The total realized
radius and all recipients match fixed half. Baseline forecasting
direction and rollout are unchanged.

Use a simple causal past hindcast rule as the first control. Train 21
tree heads with 32 trees, depth seven and minimum leaf 12 to predict
the two action contrasts (quarter or three-quarter versus half):
paired missing-step-debited gain, change in all positive harm, change
in easy positive harm, and change in signed easy error. Include the
unknown debit in the first two risk numerators. Use causal features
only and normalize by past scale. Do not estimate target availability
from held outcomes. Interpolate action predictions by the actual
fraction of an endpoint action. True observed errors and positive-harm
terms are convex along the blend; interpolation is conservative for
the true quantities, not a guarantee for learned predictions.

Compare (1) past-hindcast exchange, (2) learned utility-only exchange,
(3) learned utility subject to nonpositive predicted changes in all
three risk numerators, and (4) past-hindcast recipients at the exact
transferred radius and active queries of arm 3. Arm 4 is a matched
utility control, not selected under arm 3's constraints. Report
actual predicted and observed changes separately.

Primary contrast: guarded exchange vs fixed half, with positive
paired lower 95% location-bootstrap endpoint and no reduction in
the original risk pass count. This is a development criterion, not a
change to the full 2% recording requirement. Report required empty
matched views as unestimable. All selected easy denominators and
counts are unchanged, and unknown debit changes with allocation.
Keep no-edit CV and full incumbent as descriptive references.
No deployment, external scoring, DroneCrowd access, Stage5C or SMC.
