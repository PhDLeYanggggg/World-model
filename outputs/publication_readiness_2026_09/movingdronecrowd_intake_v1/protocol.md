## Question And Reader

Determine whether this candidate supplies sufficient past-only history and
future-label support for eight observations/twelve native annotation steps,
plus endpoint offsets10/25/50/100 where available. These are native exported
indices, not verified raw-video frames or seconds. Record contiguous-interval
and endpoint-only availability separately. The paper's mixed downsampling
does not establish per-clip time calibration, ground homography or metre scale.

Strictly parse MOT frame/identity/head-box rows and match annotation frame f
to listed image f+1. Do not silently remove duplicate identities, fill gaps,
clip boxes or invent image bounds. Current visibility determines all input
agents, including incomplete futures. Backward velocities are invalid across
past gaps. Future coordinates/masks are a separate label dictionary. On every
eligible recording, remove or perturb exported future rows and require exact
past-input equality. This tests the implemented offline dependency boundary,
not annotator sensor-time provenance.
