# Candidate Identity Is Not A Sufficient Peer-Context Repair

## Scope

This exposed-development addendum is separate from the compiled v9 manuscript.
It is not independent confirmation, a new trajectory model or a deployment result.
Six fresh neural heads and six cached-verified controls complete a2x2comparison
of same-index/proposed-candidate identity and all/active peer participation.
Each model has76,548parameters and1,000continuationupdates from a common parent.
All preprocessing, supervised targets, query draws, loss and safety rules match.

## Results

With all peers retained, proposed-candidate context changes joint fitting cost
by-0.0433%,-4.1076%,+0.6746% relative to same-index context; positive is better.
Corresponding risk-MSE reductions are+0.0427%,-0.2794%,-0.4021%. False-safe-cost
reductions are+8.9220%,-19.0946%,+9.0270%. The prespecified follow-up screen
passes0/3contexts, so no additional nested-C/H evaluation is performed.

Including fallback peers under proposed context recovers2.6809% and1.2265% of
fitting decision cost in two contexts but worsens the remaining context0.9717%.
Under active-peer participation, changing candidate identity worsens decision
cost in all three contexts. Thus removing fallback peers was not the only
failure mechanism. In the main failed context, source065 contributes97.3% of
the net cost increment. This is diagnosis, not authorization to remove that source.

Shared-parent proposed-all and same-all representations have mean cosines
0.9984,0.9910,0.9949. High similarity motivates testing more discriminative
candidate-relative information, but does not prove that pooling causes failure.
All12metrics replay exactly; exact computational reproduction adds no independent
statistical evidence. One producer seed and overlapping own-B queries limit inference.

## Positioning And Claims

Permutation-invariant set modeling is established prior art, as in
[Deep Sets](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/f22e4747da1aa27e363d86d40ff442fe-Abstract.html).
Cost-sensitive deferral also has established formulations, including
[Mozannar and Sontag, ICML2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/mozannar20b.html).
Neither a pooled bridge nor a rejector alone establishes novelty, and their
theoretical results do not automatically certify this partially observed,
dependent multi-agent forecasting setting. This experiment contributes a
controlled failure analysis, not a sufficient main-method success claim.
