\section{Admission at a Fixed Support Query}

We train a candidate head on observed features and their difference
from the frozen easy-ranked recipient. Separate outputs estimate lower
gain against CV, a local feasible-support event, and excess loss.
The guarded policy keeps the original recipient unless another
improves predicted support with no worse predicted gain or harm.
The estimates are not risk certificates. The first edit, second-slot
queries, counts and amplitude match exactly.

Learned gain yields \AdmitGainEffect{} against frozen easy but
loses joint passes. Guarded support yields \AdmitGuardEffect{}
without changing the joint count. Ranking all legal recipients
for support uses \AdmitWideOutside{} recipients below the frozen
easy threshold, yet loses joint passes. The local support surrogate
does not recover the outcome-aware recording-level capacity.
