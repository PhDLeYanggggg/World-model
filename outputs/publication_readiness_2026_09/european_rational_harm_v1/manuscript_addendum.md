# Exploratory Mechanism Result, Not a Main Accuracy Claim

We examined whether a bounded harm-adjustment parameterization obstructed
learning of selected risk. A frozen fitting-only Jacobian census showed exact
zero easy-harm derivatives on92.4% of the hard-clipped model's selected row
occurrences, accounting for96.3% of its positive easy-harm underprediction.
This motivated one registered rational-map replacement, with matched anchors,
initial weights, sampling states, objective, capacity and optimization budget.
All18 continuations and144 planned evaluation views were completed.

The replacement did not support the safety/utility hypothesis. Relative to the
hard-clipped comparator at matched intervention counts, conservative utility
decreased by0.217 percentage points (nominal locality-bootstrap95% interval:
-0.377 to-0.062). No held view satisfied complete policy support;19 of72 had
undefined easy risk. A smaller number of known violating views cannot be read
as improved safety when selected cohorts and undefined denominators change.

These experiments distinguish a concrete local optimization failure from the
broader problem of learning reliable policy-conditional risk. They do not prove
that clipping is the sole cause, that generic regression is impossible, or that
the rational map itself is a methodological contribution. They use exposed
original TRAIN recordings and repeat rows across contexts/seeds. Intervals are
exploratory, not multiplicity adjusted or independent confirmation. No newly
trained forecasting backbone, independent dataset result or deployable physical
safety improvement is claimed. Obs8/pred12 and stride12 are raw-frame protocol
quantities in detector-track pixel/dataset-local data, not metric/seconds claims.

This belongs in a mechanism/limitations appendix unless a later independently
evaluated method validates the full hypothesis. It cannot be used to present
M3W as a successful cross-dataset or foundation world model.
