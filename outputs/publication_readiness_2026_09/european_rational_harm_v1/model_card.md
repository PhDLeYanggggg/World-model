# Rational Harm Cost Head

## Intended Use and Status

Mechanism research only; the registered development gate failed. No deployment
change or independent-evidence promotion. This is not a trajectory generator,
new JEPA/Transformer forecaster, true3D or foundation world model. Historical
Stage37 scores remain exploratory and are not recertified by this experiment.

## Model and Inputs

18 fixed continuations across six contexts and three head seeds,12,258 trainable
and25,407 total parameters each. A protected utility anchor supplies benefit
and reference moments; the rational branch adjusts bounded all/easy harm.
Inputs are380 previously audited causal features and a causal envelope, with
source preprocessing fitted only on the internal fitting recordings. Targets
are five observed cost moments used in loss/evaluation, never inference inputs.
There is no future endpoint, central-velocity or test-goal construction added.
