# Project Gap and Next Experiment

## Current Evidence
The complete registered comparison supports a useful policy-learning direction:
the aligned all-risk bridge improves six-locality development ADE while keeping
net easy degradation below 2% in every seed/role view. Dual-event gating is more
conservative, sometimes removes useful interventions and still does not enforce
its realized easy positive-harm cap. No policy is promoted in this experiment.

   sites, rather than rebranding a predicted inequality as a theorem.
