# Dual-Event Bridge: Completed Development Result, No Promotion

## Material Passport
Fresh real neural-cost training and evaluation of frozen decisions. Reused
source and selection assets are hash/schema/role verified. This is development
evidence, not independent calibration, confirmation or a deployed model.

## What Ran
54 Torch cost heads completed 2,000 updates each: 108,000 updates and 54 ridge
fits across all six ordered producer/controller pairs and three seeds. Every
producer uses four localities; every new controller fits a disjoint four.
Controller fitting populations are 23,762, 116,823 or 178,384 rows. No unknown
future-label row was sampled. The real 100-step pilot resumed with 1,900 further
updates, inside the budget. The training engine took 183.27 seconds summed over
heads; the whole fitting invocation took roughly 5.5 minutes including cached
asset checking, feature construction and ridge fitting. This is cost-head
training, not fresh backbone or full world-model training.

Registration 99c1e9de and all 126 policy decisions in edef8139 were pushed before
readout. Fresh readout covers the same 38,102 past-indexed rows in six previously
opened selection localities. Independent formula checks cover 192 coordinate
arrays and 2,778 metric reductions. Full verification is in verification.json.

## Positive Evidence
The aligned **all-risk-only bridge** improves ADE over old easy add_only in
every one of 18 dependent seed/role views: **+3.947701% to +5.566952%**, with
18 positive locality-bootstrap intervals. Hard gains are **+4.390476% to
+6.286272%**. Gains versus the frozen producer-training-selected motion baseline
are **+2.893989% to +11.358978%**. Worst easy degradation is **0.730345%**;
18/18 views pass the 2% net-easy rule and none harms an exact-zero-CV case.

All six three-seed-averaged role comparisons have positive exploratory CIs.
Their mean gains are 4.397320% to 5.374677%. They share the same six localities:
neither 18 runs nor 108 locality/views are independent repetitions. Intervals
are not adjusted for model selection or multiple comparisons.

The dual-risk bridge also improves over old easy add_only: +0.000610% to
+1.778578%, with 17 positive seedwise intervals and all six positive
three-seed-averaged intervals. It preserves easy means in 18/18 views.

## Negative Evidence and Limits
The extra easy-risk veto removes useful actions. Dual-risk loses all-ADE to
easy-risk-only in all six seed-averaged comparisons; five intervals are negative
and one overlaps zero. Dual-risk also loses to all-risk-only in all six. Its
hard change can be negative (-0.042511%). Neural dual-risk is not consistently
better than ridge dual-risk: one positive, one negative and four overlapping
paired CIs. This does not support a claim that two risk heads or extra neural
capacity are intrinsically better.

Net easy degradation and positive harm are different quantities. Despite its
good net-easy result, all-risk-only exceeds the 0.02 realized positive-harm
ratio in 10/108 all-event and 68/108 easy-event locality/views (maxima 2.758736%
and 10.541443%). Dual-risk reduces these counts to 0/108 and 3/108, but its
maximum easy ratio is still 3.353010%. Ratios use the actual R reference; easy
degradation uses CV. Neither is an independently calibrated safety guarantee.

The frozen-switch accounting attributes most pooled ADE reduction to changes
between classical motion forecasts, not newly learned neural trajectories.
There is a smaller positive contribution from moves to frozen neural forecasts,
but this is descriptive routing attribution, not a retrained neural ablation.
Raw neural predictions still fail easy in all 18 views (24.77% to 30.68% worst
degradation). No new world-dynamics contribution has been proved.

## Decision
Keep every result, including the negative dual-risk ablation. No best seed,
checkpoint, threshold or deployable winner is selected in this registered study.
The aligned single-risk bridge is a promising **model-selection candidate**, not
a risk-certified policy. Independent calibration/confirmation and matched
single-risk linear/coverage controls are still required. The twelve calibration
and six confirmation localities remain closed. Stage5C and SMC remain off.

No metric, seconds, physical safety, human-gold, true-3D, foundation or submission
readiness claim follows. Eight observed/twelve requested annotation steps and
detector-track image pixels remain the actual task.
