This descriptive diagnosis is motivated after the completed unsupported
independent-risk-calibration experiment. It is not a prospective primary
efficacy test, new calibration, new training or a change to old results.

## Decisive Question

Does the current mean_absolute critic already severely underestimate selected
easy harm on its fitting SOURCE data, or is this mainly amplified on the new
calibration localities? Compare the matched original weighted_centered critic
using the same nine frozen forecasting pipelines and native cost definitions.
This distinguishes fitting-side cost error from a solely new-domain explanation;
it does not identify a causal training mechanism.
