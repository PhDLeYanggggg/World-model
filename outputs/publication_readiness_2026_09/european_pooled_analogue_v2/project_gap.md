## Still Unresolved

remains 15.32 times original; easy harm mass is 7.30 times observed; all-risk MSE
worsens against even pooled mean. Neither deploying retrieval nor blending it
with the original head is supported. Independent roles have not been opened.

The paper still requires a useful risk learner, compliant joint-versus-independent
selection comparisons, three genuine forecaster seeds, recording-level uncertainty
on independent evidence, suitable public strong baselines and a reproducible
anonymous package. Passing a fitting-information screen does not satisfy these.
This is not yet a CVPR submission-ready result or a strong world-model claim.
