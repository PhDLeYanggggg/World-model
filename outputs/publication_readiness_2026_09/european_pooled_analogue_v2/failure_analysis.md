## What Was Not Established

The experiment does not isolate whether poor magnitude transfer comes from
easy-event prevalence, conditional harm size, query-selection bias, high-dimensional
distance, unequal donor-source sizes, residual coordinate scale or their
interaction. A source ID is not a causal mechanism. No source-specific easy-cut
mismatch is asserted without a lineage check. The result does not establish
that all nearest-neighbor methods, scene context or neural risk learning fail.
