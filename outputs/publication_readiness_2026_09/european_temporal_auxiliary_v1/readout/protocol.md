# Fixed Temporal-Auxiliary Readout

## Material Passport

Mode: matched-experiment implementation and verification, before any new
validation prediction readout. This document operationalizes the parent
registered primary/selected utility-risk comparison, not a new training search.
It changes no split, target, risk tolerance, predictor or inference eligibility.

## Admission and Evidence Identity

All 216 fixed-final heads must finish before readout. Require the committed
training freeze, exact source/head/arm grid, 2,000 updates per head, checkpoint
hash/size, original TRAIN preprocessing and input hashes, upstream producer
identity, whole-recording split and matched initialization/draws. Interrupted
heads, missing controls or changed files stop the run, not fallback to a weaker
comparison. Missing training blocks before scientific array loading or remote
checkpoint access. No intermediate-checkpoint validation scoring.

The readout registration and scalar verifier must be committed before use.
Replay the original/additive/Poisson/squared-cost heads through existing owned
read-only streams, verify payload/metadata, and reproduce their old prediction,
action and metric receipts exactly. No CREATE job submission or login-node
training, no foreign project access, no extra numeric disk cache. A remote
failure is an observation blocker, not evidence of a failed remote job.

## Fixed Actions and Error Readout

Compare four old controls and three neural cost heads (none, rowmean, temporal).
Use the unchanged moving/support and signed gain/all-harm/easy-harm rule at the
original 2% budget. No validation threshold tuning, learned calibration, future
completeness filter or target-dependent switch decision.

Report normalized signed-score MSE on all known labels, the original forest's
fixed selected cohort, and each arm's own selected cohort. Retain original full
query-equal weights, then report cohort weight mass and conditional MSE. An
empty cohort is undefined, not zero. Own-selected error is descriptive and is
not a replacement for the common-cohort comparison.

For each comparator against temporal, also match intervention count within each
exact recording/frame query. Rank only by predicted utility, with row-ID tie
breaking. Do not match globally or use realized gains. Missing outcome rows
remain in all causal decisions and completion accounting.

## Utility and Missing Outcomes

Retain the original full and matched lower-utility proxy difference, normalized
by full known reference mass. Explicitly label it a difference of lower bounds,
not itself a lower bound on the paired policy difference.

Add the same-outcome paired completion interval. For actions A (comparator) and
B (temporal), known contribution is sum((B-A)*(benefit-harm)). A wholly unknown
row has signed gain in [-envelope, +envelope]; only XOR selections contribute
uncertainty. Subtract/add the XOR envelope mass for lower/upper difference.
Shared unknown selections cancel. These are finite-completion diagnostics, not
population safety guarantees. They do not repair detector or identity errors.

Report known selected easy positive harm/reference, completion-upper all/easy
positive risk, full easy degradation upper, intervention and unknown-selection
counts. Positive harm at zero easy reference is retained. Undefined selected
reference, even with zero actions, does not pass the risk gate.

## Aggregation and Decision Rule

The source/head grid is inherited exactly: 72 views across 12 already exposed
localities, with three head seeds in each source context. Average seeds/views
inside locality; perform 3,000 paired locality bootstrap draws, seed 20261005.
Do not resample overlapping rows as independent observations. If any required
head contrast is undefined, that CI is undefined; do not drop the head. These
are nominal development intervals, not multiplicity-adjusted confirmation.

Before advancing to a transfer design, temporal must satisfy every comparison
against original/additive/Poisson/cost/none/rowmean: all-row MSE CI upper <0;
original-selected MSE CI upper <=0; full and matched lower-proxy utility delta
CI lower >0; full and matched paired-completion utility CI lower >0. In addition,
every temporal full/matched head must have defined original reference support,
positive completion utility, all/easy positive risk <=2%, and easy preservation
<=2%. Requiring both utility definitions prevents a lower-bound subtraction
artifact from being called policy improvement. A loss-only win is insufficient.

This operational screen cannot promote deployment, open independent roles,
establish external transfer or claim submission readiness. Readout and scalar
replay must finish and all negative comparisons remain visible.

## Verification and Recovery

Independent scalar implementations check fixed actions, per-query tie-breaking,
all policy sums/ratios, signed MSE weights, paired unknown completion and every
contrast. Separate scalar locality resampling verifies confidence intervals and
the summary screen. This is algorithmic cross-checking, not an independent human
audit. Synthetic optimizer-to-reader tests are not real-data model evidence.

Write immutable per-group receipts and hashes; explicit resume replays completed
groups and verifies equality. The run and verification commands have a 12-hour
cap and record PID, heartbeat, elapsed time and peak memory. No trained tensor,
raw history, image or external checkpoint enters Git.

Image-local detector-silver obs8/pred12 rawstride12 only. No metric, seconds,
human-gold, physical-safety, true-3D or foundation claim. Stage5C and SMC stay off.
