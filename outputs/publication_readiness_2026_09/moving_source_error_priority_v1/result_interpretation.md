# What The Source-Held Error Trial Establishes

## Result

This fixed model-side repair does not establish safe held-source utility.
The original 2% positive-harm, easy-net and support rules remain unchanged.
No identity certificate was added. The old experiments remain failures.

Eight new scratch discovery networks and six matched warm-started students
were actually trained. All 14 completed 2,000 updates; full teacher/student
refits are exact. All three held prediction/action/metric blocks reproduce.
This is neural risk training, not a new trajectory dynamics method.

Known floor gain is 2.9544% for source-held errors versus 3.2049% for
in-source errors. The paired known contrast is -0.2505 pp, with missing-label
completion bounds [-0.8846, +0.3837] pp. These bounds span zero and are NOT a
sampling CI. Against the warm kinematic model the known contrast is -0.9874 pp,
with completion bounds [-1.6599, -0.3148] pp. Against affine it is -1.3689 pp,
bounded by [-2.4287, -0.3092] pp. No independent statistical claim follows.

Both students have 0/17 complete original risk/support passes, 14 known all
and 14 known easy risk violations. Source-held error training adds two
undefined selected-easy views. Worst defined signed easy-net degradation
is 64.1321%, versus 24.9970% for the control. Only one of three contexts
meets the prospective expansion condition. No expansion or deployment.

## The Intended Mechanism Did Change

The priority-channel sets are genuinely different. Easy-channel intersections
between the two discovery methods are only 2, 26 and 562 across the three
contexts. Source-held discovery produces 74, 144 and 2,638 easy-priority
channels, compared with 34, 60 and 962 for in-source discovery. These are
training output channels, not independent people or certified decisions.

Source-held-priority MSE decreases relative to the matched control in all
three contexts. However, common anchor MSE is worse in contexts two and
three, and false-safe easy output signs on the full fitting population are
84/91/992 versus 48/65/931 for the control. Thus correcting the selected
weighted errors does not correct the overall risk-decision boundary, even
before generalization is considered. Fitting loss is not a safety surrogate.

## Where Held Utility Is Lost

Six of seven sources have positive known paired utility contrasts, but source
078 falls by5.0345pp and dominates the equal-source average. Its intervention
rate falls from34.0673% to19.6497%. In that source,5,943 known proposed rows
become rejected; their aggregate row-wise net ADE benefit was63,880.3342.
Only276 known rows become newly admitted, contributing1,833.8075. These are
native pixel-error sums over overlapping rows, not independent effects.

The rejected group is NOT certified safe: its easy-budget excess is still
positive. Restoring every rejected action would not solve the safety problem.
The retained group also has positive easy-budget excess. This is a failure to
separate beneficial intervention from easy harm, not merely low switch count.

Source077 moves in the opposite direction: intervention rises from15.2440%
to29.0028%, and known gain increases by2.7395pp. Its851 newly admitted known
rows have positive net benefit6,072.4237, but also positive all-budget excess
5,283.8531 and easy-budget excess510.7565. Recording46 in this source has
64.1321% signed easy-net degradation. Average benefit cannot excuse that harm.

## Explanations Not Established

Feature clipping alone is not established as the cause. Most discovery
subsets have no clipping in the common380 fields; the largest kinematic
clipping fraction is in the third context, whereas the largest held utility
loss is in the second. One held B source has30.71% of rows with at least one
common field clipped, but only0.0445% of all common feature values are clipped.
This is a support diagnostic, not proof that normalization caused failure.

Each discovery teacher sees only one or two sources. Training-row counts,
source difficulty, mask cardinality and total priority-gradient mass differ.
The trial tests this fixed discovery procedure; it does not isolate an
equal-mass causal effect or prove that cross-fitting is generally ineffective.
SOURCE validation was already exposed, seed17 is the only producer seed,
and overlapping windows are not independent replication.

## Next Model Question, Not Yet Run

The immediate question is why weighted continuous risk fitting improves its
chosen targets while the complete zero-admission boundary worsens. Audit
near-zero sign errors against their actual harm magnitude and causal feature
support on B, then check whether a decision-aligned, cost-aware objective
offers a genuinely new comparison relative to earlier failed risk losses.
Do not repeat the same weight sweep or tune thresholds on the exposed held
results. Keep A/B/V roles, the reserved data and original rules unchanged.
