# Learning the risk boundary from matched substitutions

Use the v142 analytic rays and amplitudes, original CV reference,
OLS4 utility comparator, 8/12 steps, and 2% recording rules.
Keep the original three fit/calibration/held source rotations and
three seeds. Do not access A or the independent confirmation role.

Reverify the cached full and minimum-edit training witnesses in
the common bank. Keep a replacement pair only if it changes one
path or exchanges one recipient in the same query, keeps both
recording utility gains positive and easy support defined, but
crosses at least one original 2% risk constraint. Compute labels
from the additive recording totals with only this one replacement.
No witness membership, future coordinate, or validity mask is an
inference feature. Deduplicate identical pair/label tuples.

Train two otherwise identical action rankers. The risk head prefers
the feasible witness action. The utility-only control prefers the
action with the larger worst-reference lower gain on the same pair.
Use the same initialization, samples, optimization and action bank.
Their bounded scores adjust the frozen shape model's gain scores.

Compare shape, utility and risk heads at the same exact per-query
counts using independent selection; separately evaluate the risk
and utility heads with free counts under the existing risk-priced
portfolio. Calibrate only on assigned calibration sources using the
original risk and dual gain checks. Report pair geometry, risk,
coverage, easy degradation, and 3,000 paired source bootstrap draws.
Training pairs and their counts are not independent samples.
