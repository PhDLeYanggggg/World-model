# Exploratory Ablation: Conditioning Calibration on the Decision Region

After repairing a local hard-clipping gradient obstruction, we tested whether
source-excluded decision-conditional calibration could recover safe intervention.
The forecasting models and18 risk heads were frozen. Across72 dependent
original-TRAIN crossings, three source localities supplied coefficients and a
fourth supplied readout. We compared raw scores, global residual correction,
support-only rejection and a fixed16-cell conditional correction.

Conditional correction increased fully supported finite-completion views from
0/72 to20/72, but reduced selected occurrences from27,371 to5,693. Its utility
contrast against raw at matched intervention counts was-0.366 percentage points
(nominal95% locality interval[-0.705,-0.109]); the contrast against global
correction was-0.184[-0.344,-0.046].33 views had undefined selected easy risk.
The registered criterion therefore failed. Posthoc decomposition showed that
conditional penalties removed positive net-benefit decisions and that coarse
cell calibration could fail after subsequent within-cell selection.

These results reject this particular calibration overlay, not conditional risk
learning in general. They are development mechanism evidence:12 previously
exposed localities, repeated producers, overlapping windows and dependent
cross-calibration. Bootstrap coefficients were held fixed; intervals are neither
independent confirmation nor adjusted for the complete adaptive study history.
The utility contrast is reference-normalized completion-lower mass, not ADE/FDE.
The data are detector-silver image-local obs8/pred12, raw stride12. No metric,
seconds, physical-safety, true3D, foundation or deployability claim follows.
