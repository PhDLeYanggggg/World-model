# Policy-Conditional Risk Overlay: Experimental, Not Deployed

## Components and Inputs

The18 frozen rational cost heads take the existing380 past-only causal features
and disagreement envelope. They output benefit, harm and reference moments.
No future endpoint, future-availability flag, central velocity or test-derived
goal is an inference input. No new neural fitting occurred in this experiment.

A deterministic calibration layer uses16 cells defined by predicted signed
utility/envelope and the two original2% risk decisions. Coefficients come only
from other source localities' head-excluded original TRAIN recordings. The readout
source, upstream producer sources and controller sources are excluded as specified
in the frozen role passport. The full prior source-development history remains
exposed; this does not restore independent-test status.

## Training and Inference

Calibration averages normalized all/easy excess-risk residuals equally by
recording and then locality. Positive corrections penalize raw risk scores.
Support requires3 informative recordings,2 localities and32 known occurrences;
these minima are not statistical guarantees. Unsupported cells veto actions.
Inference receives only frozen scores, envelope, eligibility and coefficients.
Future losses supply calibration supervision/readout only. Unknown outcomes
are not imputed and remain in completion bounds.
