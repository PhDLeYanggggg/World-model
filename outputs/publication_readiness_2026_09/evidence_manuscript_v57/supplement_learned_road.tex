\section{Learning with Matched and Exchanged Road Context}
Sixty-three native fits compare no map, aligned map, and map features
exchanged between agents at the same observed query. All fits exclude
the outer location; donor producers additionally exclude their own
donor location, while donor refiners need not. Map availability, the
forecast menu, recipients and radii are matched. The inference rule
keeps fixed tangent if utility and harm-penalized rankings disagree.

At half radius, the aligned-map direct conservative differences are
\LearnedRoadNoMap{} percentage points versus no map and
\LearnedRoadWrong{} versus exchanged maps. It beats its own same-count
shuffle by \LearnedRoadShuffle{} points, but the paired difference
in that advantage versus the exchanged-map model is
\LearnedRoadMargin{} points. Holding map-trained weights fixed,
exchanging alignment changes \LearnedRoadChanged{} of
\LearnedRoadEligible{} eligible choices with a direct benefit of
\LearnedRoadSwap{} points for correct alignment. Intervals use
paired location resampling after averaging seeds. These results do
not establish a benefit from correct map alignment. The fixed-tangent
contrast is \LearnedRoadTangent{} points and
\LearnedRoadPass/606 views pass the original risk rule.
