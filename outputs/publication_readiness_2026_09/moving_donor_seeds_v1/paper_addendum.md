# Fixed-Teacher Risk-Head Replication And Consensus

We replicate the donor-augmentation component with risk-head seeds17/29/43,
holding the seed17forecast cache, source partitions, original120features,
source/query sampler, mixture weights and4,096-update budget fixed. Forty-eight
new heads and48full exact refits are completed before the new-seed readout;
the24seed17heads are cached-verified, not presented as fresh training. A full
real-data seed17engine-equivalence test and seed29recovery test precede training.

The predeclared information criterion is met in3/3,2/3,2/3contexts respectively.
All nine cost contrasts versus matched own-only are positive, but one false-safe
contrast and one MSE contrast fail. The macro cost-ratio reduction versus the
matched control is12.16%, with an exploratory source-cluster95%interval
[2.66%,27.18%] from3000paired replicates. The corresponding forest contrast is
4.15%[-4.75%,8.12%], which does not establish superiority to that stronger control.

Before opening the new-seed readout, we also specified two score aggregators:
the mean and per-channel maximum of all three augmented heads. Maximum implements
unanimous admission at the unchanged zero signed-risk threshold. It reduces
harmful known-proposal admissions to7/26/26, compared with34/134/323for the mean
and0/1/0for the forest. Total known-proposal cost is lower than the forest in
all three contexts for both fixed aggregators; false-safe cost is still higher.

These results support a practical donor-information component under the current
fixed teachers and exposed source folds. They do not identify source count as
the sole cause, supply three forecaster seeds, establish a calibrated uncertainty
bound or verify independent generalization. The small cluster count and prior
development exposure limit interpretation of the intervals. Seed agreement
cannot replace full-population risk/reference/support evaluation, including
unknown-label contributions. The original2%contract, empty-support NA and24-group
main NA are unchanged. No deployable-policy, metric/seconds, true3D/foundation,
Stage5C or SMC claim follows. The compiled main paper remains unchanged and is
not submission-ready; this addendum is component evidence, not a completed method.
