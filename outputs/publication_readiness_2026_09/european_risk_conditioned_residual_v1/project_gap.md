# Remaining World-Model And Paper Gaps

The full research objective is not achieved. This diagnostic closes one
specific input hypothesis; it does not replace the requested world-model,
independent-calibration or submission-readiness standard with a simpler goal.

## Evidence Added

The original neural estimators can be replayed and their fixed risk features
are causal and numerically supported. New score-conditioned residuals and all
controls were frozen before this readout. The registered improvement and guard
criteria fail. Negative and inconclusive results, matched controls, motion-only
arms and uncertainty are retained. No new model is promoted.

## What Still Must Be Demonstrated

1. Stable baseline-relative risk estimates that improve actual neural
   intervention utility, not merely membership ranking or a weaker comparator.
2. Scene-level joint decisions versus independent-agent and whole-scene
   controls at matched intervention rates or budgets, with strong forecast
   baselines on the same data/protocol and source exclusions.
3. Independent scene-level calibration and confirmation after development
   choices are frozen. Earlier development exposure cannot be undone by
   renaming a split or producing another manifest.
4. Accuracy, easy degradation, tail/worst-locality error and interaction
   consistency, with locality/recording uncertainty and at least three seeds.
5. A defensible method contribution beyond conventional ridge post-processing
   or combining JEPA/Transformer modules, plus complete main experiments,
   manuscript, supplementary material and anonymous reproduction package.

The next smallest useful diagnostic is the fitting-only constraint/conditional-
signal separation in the failure analysis. It is not a reason to open reserved
data or fit a policy on held outcomes. Larger training is justified only by a
specific remaining limitation, not by the number of completed experiment stages.

not metric/seconds, true 3D, foundation, human gold or physical safety.
