# Method Position And Scope

Model auditing and correction across identifiable subpopulations are established
research directions. [Multicalibration](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/hebert-johnson18a.html)
studies subgroup calibration and its relationship with learning. [Multiaccuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12317)
develops black-box auditing and post-processing for classification. The primary
paper records were checked on 26 September 2026.

This experiment is a small fixed ridge diagnostic for continuous expected
trajectory harm. Adding model-score bins is not claimed as a new algorithm,
nor as implementing either paper's full procedure or inheriting its guarantees.
Our dependent development localities and unbounded observed trajectory errors
do not establish subgroup calibration, fairness, future safety or independent
generalization. A positive result would only justify the next controlled
intervention-utility test; a negative result is retained.

The intended contribution remains baseline-relative benefit/harm estimation
and scene-level joint intervention at matched budgets, with independent scene
calibration and support-aware fallback. This diagnostic must serve that question,
not replace it with an easier claim about cost regression alone.
