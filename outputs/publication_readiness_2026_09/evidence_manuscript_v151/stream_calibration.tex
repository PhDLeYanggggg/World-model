\section{Calibration on realized streaming slots}
The previous experiment calibrated on a history-based proposal
roster. We replace it with the chronological controller's slots,
leaving outer recipients, paths, peaks and selector checkpoints fixed.
The count and ranking heads exclude both outer and calibration
locations; each count training menu also excludes its own location
from the ranker. This adds \StreamSplineFits{} count fits
(\StreamSplineUpdates{} updates) and calibrates on \StreamSplineActual{}
actual slots rather than \StreamSplineProxy{} proxy slots, aggregated
over the overlapping donor folds and three seeds.

Neither aggregate risk calibration nor calibration directly against
the original per-recording checks chooses any spline replacements
on the held slots. The proxy-calibrated rule chose \StreamSplinePriorChoices{}.
At the same outer intervention budget, the conservative contrast
of streaming versus proxy calibration is \StreamSplinePrimary{} pp
(location bootstrap, 3,000 draws). They both pass risk on
\StreamSplineJointRisk{} of 606 recording--seed views and both references
on \StreamSplineJointDual{}. The past-prefix control yields
\StreamSplinePrefixRisk{} and \StreamSplinePrefixDual{} respectively.
Against that control, the lower utility contrast is
\StreamSplineAgainstPrefix{} pp. The zero spline count means the
original analytic edits remain, not zero interventions against CV.

Only \StreamSplineDoubleQueries{} of \StreamSplineActiveQueries{} edited
queries contain two slots, across \StreamSplineDoubleViews{} views.
With outcomes known, restricting shape substitutions to those
queries yields \StreamSplineRestricted{} qualified views, versus
\StreamSplineAll{} if all slots are eligible. This is an upper bound
for the two-shape menu restricted to those queries, not a bound on
all ways of modeling interactions. It motivates studying decisions
across queries or earlier nomination rather than a different
same-query penalty. The original rule, undefined support cases, and
confirmation status do not change.
