\section{Direct Ranking at the Frozen Edit Clock}

All arms retain \RankClockEdits{} edits at identical queries.
The pairwise target is $\operatorname{sgn}(g)(|g|-b)_+$,
where $g$ is observed gain difference and $b$ is the
missing-step debit on changed recipients. The guard requires
positive predicted gain and no increase in three predicted
risk components; it is not a certificate. Fitting trajectories
and release heads exclude donor and outer site. The scalar
control uses the same examples and the earlier additive proxy.

Direct lower gain over fixed rank is \RankClockPaired{} for
pairwise training, \RankClockGuard{} with the harm guard,
and \RankClockScalar{} for scalar regression. The direct
comparison with scalar regression is \RankClockPairScalar{}
for pairwise and \RankClockGuardScalar{} with the guard.
Missing-step debits make these paired comparisons
nonadditive. The guard's direct effect versus CV is
\RankClockCV{}; error-mass weighting instead gives
\RankClockWeightedCV{}. Removing the site with the largest
guarded effect leaves \RankClockGuardWithoutHigh{} for
the guard and \RankClockPairWithoutHigh{} for pairwise
over fixed rank in a post-hoc sensitivity analysis.
Neither beats CV or certifies the full roster. The local
oracle yields \RankClockOracle{} over fixed rank, while
even the exact original-risk optimum at this clock passes
only \SlateQueueClockCapacity{} views. Thus the ranking
and the support of the fixed clock remain separate limits.
