# Matched Candidate Replacement: Development Evidence Addendum

## Question

Does an unchanged cost-aware intervention learner benefit from replacing its
neural forecast with the matched active producer's detector-history forecast?
This is a more specific question than whether a raw forecaster has lower ADE.

## Controlled Method

We retain six physical actions, the frozen causal floor, source fitting
recordings, causal row eligibility, normalization, common sampling draws,
head seeds and decoder. We replace only candidate7 and rebuild its motion,
region and disagreement fields together with its supervised costs. Separate
causal and label interfaces prevent future targets from becoming features.
The54 heads each receive2,000 optimizer updates. All36 old-control endpoints
and readouts must match their original scientific states. The new candidate
comes from a frozen actual active producer, not the earlier flat backbone.

Paired comparisons use the actual paths and their own costs across banks.
An integer action index is not sufficient when action7 denotes different
forecasts. The reference mass is counted once. Missing outcomes contribute
causal disagreement bounds rather than imputed trajectories. Count-matched
utility is separate from the full-policy recording-risk requirement; empty
comparisons remain undefined. Three head seeds are averaged within the
original contexts before3,000 locality resamples.

CI[-0.171084,+0.012147]; the completion-lower contrast is-0.681429pp,

benefit from actual neural use coexists with failure of the full policy.

## What Can And Cannot Be Claimed

The controlled negative result and the distinction between raw proposal,
actual neural-use and complete-policy utility can be reported as development
evidence. They do not establish an independently generalizing method,
scene-joint selection, a calibrated risk guarantee or submission readiness.
SOURCE has historical development exposure. All independent outcome roles
remain closed. Full-roster FDE is undefined because a required locality has
no requested final labels; partial ADE is not the complete12-step path error.

Labels are supplied detector-silver bbox-midpoint pixels at8observed,
12predicted steps,stride12raw frames. Not human gold,metric/seconds,
verified live sensor-as-of,true3D or foundation evidence. No Stage5C orSMC.
The complete result,actual loss traces,exact-replay receipt and limitations
are linked in[readout.md](readout.md),not silently promoted to a main claim.
