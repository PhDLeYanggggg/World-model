\section{Future source qualification on WEPDTOF}
WEPDTOF~\cite{wepdtof} supplies \WepClips{} fisheye clips from
\WepSources{} original videos. The two pairs of clips sharing a
video are grouped together. We remove ambiguous identity/frame
annotations (\WepAmbiguous{} keys) and retain \WepUnlisted{} images
without annotation entries as unlabeled. At stride four on the
released frame grid, \WepEligibleSources{} sources contribute
\WepQueries{} nonoverlapping queries. Of \WepTargets{} targets
with complete histories, \WepComplete{} (\WepCompleteRate{}\%)
have all twelve future labels. History eligibility does not
require future visibility. These are structural counts, not
performance estimates or independent sample counts.

For engineering, we freeze \WepWindows{} disjoint windows in one
source using only the frame grid. The same online tracker consumes
the current-frame MobileNet or RAPiD detections; it never sees
reference tracks. CV and OLS4 trajectories freeze before the
reference comparison. Of \WepRef{} labeled histories, the detector
arms match \WepMobileHist{} and \WepRapidHist{} stable histories in
\WepMobileWindows{} and \WepRapidWindows{} windows, respectively.
Their overlap is \WepBothHist{} histories. \WepCommon{}
The first window is cold-replayed in both arms. This is an input
and baseline pilot on one source; no neural policy is calibrated
or confirmed. The other \WepReserved{} source groups are reserved
for a frozen one-shot transfer test and have no forecast readout.
Neither physical calibration nor seconds-level
timing is assumed for this release.
