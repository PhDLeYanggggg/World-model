# Remaining Research Gap

effects, but its registered broad gradient-concentration and radial-support
flags are false. Held excess error is concentrated without corresponding
evidence of general fitting-gradient domination. This prevents an unsupported
sampling or gradient-surgery explanation from becoming the next model claim.

## Core Gap

scene-level joint intervention without harming easy cases. Neither ordinary
nor severity-weighted auxiliary supervision has established that. Fitting
loss reduction and event ranking are insufficient. Independent selection,
risk calibration and confirmation remain reserved; they must not be spent
on debugging this failed estimator.
