\section{A reference bridge with predicted future-label support}
The sum of the distances to CV and OLS4 is minimized at any
point between their forecasts. We fit that interpolation by
lead, optionally adding a bounded motion-frame spline offset.
The offset is either gated by the mean label availability
estimated on fitting sources, or by a probability predicted
from past motion and current neighbors. A third arm has no
offset. All arms have the same availability head and loss;
the trajectory gradient is stopped at the probability gate.
No realized future mask enters the model at inference.
This predicts support for evaluating an intervention, unlike
imputing a missing observed history~\cite{trajimpute}.
Availability is not a proxy for physical presence.

The training objective rewards conservative OLS4 gain and
penalizes negative CV gain and excess CV recording risks,
with the same separate calibration and exact evaluator.
We fit \RefBridgeFits{} models for \RefBridgeUpdates{} updates
across three source rotations and three seeds. We use
\RefBridgeRecords{} exposed development recordings from
\RefBridgeSources{} sources (\RefBridgeViews{} recording--seed views).
Strict calibration chooses \RefBridgeSelected{}/\RefBridgeCells{}
options. A failed screen returns CV without certifying its
undefined edited support.
