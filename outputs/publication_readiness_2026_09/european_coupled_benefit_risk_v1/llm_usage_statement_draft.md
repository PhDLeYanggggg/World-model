# LLM Usage Statement: Draft For Author Review

This is a disclosure draft,not a claim that a final manuscript or all scientific
claims have received the author's review. It has not been submitted.

OpenAI Codex was used for assistance with experiment implementation,debugging,
test development,exploratory analyses,literature retrieval,figure preparation,
and drafting/editing research text. Its assistance also covered proposing and
implementing controlled hypotheses during the research iterations. Model outputs
were checked against executable code,registered experiment definitions,stored
artifacts and replay results where available. Those checks do not establish
scientific validity or replace the author's responsibility. The final author
must review and approve the manuscript,disclosure and claims before submission.
