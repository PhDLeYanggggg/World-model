\section{Release Timing With The Frozen Neural Bank}

We retain the raw comoving EqMotion paths and treat the
hardness rule's count as a stream of released unit credits.
No credit can be used before release or transferred across
recordings. At a query we allow at most the current release
plus one delayed edit, bounded by available legal rows.
The diagnostic moves only the time at which a credit can
be spent; the native prediction grid and original 2\% risk
checks are unchanged.

For release prefix $R_t$ and edit prefix $E_t$, spending
all credits with a maximum delay $d$ requires
$R_{t-d}\leq E_t\leq R_t$ and $E_T=R_T$, with negative
indices interpreted as zero. These constraints are sufficient
by matching spends to released credits in FIFO order.
Adding them to the outcome-aware maximin risk program
retains the same per-recording total and tests timing
without changing the path bank. Zero, two and eight
queries of delay qualify \NeuralCreditFixed{}, \NeuralCreditShort{}
and \NeuralCreditLong{} of \NeuralCreditViews{} recording--seed views.
The numbers lacking eligible easy reference at reachable
queries are \NeuralCreditFixedNoEasy{}, \NeuralCreditShortNoEasy{} and
\NeuralCreditLongNoEasy{}, with another \NeuralCreditNoRelease{} views
receiving no credit in any arm. These are label-aware
witnesses, not causal intervention counts.

The fixed rule holds a credit until at least the next query.
The value rule defers a nonpositive predicted risk-adjusted
value, and the support rule also waits for predicted easy
support until it has accumulated sufficient estimated
support. Their deadline is two queries, and an unused
credit expires. Neither has access to the recording end.
Against spending the same used credits immediately, direct
lower utility changes by \NeuralCreditFixedEffect{},
\NeuralCreditValueEffect{}, and \NeuralCreditSupportEffect{}.
Higher hindsight capacity does not establish a causal
timing improvement or qualify a policy for confirmation.
