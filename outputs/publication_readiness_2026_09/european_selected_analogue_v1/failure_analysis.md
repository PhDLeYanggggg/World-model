# Failure Taxonomy and Useful Signal

## Confirmed Support Failure

Eight source localities cannot supply another eligible fitting recording for
the current within-source donor rule. All48 unsupported banks have zero donors;
reducingk from32 would not repair them. Their10,248 queries remain explicit.
This is a limitation of the reference design and existing fitting coverage,
not a runtime crash, proof of missing trajectories everywhere or a hard blocker
on all project work. The full scientific screen cannot be evaluated as positive.

## Confirmed Magnitude Failure Where Supported

On24 supported views, nearest retrieval reduces easy MSE relative to both
matched analogue controls, but is3.90x worse than frozen original. Its predicted
easy harm is502.945952 versus observed91.730953. The original is also badly
miscalibrated in aggregate (1.291178). Neither prediction is a safety certificate.
Event discrimination and conditional magnitude are different requirements.

Reference donors are all eligible rows whereas queries are the original selected
cohort. Donor-to-query selection shift and sparse harms are plausible causes;
this experiment has not isolated their causal contribution. Standardized
Euclidean distance on380 dimensions may also be inadequate. It is not evidence
that the network architecture alone caused the error or that causal information
is absent. Do not prescribe another blind threshold or temperature sweep.

## Representation and Coherence Limits

The probe changes only H and easy-H. Original benefit and reference predictions
stay fixed. Nearest32 happens to produce zero B+H envelope exceedances here;
source mean produces2 and shuffled3. They are counted rather than projected
away. No policy is executed and this comparison is not a coherent neural head
replacement. Different constraints also limit direct causal interpretation of
its comparison with the frozen neural outputs.

Unknown outcomes remain in prediction and support counts, outside supervised
MSE. Shared-support tables are clearly secondary; the full primary keeps all72
views and undefined values. No gain is established on a new held recording or
independent role. Existing training exposure prevents a whole-pipeline OOF claim.
