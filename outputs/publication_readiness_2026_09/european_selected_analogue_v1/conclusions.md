## Decision

clip values or a blend coefficient from these results. Before another registered
model comparison, audit whether other approved fitting sources can supply a
compatible recording-excluded reference bank. A pooled-source control must
preserve target exclusion and unknown queries and keep donor shift explicit.
That follow-up is **not_run**, not an already repaired model.

exposure. This is not whole-pipeline OOF or independent generalization. No
