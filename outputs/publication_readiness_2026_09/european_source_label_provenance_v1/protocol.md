## Question And Fixed Scope

Determine whether absent future labels are an import error or absent exact
scoped agent/frame observations in the supplied export. Keep obs8/pred12,
stride12 raw frames, all original query IDs, localities and masks. Do not drop
locality082, shorten the horizon, interpolate, repair a label or open the
89 controller-held recordings. Independent selection/calibration/confirmation
and DroneCrowd remain closed. Previously exposed SOURCE is not made independent.

The independent lookup constructs scoped agent/frame keys from freshly parsed
raw rows. It does not call the source converter's per-track lookup. Check
every current midpoint, every future-valid flag and every stored known target
coordinate. Invalid numeric placeholders are not labels.

## Missing-Step Partition

Use the following exclusive priority on each absent requested step:

1. Beyond the largest frame with a detection in this supplied record.
2. Within the record's observed range but after the last detection of that ID.
3. Within that ID's observed span and other agents have a row at the frame.
4. Within that ID's span and no agent has a row at the frame.

These are export-level descriptions, not verified video ends, physical exits,
occlusion mechanisms, homography, seconds or human-gold labels. Per-query
any-reason counts can overlap; per-step and final-step counts are disjoint.
Record/track maxima and future support are audit outcomes, never features.

explicit --resume support. A separate verify process reparses all raw records

per record, a 12h boundary and 10GiB disk reserve. Do not restart live work on

import discrepancy to investigate in a new version, not a silent overwrite.
