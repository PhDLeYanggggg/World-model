\paragraph{Matched two-edit choice.}
With the original candidate paths and risk rule, exact enumeration
raises the one-edit ceiling from \TwoEditOne{} to \TwoEditAny{} of
\TwoEditViews{} credit-starved recording-seed views. Two edits in
one query have a witness in \TwoEditSame{} views, versus
\TwoEditCross{} across different queries. The additional feasible
views do not exhibit an easy-plus-hard witness under this menu.

On the full development roster, we train one shared value head per
held location and seed. All causal arms use the first eligible
two-edit query and emit exactly \TwoEditEdits{} edits per arm.
Aggregating predicted gain and risk before choosing the pair, rather
than summing individual scores, gives \TwoEditJoint{} joint checks
versus \TwoEditIndependent{} for independent choice.
The paired conservative gain is \TwoEditGain{} percentage points
(95\% location-bootstrap interval). Adding a relative separation
penalty does not improve the held-query relative-path diagnostic.
The chronology used by the oracle is not available to these causal
policies. No operating policy qualifies, and independent confirmation
remains unopened.
