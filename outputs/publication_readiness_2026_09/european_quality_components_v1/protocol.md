# Frozen Quality-Component Attribution

Researcher-owned source-only diagnostic, registered after the complete quality
auxiliary result and before these component readouts. It is not an independently
preregistered confirmation study and does not train/select a new policy.

The paired quality experiment improved MSE and matched utility but increased
risk violations. Freeze its72 quality checkpoints and their original forests,
source recordings, observed/unknown labels, original preprocessing, action
thresholds, support masks and2%selected-risk budget. Use only past features at
inference. Read immutable checkpoint bytes from owned CREATE storage into memory;
do not write a local numeric cache below the existing10GiB reserve.

For each of five raw cost corrections (benefit, harm, reference, easy-reference,
easy-harm), compute both an only-this-component and an all-except-this-component
intervention before the original feasibility projection. Include original and
full-quality anchors, plus nonnegative full-quality before projection. All13
variants are mandatory. The preprojection variant is diagnostic and may violate
moment constraints; it is never a proposed deployable predictor. Feasibility
projection can couple components, so these are not additive causal effects or
retrained ablations.

Evaluate full and exact per-recording/frame count-matched selected utility and
completion bounds. Compare to the original, retaining unknown outcomes and
undefined denominators. Decompose known actual risk excess into predicted excess,
harm underestimation and budget-weighted reference inflation for added, removed
and retained actions. Actual excess=predicted excess+(true harm-predicted harm)
+0.02*(predicted reference-true reference). Report unknown envelopes separately;
do not call their worst-case bound observed harm. Old failed constraint counts
among added actions overlap and must not be summed as exclusive causes.

Keep all72 views and three head seeds sharing upstream43; not three independent
end-to-end seeds. Average views within locality before3000 paired bootstrap draws.
All12 previously exposed localities stay development only. Multiple component
contrasts are descriptive, nominal and not selection criteria; no winning
component/threshold/seed, no transfer, no deployment and no independent-role
access. Scope remains obs8/pred12 raw stride12, image-local detector-silver, not
metric/seconds, human-gold, physical safety, true3D, foundation or submission-ready.
Stage5C and SMC remain off.

Pilot one immutable group with exact inference and arithmetic replay. Complete
all72 if feasible without reducing scope. Save config, heartbeat, immutable group
reports and parent hashes. Resume rechecks existing groups; completed outputs
cannot be overwritten. Independently verify risk arithmetic and aggregate
reduction. A completed diagnosis may motivate a separately registered targeted
learning experiment; it does not itself repair the predictor.
