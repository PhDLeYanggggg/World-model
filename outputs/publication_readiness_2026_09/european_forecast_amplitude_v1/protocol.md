## Fixed Causal Construction

For floor B and each of the original seven complete causal candidates P,
construct P_alpha = B + alpha*(P-B) with the predeclared coefficients
1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 and 1/16. All physical and neural candidates receive the same
grid. Alpha=1 must exactly replay original paths, choices and policy metrics.
Alpha=0 is an engineering-only no-op test, not a scientific candidate.

For the original predicted benefit/harm/reference/easy-reference/easy-harm
vector, use alpha*benefit, alpha*harm, reference, easy-reference,
alpha*easy-harm in the unchanged selector. These are convex-loss surrogates,
not refitted expected moments. For fixed known masks, realized fractional
benefit is at least alpha times endpoint benefit, and fractional positive
harm is at most alpha times endpoint harm. Numerical checks verify this on
all known rows. It does NOT establish that the original learned estimates
are calibrated, or provide a conditional/population/physical risk certificate.

Cost estimates come from the original causal endpoint features, not features
recomputed with future labels. Every action and analytic no-op exclusion uses
past geometry and predicted paths only. Preserve causal eligibility. Future
targets and masks enter a separate scoring function after decisions, never
the cost head or coefficient. Actual fractional ADE/FDE and labels are
recomputed from the unchanged original target/mask; scaling observed endpoint
errors is invalid and forbidden. Canonical completion envelopes are recomputed
from CV, floor and the actual fractional candidate, not naively scaled.

not deployment or automatic opening of held/independent roles.
