# Past-Only Prefix-Adaptive Velocity

The fixed recipient and two-path affine family cannot meet the
original recording gate. Test a different causal direction: anchored
OLS slopes fitted over two, four and six observed positions. Hold
the forecast basis fixed and vary only the mixture weights.

Use pseudo-origins 3 through 6 (zero-based) and 1/2-step endpoints
when they lie in the eight observations. At each origin fit on the
prefix only, with windows truncated at its start, and measure error
in displacement per step. Weight the two-step error by 0.5 and the
origin by 0.8 per step of age. Use RMS observed displacement floored
at 0.05 image pixels per step to scale the weighted errors. Multiply
the prior weights (0.25, 0.50, 0.25) by the exponential of minus
scaled error over temperature 0.5 and normalize. Compare the prior
alone, query-mean adapted weights and independent adapted weights.
No fit, outcome-dependent temperature or test statistic is used.

Primary: paired completion-lower trajectory ADE contrast against
the fixed mixture at the original 9,675 genuine switch recipients.
If a new forecast is exactly CV there, retain the original switch
path. Compare against query-uniform weights, OLS4, CV and the original
gain selector. Report unchanged recording support and 2% checks,
matched pair geometry, and the whole-roster forecast as a separate
diagnostic with OLS8 included. Score after freezing past-only path
digests. Use 3,000 paired draws of the 14 development sources after
seed averaging across 34 exposed recordings. This is not an independent
test. Pursue a new candidate allocation only if the forecast contrast
improves without a compelling negative CV/OLS4 accuracy result.
