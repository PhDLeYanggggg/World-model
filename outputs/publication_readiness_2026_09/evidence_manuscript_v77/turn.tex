\paragraph{Turn persistence and the one-edit geometric ceiling.}
Adding a constant turn to the original candidate bank at the same
\TurnEdits{} recipients, times, and peak offsets gives
\TurnJoint{} joint passes versus \TurnAnchor{} and a paired
conservative gain of \TurnEffect{} percentage points.
No fitted selector is changed. On the \TurnViews{} credit-starved
views, adding a matched straight, tapered or sustained path raises
one-edit hindsight capacity from \TurnLegacy{} to \TurnExpanded{}
in each case.

To remove the finite menu, allow any path in the legal CV-centered
displacement balls. A single edit must supply positive easy
reference. At known steps the projection toward the label jointly
minimizes error against both references. Let \(C\) be the gain over
CV there and \(O\) the whole-recording gain over OLS4 when the missing
steps stay at CV. Moving \(x\) toward OLS4 at missing steps attains
the frontier \((C-x,O+x)\) for \(0\leq x\leq L\), where \(L\) sums
the reachable distances to OLS4. Hence strict gain over both
requires and suffices that \(C>0\) and \(O+\min(C,L)>0\).
Witnesses preserve the original risk tests and were rescored.
The exact ceiling is \TurnBounded{}/\TurnViews{} at the largest
legal radius and \TurnUnbounded{}/\TurnViews{} without a radius
bound. These are label-aware one-edit bounds, not causal model
gains or independent evidence.
