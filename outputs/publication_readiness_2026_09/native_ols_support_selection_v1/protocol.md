# Support-aware selective OLS4 replacement

The source-separated OLS4 hindcast improved observed labels but its conservative gain was negative; no common shrinkage helped the fixed directions. Earlier label-availability factorization fixed recipients and neural counts. Here ask if OLS4-relative support prediction helps when recipients and amplitude can both change. Do not access DroneCrowd calibration or confirmation, and do not alter the official CV risk rule.

Use the same nine fitting sources (69,746 windows), excluding all fourteen exposed development sources. Native observation-8/prediction-12, image pixels. The same four causal motion options as the prior ranker are allowed at amplitudes 0.25, 0.5 or 1 relative to OLS4; zero is fallback. Fit three MLP arms with paired initialization and draws at seeds 17, 29, and 43, 600 updates each and batches of 128. Feature schema: past motion, candidate geometry, causal prefix hindcasts, and nearest-eight complete-history neighbor summaries. Only the model's support branch loses neighborhood features in the ego-support ablation.

The direct control predicts signed gain and positive harm including the maximum path-disagreement debit on missing rows; its auxiliary missingness output is not used at inference. Factorized arms predict both moments only on observed rows and estimate the probability of a wholly missing future. At inference, expected net score is `(1-p)*(conditional gain - conditional harm) - 2*p*debit`; the two debit terms are the negative gain and positive harm for an unsupported forecast. The constant-support comparator uses the same fitted neighborhood conditional heads and replaces only its probability with equal-source training prevalence. Scores are normalized using past RMS displacement. No model or threshold selection on exposed development.

Only positive-score replacements activate, with at most two per query and never all agents. This is a diagnostic proposal rule, not a calibrated safety guarantee. In secondary equal-count controls, enforce the neighborhood factorized arm's exact realized count per query for direct, ego, and constant alternatives. Score the complete frozen development roster against OLS4 and CV, plus the original full-amplitude ranker. Primary endpoint: conservative individual ADE gain against OLS4. Mechanism contrast: neighborhood factorization versus matched constant probability. Bootstrap 3,000 paired sources after seed averaging; report signed gain, harm, unknown switched rows, and AUROC/AUPRC/Brier for the released label mask. The mask is not physical visibility. Missing risk support does not pass. No independent confirmation, deployment, Stage5C, SMC, metric or seconds claim.
