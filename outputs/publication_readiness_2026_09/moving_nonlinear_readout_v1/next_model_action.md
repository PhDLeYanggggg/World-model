# What To Change Next, Without Changing The Rules

## Evidence Now Available

The affine solve and nonlinear readouts address two specific explanations:
incomplete final-layer optimization and a linear output bottleneck. Neither
repairs admitted-set risk. The hidden-feature readout improves fitting utility
over its score-only control, but not held-source utility. It still admits
actions with positive observed budget excess while predicting negative excess.

This is a representation/conditional-generalization problem to investigate,
not proof of an irreducible information limit. The six fits are complete;
the next training intervention below has not run.

## Next Model-Side Decision

Before another large run, inspect the retained-harm fitting coverage and the
causal history/interaction inputs of the frozen encoder. Compare its errors
on fitting and exposed held SOURCE within the same pre-existing action and
context groups. Do not select only favorable scenes or discard undefined views.
Determine whether useful distinctions are missing from the encoder input,
compressed out of the representation, or present but not learned by the loss.

The next model comparison should change that identified representation or
conditional-supervision defect with a matched control. Its configuration,
source roles and expansion rule must freeze before new held-source readout.
Another threshold sweep, larger forest, repeated identical affine solve or
extra iterations of the already negative selected-set calibration is not
justified by the current evidence. Better fitting MSE alone cannot authorize
expansion or deployment.

## Non-Negotiable Evaluation Boundaries

- Original 2% positive-harm, easy-net and support rules remain unchanged.
- No identity-fallback certification; unavailable comparisons remain NA.
- Missing full easy-reference support cannot be supplied by a model change.
  The old 24-group primary remains unavailable, not silently repaired.
- Exposed SOURCE and nested C may support development, not independent claims.
- Reserved Moving groups, DUT, DroneCrowd and H remain closed.
- No new test tuning, future endpoint input, central velocity or test goals.
- No deployment promotion, Stage5C execution, SMC or submission-ready claim.

The immediate objective is a model that retains useful interventions and
predicts admitted risk reliably under the original contract. Statistical
confirmation and independent evaluation remain later requirements, not
properties conferred by exact replay of this negative pilot.
