# CVPR 2027 Policy Refresh

Checked against the official pages on 2026-09-24. Registration remains
10 November 2026, full submission 16 November, and supplementary material
23 November, all Anywhere on Earth. The existing internal schedule is unchanged.
Source: [official dates](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/Dates).

The [official call](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)
requires current OpenReview profiles for all authors, includes dual-submission
and invited-reviewer obligations, and still says its detailed LLM guidelines are
being finalized. This is not a complete compliance audit or authorization to
submit a paper.

The linked [2027 Author Guidelines](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines)
again returns404. Exact 2027 template, page limit, detailed anonymization and
final LLM policy therefore remain unverified; do not substitute last year's rules.
Author/profile readiness and institutional clearance remain separate checks.
Submission requires the author's final confirmation and sufficient research
evidence. A verified date is not evidence of a submission-ready method.
