\section{Learning query plans from verified recording plans}
The teacher uses donor outcomes to solve the two-reference
maximin problem under the original recording risk constraints.
It keeps at least one CV agent and changes at most two paths in
each query. Only checked positive plans or certified exclusions
supply labels. The student ranks query plans including abstention.
Its additive and pair-coupled scores share the action encoder and
training examples. The pair term sees relative displacement of
the proposed paths, not future observations.

We train \SlateFits{} heads for \SlateSteps{} updates. For the
held-site OpenDD experiment the bank contains OLS4 and median
slope, so no learned forecaster can carry the held site into
another fold. Cuts for scoring come only from fitting sites.
The independent and common-family matched controls receive the
joint policy's count and peak slots per query. They may shorten a
path but not enlarge it. Unsupported common-family matches are
not included in its paired contrast.

These are development recording--seed views. Joint minus matched
independent lower utility is \SlateUtility{} CV-normalized points,
with a bootstrap over \SlateSources{} estimable locations after
averaging their recording--seed views. No edit is not an
estimable matched utility contrast. A difference in pass counts
does not establish a utility advantage.

For the exposed WEPDTOF pilot, the training donors receive both
splines from forecasters excluding their site. The camera uses
the frozen all-donor spline bank. The joint rule makes
\SlateCamEdits{} edits across the three seeds and passes both
checks in \SlateCamPass{}/3. Its conservative gain versus OLS4
is \SlateCamOls{} points. No edited trajectory lacks a matched
future, so missing edited outcomes do not explain the risk failure
on this camera. This does not change the fallback or the
independent confirmation status.
