# Learning the number and composition of edits

Use the original risk scorer, CV risk reference and OLS4 utility
comparator, frozen three-way source roles, 8/12 native steps and
three seeds. A and independent confirmation outcomes remain closed.
Keep a common analytic multi-amplitude path bank in fit, calibration
and held roles. The earlier frozen shape predictor supplies path
costs; only this earlier model has seen fit-role outcomes.

For each query, construct legal distinct whole-query choices at
25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of eligible rows (rounded up), plus the
frozen analytic portfolio count and no change. At each count,
retain independent and signed-risk-priced path/recipient rankings
as well as an OLS4 proximity ranking. Train two query scorers on
the same choices and outcomes, with and without pair geometry
features. Predict conservative dual gain increments, two positive
harm excesses, signed easy change, and conservative pair-distance
gain. Both use the same targets and loss.

Compare the geometry scorer with the independent choice at its
realized counts; with the same geometry scorer at the frozen
portfolio counts; and with the scorer without geometry at the same
counts. Also report independent at a fixed 25%, both learned
scorers with free counts, CV, OLS4 and the analytic frozen
portfolio. Calibration chooses risk price, pair weight and abstention
margin only on its own source role. It requires all recording
checks and positive lower gain against both comparators.

Report exact query counts and pair-distance contrasts, signed easy
degradation, realized rates and source-group bootstrap with 3,000
draws. The candidate search is finite and not an oracle. Diagnostic
outcome-aware menu maxima ignore risk and do not establish joint
feasibility or a deployable policy.
