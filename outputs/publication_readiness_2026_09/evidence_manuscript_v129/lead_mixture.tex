\section{Lead-dependent proposals and required coverage}
We replaced the path shape at the same \LeadMixEdits{} recipients with
a convex mixture of six analytic experts. The network produces three
simplex vectors. The control averages them; the treatment interpolates
them across the forecast using quadratic Bernstein weights. Both use
identical initial parameters, draws and objectives
(\LeadMixUpdates{} total updates). The objective penalizes the
conservative error difference versus OLS4 and CV-relative
all and easy positive-harm excesses. It does not certify risk.

The frozen, shared-weight and lead-dependent paths pass
\LeadMixFrozen{}, \LeadMixShared{}, and \LeadMixLead{}/\LeadMixViews{}
recording views. Lead-dependent minus shared conservative gain is
\LeadMixLeadContrast{} points, and the neighbor lower contrast is
\LeadMixLeadPair{} pixels. After matching each path's peak displacement
from CV, the conservative gain is \LeadMixShape{} points. The intervals
are 95\% source-group bootstrap intervals after seed averaging.
Calibration qualifies \LeadMixSharedQual{}/9 and \LeadMixLeadQual{}/9.
Adding both new paths to the previous screened menu preserves
\LeadMixCapacityBefore{} of \LeadMixViews{} dual-reference hindsight
witnesses and gives \LeadMixCapacityAfter{} total.

To test whether new paths alone could solve the deficit, we remove
the risk and candidate restrictions. For each comparator separately,
we grant zero error on known steps and zero disagreement from that
comparator on missing steps for every edited row. The reference
benefits of all untouched rows are unchanged. The sum of the best
such row gains under the query cap and recording budget is an upper
bound, even if no single trajectory attains both reference bounds.
At the original total counts, this relaxation excludes
\LeadMixExcluded{}/\LeadMixViews{} views. \LeadMixNeedMore{} need more
edits even in this relaxation, and \LeadMixUnreachable{} cannot meet
the positive utility threshold at any count under the cap of eight.
Among those reachable with that cap, the median required increase is
\LeadMixExtra{} edits. This is a necessary coverage calculation, not a
prescription for changing the risk threshold or a learned policy.
