# Stop Calibration-Only Iteration

The current three controls are now closed negative results: one-pass component
calibration, repeated actual-selected-set calibration, and recording-deletion
consensus. Do not repeat them with new thresholds, more iterations, extra seeds,
or a renamed support guard. The independent data roles stay closed, and the
deployment floor is unchanged.

The next work should return to the information learned by the conditional-cost
head, not try another globally conservative multiplier. Before another fit,
reconcile the existing feature/loss/conditional-support results with the current
estimator's source-held residuals. The discriminating question is whether
benefit and harm events with materially different outcomes remain mixed inside
the same learned source representation. A new experiment must identify a
specific missing causal signal or supervision mismatch; simply making the same
head larger is not justified by these controls.

This cannot be framed as a new finding that labels are incomplete. The earlier
fixed-floor slice study already found substantial observed harm among complete
labels and safe-oracle opportunity inside the causal pool. Likewise, earlier
nested/bridge calibration studies already showed pooled source protection need
not transfer. Reuse those results rather than repeating their audits.

Priorities for the next registered comparison are:

1. Trace current source-held false-safe predictions back to the fitted cost
   representation and training support, keeping whole recordings excluded.
   Compare existing neural and tree error patterns before choosing a new target
   or feature. Posthoc case IDs cannot become input features or selection rules.
2. Only if that trace supports a materially different intervention, train a
   matched estimator with unchanged producers, labels, role boundaries and risk
   budget. Evaluate retained utility and equal-coverage ordering together.
3. Keep unknown-completion support separate from observed harm. Additional
   annotations/data may be necessary, but do not silently change masks, call
   silver human gold or use future availability at inference.

No next model is claimed trained here. Method contribution and independent
confirmation remain unresolved; this experiment alone does not make M3W a CVPR
submission candidate. The original full research objective remains active.
