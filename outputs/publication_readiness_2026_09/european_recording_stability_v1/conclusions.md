The primary raw-pool joint calibration arm does not support stability filtering
as a repair. The unchanged2%positive-harm/reference criterion is retained.

All10,692 removed occurrences are from two-recording source groups. After
holding out one recording, deleting the remaining one leaves no calibrator.
No originally selected occurrence was removed when all nested deletions were
estimable. All original risk-violating views remain. This is loss of support,
not learned discrimination of harmful interventions.

Conservative utility change is **-0.05140487% of full known reference mass**,
nominal95% locality interval **[-0.11461002%,-0.00736735%]**. This is not an ADE
or FDE improvement percentage. Against exact uniform count-matched thinning
within each held recording, the contrast is zero to numerical precision
(-1.53e-18%). Because every recording is wholly retained or rejected in this
primary result, the count-matched comparator is effectively degenerate. It
does not establish ranking ability or a useful confidence score.

## Secondary Mechanism Control

Applying the same rule to the previously rejected selected-set calibrator also
fails. Complete support falls23->5; defined views27->8; occurrences67,160->56,929.
Of10,231 removals,10,227 arise from unestimable deletion fits, and only4 occur
despite all deletion fits being supported. Upper-risk failures fall4->3 only
on a substantially reduced population, and the worst completion upper rises
28.0208%->44.2450%.

Utility change is-0.04904674%, CI[-0.11261770%,-0.00361132%]. The count-matched
expectation contrast is-0.0000020044%, CI[-0.0000060132%,0%]; no positive
allocation advantage is supported. These secondary results do not replace the
registered primary. No alternative quantile, deletion count, seed or threshold
was chosen after examining outcomes.
