# Stress-test all-edited risk on the complete twelve-step grid

Use only the 160 previously scored Songdo confirmation flights and their
unchanged matched proposals. Open no calibration or new external labels.
Verify the original all-edited risk ratio on each original arm and seed.
The original easy/hard cutoff, signed-easy check and release decision are
not refitted, replaced or recertified.

The original row-based ratio averages errors over available steps on each
partially observed track and charges max disagreement for entirely unknown
tracks. Separately define a twelve-step diagnostic using the sum across
edited actors of positive per-track full-grid ADE deterioration divided by
the sum of their full-grid CV ADE. Hold the same two edited actors fixed.
The denominator's minimum is the observed sum of CV errors divided by 12;
an undefined minimum is not certifiable. On every missing step put the
unknown target at CV. This attains both the denominator minimum and the
largest possible per-step deterioration (the CV-to-proposal distance).
Therefore it attains the worst-case ratio whenever the minimum denominator
is positive. This argument permits free missing positions per actor-step,
and applies to the all-edited ratio only, not to an outcome-defined easy
subset or any physical safety notion.

Report original all-edited pass, original full three-check pass, whether
the latter retains the diagnostic, and partial/unknown edited support.
Report both matched proposals and actual releases, with abstentions
separate. A diagnostic failure is an adverse completion witness, not a
claim that the real missing targets violated the threshold.
