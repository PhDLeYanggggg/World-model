# Frozen Feature-Head Selected-Cost Diagnosis

## Material Passport

Run/diagnosis, not a new training or policy experiment. The completed matched
feature study is the parent. Its primary is inconclusive and complete risk
fails. SOURCE is exposed development; no reserved role is opened here.

## Question And Fixed Scope

Does the new interaction feature head underpredict harm already on its fitting
rows, or only on excluded localities? On identical selected sets, distinguish
H/EH numerator error from R/ER reference error. Better whole-bank fitting alone
does not answer this question. The older head's diagnosis cannot stand in for
fresh measurement of these new frozen heads.

Use all 18 parent jobs, three actual seeds and both matched arms. For each,
run frozen inference on critic G and double-excluded H. All 36 models remain
unchanged. No optimizer, preprocessing fit, threshold search, favorable seed
selection, new trajectory or new intervention rule. Fitting-role data travel
through the inference-only packet path; future labels are read only after
prediction and used for diagnostic targets.

For each role, retain every 444 repeated recording/pipeline views (74 distinct
recordings, not independent samples). Describe four fixed sets: all legal
neural actions, the selected full proposal, the amplitude-scaled proposal and
the actual interpolated prediction. References remain unscaled. Report all
five predicted/target masses, defined/undefined ratio support, falsely-safe
all/easy counts and numerator/denominator error directions. Compare selected
full proposal with full legal proposal before amplitude to isolate the set
change. Also describe nonempty selected views with no wholly-unknown rows;
this is posthoc descriptive, not a replacement cohort or primary endpoint.

Every H cost/amplitude array must reproduce the frozen parent exactly, and
actual H completion-risk ratios must match the parent. Separate full re-run
must reproduce all 36 role groups. No bootstrap claim or independent-scene
inference is attached to these descriptive repeated-view medians.

transfer or duplicate scheduling. Keep heartbeat, immutable completed groups,
same-definition resume, the existing disk reserve and 12-hour phase boundary.
Private output contains only logs/locks; public outputs are aggregate scalars.

No risk threshold, benchmark reference, data role or paper primary is changed.
