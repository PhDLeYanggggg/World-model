# Selected Easy-Harm Underfitting Precedes Domain Transfer

The fresh complete diagnosis and its independent execution replay cover both
roles, all 18 jobs, both feature arms and three actual seeds. All 36 role
groups reproduce exactly. H-role predictions and completion risks match the
completed parent, with no new optimizer updates or changed decisions.

The interaction feature head's median predicted/actual selected EH mass ratio
is 0.003209 on fitting G and 0.025886 on excluded H. Every one of its 324
fitting and 330 held falsely-safe easy views underpredicts EH. Only 31 and 92,
respectively, overpredict the easy-reference denominator. Thus denominator
inflation and new-locality transfer are not necessary for most failures.

Before amplitude, median full-legal EH ratio is 0.157515 on fitting and
0.222641 on held rows. On selected full proposals it falls to 0.027610 and
0.051748; this direction holds in 416/444 and 419/444 repeated views. Selection
concentrates cost optimism. These are descriptive same-target comparisons,
not independent population estimates or a uniquely identified causal mechanism.

Unknown-label bounds are not the sole explanation: among 19 nonempty fitting
views with no wholly-unknown selected rows, 15 have defined EH ratios with
median 0.021595 and seven violate easy risk. Partial missingness still exists
and these small posthoc subsets do not replace the full original cohort.

Better total fitting loss therefore has not solved selected-tail fitting.
The result does not justify declaring the latent useless, an irreducible
feature ceiling, or a pure domain-shift failure. Earlier generic loss weights,
selected-region emphasis, hurdle/deviance and width controls remain negative.

The next single-factor optimization-adequacy check will keep the current
features, architecture, loss, learning rate, sampler and seeds fixed, extend
all 18 interaction heads to four times their original budget, and require
exact old-budget prefix reproduction. It will compare old and new predictions
on identical original selected sets, not claim success by abstaining more.
Only G fitting labels are needed; no new H scoring or threshold selection is
part of that check. Fitting success, if any, will still need a separate frozen
held evaluation before a policy claim. Original failures and deployment stand.
