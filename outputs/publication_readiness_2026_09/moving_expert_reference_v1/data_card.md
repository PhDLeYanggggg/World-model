## Units And Limits

Coordinates are image pixels. Past-derived normalization does not establish
meters,homography,FPS or effective seconds. Native steps are not seconds-level
or the historical SDD t50 benchmark. No true3D or foundation claim follows.
Upstream annotation-generation causality is unverified; silver labels are not
human gold. Eligible queries need not contain every visible scene agent, and
the allocator does not optimize physical collisions.
