## Controlled intervention

For predicted moments(B,H,R,ER,EH),keep the existing causal allowed mask and
strictly positive R/ER requirement. The old legal set additionally requires
B>H per action. The signed set removes only that per-action inequality. At most
one action per row,positive total utility,no future fields,and BOTH original
sum(H-.02R)<=0 and sum(EH-.02ER)<=0 remain. Benefit never cancels positive
harm in the risk numerator. This differs from older net/clipped-risk subsidy
controls,which changed risk coefficients on positive-utility candidates.

Four policies on all72 context/source/head-seed groups:

1. positive_matched:immutable corrected-unit set-joint control.
2. signed_matched:joint optimization in the enlarged set at exactly that
   control's intervention count in EACH current query.
3. positive_free:original positive-action joint solver without a count constraint.
4. signed_free:enlarged-set joint solver without a count constraint.

Each new solve retains a genuinely feasible incumbent on incomplete status or
strict original-inequality postcheck failure. No rounding tolerance relaxes a
risk inequality. For signed_free the signed_matched solution is an incumbent.
Predicted utility nesting is checked;it is not a guarantee of observed safety.
No solver objective or result is called globally optimal after a fallback.

Primary:signed_matched minus positive_matched full wholly-unknown-completion-
lower utility,with original known floor-reference mass. Secondary:free versus
free and matched known utility. Common counts assess utility only,not safety.
Final-policy risk retains every original recording and the existing2% all/easy
and easy-preservation requirements. Empty comparison risk is not_estimable.
