\section{Independent Gain and Support Slots}

The gain slot retains the one-edit policy. A distinct
support slot at amplitude 0.10 uses the first query with
an available recipient above the frozen easy threshold.
The gain edit takes precedence on a shared query. The
matched control uses the same support queries and amplitude,
but ranks available recipients by gain. These are distinct
development policies, not a change to the main risk rule.

At the same query, selecting for easy support rather than
gain yields \TwoSupportPrimary{} in lower utility. The
gain-ranked policy yields \TwoSupportMatch{} versus CV;
the two-role policy yields \TwoSupportRoles{}. Increasing
only support amplitude yields \TwoSupportBigger{} versus
the original two-role arm. Ranking gain within predicted
easy gives \TwoSupportRerank{} versus easy rank.
Neither causal strategy meets the recording risk roster.
Moving only the second edit to any query in the same
recording raises the outcome-aware count from
\TwoSupportOraclePass{} to \TwoSupportAnyTime{}, an additional
\TwoSupportExtraTime{} views with no budget increase.
