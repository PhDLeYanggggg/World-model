# Why The Matched Forest Control Does Not Repair The Policy

## Verified Outcome

All 54 registered ExtraTrees models complete on full fitting packets. All 144
inference groups and 1,332 paired fitting-cost views replay exactly in separate
processes. The pipeline is operational; its primary utility and full-record
risk requirements fail. No deployment changes.

The mixed forest's known ADE gain is -0.00007346%, compared with 0.982769% for
the static-scene neural cost head. The paired change is -0.982843 percentage
points, conditional 95% locality interval [-2.301874,-0.242325]. This is not
successful positive transfer or an improvement rounded to zero. Count matching
does not rescue the comparison: -0.009443pp, CI [-0.023258,+0.000643].

## A Different Failure From The Neural Head

The forest greatly reduces intervention: 3,457 exposed held row views instead
of 32,219. Known all-risk violations fall from 34 to zero and known easy-risk
violations from 96 to two. Required recording-risk support increases from
23/186 to 118/186. Nevertheless 66 easy-risk record views remain undefined,
and 43 selected outcomes remain unknown. Partial support is not a full pass;
empty risk is not a safe zero. Fitting support is also incomplete at 330/444.

Thus the forest is not simply another high-gain unsafe policy. It largely
rejects useful substitution while still failing complete support. It selects
only 182 neural-action held row views in the mixed bank, versus 31,959 when a
separately trained neural-only forest is used. This is evidence of a bank-
dependent operating point, not proof that bank dilution is the unique cause.
No bank is selected after readout for deployment.

## Fixed-Region Cost Evidence

The all-legal mixed-bank fitting median predicted/observed easy-harm ratio is
1.016480 for forests and 1.026498 for neural heads. These aggregate medians
alone look close to one. They do not establish conditional calibration.

Inside the identical frozen neural choice regions, the forest ratio is
4.576410, whereas the neural head ratio is 0.021367. Both use exactly the same
observed cost mass: 341 positive-denominator views and 103 undefined views
out of 444. On frozen forest regions the corresponding medians are 4.456405
and 0.143958, with 323 finite and 121 undefined views.

This distinguishes changed cost estimates from changed selection composition.
The ordinary forest overestimates EH in these fixed regions while the neural
head underestimates it. It does not justify a generic global multiplier: the
all-legal aggregate EH median is already close to one for both. Ratios can
also be sensitive to small observed denominators; the archive preserves the
cost masses, counts and normalized errors, not just their medians.

## What Is And Is Not Located

- Runtime/SHM failure: not observed. Native arm64 CPU4, single-process recovery
  and complete fitting work; no scientific failure is attributed to a hang.
- Reusing an incompatible baseline: repaired by fresh seven-action fitting,
  rather than relabelling the older binary-forest weights.
- Selection-region cost error: directly measured on matched frozen regions.
  Its magnitude and direction differ across estimator families.
- Insufficient neural optimizer updates as the sole explanation: unsupported;
  a nongradient conventional family also fails this registered comparison.
- Architecture as a unique cause: not identified. Symmetric forest regression,
  asymmetric neural loss, decoder/projection and compute differ.
- Feature insufficiency or irreducible future ambiguity: not proven. This
  experiment neither estimates a causal-policy upper bound nor tests every
  possible estimator. Future-informed oracle opportunity is not learned success.
- Independent generalization and full world dynamics: not evaluated by this
  source-development estimator control.

## Next Decisive Work

Do not run another held-result threshold sweep. First reuse the frozen fitting
predictions to quantify how mixed-bank fitting changes action-6 gate rejection,
separating predicted gain, all-harm and easy-harm rejection. Compare a fixed
two-expert composition only within original fitting roles before deciding
whether a bank-factorized risk estimator merits a registered training trial.
Reuse the earlier recalibration failures; do not present interpolation between
an overestimate and underestimate as a certified risk interval.

Any later repair retains the 2% recording contract, all unknown/empty cases,
and fitting-only normalization. It needs a predefined source-internal selection
rule before another exposed readout, then genuine independent evidence if it
earns admission. Independent selection/calibration/confirmation and DroneCrowd
stay closed here. Stage5C execution and SMC remain prohibited.

## Scope

These are oblique webcam detector-track pixels, obs8/pred12/stride12 raw frames.
They are not human-gold SDD labels, metric/seconds-level prediction, true 3D,
foundation-model evidence, or a CCF-A submission-ready result. Three controller
seeds and 3,000 locality resamples are conditional development evidence, not
three independently trained forecasting pipelines or new test recordings.
