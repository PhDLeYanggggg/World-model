# Method Position

Reviewed 26 September 2026, after registration and before any new readout.
No registered training setting changed following this reading.

Wolpert's original stacked-generalization paper explicitly studies learning
from predictions on held-out portions of a training set, including correcting
a single predictor's errors. It also discusses the lack of universal improvement.
Consequently, out-of-fold residual correction is established methodology, not
an M3W novelty claim. [Wolpert, Stacked Generalization, Neural Networks 5,
241-259 (1992), original paper](https://cafri-labs.github.io/lab-manual/papers/wolpert1992.pdf).

The present experiment tests that construction under complete locality
exclusion for a constrained easy-harm cost, using unchanged trajectory
producers and matched cyclic controls. Inner-fitting label cuts are rebuilt
with the head, so target-cut transport remains a measured limitation.

This is not proof of statistical independence, causal identification,
conformal coverage or physical safety. A useful error correction would still
need an improvement over the original estimator, downstream policy evidence,
and the untouched role separation before a stronger method claim.
