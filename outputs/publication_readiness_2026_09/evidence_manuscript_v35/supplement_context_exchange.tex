\section{Time-Aligned Context and Negative Control}
We reuse only the past eight position timestamps in the verified
OpenDD context cache. Each recipient gets relative distance, bearing,
availability, observed closing motion and a constant-relative-motion
approach descriptor, from up to two pedestrians and two motor actors.
There is no future availability indicator, released velocity, or
test-site fitting. Motor context is present at the current step for
\AuditMotor{} of 10,475 target windows.
