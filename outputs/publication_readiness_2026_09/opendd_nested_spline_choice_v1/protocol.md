# Nested causal choice at fixed OpenDD intervention slots

The preceding OpenDD spline adds hindsight capacity but loses
dual-reference passes when forced into all fixed slots. I keep the
same 1,457 recipients, query times and peak displacements and learn
whether to use the original analytic direction or the frozen
comoving spline direction. The published primary contrast is the
calibrated query-joint rule against the existing past-prefix rule.

Each outer held site has six donor sites. For each donor used for
calibration, both the selector and its forecast generator exclude
the outer and calibration sites. Candidate forecasts for each
selector fitting source also exclude that source. On the outer site
I use the unchanged six-donor forecast from the preceding study and
a selector trained on out-of-source donor predictions. The underlying
original paths are analytic past-prefix choices, not neural predictions
trained on the scored site. The existing donor-prefix comparator
excludes the outer site. No new independent labels are opened.

Each shared regression head predicts a direct missing-step conservative
benefit and changes in original all, easy, and signed easy risk masses.
The independent rule prices positive risk per slot. The joint rule
enumerates the up-to-two slot substitutions at one query and prices
positive total risk. A joint arm with the independent rule's parameters
isolates the decision grouping. The gain-only rule, original, frozen
past-prefix rule, forced spline and outcome-aware capacity are controls.

Thresholds are selected only on nested donor predictions at a
deterministic calibration proxy: up to two legal recipients per query,
ranked by past step variability, using the legal amplitude closest to
0.5. The outer nominations come from the unchanged frozen streaming
controller, not the proxy. This transport difference is reported.
Calibrate guarded arms to maximize mean conservative gain with no
increase in the three aggregate risk masses; include the original-only
fallback. Relative donor constraints are not certification under
the original 2% recording rule, which is applied unchanged to every
held forecast. Empty support remains undefined, and no deployment
or independent-confirmation claim is made.
