# Contribution and Evidence Gap

This experiment is a controlled negative ablation, not a new successful method.
It tests whether query-level supervised aggregation repairs the gap between
predicted joint risk feasibility and observed intervention harm. The new heads
are real Torch models with matched initialization, draws and budgets; the
forecasters and utility allocation mechanism are unchanged.

## What Can Be Reported

- Exact design and its pre-readout primary-feasibility disclosure.
- Genuine matched training, fitting curves, fixed-count and fixed-budget
  comparisons, all dependent locality views and three forecasting seeds.
- No supported same-count accuracy advantage from pure query supervision.
- Worse joint-policy ADE and worse held-risk MSE point estimates.
- Mean accuracy gain and net easy preservation do not imply positive-harm
  control, calibration or safe intervention.

## What Cannot Be Claimed

- A successful registered primary, calibrated or conformal risk guarantee.
- A new dynamics predictor, learned physical interaction model, or pairwise
  collision improvement: none was trained here.
- Independent external confirmation or generalization to unseen research
  domains: all twelve localities are already-opened development sources.
- Novelty from summing errors, a conference-ready main contribution, or a
  deployment upgrade from selecting the best mean after reading all outcomes.

The next evidentiary requirements are an identifiable risk/coverage protocol,
causally generated selected-subset supervision, a real predictive benefit over
matched simpler controls, and later properly separated selection/calibration/
confirmation. Existing reserved data stay closed until their protocol is fixed.
Reproducibility checks verify computation; they cannot supply missing science.

The data remain image-local detector silver, obs8/pred12 raw-frame stride12.
No metric, seconds, human-gold, physical-safety, true3D or foundation claim.
Stage5C/SMC remain disabled, and actual submission requires author confirmation.
