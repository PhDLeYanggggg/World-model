# Risk-Control Boundary

Anastasios N. Angelopoulos, *Conformal Risk Control for Non-Monotonic Losses*,
arXiv:2602.20151. Sections1,2.1 and the selective-classification formulation
were inspected at the [primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.20151v1).
This is a scoped reading, not a reproduced method or author read attestation.

The manuscript treats risk guarantees through exchangeability and algorithmic
stability; selective risk can be expressed using signed excess and need not be
monotonic in an abstention parameter. These assumptions do not follow from a
small empirical selected-harm ratio. M3W's overlapping queries, source shift,
undefined denominators and reused development sites do not supply them.

The present experiment changes a supervised aggregation loss only. It neither
implements that paper's guarantee nor establishes independent calibration.
Formal risk control, prediction of costs and observed development constraints
must remain separate claims. The original CRC OpenReview PDF encountered a
browser challenge during this lookup; its full text was not newly inspected.
