# Pre-Readout Primary Feasibility Limitation

Recorded during fitting, before this experiment's decisions or held-out
outcome readout. This is an additive disclosure, not a protocol amendment.
The registered objective, estimators, count anchor, risk threshold, roster,
training budget and gates are unchanged.

The cached_verified parent independent policy has zero interventions in ten
dependent locality views. Each new ranking arm must retain that parent's
intervention count within every current query. Therefore both arms necessarily
have zero interventions in these views, and their selected positive-harm ratios
are undefined. The registered primary requires defined ratios on the full
roster. It cannot be satisfied by this design, irrespective of model learning.
This could have been caught before registration and is a design error, not
evidence that either fitted objective has or has not learned useful risk.
