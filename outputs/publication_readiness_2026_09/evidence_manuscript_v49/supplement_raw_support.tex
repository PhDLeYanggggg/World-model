\section{Released-Label Support and Held-Location Control}
An independent CSV parser reconstructs the original MovingDroneCrowd++
fitting roster by recording, track and step. All \RawSupportSteps{}
requested validity flags and all available centers match the frozen
targets. The \RawSupportAbsent{} missing step occurrences represent
\RawSupportKeys{} distinct track-frame keys, with no exact same-key
observation to recover from this release. Of \RawSupportWhole{}
wholly unlabeled windows, \RawSupportNoLater{} have no later
observation for that ID and account for \RawSupportTerminalShare\%
of the reference disagreement floor. This is an annotation-tail
diagnostic, not proof of physical exit.

On the exposed OpenDD roster (\RawSupportOpenRows{} windows from
seven held locations), the same model is refit locally, not
transferred zero-shot. Its native sampling differs from the moving
drone source. The disagreement floor is \RawSupportOpenFloor{} pp.
The dual head beats OLS4 by \RawSupportOpenOLS{}, but loses to CV by
the contrast \RawSupportOpenCV{} and passes \RawSupportOpenRisk/
\RawSupportOpenViews{} original risk views. Better label support
does not by itself establish useful intervention against the
stronger reference.
