# What The Fixed-Priority Experiment Actually Shows

## The Mechanism Worked On Its Chosen Errors

The fixed warm-error component of fitting MSE falls by 91.26%, 70.61% and
62.78% relative to the warm model. Against ordinary continued training, it
is 89.21%, 60.22% and 57.96% lower. Thus a missing gradient or inactive
priority mask is not a supported explanation for this experiment's failure.
The complete anchor MSE is worse than the matched control in all three
contexts, so the objective trades accuracy elsewhere for its selected errors.

Across the three repeated fitting contexts, the warm easy output has 1,002
observed false-safe signs. Priority training corrects 650, retains 352, and
creates 333 new ones: 685 remain in total. Ordinary continuation corrects
290 and creates 389, leaving 1,101. These are output-sign diagnostics on
training proposals, not independent observations, full-policy admission
counts, uncertainty bounds or certified risk reductions.

## Policy Improvement Does Not Follow

Fitting source-macro known utility is 7.7923%, below the control's 8.2916%.
Complete fitting risk/support improves from 2/24 to 4/24 repeated recording
views, but undefined selected-easy views also rise from five to six. This
does not meet the complete contract. These 24 fitting views are not the
unavailable original 24-group primary evaluation.

Held SOURCE known utility is 3.0323%, below continued training's 3.6006%
and the warm model's 3.9418%. The priority-minus-control contrast is
-0.5683 percentage points with missing-outcome completion bounds
[-0.8147, -0.3218]. These are not confidence intervals. Hard utility also
falls, from 4.6257% to 3.8505%, and intervention falls from 15.0144% to
13.5856%. Both arms retain 14 known all-risk violations, 13 easy violations
and two undefined selected-easy views. Complete risk/support stays 0/17.
Worst defined signed recording easy-net degradation is 22.6698%, not a
2% pass. One favorable context cannot replace the other two or the aggregate.

Pooled known-label admitted easy budget excess drops substantially on fitting
data, from 1,543.25 to 320.58 native-coordinate units, but barely on held
data, from 9,777.87 to 9,508.29. Both held values remain positive while the
corresponding predicted sums are negative. These correlated sums are
diagnostic, not the official source-macro risk or a formal calibration test.

## What Is Identified And What Remains A Hypothesis

The intervention operates, corrects many fixed training errors and reduces
intervention. It fails to improve held utility or complete risk/support.
This rejects the tested fixed weight-10 replay repair; it does not prove
all weighting objectives or all causal risk models impossible.

The fixed error list misses errors created by training. Its labels were
discovered by warm-model fitting-data resubstitution, not by source-held-out
error discovery. The three fitting contexts contain only two, three and
three independent source groups. Many overlapping windows do not compensate
for that limited source diversity. This supports investigating error transport
and representation generalization, but does not uniquely identify data volume,
label noise or architecture as the causal root of failure.

The fixed update budget may not optimize the new objective fully: in the
second context its combined weight-10 fitting objective is worse than the
continued-MSE control under that same weighted measurement. That optimization
gap is retained, not hidden behind a lower selected-error component. More
identical updates or selecting an intermediate checkpoint would be a separate
experiment, not a retrospective repair of this result.

## Next Decisive Question, Not Run

Do training-source-held-out errors define a more transferable teaching signal
than resubstitution errors? First audit whether each existing fitting partition
can support an inner leave-one-source-out risk teacher. Use only B fitting
sources; keep the foreign proposer A, held development V, all original
evaluation rules and reserved data unchanged. If feasible, compare an
out-of-source error mask against the fixed resubstitution mask with matched
final architecture, budget and loss. No held-V multiplier or threshold search.

This is ordinary cross-fitting, not a new method claim or a guaranteed fix.
The current weight-10 variant is not expanded or deployed. Earlier online
admission and gradient-parity failures remain failures. If source support is
insufficient, record that limit instead of treating correlated windows as new
independent data. Model work cannot create the original missing easy-reference
mass; original primary NA remains binding.
