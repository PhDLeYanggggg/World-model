\paragraph{Hindsight feasibility at the fixed budget.}
For a target with $n>0$ observed future steps, let $d_t$ be its true
distance from CV and $r$ the fixed maximum prediction departure.
Allow arbitrary, independently chosen offsets at each step.
Projection onto the radius-$r$ disk gives the optimal observed error
except when all steps are known and no projected offset reaches $r$.
Then imposing equality on the step with largest $d_t$ costs
$(r-\max_t d_t)_+$. With a missing step, equality can be imposed there
at no observed cost. The resulting minimal observed ADE is
\[
\frac{\sum_{t\ {\rm obs}}(d_t-r)_+
+\mathbf{1}\{n=12\}(r-\max_{t\ {\rm obs}} d_t)_+}{n}.
\]
The bound preserves all denominators and the wholly unknown debit.
With no smoothness constraint, all three recording ratios are
nondecreasing in per-target ADE. Therefore a failed bound excludes
every path at those exact radii and recipients. Out of 606 views,
\FlightUndefined{} are undefined and \FlightDebit{} fail from the
unknown debit alone; \FlightFeasible{} remain hindsight-feasible.
This bound uses outcomes and cannot select edits or certify deployment.
