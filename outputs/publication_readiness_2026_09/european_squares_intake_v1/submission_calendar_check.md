## Internal Working Targets, Not Conference Rules

1. By 4 October: finish admissible-source, physical-site grouping and role
   assignment work. If the intake cannot support independent calibration, keep
   that blocker explicit and constrain the claims before committing more compute.
2. By 18 October: complete source-only method tests and matched controls on the
   unchanged prediction protocol. Archive rejected hypotheses and all seeds.
3. By 25 October: freeze method, risk rules and proposed independent readout.
   Do not open reserved confirmation to choose a winner.
4. By 2 November: complete allowed independent evidence, uncertainty analysis
   and claim review. A failed hypothesis stays a failed hypothesis.
5. By 8 November: prepare the complete draft and reproduction package, leaving
   buffer before registration and submission. Formal submission remains the
   user's final decision; no automatic submission is authorized by this file.

These targets can move if evidence or access fails; deadlines never justify
relabelling exposed data, hiding failures or presenting unrun results as complete.
