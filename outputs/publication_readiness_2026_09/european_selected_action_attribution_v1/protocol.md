# Selected-action arithmetic attribution

I replay the already frozen 54 new-bank cost heads from the three budget-score
objectives, three controller seeds and six SOURCE fitting contexts. All 216
context/source/seed/objective groups must reproduce their saved prediction,
action and full-policy metric hashes exactly. This is development diagnosis,
not a new independent test or a training experiment.

For the fixed selected known rows, separately for all and easy channels,
`q_true - q_pred = (H - H_hat) + .02 * (R_hat - R)`.
Four mutually exclusive witnesses partition individual false admissions:
numerator-only, denominator-only, either single substitution failing, or only
the joint substitution failing. These use evaluation labels and are not
alternative deployment policies or causal identification. No future quantity
enters the predictor, selected set, thresholds or frozen metric calculation.

Easy membership uses the original frozen CV-error cut, not positive easy
reference mass; zero-error easy rows and noneasy predicted easy support remain
separate. Unknown outcomes stay wholly unknown, with separate selected counts
and causal envelope mass. Per-action and per-recording attribution is retained.
Pooled counts across overlapping contexts, objectives and seeds are repeated
views, not independent observations. The underlying support is the same
149510 queries, 74 recordings and 12 localities. The selected-reference
denominator, original 2% risk limits and original failure conclusions do not
change. No held, calibration or independent-confirmation role is opened.

This diagnosis will determine a separately registered repair; it does not
itself improve, calibrate or certify a policy. No deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
