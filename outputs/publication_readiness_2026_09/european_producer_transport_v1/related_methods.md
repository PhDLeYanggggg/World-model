# Related-Method Boundary

[Ovadia et al., NeurIPS2019](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/8558cb408c1d76621371888657d2eb1d-Paper.pdf)
evaluate classification uncertainty under dataset shift and find limitations of
post-hoc calibration. Their abstract and Sections1--3 motivate checking
calibration beyond the distribution on which it was fitted.

Our diagnostic concerns continuous trajectory gain/harm and a changed predictor
inside a frozen intervention system. It is neither a replication of their
classification benchmark nor proof of a new calibration method. Their results
do not establish M3W's failure cause, safety or benefit. Those require the
paired, source-excluded experiment reported here.

Read scope: abstract, problem framing and methods/metrics in the official paper;
not a new exhaustive literature review. Accessed2026-09-25. No citation confers
metric/seconds, physical-safety or independent-confirmation status on our data.
