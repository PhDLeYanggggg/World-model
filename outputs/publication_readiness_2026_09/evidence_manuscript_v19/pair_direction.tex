\paragraph{Query-local headroom does not establish recording feasibility.}
With the same recipients and frozen paths, a convex
directional test asks if any affine pair correction can
improve the relative-path lower score. For a feasible
direction $u$ the normalized loss increase is convex along
$u$ and starts at zero. A strict improvement exists exactly
when some one-sided derivative is negative. At most two
recipients switch, reducing the directional problem to one
dimension. We verify concrete gains in \DirectionShared{}
of \DirectionQueries{} query-seed views with one shared
weight, and in \DirectionSeparate{} with separate weights.
There are \DirectionOnly{} gains that shared weights cannot
achieve to numerical tolerance. These are hindsight
opportunities, not causal decisions. After assembling the
query-local witnesses, the original recording checks pass
\DirectionSharedPass{} and \DirectionSeparatePass{} of 102 views,
respectively.
