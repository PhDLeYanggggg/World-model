# Remaining Research Gap

The research goal is still active. This frozen readout is a completed experiment,
not completed world-model or CVPR evidence.

1. **Stronger accuracy with easy protection.** The all-risk branch gives larger
   mean gains but harms easy localities. The easy-risk branch protects the observed
   easy means but sometimes loses to the training-selected classical baseline.
   Improve candidate/floor quality and conditional gain/harm supervision before
   spending the independent calibration sample. Keep all three seeds and fixed
   model-selection rules; do not deploy a favorable readout seed.
2. **Independent calibration and confirmation.** Twelve calibration localities and
   six confirmation localities remain closed. Freeze one bounded family before
   calibration; explicitly state assumptions and whether12 locality groups can
   support the intended2% tolerance. A predicted budget or a bootstrap on opened
   selection data is not such a certificate. Confirmation must remain a separate
   final use. DroneCrowd is not silently opened by this experiment.
3. **Method attribution and public controls.** Joint geometry has not beaten its
   same-count unary/independent controls. Strong task-matched public predictors,
   ablations, source-limited data efficiency and a method that adds more than
   architecture combination are still required for a compelling paper.

The shortest justified next step is a preregistered conditional-risk/floor repair
using only source training plus the declared selection role. Preserve negative
results and make no formal deployment claim until independent gates pass.
