# Frozen Policy Readout on Six Model-Selection Localities

## Question

I will test whether the incumbent-relative additions that helped on opened source
localities transfer to the six preassigned model-selection localities. The recent
matched joint-control experiment failed its easy guard and did not establish a
pairwise contribution. I will not tune that experiment on another slice of its
opened outcomes. This readout asks about generalization of the complete frozen
policy family, not a new neural architecture or deployment guarantee.

## Role and Cohort

The unchanged European role manifest assigned localities 087, 092, 093, 103, 104
and 125 to model selection before predictive outcomes were read. All 28 recordings
in those groups enter this readout. Per recording I retain up to 256 uniformly
spaced, past-supported query frames, as in the source adapter. Every indexed target
has eight observed positions at raw stride12; twelve future positions are requested.
Partial and absent future labels do not filter inference eligibility.

This is the complete registered engineering cohort, not the complete dataset or an
official benchmark reproduction. These localities become development-exposed after
this experiment and can never be relabeled final confirmation. The twelve risk
calibration localities, six confirmation localities and DroneCrowd confirmation
remain closed. The original role manifest is historical and will not be overwritten;
a separate, purpose-limited access receipt records this opening.

## Frozen Family

Use all 36 producer/controller/event/seed groups from incumbent_relative_v1, with
seeds17/29/43 and all/easy risk events. Each has a frozen four-source forecaster,
a frozen CV/damping motion floor, and frozen old/new neural or ridge decision heads.
Reuse exact training-only normalization, easy/hard cutoffs and 0.02 predicted-risk
threshold. Verify all forecasting, floor, features and policy choices on 4,096
previously opened source rows per group before any new predictive readout.

Compare floor_reference, incumbent_reference, add_only, remove_only,
ridge_incumbent, old_stop, previous_matched and raw_neural. Report the protected
motion floor and all six existing classical baselines. The strongest *selected*
baseline is the one already chosen on each producer's training sources, not the
best baseline observed on these six localities. Reported best-on-readout classical
performance, if computed, is diagnostic, never an inference input.

No new training, preprocessing fit, threshold search, curriculum, calibration or
deployment occurs. The joint-control family remains a documented negative study;
it is not silently included or re-tuned. No candidate or best seed is selected in
this first readout. Every group and seed is reported, including failures.

## Accounting and Sequence

1. Source-only exact replay and scoped synthetic tests.
2. Freeze config, implementation, artifact manifest and registration; commit/push.
3. Verify the raw archive and every admitted member hash. Build inputs and labels
   separately; replay inputs with all future rows removed at three queries/recording.
4. Infer using input-only arrays, verify producer exclusion, freeze every action
   and prediction hash, then commit the aggregate freeze before outcome evaluation.
5. Evaluate once, with hash-verified resume. Report source-equal ADE/FDE, all/easy/
   hard/complete support, partial labels, exact-zero CV harm, intervention, p95/p99,
   worst locality and 3,000 paired locality bootstrap draws. Never bootstrap rows
   as independent or multiply the source sample size by group/seed count.
6. Compare positive predicted harm with realized positive harm on the registered
   event. A 0.02 predicted cap is not a calibrated realized-risk certificate.

Easy preservation requires <=2% worst-locality degradation on the same positive-CV
easy subset, with zero-CV cases separately requiring zero new harm. Missing support
is reported undefined, not passed. Improvement alone cannot authorize deployment;
independent calibration and confirmation remain outstanding.

## Boundaries

Labels are released detector tracks, not human gold. Coordinates are image pixels,
time is raw annotation steps, and online detector causality and universal prior
nonexposure are not proven. No metric/seconds/physical-safety/true-3D/foundation
claim. Historical Stage37 figures remain exploratory. No Stage5C or SMC.

Local inference uses the existing arm64 Torch environment, four computation threads,
one interop thread and no loader multiprocessing. Each record/group is resumable,
with PID/heartbeat and a 10GiB free-space floor. CREATE queue was checked read-only;
no new remote job is necessary for this frozen inference workload.
