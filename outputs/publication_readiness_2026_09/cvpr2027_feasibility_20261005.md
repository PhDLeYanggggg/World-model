## Priority Gaps

1. Show that a learned intervention rule improves controlled trajectory utility
   while preserving the fixed selected easy-risk budget, against all strong
   controls rather than only a weaker comparator. The current best development
   alternatives do not satisfy this joint requirement.
2. Establish an identifiable contribution of scene-joint selection under a
   matched predictor and intervention budget. Architecture names, cost-MSE
   reductions and implementation checks alone do not supply it.
3. Freeze a supported method before independent model-selection, calibration
   and confirmation readouts. Reused development evidence cannot be renamed as
   independent test evidence, and unknown outcomes remain unresolved support.
4. Complete matched forecaster comparisons and three end-to-end seeds, then
   final figures, uncertainty, anonymous reproducibility and the paper package.
   Existing explanatory notes are not a complete submission.

No deadline justifies weakening these requirements or hiding negative results.
The 25 October evidence lock remains a decision point, not a promise that the
method will pass. If these gaps remain, the honest delivery is an exploratory
study and a precise gap analysis, not a claim of CCF-A readiness.
