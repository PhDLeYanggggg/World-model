# Direct Risk Supervision: Fit Improves, Safe Selection Does Not

## Material Passport

Fresh108 neural risk heads,216,000updates and source-development readout.
Cached_verified108 matched moment-MSE controls, frozen forecasting/floor/utility
producer chains. All twelve localities were already development-exposed.
Independent selection/calibration/confirmation remain unopened.

## Result

The registered objective-only repair **does not pass**. Direct signed-excess
supervision yields0.1848% [0.1101%,0.2753%] ADE gain over the protected floor,
but improvement versus ordinary MSE is only0.0306% [-0.0334%,0.1086%]. At the
same current-query intervention count, the difference is **-0.0168%
[-0.0539%,0.0169%]**. Neither contrast establishes stable superiority.

The direct model's selected positive-harm primary is undefined on the fixed
roster because14/216 held views completely abstain. Empty views occur at
locality082(6),112(6),124(2). They are not assigned zero risk or dropped.
Among remaining views,79 exceed the2% budget. The worst ratio is51.6175%, based
on only5known selected rows in locality110; sparse support is part of the
failure, not a reason to suppress it. No deployment upgrade.

## What Worked

- Real native training completed in240.02seconds, peak RSS9.71GB;108 fixed
  training monitors improved and no unknown-label row was sampled for loss.
- All108 cached controls match initial models, complete draws/RNG and fit-only
  transforms. Only the registered objective changed.
- All three forecaster seeds retain a small positive gain over the floor.
- Easy net error is preserved in every observed direct-policy held view, with
  worst easy gain+0.2431% overCV and no zero-CV-reference harm.
- All/hard/FDE gain overfloor are0.1848%/0.1360%/0.2678%; these are development
  gains, not proof of independent generalization or safety.

## What Failed and Why It Matters

The all-risk signed-score squared error improves on fitting sources:
0.1053 to0.0730. The paired difference is-0.03230 [-0.03575,-0.02861]. On held
sources, the corresponding error is0.1836 versus0.1900; difference+0.00636
[-0.00227,+0.01801]. This shows **no supported held-score improvement**, not
statistically certain deterioration. Paired fitting references are descriptive
context values, not independent test observations.

The new loss did what it optimized on fitting data. Its advantage did not
transport into reliably calibrated selection. Component score bases are
unidentified under combination-only loss, so their apparent reference/harm
ratios must not be presented as improved moment calibration. A model can
predict negative budget excess while still selecting harmful outcomes.

The direct model beats the prior tail-weighted action by0.1179%
[0.0492%,0.2050%], but loses to the unsafe frozen ridge action by0.3415%
[-0.4860%,-0.2034%]. Choosing the weakest comparison would hide the central
failure. Easy preservation, training fit and risk-budget validity are separate.

## Next Action

Pause blind loss variants, not the research goal. Localize the fitting-to-held
gap using fitting-defined causal-support, motion/error-scale and label-quality
strata. Quantify safe oracle opportunity and risk-score reliability within
supported versus unsupported regions, including sparse and unknown labels.
Only then register a concrete representation or source-support repair. Do not
change the2% budget, open reserved roles, or tune held thresholds to rescue
this result. The current experiment remains a negative development ablation.

No new trajectory/JEPA training, independent confirmation, deployment,
Stage5C or SMC. Image-local detector silver, obs8/pred12 rawstride12; not
