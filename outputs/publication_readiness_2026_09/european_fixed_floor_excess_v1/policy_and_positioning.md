## Positioning

The experiment estimates a signed loss-budget combination. The conditional
squared-loss minimizer is its conditional mean; this elementary fact is not
a new theorem. Separate nonnegative score bases are not uniquely identified
by combination-only supervision. Good fitting loss cannot establish external
conditional calibration or finite-sample safety after selection.

Risk/coverage is an established framework; same-query count controls are
necessary to distinguish ranking from abstention in this study. We have not
implemented or outperformed [SelectiveNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html).
Likewise this is not [Learn then Test](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052): independent
calibration, valid testing and simultaneous error control are not provided by
the current source-development loss contrast. The prior study's
method_positioning.md retains the primary-source reading record.

Any future contribution must be demonstrated as useful neural improvement
under a defensible risk/calibration protocol and meaningful scene-level
comparisons, not the algebraic reparameterization itself. No independent
source reading, deployment, Stage5C or SMC is authorized by a calendar check.
