## Hypothesis

Independent reference/harm moment fitting can yield incorrect selected risk
because reference cost is overpredicted. Directly supervising the signed
combination H-0.02R may align optimization with the deployed budget test. This
is not a new theorem or guarantee. The earlier CV-referenced excess experiment
failed; this experiment specifically fixes the repaired forecaster bank and
same protected-floor producer for fitting and readout.
