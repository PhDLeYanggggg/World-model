\section{Learning from full-record feasible assignments}
We permit a slot to move to a different visible agent at the same
query and retain its peak displacement. Both a past-prefix analytic
shape and a radius-matched spline are available at each legal edge.
Training menus and spline candidates exclude both the donor and
outer location. Two teachers maximize the smaller conservative gain
against CV and OLS4; only one requires easy support and the original
recording constraints. Infeasible or nonpositive cases teach the
analytic anchor. The learned edge scores use the same pairwise
imitation loss and distinct assignment at each query. This draws on
optimization-based ranking~\cite{mandi2022rank} but does not
differentiate through the solver or impose recording constraints at
inference. We run \FeasibleRecipientFits{} fresh fits
(\FeasibleRecipientUpdates{} updates) across seven locations and three seeds.

Every policy uses 1,457 slots across 606 recording--seed views.
Risk versus gain teaching gives \FeasibleRecipientPrimary{} conservative
percentage points, with 3,000 paired location resamples. Against
past-prefix substitution the contrast is \FeasibleRecipientAgainstPrefix{}.
The risk teacher helps relative to the gain teacher on some risk checks,
but does not recover its accuracy or beat either fixed-recipient control.

An outcome-aware solve with analytic paths alone qualifies
\FeasibleRecipientAnalytic{} views; the same solve with both families
qualifies \FeasibleRecipientBoth{}. The 78 additional views include
76 at just two exposed locations. Fixing recipients and radii chosen
by the risk-trained model leaves only \FeasibleRecipientChosen{}
qualifiable views even with oracle shape selection. The full-menu
capacity cannot be attributed to the online policy; it also cannot
resolve missing selected easy support by re-scoring the same paths.
