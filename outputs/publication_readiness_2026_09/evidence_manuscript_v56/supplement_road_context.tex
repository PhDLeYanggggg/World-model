\section{Static Road Context}
We align the publisher's static OpenDD drivable-area raster and
affine world file to observed positions. The raster describes vehicle
space rather than pedestrian walkability. At half radius,
\RoadContextMapped{} of \RoadContextEdited{} edited row-seed cases
have map support for the full observed history and all six paths.
Continuation chooses the path closest to a past-only extrapolation
of signed road-boundary distance, with matched hindcast at unsupported
locations. The intervention recipients and peak radii are unchanged.

The direct conservative contrast with fixed tangent is
\RoadContextTangent{} percentage points; the original safety rule
passes in \RoadContextPass/606 views. Against same-count assignments
shuffled within a recording and seed, the contrast is
\RoadContextOverall{} points. A secondary shuffle additionally
preserves map support, current road side and observed crossing status
and yields \RoadContextState{} points. Intervals resample seven
locations after seed averaging. The secondary result suggests a small
assignment signal beyond coarse state, but does not reverse the
primary result or support deployment or independent confirmation.
