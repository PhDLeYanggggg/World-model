# Development Addendum: Direct Budget-Excess Pilot

After the negative proposal-conditioned risk experiment, we tested direct
signed prediction of H-.02R and EH-.02ER. The matched pair used mean-squared
or fixed .9-quantile loss, identical query-context networks and2,000updates.
Three contexts were fixed by existing registry order before training. All six
models were frozen before reading their third-source development partitions.
This is one seed and seven already exposed SOURCE units, not a new test set.

On the same pilot sources, known ADE improvement over the physical floor was
5.33%for the previous proposal-conditioned veto,4.09%for direct mean risk,
and0.15%for direct quantile risk. Intervention fell from31.79%to21.31%and1.52%.
Original full risk/support passed1/17,0/17and2/17recording views, respectively.
The quantile arm still had10known-all and9known-easy violations and4undefined
selected-easy views. Two matched utility source views were unavailable.
All three paired completion-lower context contrasts favored the mean control
over the quantile treatment. These descriptive pilot results are not accompanied
by a significance or independent-generalization claim.

The experiment does not support promoting direct upper-quantile risk learning.
Admitted-set underestimation was present even on fitting sources. This narrows
the hypothesis beyond unseen-source shift but does not identify a unique cause.
An exact marginal quantile does not imply a selected conditional-mean harm
constraint. No original threshold, support rule or missing-value definition was
changed; prior failures remain failures. No deployment or submission-readiness
claim. The original24group primary remains not estimable.
