used by the evaluation bound. The explicit bound-target constructor now exists
and has two passing tests, but no new critic or policy has used it. The proposed
matched comparison must exclude evaluation sites from forecaster and critic
fitting,keep the original risk contract,and log observed targets separately from
conservative bound supervision. Future masks remain supervision only.
