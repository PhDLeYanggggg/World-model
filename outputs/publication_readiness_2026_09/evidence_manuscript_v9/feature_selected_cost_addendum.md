# Selected-Cost Failure With Frozen Interaction Features

This addendum records the later completed feature-head diagnosis. It is not
yet part of the compiled main PDF. It does not revise the original feature
primary, complete risk rule, or failed independent calibration result.

We run every frozen matched feature head on both its fitting G localities
and the double-excluded H localities. The complete 72 model-role passes and
separate replay reproduce all 36 role summaries exactly. H cost/amplitude
arrays and completion-risk ratios also match the original experiment.

The interaction head has median selected actual EH predicted/target mass
ratios of 0.003209 on G and 0.025886 on H. All 324 G and 330 H falsely-safe
easy recording/pipeline views underestimate EH; only 31 and 92 overestimate
ER. Underprediction is therefore present before locality transfer and is not
explained solely by reference-denominator inflation. These repeated-view
statistics are descriptive, not independent-scene effect estimates.

At fixed full-proposal targets before amplitude, the median EH ratio falls
from 0.157515 on the full legal G bank to 0.027610 on the selected G bank.
The corresponding H ratios are 0.222641 and 0.051748. The decrease occurs in
416/444 G and 419/444 H views. This isolates selection-conditioned cost error
from differences introduced by actual interpolated trajectories. It does not
identify a unique source in the architecture, information or optimization.

Among nonempty G selected sets with no wholly-unknown rows, seven easy views
still violate risk. This posthoc sensitivity result does not repair partial
futures, remove unknown rows from the main cohort or establish physical risk.

The next fitting-only experiment multiplies the budgets of all 18 interaction
heads by four, with data, initialization, features, loss, optimizer and draw
stream fixed. Exact old-budget prefix reproduction and a fixed-old-selected-
set comparison distinguish additional optimization from switching less. No
new H policy scoring or deployment follows from this training check. Even a
positive fitting result will need a separately frozen held evaluation.
