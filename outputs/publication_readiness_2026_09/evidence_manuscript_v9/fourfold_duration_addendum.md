# Fourfold Fitting-Duration Negative Result

This is an evidence addendum, not text already present in the compiled v9 PDF.
No new held-policy score or independent confirmation is reported here.

All18 interaction-feature cost heads complete the fixed fourfold budget,
232,320 primary optimizer updates, with three actual seeds and unchanged
features, architecture, loss, learning rate, original draw stream and source
roles. All old-budget prefixes and the first8,000-update scratch refit match
exactly. A separate18-head fitting diagnostic replay is exact.

Mean full-bound fitting objective falls34.77%. On identical original selected
sets, the median easy-harm prediction/target ratio rises0.003209->0.009333;
on extended own selection it falls to0.002782. Own-selection fitting views
still have307easy-risk violations out of444 repeated recording/pipeline views.
These are not independent scenes, and unknown support remains undefined.

The result supports better average fitting but not reliable costs on the
decisions selected by the model. It is not a matched-compute advantage, proof
of an irreducible representation ceiling, a unique mechanism attribution or
a deployable new policy. The original feature primary and risk failure stand.
The initial disk failure, unchanged recovery and separate completed backup
are retained. SOURCE is exposed development; pixels/raw frames/silver are
not metric/seconds/human gold. Stage5C/SMC remain off.
