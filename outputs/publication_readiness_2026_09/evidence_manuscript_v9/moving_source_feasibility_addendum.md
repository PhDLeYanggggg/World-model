# Moving-Source Forecasts, Conditional Risk And Candidate Feasibility

This addendum records the completed MovingDroneCrowd++ follow-up. It is not
yet integrated into the compiled v9 main PDF; that PDF and its visual receipt
are unchanged. Scientific numbers below are cached_verified completed results,
not fresh replications created by writing. None establishes independent final
confirmation, deployment or submission readiness.

## Population And Matched Forecasting

The admitted 64 recordings contain 195,286 past-eligible target windows for
eight observed and twelve requested native annotation steps. Fifty-eight
recordings in 23 conservative source groups support actual queries. One of
the original 24 declared groups has no query, so the full-roster primary is
not estimable. All supported-group comparisons remain development secondary
evidence; the six zero-query recordings and empty declared group are retained
in the scope ledger. Sources are not certified independent physical sites.

Nine PCT and nine fixed-head K=1 EqMotion producers complete identical
4,000-update budgets and matched sampling using three actual seeds. Both
families use the same causal histories and nearest eight complete-past
neighbors. This is an existing-method control, not a new M3W architecture or
published best-of-20 reproduction. Every saved forecast and two complete
first-model refits replay exactly.

The paired EqMotion-minus-PCT known-label gain is +55.02 percentage points

The larger past-only social-kinematic candidate inventories select their

The selected social control has -2.58% held known gain over CV, so it is not

## Better Fitting Is Not Safe Admission

Thirty-six matched cost heads compare local history with 48 additional
all-current observed-motion fields. Predictor, critic and evaluation source
groups are disjoint within each pipeline; outcomes are nevertheless already
development-exposed. Both feature arms finish 2,000 updates per head.
Shared-minus-local joint-free known utility is -0.41 pp [-2.66,+2.00]; its
paired completion lower is -5.33 pp [-8.95,-2.27]. The information addition
does not establish a positive effect or a complete risk pass.

A further matched continuation starts from the same 18 frozen local heads,
restoring weights, Adam state, sampling and normalization. Original-loss and
stable-logit-auxiliary arms each receive 2,000 additional updates. All 36
continuations, two full first-job refits and the complete policy replay finish.
The fixed auxiliary-minus-continuation joint-free known contrast is -9.31 pp
[-15.59,-3.44]; the paired completion-lower contrast is -16.86 pp
[-27.30,-7.69]. These are fixed contrasts, not a selected favorable policy.

The fitting-region check was run before this continuation's held readout.
Auxiliary known easy-harm/predicted easy-harm mass is 0.485 on the frozen
parent's selection region but 19.83 on its own region. Thus repairing old
selected cases is not calibration of new choices. The group-macro joint
switch rate falls from 70.00% to 11.86%, while only 4/348 auxiliary repeated
recording views meet the full original contract. Reduced intervention is not
a complete safety result. Full-scope same-count utility is not estimable;
empty controls are neither zero utility nor a safety pass.

A separate fitting-only input audit finds clipped fields on just 0.39% of
parent-selected row views, containing 0.95% of their known easy-harm mass.
It does not support hard input clipping as the dominant explanation. Context
and optimization effects are not causally ruled out, and no clipping-map
retraining sweep is launched from that diagnosis.

## Fixed-Candidate Feasibility

Before more selector training, test whether the original recording gate is
attainable with this binary neural/floor proposal at all. For known legal
easy rows with positive floor error R, let H be the nonnegative increase in
available-label error. A finite-support binary policy satisfies both 2%
positive-harm ratios and the signed easy-net requirement if and only if some
such row has H/R <= 0.02. Necessity follows because selected easy risk is a
reference-weighted average of these ratios. Sufficiency follows by selecting
one qualifying row and keeping the remainder at the floor. Unknown selected
rows contribute bound mass but no observed easy reference and cannot rescue
an otherwise infeasible easy constraint.

This is elementary feasibility accounting, not a new learning guarantee or
an architecture contribution. All single-row witnesses were checked against
the unchanged evaluator and replayed exactly. Thirty additional synthetic
cases exhaust every binary policy and confirm the same criterion.

Only 299/348 repeated recording views are feasible: 34 are candidate-limited
and 15 have no observed easy event under the original fitting-derived cutoff.
Fourteen of 58 unique actual-query recordings have at least one infeasible
view. The entire fixed binary roster therefore cannot pass through better
critic optimization alone. This is not an impossibility claim about every
forecaster, future annotation process or scientific protocol.

## A Constructive But Insufficient Action-Class Change

A prospective follow-up fixes all six amplitudes
{1/32,1/16,1/8,1/4,1/2,1} before scoring. Candidate paths are the physical floor
plus a fixed amplitude times the past-only neural/floor difference. Every
fraction is reported; no winner is chosen from held outcomes. No neural
model, residual head or selector is trained by this control.

The candidate set admits a label-aware witness in 31 of the 34 formerly
candidate-limited views, increasing total feasibility to 330/348. Fifteen of
these newly feasible views admit a half-amplitude witness, and six admit a
quarter-amplitude witness, so the opportunity is not confined to arbitrarily
near-zero changes. It still uses labels and is not a learned policy result.
Fifteen empty-event and three candidate-limited views remain; none is dropped.

Uniform causal interpolation also remains unsafe. At amplitude 1/32, known
gain is +1.15% [+0.88,+1.43] and completion lower +0.48% [+0.16,+0.78], but
only 96/348 views meet the original complete contract, with 65 known easy-risk
violations. Larger amplitudes trade more utility against failed risk. These
development intervals are not independent success or permission to deploy.

The motivating next method would need to learn past-conditioned amplitude costs and
joint allocation without inheriting full-amplitude costs for changed paths.
The empty-event problem separately requires explicit data/event support;
the original 2% requirements are not silently relaxed. Independent model
selection, risk calibration and final confirmation remain outstanding.

## Matched Amplitude-Cost Learning Does Not Yet Realize Safe Opportunity

That hypothesis has now been tested with 36 fresh Torch cost heads and
72,000 primary updates. Both arms share candidate inputs, capacity, initial
states, fitting normalizers, source-balanced query draws and 2,000-update
budgets. Binary-only supervises/permits full neural actions; amplitude-bank
supervises/permits six fractions. This changes learning and deployment
availability together, not only the inference action space. All new outputs
and scalar metrics replay exactly; two complete first-job refits also agree.

The fixed joint-free amplitude-minus-binary known effect is +0.46 pp
[-0.48,+1.49], while its paired completion lower is -1.16 pp [-2.91,+0.22].
The registered supported-group secondary therefore does not pass. A same-weight
binary deployment gives a small known-label action-menu effect of +0.59 pp
[+0.30,+0.92], but its paired completion lower is +0.10 pp [-0.43,+0.54].
That secondary known signal cannot replace the failed primary or prove safety.

Only 10/348 repeated amplitude-bank recording views satisfy the original
complete contract, compared with 6/348 for the newly trained binary control.
There are 273 known all-risk and 281 known easy-risk violations, 46 undefined
selected easy denominators and worst known easy-net degradation 376.33%.
These include substantive harm failures, not only missing-event support.
No unsupported view is removed. Mean source-macro switching rises from
63.24% to 70.93%; mean amplitude over all rows also rises, from 0.6324 to
0.6582. Among repeated selected row views, 92.73% still use the full action.
Thus richer actions do not automatically yield a conservative policy.

Fitting-side own-selection EH/predicted-EH mass remains about 202 for the
amplitude head versus 732 for binary, despite globally overpredicting EH on
all legal full actions. This localizes a selected-cost fitting deficit before
held shift, without isolating one causal mechanism. It motivates checking
cross-amplitude cost shape and selected-risk learning rather than another
held-threshold sweep. No new constrained model, calibration or deployment
result is claimed. These are still exposed development intervals, not
independent confirmation. This addendum remains separate from the compiled
v9 PDF; the PDF was not regenerated by this result update.

## Cross-Amplitude Shape Repair: A Completed Negative Control

The next frozen fitting-only audit checks all 36 cost heads and separately
replays every original fitting prediction hash and diagnostic. Among the
supervised amplitude head's 1,171,716 repeated fitting rows, 822,588 violate
H convexity and 542,904 violate EH convexity; true bounded-label curves have
zero violations. H/alpha inversions at the selected action contain 46.85%
of known selected EH mass. These are descriptive repeated-view associations,
not a unique causal attribution or independent calibration.

We then prospectively test a nonnegative hinge decoder for H, EH and non-easy
harm. Inputs, raw capacity/initial parameters, source sampling, loss and update
budgets match. There are 18 fresh heads and 36,000 new primary updates, not
36 fresh fits: the 18 original amplitude heads are cached_verified controls.
Both complete first-job refits and every initial-state/draw check agree.
All new held outputs/scalars and original-control policies replay exactly.
Stable hinge arithmetic is disclosed as part of the decoder, not a separate
precision ablation. Convex/monotone networks themselves are established prior
work; the task-specific derivation is elementary, not a new safety theorem.

All constrained fitting-shape checks become zero, but own-selection EH mass
remains underestimated by about 245 times predicted mass, versus 202 for
the old head's different selected region. The joint-free known paired effect
is -0.02 pp [-0.53,+0.51]; the registered paired completion lower is -1.83 pp
[-3.62,-0.42]. This negative lower criterion does not identify the unobserved
true effect's sign. It does not support robust superiority. Separate floor-
relative lower scores cannot be subtracted to replace the paired comparison.

Complete original risk support remains 10/348 repeated recording views.
Known all/easy violations are 268/278 and worst known easy-net degradation
428.91%, versus 376.33% for the control. Mean switching and amplitude decline
slightly, but every non-floor policy still fails the full original contract.
All 23 supported source points, empty utility comparisons and missing-label
support are retained; no favorable source subset is promoted. This result
separates correct structural constraints from accurate selected magnitudes.
It is not a new trajectory dynamics result, deployment or submission upgrade.
No new independent role, Stage5C execution or SMC is introduced. The compiled
v9 PDF is unchanged; this remains a separate source addendum.

## Claim Boundary And Evidence Links

These results provide a real stronger forecast component, negative risk-learning
controls and a constructive candidate-space diagnosis. They do not yet support
the complete M3W method or a submission-ready claim. This is 2D image-plane
forecasting within a 2.5D trajectory/world-state research system, not true 3D
or foundation success. Native annotation steps are not verified video frames
or seconds. Pixels are not metres. Partial available-label ADE and bounds for
wholly unknown outcomes do not identify missing steps within partial futures.
No independent role, future endpoint input, central velocity input or test-goal
construction is introduced. Stage5C execution and SMC remain disabled.

## Harm magnitude repair without deployment certification, 4 November

After the negative convex-decoder comparison, a fixed three-arm study fits
54 small upward harm refiners on the same 18 frozen amplitude heads. This is
1,080 retained tree fits, not new neural trajectory training. References,
forecasts, data roles, amplitudes and the original 2% contract remain fixed.
Global, fixed-region and refreshed-region arms have matched tree budgets.

The common-region diagnostic rules out a purely selection-based explanation
for fitting improvement. On the original frozen choice region, adaptive
actual/predicted H changes 42.7012 to 0.7856 and EH 202.2497 to 0.3729: severe
underestimation becomes overestimation. All 288 fitting cells are retained;
72 prediction hashes and 18 original scalar blocks match earlier receipts.

The registered joint-free adaptive-minus-fixed known effect is +0.07047 pp,
CI [+0.01397, +0.13662]. Its paired completion-lower effect is +0.00267 pp,
CI [-0.14122, +0.10866], so the robust primary is not passed. Worst signed
easy-net degradation improves to 1.37061%, but known all/easy positive-harm
violations remain 42/72. Only 47/348 repeated recording views meet the original
full contract. Of 214 undefined selected-easy views, 161 have no trajectory
change and 53 have active but unsupported decisions; neither is reclassified
as an empirical selected-risk pass. There are 48 empty same-count contrasts.

All 72 held cost outputs and original metrics replay. Post-decision diagnostics
do not change prediction or allocation hashes. Remaining held pooled H/EH
underestimation factors are 1.354/1.442, with easy-reference inflation also
contributing. These averages do not identify a unique shift mechanism or
replace recording-level checks. The registered 0.25 s/query solve budget is
retained; saved feasibility replay is not bitwise MILP reoptimization.

The original 24-group primary remains not estimable, and all 23 supported
groups are exposed development. Completion effects use the fixed observed-
floor denominator and available-label ADE; they do not reconstruct missing
steps of partially observed trajectories. No independent confirmation, new
deployment, dynamics contribution, true-3D/foundation claim, metric scale or
effective-seconds verification. Stage5C and SMC stay off. This is a source
addendum; the main v9 PDF has not been recompiled by this task.

## Equal-width shared-context refinement, 4 November

A separate prospectively committed feature comparison retrains 18 local-zero
and 18 shared-context refiners, both 435 columns and 20 trees, on the same
frozen neural costs and fitting roles. The added fields are past group motion
and current crowd geometry, not images or verified camera-motion calibration.
All 720 primary trees finish before held scoring; six recovery cells and two
complete refits are exact. The original shared-neural-head negative stays
negative; this tests a different cost estimator, not a new pooling architecture.

The registered shared-minus-local direct completion-lower effect is -0.05387 pp,
95% grouped development CI [-0.21678, +0.08149]. The known-label effect is
-0.04198 pp [-0.20440, +0.09306]. Neither establishes a feature gain. Shared
worst signed easy-net degradation improves from 2.06315% to 0.90200%, but known
all/easy positive-harm violations remain 56/67, with complete support in only
37/348 repeated recording views. There are 54 empty same-count contrasts.

All 54 freshly inferred cost arrays, 108 decision metric blocks and post-decision
diagnostics replay exactly. The previous 387-column controls reuse hash-bound
decisions and fresh exact scalar replay, not fresh old-cost inference. Shared
own-selection H/EH mass ratios improve, but this does not hold held selection
fixed and cannot establish calibration. The known-label contrast also fails,
so missing-outcome uncertainty is not the sole explanation. All 162 common-region
fitting cells and every source remain available.

No deployment or submission-readiness promotion. Original 24-group primary
remains not estimable; supported 23 groups remain exposed development. Reserved

their prior limitations. Stage5C/SMC are off. This updates source prose only;
