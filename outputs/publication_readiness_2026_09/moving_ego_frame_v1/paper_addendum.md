The existing representation scales ego motion using the maximum of ego and
neighbor-history radii. The alternative uses ego-history scale and retains the
old-to-new scale ratio. Original signed-risk labels, units, weights, target
normalization and data-role boundaries remain unchanged. Sixteen fresh forest
fits are compared with eight verified controls; all24models reproduce by refit.

admission, not proof of an irreducible data limitation or of universal method
