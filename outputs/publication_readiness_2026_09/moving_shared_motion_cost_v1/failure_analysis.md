The separate [selected-cost diagnosis](selected_cost_audit/readout.md) is
post-readout descriptive analysis, not a new preregistered confirmation. All72
job/role/arm views independently replay. Choices are fixed before future labels
are used for diagnosis. Harm-mass comparisons use the same known selected rows.

This is not simply a uniform global underprediction. On all-neural fitting
rows, known EH/predicted EH is0.6461for local and0.4664for shared motion: the
global EH mass is overpredicted, while the policy-selected region is severely
underpredicted. The choice rule selects apparently very low-harm outputs that
are not conditionally reliable, even on fitting sources. Held shift aggravates
the problem but is not its sole origin.

These descriptive pooled repeated-view mass ratios are not official macro
scores or new risk gates. They do not uniquely identify sparse-tail loss
weighting, decoder saturation, optimization or missing causal information as
the cause. The cost decoder can represent coherent nonnegative moments; these
results alone do not prove a structural impossibility or a specific code bug.

## What Joint Allocation Does And Does Not Show

Free-count joint policies have higher observed mean utility than their own
independent policies, but intervention counts differ. That is not an isolated
same-count joint-method contribution or evidence of geometric consistency.
The all-required-group matched utility contrast is not estimable: local has4
unsupported source views and shared has3. Empty matched controls remain
explicitly not estimable, never zero utility or a safety pass. Full strategy
risk is still evaluated on the entire original population.

## Next Controlled Repair

This new-source audit corroborates earlier European selected-EH diagnosis;
it is not a newly discovered general mechanism or a standalone novelty claim.
The earlier sparse-EH, conditional-cost and cost-loss controls also failed and
remain negative. Do not simply rerun them under another dataset or name.

First distinguish numerical saturation from an incorrectly weighted selected
objective on critic-fitting sources. A subsequent risk/calibration control must
be a separately frozen, substantively different test justified by that diagnosis.
Keep the original moment bounds, legal inputs, disjoint roles and2%requirements.
Do not tune a multiplier on the exposed held scores, widen the network again
without evidence, or assume more camera-motion features will fix this failure.
Numerical saturation versus loss weighting is still an untested distinction.

Any new source allocation for model selection/calibration/final confirmation
must be resolved prospectively. Existing DUT/DroneCrowd roles and unopened
confirmation remain unchanged. This diagnostic does not grant new source use.
