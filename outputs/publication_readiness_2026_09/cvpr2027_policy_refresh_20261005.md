# CVPR 2027 Policy Check: 5 October 2026

The official dates remain 10 November for paper registration, 16 November for
the full submission and 23 November for supplementary material, all AoE.
Internal locks remain 9, 13 and 20 November respectively; these are project
buffers, not conference deadlines. [Official dates](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/Dates).

The official call still requires current OpenReview profiles for all authors,
sets dual-submission restrictions and describes invited reviewer obligations.
Its detailed LLM guidance remains pending. No account or submission has been
changed. [Official call](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers).

The linked Author Guidelines page still returns 404. Exact 2027 formatting,
page limits and detailed anonymity rules remain unverified; earlier years'
rules are not substituted. The identity-minimized aggregate replay package is
therefore not described as anonymity-compliant or submission-ready.
[Linked guidelines](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines).

The 25 October evidence lock remains conditional on a defensible method and
independent evidence. A readable draft, working archive or queued training job
does not establish those conditions. Actual submission requires final author
confirmation.
