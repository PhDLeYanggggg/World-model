\section{Exact Budgets and Learned Recipient Decisions}

Holding recipients fixed can make risk infeasible before a
path ranker is trained. Componentwise minima rule out
\FixedPeople{} of the 102 views at the original neural counts
and \FreeNeural{} if those counts are free. These are bounds
for the specific frozen recipients and allowed replacements,
not infeasibility claims about the entire forecast bank.

When recipients may change, the constraints are jointly
feasible in \NominationFeasible{} views using one frozen
nomination per person, and \FullFeasible{} with all paths.
The solver keeps the exact original query counts in both
banks. On known rows the lowest-error legal path weakly
improves all risk components and CV utility; on wholly
unknown rows the path with the smallest CV disagreement
does likewise. This reduces full-bank CV optimization to
one choice per row without assuming the same dominance
for OLS4 utility. Every witness is rechecked by the original
scorer. Figure~\ref{fig:recipient} contrasts this capacity
with the actual pass counts from causal recipient selection.

\paragraph{Distilling the recording teacher.}
Decision-focused learning already optimizes predictors for
downstream objectives~\cite{spo,wilder}. Our diagnostic does
not differentiate through the optimizer or implement SPO+.
It learns pairwise preferences from verified whole-recording
solutions on training sources. For each teacher action we
compare the past descriptors with legal alternatives on the
same recipient and on unselected recipients. Ridge fitting
balances these two comparisons and excludes the scored
source. We then use the same score to select recipients with
the original nominated paths or to select both paths and
recipients. Neither student sees outcome labels at inference.

At the same \StructuredSwitches{} switches, the full-bank
student changes conservative utility relative to gain
ranking by \StudentVsGain. The fixed-nomination student
changes it by \NomineeVsGain. The original recording
pass counts are \StudentPass{} and \NomineePass, respectively,
versus \GainPass{} for gain ranking. The full student changes
observed pair ADE by a signed reduction of \StudentPair{} pixels.
The predicted screen retains \ScreenSwitches{} full-student
switches and passes \ScreenPass{} views; this screened count
is not a matched-utility result. For the full student versus
gain, the observed contrast is \StudentKnown; disagreement
on wholly unobserved rows accounts for another
\StudentMissing{} percentage points in the lower envelope.
Closer agreement with a training teacher is not enough to
recover its recording-level constraints on a new source.
