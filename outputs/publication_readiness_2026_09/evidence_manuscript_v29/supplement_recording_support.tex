\section{Does Selection Preserve the Risk Denominator?}
On OpenDD development, the uniform blend passes \RecordUniformPass{}
of 606 recording--seed views. Streaming joint selection passes
\RecordJointPass{} despite lower site-level easy harm. At equal query
counts, joint selection reduces pair compression but loses
\RecordJointGap{} gain points vs. independent utility.

For a fixed forecast per target, an unrestricted subset can satisfy
the observed recording-risk algebra exactly when an eligible easy
target has positive reference mass and harm at most $2\%$ of that
mass. Necessity follows from the mass-weighted easy harm ratio and
the nonnegative unknown-outcome debit; sufficiency follows by
selecting the single qualifying known edit. It guarantees neither
positive utility nor the ability to find that edit from the past.
Among the \RecordUniformFailures{} uniform failures,
\RecordFeasibleFailed{} admit such an edit in hindsight,
\RecordNoAffordable{} have no affordable eligible easy edit, and
\RecordNoReference{} have no full easy reference under the frozen cut.
