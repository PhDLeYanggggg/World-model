# Independent Risk Calibration

## Frozen Question

Does an independent recording/locality role permit empirical risk calibration
of the already trained mean_absolute cost family, without changing its native
utility objective or the original two percent recording-level requirements?
Previous uncertainty and frozen-utility repairs remain negative. This is not
another SOURCE model search or another exposed-selection threshold search.

## Roles And Authority

The existing immutable roles.json assigns 102 recordings in 12 localities to
risk_calibration_reserved. Broad delegated research authorization permits this
registered calibration purpose. A new narrow accessor admits only this role;
original SOURCE/selection accessors and original role flags remain unchanged.
Confirmation and DroneCrowd stay closed. Calibration is not model training;
no model, representation, normalizer, prototype or easy/hard cut is refitted.
The 9 mean_absolute critics and 9 corresponding history forecasters, all
SOURCE-trained and hash-checked, are fixed before calibration input access.
No selection of seed or SOURCE fold is permitted.

## Inputs And Complete Cohort

Use all 102 recordings, 256 uniformly spaced past-supported query frames per
recording at most, obs8/pred12/stride12. Targets require complete past only;
partial-history neighbors and wholly futureless targets remain. Read released
detector/tracker silver in local image pixels. This is retrospective past-only
conditioning, not proof of online detector causality, human labels, true 3D,
metric units, seconds or foundation-model success. No future endpoint, future
validity, future support or track remaining length enters inference.

Input arrays and the exact existing 476+64 detector bridge are separate from
labels. Recording IDs are 191+index in source-member order; causal target row
IDs are (recording_id << 32)+local target index. Cache identity includes member,
raw-row hash, original roles hash and new registration. Largest-record pilot
precedes full input conversion and complete raw replay. No raw archive copy.

## Fixed Calibration Operator

The registered grid is lambda=1,2,4,...,4096. Keep original B,H,EH and native
G=B-H EXACTLY unchanged. Divide the two reference budget coefficients R,ER by
lambda before calling the unmodified, hash-bound existing four-arm allocator.
This is an optimization-coefficient view, NOT a coherent moment distribution
or a calibrated posterior. It implements lambda*H<=.02R and lambda*EH<=.02ER.
Independent, frame_uniform, joint_matched and joint_free are all frozen for
every grid point, producer and recording BEFORE any calibration labels are
constructed. joint_matched retains the independent intervention count in each
query. Existing solver timeout/postcheck failures retain the feasible parent.
This is budget pooling, not optimization of pair-interaction conflicts.

Choose ONE common, least-conservative lambda passing every original recording
view for BOTH independent and joint_matched: 918 views per required policy,
finite completion all/easy positive-harm ratios <=2%, and known easy-net <=2%.
Undefined/empty denominators do not pass. No scene removal, per-scene threshold
or altered denominator. Other arms are diagnostic at that same lambda.
If none qualifies, report unsupported calibration and separate global-floor
fallback; neither empty support nor zero intervention is learned safe success.

## Interpretation And Verification

Calibration utility is in-sample after calibration-parameter fitting, not an
independent test or a new primary efficacy result. A locality bootstrap may
describe conditional uncertainty but cannot undo parameter selection. Twelve
localities, dependent recordings, partial labels, nonmonotone reselected
actions and ratios do not establish a general population 2% risk certificate.
Available-label ADE is not full12 ADE. Completion bounds address wholly unknown
rows only, not missing steps inside partially observed trajectories. Missing
endpoints make FDE diagnostic. No final confirmation is opened or promotion
made merely because a calibration candidate exists.

Use CPU4/inter-op1/workers0 native arm64, real frozen Torch inference, immutable
phase receipts, per-record and per-query atomic checkpoints and heartbeats.
Keep 10GiB disk reserve, private cache <=6GiB and a 12h per-phase boundary with
unchanged resume; a healthy slow job is not automatically downgraded. Store
choice matrices as int8 and remove only this operator's committed transient
query checkpoints. A full raw/input replay, fresh forecast/cost replay and
feasibility/count/metric replay are reported separately from rerunning MILP.
Stage5C and SMC remain off. Old failures and unrelated staged files stay intact.
