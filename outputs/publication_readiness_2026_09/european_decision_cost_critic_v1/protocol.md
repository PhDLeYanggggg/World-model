## Hypothesis And Changed Factor

Moment-score MSE did not align downstream decisions with realized intervention gain/harm.
Add weight1 expected seven-action decision cost to the original coupled regression objective.
For every causal query row,softmax over fallback logit0 and seven predicted net gains divided by
the SOURCE fitting RMS of benefit/harm;unavailable physical actions are masked.
No oracle choice,future coordinate,outcome-support mask or training dual enters inference.
Targets are realized gain and three recording constraints:positive harm minus2%reference,
easy positive harm minus2%easy-reference,and easy net increase minus2%full easy-reference.
Wholly unknown rows use the existing causal rollout-disagreement envelope as a pessimistic
bound,not a supervised zero or fabricated label. Known rows retain original labels.
Source-balanced/query-balanced draws are weighted to reproduce equal-source full utility and
equal-source/equal-record constraints. Constants in the easy-degradation constraint affect
the dual update,not parameter gradients. Past recording-scale sums normalize constraints.
Every100 updates,a full deterministic inference over every fitting recording updates three
nonnegative duals by unit step,clipped at100. No sampled-only dual or selected-record omission.
No outcome-selected coefficient,threshold,temperature,checkpoint or seed.

## Relation To Existing Work

This differs from this repository's old binary cost-sensitive primal by using the current seven-action
selected-query critic,unchanged moment decoder and calibration auxiliary. It is not a novel
principle of learning decision costs. [Smart Predict then Optimize](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922)
studies optimization-induced loss and SPO+;the current softmax empirical surrogate is not SPO+.
[Regression with Multi-Expert Deferral](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html) studies
multiple-expert regression and bounded-cost surrogate guarantees;those guarantees are not
transferred to native ADE,missing-outcome bounds or recording-risk ratios here.
