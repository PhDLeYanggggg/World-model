# Draft Results Component,Not A Submission-Ready Paper

On the same frozen seven-forecast bank,we compared a continuous moment-regression
critic with an otherwise matched decision-cost critic. Each new head used30,857
parameters and2,000updates. Across12exposed SOURCE localities,known-label ADE
gain over fallback increased from7.67%to9.14%;the paired difference was1.47
percentage points(conditional locality-bootstrap95%CI0.53to2.34). However,the
preregistered missing-outcome completion-lower contrast did not support improvement
(-2.74pp,95%CI-6.59to0.21). Only9of444original record/context/critic-seed views
satisfied the full risk requirement,and worst known easy degradation was28.33%.

A post-hoc training-support diagnostic identified a representational mismatch:
bounded cost logits with a fixed fallback score implied high minimum soft
intervention probabilities,and a relaxed best-case family could not satisfy the
training constraints in any of444record views. This does not establish hard-policy
infeasibility. It motivates separating fallback decisions from calibrated moment
outputs,rather than treating lower cost-prediction error or average utility as
sufficient evidence of safe deployment. The present comparison is SOURCE fitting
resubstitution,not independent confirmation,and no model was promoted.

This component belongs in exploratory method/failure analysis. It is not a full
English manuscript,a new theory of decision-focused learning,or evidence of
foundation/true3D world modeling. Current coordinates are detector-silver local
image coordinates with obs8/pred12/rawstride12,not verified meters or seconds.
