\section{Recording Teacher and Missing Support}

On donors, we solve the original recording risk problem
at fixed immediate and one-query-delay counts. Both
teachers use only recordings with a verified feasible
witness and positive lower gains over CV and OLS4.
The recording teacher selects its witnessed slate;
the local teacher instead maximizes the query's lower
gain over CV. They differ on \RecordingTeacherDisagrees{}
of \RecordingTeacherChoices{} query examples from
\RecordingTeacherDonorQualified{} donor clock views
(repeated across folds). Both use the same donor features,
with trajectories and release heads excluding donor and
outer site, and all held edit counts are identical.

The recording teacher changes lower gain by
\RecordingTeacherDirect{} versus the local teacher and
\RecordingTeacherGuard{} versus the harm guard. It does
not improve on CV (\RecordingTeacherCV{}).

Separately, as an outcome-aware budget relaxation, we retain
all existing edits and allow one extra recipient at any query,
with a neural amplitude of 1, 0.5, or 0.25. The original
scorer verifies a positive witness in
\RecordingTeacherZeroQualified{}/\RecordingTeacherZeroTotal{}
zero-edit views and
\RecordingTeacherNoEasyQualified{}/\RecordingTeacherNoEasyTotal{}
active views lacking reachable easy support. At full
amplitude alone the corresponding counts are
\RecordingTeacherZeroFull{} and \RecordingTeacherNoEasyFull{}.
This diagnostic neither changes the main budget nor implies
that the cohorts or helpful edits can be recognized causally.
