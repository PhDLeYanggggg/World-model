\paragraph{Does local context help beyond pooled motion?}
Marginal forecast errors do not measure joint outcomes~\cite{jointmetrics}.
We train matched ego, pooled-neighbor and relative-neighbor
adapters over frozen motion backbones, using only fitting
sources and single-agent ADE for supervision. Pooled motion
is the average motion of the available complete-history
neighbors, not a camera-motion estimate. At fixed recipients
and identical substitution counts, local relative features
change conservative utility versus pooled motion by
\LocalVsPooledLower{} pp. They change observed relative-path
ADE reduction by \LocalPairObserved{} pixels and its
full-grid lower envelope by \LocalPairLower{} pixels.
Local and pooled policies pass \LocalPass{} and \PooledPass{}
recording views, respectively. This controlled contrast
does not establish a local interaction benefit. The
relative-path metric is not a published joint-minimum
forecast score, and we did not train on this pair metric.
