\paragraph{Whole-recording supervision for the first edit.}
We train \FirstFits{} heads with location-held folds. Every edited
arm uses the same one-edit budget and first eligible query. The
training target checks the full recording under the original risk
rule and both reference gains; the recording-wide OLS4 offset is
never an inference feature. The direct and factorized event readouts
give \FirstDirect{} and \FirstFactor{} joint passes versus
\FirstRefit{} for the shared head's moment readout and
\FirstFrozen{} for the frozen moment control. Their conservative
differences from the refit readout are \FirstDirectEffect{} and
\FirstFactorEffect{}; neither establishes improved forecast utility.

At the same one-edit budget, the exact feasible count is
\FirstEarly{} of \FirstViews{} at the first eligible query and
\FirstAny{} at any eligible query. On the \FirstShort{} short views
the counts are \FirstShortEarly{} and \FirstShortAny{}. These are
hindsight bounds, not evidence that the later windows are identifiable
causally. No first-query policy qualifies for deployment.
