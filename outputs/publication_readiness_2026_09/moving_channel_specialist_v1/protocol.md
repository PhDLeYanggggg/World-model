evaluation protocol. All original 2% all/easy positive-harm, signed easy-net,

## Hypothesis

The fresh boundary audit finds that the ego-frame model's context2 MSE reduction
occurs entirely among unchanged rejects. In source048 its seven accepted
known proposals contain no easy reference; in076 the accepted set's normalized
known easy-harm/reference ratio is 3.67%. These are partial known-row diagnostics,
not complete-policy risk estimates. Almost all rejects have positive predicted
easy excess. This does not prove every reject is erroneous: known easy excess
is tail-dominated and predictions may reasonably remain conservative.

Test whether sharing forest partitions between two very different targets is
an avoidable source of boundary error. Preserve the original120 causal
features, known-label masks, signed all/easy targets, output units, fitting-only
normalization, source/query weights and zero decision boundary. Do not use
future validity, easy membership or labels as model inputs. Unknown proposals
are counted, not manufactured into easy/safe labels.

## Fixed Matched Arms

- shared64: eight cached, hash/schema/ID-verified two-output ExtraTrees controls.
- separate64, primary: two independent 32-tree regressors, one per risk channel.
- shared32, secondary: one fresh 32-tree two-output control. Its predictions must
  equal the first32trees of shared64, checking the tree-count implementation.

Depth8, min_leaf32, max_features1, no bootstrap, seed17, n_jobs4 are unchanged.
Primary total tree budget64 is matched; per-channel tree budget and tree shapes
are not parameter-exact matches. Report actual nodes. Each channel keeps its
original own-fitting RMS; no loss weighting or threshold sweep. Primary is not
allowed to switch to shared32 after inspecting results. Every source remains,
including the44-row source065; contexts are not independent seeds.

This differs from the earlier sign/cost partition study: it changes whether
all/easy outputs must share splits, not the split impurity or mean decoder.
It does not claim novelty, calibrated safety or full world-model dynamics.
