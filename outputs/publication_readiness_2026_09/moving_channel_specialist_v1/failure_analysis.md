# Failure Analysis

## What Was Changed And What Failed

The hypothesis was narrow: a shared two-output partition may lose distinctions
needed by sparse easy-risk examples. The repair fits one regressor per channel,
with the original 120 causal features, exact signed targets and weights. Two
32-tree models retain the total 64-tree budget. A fresh shared32 control checks
the per-output budget difference; it exactly matches the first32 trees of the
old shared64 model. Actual nodes differ and are disclosed, not parameter-matched.

The primary decision-cost reductions are -0.033689%, +0.461475%, 0%. None meets
the fixed internal screen. Context1 harmful admissions rise from 1 to 19 and
harmful-admission cost from 4.29845e-7 to 4.85530e-6. Every primary admission is
in source076. Their descriptive known normalized easy-harm/reference ratio is
5.820542%; this is not the original raw recording-level risk calculation.
The other seven source folds admit no proposal. Empty support remains NA.

MSE reductions are +2.908763%, +0.372358%, -3.898268%. The repair neither gives
a consistent regression gain nor solves admission. A new head architecture or
more switches is not evidence of a world-dynamics contribution.

## Supported Mechanisms

1. The preceding ego-frame MSE gains occur almost entirely in unchanged rejects.
   One source's large regression gain can dominate the average without moving
   the decision boundary. This explains the observed metric disagreement; it
   does not establish that every rejected proposal was inferentially knowable.
2. Shared output partitions are not a sufficient explanation. Separating them
   still leaves context2's 38,579 known proposals rejected, while context1 gains
   harmful admissions. Purely increasing admission is not a safe fix.
3. There is not a uniform train-success/held-failure pattern. Posthoc fitting
   decision cost improves 21.98% in the source053 exclusion, but the source-held
   cost is unchanged. In exclusions060/073, fitting cost itself worsens 13.70%
   and 10.08%. Both capacity tradeoffs and cross-source information limits
   remain possible; neither irreducible noise nor a unique root cause is proven.
4. Missing support is separate from prediction accuracy. A model cannot turn
   absent easy-reference labels into observed evidence. The original full-view
   support contract remains unsatisfied by this known-row probe.

## Next Action

Do not expand this failed separate-head forest into a larger neural run or
search a more permissive threshold. Keep the original thresholds and failed
results. The next model experiment needs evidence of a missing causal signal
or a different candidate-forecast error pattern, not another aggregate MSE win.
Use the existing fitting-only cache and source-specific false-admission cases
to check whether prediction-error direction and missing observation context
can be separated from genuinely similar causal histories. Any nearest-history
comparison must exclude the same track/overlapping windows and report source
coverage; a random-row neighbor match would manufacture apparent predictability.

If the current 120-feature histories cannot distinguish high-value safe and
harmful forecasts under source exclusion, return to the admitted forecast/input
representation instead of more classifier heads. Do not label that uncertainty
an impossibility proof. A new independent dataset role would need its own fixed
protocol; no reserved outcomes are opened by this negative study.
