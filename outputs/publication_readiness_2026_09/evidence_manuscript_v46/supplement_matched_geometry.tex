\section{Timing, Direction, and Recipient Capacity}
Two matched development tests preserve 95769 interventions over 24
recordings and three seeds. Learning a temporal envelope at the same
per-row peak gives \MatchedGeoTiming{} against flat quarter scaling.
Learning waypoint rotations at the same per-step radii gives
\MatchedGeoEgo{} for ego-only versus flat; adding four past neighbors
gives \MatchedGeoPeer{} versus ego-only. Intervals are paired 95\%
source bootstraps. None of these policies qualifies on the inner
calibration recordings.

At the original recipients, hindsight ray projection for timing
alone passes \MatchedGeoTimingPass/24 risk checks. Closest-point
projection onto waypoint circles minimizes observed ADE and
preserves the missing-label CV debit; direction alone passes
\MatchedGeoDirectionPass/24. Releasing recipients and path identities,
while preserving each query's count, verifies positive lower gains
against both references under the original risk rule in
\MatchedGeoFlatBoth/24 recordings for quarter paths and
\MatchedGeoCircleBoth/24 for hindsight circles
(\MatchedGeoFlatUnresolved{} and \MatchedGeoCircleUnresolved{}
unresolved). The solver uses future labels, so these are capacity
results, not forecasts or independent confirmation.
