\section{Independent Hindcast Control}
We choose among four fixed CV--OLS4 blend strengths and four
past-only evidence rules on the nine original fitting groups.
For each candidate and four origins inside the observed prefix,
we compare the candidate to CV on the remaining observed positions.
Selection maximizes the worst group pass fraction, then the mean
pass fraction, then conservative gain. Leave-one-group-out selection
passes \HindOOFPass/\HindFitCount{} fitting recordings. The final
choice blends halfway when at least three origins favor the blend
and its mean gain is positive. It does not borrow learned counts.
