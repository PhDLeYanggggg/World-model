# Statistical Interpretation Boundaries

## Material Passport

Scope: registered developmental comparison of source-validation-selected and
fixed-final checkpoints from identical training trajectories. No independent
confirmation, deployment upgrade, risk certificate or population-safety claim.

The primary contrast is validation-selected minus final normalized signed-score
MSE. Lower is better. The secondary same-count ADE contrast uses final-policy
error as its denominator. The original positive-harm screen retains its 2%
budget and selected-floor denominator; net ADE gain cannot compensate for a
positive-harm violation. Whole-population easy ADE degradation is a different
quantity and is reported separately.

All 216 directional role/seed views are reduced within each of the 12 localities
before the 3,000-draw paired locality bootstrap. This is a nominal developmental
interval: shared producers, overlapping windows and repeated adaptive research
on these localities are not removed by resampling. Seeds are not independent
external datasets. A missing subgroup denominator keeps its full-population
summary undefined rather than silently discarding the source.

## Eleven Interpretation Checks

1. Simpson: preserve locality and seed contrasts, plus independent and count-
   matched policies. Report a disagreement with the aggregate rather than hide it.
2. Ecological: locality-average evidence cannot certify individual-agent safety.
3. Selection: only the 12 already exposed development localities are used; this
   is not a new independent sample or a representative population survey.
4. Collider: selected and outcome-defined easy/hard subsets are descriptive.
   Count matching equalizes query intervention counts, not eligible/chosen sets.
5. Base rates: disclose known, unknown, selected and undefined-denominator views.
   Unknown outcomes are not harmless outcomes; no future-validity inference gate.
6. Regression to the mean: preserve the final-step control from the identical
   run and all registered sources. Do not present selected validation minima as
   independent generalization performance.
7. Survivorship: require all 72 full-budget fits and all 216 frozen decision
   views. Preserve step-zero selections and the recovered disk-reserve failure.
8. Look elsewhere: one registered primary contrast; secondary intervals are
   descriptive and not multiplicity-adjusted discoveries. No p-value is claimed.
9. Forking paths: registration precedes fitting, training manifests precede
   decisions, and decision hashes precede readout. Earlier adaptive exposure is
   still a limitation and is not erased by this experiment's registration.
10. Causation: the paired computational control tests checkpoint selection in
    this pipeline. It does not identify a unique real-world failure mechanism.
11. Reverse causality: outcomes supervise source-only training/validation and
    later frozen evaluation, never causal features or inference eligibility.

Coverage: 11/11 interpretation risks considered, not 11 assumptions proved.
All evidence remains image-local detector-silver, obs8/pred12 at stride12 raw
frames. No metric, seconds, human-gold, true-3D, foundation or physical-safety
claim. Stage5C execution and SMC stay off.
