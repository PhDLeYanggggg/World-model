## Failure Taxonomy And Next Action

- The finite minibatch estimator/region-coverage defect was real enough to test,
  but fixing within-region gradients did not recover fitting EH costs or utility.
- Exact gradients are not exact optimization of all regions at once,convergence,
  representation sufficiency or decision calibration. Shared output constraints,
  cost weighting,information limits and nonconvexity remain possible contributors.
- The causal router still lacks a robust positive neural increment;lower known
  easy violations do not establish scientific success.
- Additional same-family weight/threshold sweeps are not justified by this result.

Before another routing loss,quantify the frozen neural candidate's marginal
known-ADE opportunity above a per-query physical oracle. Existing feasible
oracle checks establish support,not the size or distribution of that marginal
gain. A new labeled diagnostic can distinguish limited candidate opportunity
from a remaining causal-routing problem. It will explicitly use future labels
only for diagnosis,not export oracle decisions as features or learned results.
That next diagnostic is not claimed complete here.
