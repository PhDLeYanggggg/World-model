# Frozen Forecast Tail: Causal Information Test

This is an adaptive, exposed own-B fitting diagnostic after the negative
partition study, not independent confirmation, a new trajectory predictor,
or a deployment experiment. The original 2% all/easy positive-harm, easy-net
and support requirements stay unchanged. No identity exemption, threshold
search, new C/H readout, reserved data, Stage5C or SMC.

Question: are high positive easy-channel excess costs concentrated in particular
candidate amplitudes, sources or causal motion regimes, and can past-only
information rank these costly forecasts on an excluded fitting source?

Reuse the fixed one-proposal bank, original source/query weights, available
labels and summary120 inputs from the preceding probe. Unknown futures remain
unknown and are counted. For each of eight source-held folds fit one64-tree
depth8/minleaf32 ExtraTrees model, seed17, original settings. Only its target
changes: positive parts of the original all/easy signed budget excesses rather
than signed means. Compare cached original signed-risk predictions and a
nonlearned candidate-minus-floor maximum path displacement score. Forecasts
remain frozen; metadata, action/source IDs and all outcome-derived fields are
excluded from the model features. Preprocessing and target RMS use fitting
sources only. This is a conventional positive-target regression probe, not a
novel loss or calibrated upper bound. Earlier hurdle/neural cost experiments
remain negative; this narrower same-feature test isolates tail ranking.

Report positive/negative cost mass by source, selected amplitude, and causal
motion quartile. Quartile cuts use *all proposed causal rows in fitting sources*,
before outcome availability filtering; attribution uses only known outcomes.
They are descriptive, not deployment rules. Costs are available-label ADE
budget excesses divided by the existing causal row scale, not raw prediction
errors, event probabilities, metres or seconds.

Ranking diagnostics use top1/5/10/20% of original query-balanced weight,
separately within each excluded source. A tied score gets fractional credit
uniformly across the tied mass, never a label-informed tie-break. This is NOT
an implementable selected subset or a safety certificate. Label-sorted ranking
is marked oracle_diagnostic and never supplied to the model. No positive mass
means NA, not zero or pass. Source-macro means retain that NA.

Fixed information screen: at the primary10% weight budget, positive-tail
regression captures >=50% of easy positive-excess mass and exceeds both signed
risk and envelope ranking by >=5 percentage points, in at least2/3 contexts.
These constants decide whether to investigate the tail branch further; they
do not replace any original scientific evaluation metric or risk gate. All
fractions, sources and controls are reported regardless of this screen.

One producer seed, dependent overlapping windows, exposed development and
partial labels limit inference. No bootstrap claim from rows. The only new fits
are eight risk-information models, not eight independent trajectory trials.
Pilot, per-fold checkpoints, explicit resume, heartbeat, exact full refits and
read-only replay record engineering reproducibility separately from science.
Local CPU4/workers0 is sufficient based on completed same-size experiments.

Prior routes checked: amplitude-bank learning, convex amplitude risk decoders,
paired/sign cost objectives, peer-path information and cost-weighted partition
learning. Do not repeat their negative results as a new discovery. This study
must distinguish empirical tail concentration from causal predictability; a
negative ranking result alone cannot prove irreducible uncertainty or that
all candidate predictors must fail.
