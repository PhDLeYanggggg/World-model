## Diagnosis Supported Here

The 64-field motion basis reduces full fitting MSE more in all three contexts,
yet worsens fitting policy utility and does not improve aggregate held utility.
Known easy-risk excess on admitted actions is still underpredicted on fitting
data. Generalization shift compounds, but does not originate, the problem.
This rules out treating lower mean loss as sufficient repair evidence.

## Next Controlled Model Intervention

easy reference support and admission status, using only fitting sources.
Separate unknown-outcome bounds from observed errors. Check whether dangerous
negative predicted excess has too little influence on optimization compared
with rejected high-risk actions. This is a diagnostic of the learning target,
not a new evaluation endpoint or validation threshold search.

If that imbalance is confirmed, register one controlled objective change:
retain the full regression loss but add a fixed training penalty for observed
positive budget excess predicted below zero, with a matched original-loss
control. Keep architecture, preprocessing, source roles, sampled queries,
initial weights and update budget identical. Do not select the favorable
context or change the zero-excess admission rule. Existing failed quantile,
hurdle, proposal-conditioned and selected-set calibration controls must be
compared explicitly before treating this as a distinct experiment.

This intervention is not run, is not a proposed novel theorem, and is not
guaranteed to work. Coefficients and the falsifiable expansion condition must
be fixed before its held-source readout. A further failed minimal contrast
must not automatically expand to all directions or be called model progress.
