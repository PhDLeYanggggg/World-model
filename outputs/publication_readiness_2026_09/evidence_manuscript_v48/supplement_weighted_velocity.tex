\section{Weighted Velocity and Reference Disagreement}
A network estimates positive reliability weights for eight past
observations. A weighted slope gives a full-strength path anchored
at the last position. At \WeightedVelocityEdits{} matched edits,
adding dual-reference risk and missingness loss to ADE yields
\WeightedVelocityDual{} (95\% source interval) and passes
\WeightedVelocityRiskPass/72 original risk checks. Its whole-roster
lower gain over OLS4 is \WeightedVelocityWhole{}. None of the
inner calibrations qualifies. Adding the paths to the full bank
retains \WeightedVelocityOld{} older witnesses and verifies
\WeightedVelocityNew{} additional views, with
\WeightedVelocityUnknown{} unresolved.

On wholly unlabeled rows, the sum of the two disagreement debits
satisfies $d_\infty(P,C)+d_\infty(P,O)\geq d_\infty(C,O)$
for candidate $P$, CV $C$, and OLS4 $O$. On this roster the
CV-normalized source mean of that unavoidable sum is
\WeightedVelocityFloor{} pp. The matched dual head exceeds the
floor by only \WeightedVelocityExcess{} pp. Even removing that
excess at unchanged observed gain leaves a sum of
\WeightedVelocityOptimistic{} pp, precluding both conservative
gains being positive for that hypothetical aggregate. This is a
property of the registered score, not a statement about true
unobserved motion or independent performance.
