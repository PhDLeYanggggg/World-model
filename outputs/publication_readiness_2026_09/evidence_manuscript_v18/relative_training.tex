\paragraph{Training on relative paths.}
A separate matched objective ablation keeps the local
architecture, training draws and forecaster seeds fixed.
Both arms include the same edge endpoints in individual ADE;
the treatment adds a weighted relative-path loss only at
steps with both labels present. At the original recipients,
the signed relative-path error reduction is
\PairTrainedObserved{} pixels on observed steps and
\PairTrainedEnd{} pixels at observed endpoints. The
full-grid lower contrast is \PairTrainedLower{} pixels,
and the individual conservative contrast is
\PairTrainedUtility{} pp. Both arms pass
\IndividualPass{} and \PairTrainedPass{} recording checks,
respectively. A small observed geometry benefit does not
establish conservative improvement or deployment eligibility.
