# Failure Analysis

## Rejected Repair
The parent study left open whether temporal information was present but
suppressed by the frozen all-harm cap. The new fixed counterfactual does not
support that repair. Cap relaxation has no positive primary intervals for
any of the four input arms in either forecast family. History-neighbor remains
inferior to the old summaries even under matched relaxed projections.

The current cap therefore has a protective effect for these particular fixed
scores in many source views. This is not an optimality proof, a safety
certificate, evidence of irreducible error, or a rejection of all sequence
models. Frozen coefficients were fitted under the parent's unconstrained
residual loss; a separately designed estimator could behave differently.

## Error Accounting
Relaxation can reduce error on realized positive easy-harm labels while
raising predictions on the more numerous zero labels. In141/216 dependent
full-input history-neighbor views, the event contribution improves but total
MSE worsens. This statement follows an exact additive squared-error
decomposition, not a story inferred from a correlation or a pointwise oracle.
The zero-label group contains both non-easy agents and easy agents without
harm; the current summaries do not separate these groups. That distinction
must not be invented or used as an inference label.

In full input, the median across dependent views changes from0.04605 capped
easy-harm MSE to0.04638 envelope MSE. Corresponding predicted easy-harm mean
medians rise from0.02804 to0.03542. These descriptive medians are not the
registered paired effect or a pooled calibration statistic. The full paired
effect ranges from-11.82% to-0.20%; five intervals are negative.

Increasing predicted all-harm to match the raised easy-harm value improves
some all-harm results. Four of six full intervals are positive, and five of
six motion-only intervals are positive. These small gains do not validate
easy-harm prediction or the intervention policy. A multi-output repair cannot
be justified by selecting only the favorable task after readout.

## Remaining Explanations
Unreliable conditional membership, noisy detector-box motion, limited event
support and linear readout capacity remain plausible. The parent analysis
also retains a single-locality nuisance versus two-locality scoring-model
transport difference. This cap experiment does not identify which of those
causes dominates. It does rule out this simple projection relaxation as a
sufficient repair and removes a reason to keep sweeping cap parameters.

Past neighbor availability is not evidence of predictive interaction context.
The relaxed full neighbor increment has1 positive,3 negative and2 overlapping
primary intervals. The matched relaxed summary control is stronger than the
new contextual probe on every full point estimate and every motion-only
interval. Those negative ablations remain in the report.

## Interpretation Boundaries
The retrospective pointwise ceiling uses realized outcomes; a conditional
mean is not an upper bound on every event. No oracle-adjusted output is a
model result. Missing tail/coverage support is retained, not dropped. Windows,
outer-context repetitions and seeds do not multiply independent localities.
The source-development bootstrap cannot establish independent deployment.

No new fitting, threshold selection, trajectory result, metric/seconds,
physical-safety, human-gold, true3D or foundation claim. Stage5C and SMC stay off.
