\section{Generating query options at predicted risk budgets}
Keeping the analytic paths and shape predictions fixed, we add
integer roundings of maximin query LPs at six predicted risk and
support budgets. These budgets generate options, not evaluation
exceptions. Original/expanded option counts are
\RiskFrontierOptions{}. With outcomes and the original recording
checks, verified plans are \RiskFrontierVerified{}/72 for the original
menu, expanded menu, and unrestricted per-row choices, respectively
(\RiskFrontierUnresolved{} unrestricted searches unresolved).
The expanded-minus-original source-balanced verified rate is
\RiskFrontierCoverage{} pp (95\% paired source-bootstrap interval).

The frozen scorer selects a new option in \RiskFrontierNew{} query
views. At original query counts the expanded-minus-original
conservative contrast is \RiskFrontierCount{} pp. Against independent
selection at the expanded scorer's counts it is \RiskFrontierJoint{}
pp. Original/expanded risk passes are \RiskFrontierPasses{}/72;
the expanded worst signed easy change is \RiskFrontierWorst{}\%.
Calibration qualifies \RiskFrontierQual{}/9 cells. This separates
outcome-aware capacity from causal use of the new options.
