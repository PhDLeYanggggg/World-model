2026-09-24. Frozen before any European Squares forecast error or fit is opened.
Routine protocol/data-role decisions are delegated by the user. This does not
relax the primary SDD task, risk limit or confirmation protections.

## Role Assignment

Use the verified raw archive, replayed raw audit, locality manifest and bounded
pre-intake exposure search. Require completion and replay of the eight-frame
partial-clip screen before writing the role manifest. Any cross-locality match
blocks this version for review; no outcome-based file deletion is allowed.

Use the conservative 10 km source-location sensitivity grouping, in addition to
same-city and shared-camera rules. This is a locality isolation heuristic, not
proof of independent populations. Bind all recordings of a group to one role.
Date/metadata conflicts remain recording-provenance flags; raw archive member
and parsed row hashes, not conflicting statistics-table dates, identify rows.

Rank groups by decreasing K8/stride12 past-eligible count, tie by group key.
Within consecutive blocks of six groups, rank once by SHA256 of the fixed string
`m3w-european-squares-role-v1-2026-09-24|GROUP_KEY`. Assign in order:
source_training, risk_calibration_reserved, source_training,
risk_calibration_reserved, model_selection_reserved, confirmation_reserved.
This gives 12 training, 6 selection, 12 calibration and 6 confirmation groups
if the verified 10 km grouping has 36 groups. Stop if the grouping is not 36.
The blocks balance a coarse support scale, not prediction accuracy, country,
future completeness or difficulty. Do not try another seed or role ratio.

## Admission Boundary

Only source_training is opened here, for retrospective raw detector-track
support and subsequent source-only learning. All other roles remain locked to
predictive outcomes. Each later selection/calibration/final-readout experiment
needs a fixed versioned registration, frozen producers and matched masks first.
No teacher, scaler, goal, prototype or normalization may fit the reserved roles.
DroneCrowd remains separately closed confirmation. Existing exposed SDD sites
remain development-only. Unknown remote M3W assets are not imported.

There is no universal prior-exposure proof. The bounded audit searches the
pre-intake Git text and explicit local experiment identity receipts; it cannot
exclude unidentified binary/remote weights or the publisher detector's training
data. This qualification persists in any future claim. New predictors must fit
from verified fresh/source-only provenance; arbitrary pretrained weights are
not admitted by this role manifest.

The public raw detections can support a released-annotation forecasting task.
They do not establish a sensor-online ground-truth world state. No full-track
survival/length filtering, median smoothing or future-based interpolation is
used. Missing labels remain missing and never define input eligibility.
Publisher numeric class_id=0 is retained without inventing missing class text.

## Source Adapter Contract

Point: arithmetic bounding-box center in native image pixels, without the
publisher's median smoothing, homography or processed trajectory files.
Observed grid: eight samples at raw frame offsets [-84,-72,-60,-48,-36,-24,-12,0].
Requested future grid: twelve samples at offsets [12,24,...,144], supervision only.
This is an explicit external raw-index task, not common physical time across
SDD and European Squares and not an official published benchmark reproduction.
The primary SDD protocol remains unchanged. No seconds or meters claim.

Retain all agents visible at each sampled query; masks mark missing history.
Targets require complete past support only. Future support, track end and track
remaining length must not enter inputs, query sampling or target eligibility.
Interactions are image-plane proximity proxies, not physical collision labels.

The first source cache uses at most 256 unique query frames per recording,
uniformly spaced over the sorted past-supported query list. Keep every visible
agent at each selected frame and every past-eligible target, including unknown
future targets. Query overlap is retained and recorded, not counted as
independent evidence. This is a fixed engineering/source-support cohort, not a
full-medium result or an independent efficacy evaluation. Future sampling and
model experiments must bind this identity or explicitly register a new version.

## Claims That Remain Closed

Twelve reserved locality groups are not enough by themselves to certify a 2%
population risk constraint. Formal finite-sample claims require their actual
assumptions and bounds; a rejected policy must fall back, not relax the limit.
Exact-zero-reference allowed added harm stays zero and positive-easy degradation
stays <=2%. No new deployable model, model improvement, independent confirmation,
metric/seconds/true-3D/foundation claim follows from admission. No Stage5C or SMC.
