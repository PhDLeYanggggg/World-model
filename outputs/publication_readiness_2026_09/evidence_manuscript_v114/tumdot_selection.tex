\subsection*{Learned shape choice on TUMDOT development}
I keep the same six directions, recipients and radii on nine exposed
recordings from one campaign. Fitting excludes both the held
intersection and day. The first trial predicts conservative CV
gain; adding a short past backtest does not help its direct paired
ablation. The follow-up predicts gain directly against the fixed
hindcast, then guards against higher predicted all/easy harm.

Joint means positive against CV and OLS4 with the original risk
checks. The guarded arm improves the joint count but loses to
hindcast directly: \TumSelectEffect. Intervals resample only
three intersections and are descriptive. The outcome-aware
witness reaches \TumSelectOracle{} pp against CV at 27/27
joint passes. Quarter radius improves risk coverage, but no causal
arm has positive average CV lower utility. This is not confirmation.
