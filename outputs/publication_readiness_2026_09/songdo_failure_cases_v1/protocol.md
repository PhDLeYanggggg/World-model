# Explain three observed Songdo failure mechanisms

Use the already-scored Songdo confirmation and the frozen crossed
recipient/path forecasts. Use seed 17 only for the illustrations.
The cohort estimates and uncertainty in the paper still use all three
seeds and all 14 source components.

Choose cases by lower median of the stated rank within each category;
ties use the stable local member ordering. Do not choose extrema.

1. Recipient contrast: compare the original independent and joint
   proposals. Require different recipients, complete future labels on
   their union, a negative observed contrast and a recipient component
   whose magnitude is at least twice the path component. Rank by
   absolute observed contrast.
2. Path contrast: keep the independent recipients. Require the joint
   rule to change at least one path, with complete future labels for
   both edited vehicles. Rank by absolute observed contrast regardless
   of sign.
3. Missing-label contrast: compare the original independent and joint
   proposals. Require positive observed gain but negative conservative
   gain. Rank by the missing-label debit.

Use the verified scalar scores and frozen masks for selection. Join
numeric labels only for the selected examples, checking the endpoints
against their archived scores. Plot edit membership, observed target
steps and cumulative observed/lower/upper gain, with the original
whole-flight CV denominator. A missing-label band is not a confidence
interval. These are explanatory examples, not a frequency estimator
or evidence of generalization. No raw positions, absolute times,
vehicle IDs, or flight identities leave the local cache.
