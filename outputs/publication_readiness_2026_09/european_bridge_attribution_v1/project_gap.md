# Remaining World-Model and Paper Gaps

This study is complete; the research goal is not. M3W remains a development
track, not a submission-ready or foundation world model.

## Closed This Round

- Refit a motion-only controller instead of relying on a zero-shot module removal.
- Compare neural and ridge scoring on identical forecast pairs and matched query counts.
- Cross utility and risk heads; include deterministic hash ranking.
- Complete three seeds, full fixed readout, locality-bootstrap intervals, tail
  errors and negative attribution results. Freeze decisions before readout.

## Still Open

- Neural ranking does not consistently outperform ridge. Its model/loss contribution
  cannot currently be the main methodological claim.
- Existing neural trajectory access helps the easy subset but not robustly all/hard.
  No new dynamics learning was established in this cost-head experiment.
- Net easy preservation does not certify positive harm. Independent calibration
  remains unperformed;12 calibration localities and6 confirmation localities stay closed.
- Joint multi-agent control has no established benefit from the previous fixed
  comparison. Do not rebrand independent per-agent selection as joint reasoning.
- Public matched prediction-method comparisons, broader domains, reliable context
  contribution and independent confirmation are not completed by these six scenes.
- Released detector labels, pixel coordinates and unverified time/geometry prevent
  physical-world, metric/seconds or human-gold claims.

## Shortest Next Experiment

Before another architecture change, test whether moment miscalibration and
support mismatch account for the neural/ridge coverage difference. Use exposed
source/development roles for that diagnostic. Estimate scene-level calibration
power for the unchanged2% risk tolerance, then register the minimal frozen family
and independent-calibration procedure. Do not open confirmation during repair.

Keep real risk-control limits and abstentions visible. An honest statistical
limitation is preferable to another threshold chosen on repeatedly opened test
data. Deployment is unchanged; Stage5C and SMC remain prohibited.
