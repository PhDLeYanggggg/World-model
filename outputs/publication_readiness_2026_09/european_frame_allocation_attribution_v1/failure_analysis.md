# Neural-use signal does not rescue selected easy risk

## Completed evidence

Fresh inference and attribution on all72 fixed choice groups complete in
101.95s,PID25357,peak RSS10.91GB. Separate replay completes in118.48s,
PID26550,peak10.83GB. All72 legacy independent attributions,288 action hashes
and complete diagnostics agree exactly. No new model,selection or threshold.
The registration is explicitly post-main-readout and pre-attribution,not a
retroactive primary-test registration.

Thus the joint fitting effect is not merely a physical-action reshuffle.
However,neural deletion is NOT a reoptimized physical-only allocator. Removing
a reference-contributing action can change pooled risk,so the deleted policy
is not presented as a new safe deployment. This is not new dynamics training,
causal identification or independent neural superiority.

## The selected-risk problem is still specific and severe

Joint matched observes easy harm33,267.35 versus predicted429.77 over reused
local-image-coordinate views. These sums are bookkeeping,not physical or
cross-domain metrics. Across records with positive observed easy harm,the
median predicted/observed ratio is0.015205. Total all-channel harm can be
conservative in aggregate while this easy subchannel remains badly underestimated.

Among58,970 fully known selected query views,18,324 have positive observed
easy excess.17,728 of these are numerator-only arithmetic witnesses;32 are
denominator-only,326 require both replacements and238 only the joint replacement.
3,294 selected-unknown query views are separately retained in full policy scores.
Per-row excess is not automatically a false query promise:budget pooling
deliberately allows some individual positive predicted excess.

Compared with independent,the number of observed positive-excess easy row
views falls33,143 to30,447,yet observed easy harm bookkeeping rises19,255.86
to33,267.35 and recording easy violations rise308 to324. Counts alone conceal
severity. These witnesses do not prove a unique causal root cause or that the
current features are insufficient;they do justify focusing on selected-query
easy-harm magnitude instead of another global harm-weight increase.

## Decision and next control

Keep the main risk failure and no-deployment decision. First compare fully
reoptimized six-action and seven-action allocators using the physical independent
count as a common feasible current-query anchor. Then test a distinct selected
easy-risk repair after checking the already completed conditional/hurdle/deviance
experiments. Preserve external exposure and roles;DUT's historical readout
cannot become untouched test evidence by renaming it. No Stage5C or SMC.
