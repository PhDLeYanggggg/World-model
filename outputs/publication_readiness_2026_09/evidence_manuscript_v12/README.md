# Integrated Decision Manuscript, v12

This four-page anonymous working draft integrates the matched decision
study, the hindsight capacity comparison, the scene-input and relative-path
ablations, and the post-selection risk refits. It is a new manuscript build
from verified prior development evidence, not a new model run or an
independent external result.

At the same intervention budget, the joint method does not reliably improve
the conservative objective over the additive control. At 72 switches, exact
search still passes only four of 102 recording-seed views. Screening all
assignments before choosing raises the switch count to 2,194 and all-risk
violations from 12 to 40, without increasing the number of passing views.
The source-matched image comparison and the post-search risk refits likewise
do not establish a deployment gain. The manuscript reports these results
alongside the available headroom and a large observed easy-case failure.

The Moving A and own-B cohorts are exposed development evidence and are
never pooled. The independent DroneCrowd study uses 15 groups (40 recordings)
for calibration and 30 separate groups (72 recordings) for confirmation.
No independent result is included. The original 2% and reference-support
requirements still apply. No model promotion, Stage5C execution or SMC.
Forecasts are in image pixels and native annotation steps, not meters or
seconds. No true-3D or foundation-world-model claim is made.
