\section{Data and Experimental Protocol}
The native Moving A development roster contains 125,540
past-eligible agent windows in 1,613 queries, 34 recordings and
14 source groups. We retain partial and wholly unobserved futures.
The three forecast seeds repeat this roster. Earlier image experiments
use a distinct, exposed own-B development roster of 62,466 windows
in 24 recordings and eight source components. These roles overlap
globally and are not pooled or counted as independent tests.
All data are native annotation steps and image pixels.

\paragraph{Decisions.}
An action is one forecaster or the CV fallback for one agent. The
matched experiment keeps at most two genuine switches per query and
uses the same requested query count for all four decision rules.
The independent control ranks predicted gains by agent; the
shared-family control uses the same forecaster for all switches
within a query. The graph controls use the causal neighborhood
from the union of four-nearest-neighbor links at the last observation.
For an edge $(i,j)$, let $d_i$ denote the change from CV, and $r_{ij}$
the CV relative path minus its OLS4 counterpart. The quadratic
relative-path change is
\[
  \lVert r_{ij}+d_i-d_j\rVert^2-\lVert r_{ij}\rVert^2 .
\]
The additive arm omits the cross term in $d_i,d_j$ but retains
the same single-agent marginals. Predicted CV gain, positive harm,
reference error and visibility are estimated from causal trajectory
and forecast-disagreement features. A local screen uses the fitted
risk costs, not observed outcomes; all reported passes are evaluated
by the original recording rule. The screen does not imply a
finite-sample risk guarantee.

\paragraph{Role separation.}
Development model fits exclude the scored source for each comparison.
For thresholded post-search studies, seven sources fit the search
and risk heads, six disjoint sources choose operating thresholds,
and one source is held out. Search choices used to fit the risk head
are themselves cross-fitted within the seven sources. No fold is a
substitute for an independent, unexposed evaluation. The separate
DroneCrowd protocol reserves 15 exclusion groups (40 recordings)
for calibration and 30 groups (72 recordings) for confirmation.
Predictions for all calibration recordings must freeze before the
calibration scorer reads their future labels. A qualifying frozen
rule must meet all required recording checks and the registered
paired CV criterion before any one-shot confirmation proceeds.
The final confirmation comparisons and matched controls are fixed
ahead of its readout. This manuscript contains no external scores.
