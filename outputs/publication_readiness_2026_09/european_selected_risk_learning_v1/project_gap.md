# Project Gap After Controlled Risk-Head Training

## What Is Supported
The implementation now separates four coherent cost moments, a selected-group
training penalty and four intervention rules under matched forecasts. Real
Torch training, exact resume, source exclusion, independent error reductions
and three-seed locality uncertainty are executable.

Query-level allocation has a reproducible development advantage over
individual all/easy gates. A matched-query-count control retains some positive
evidence, but not in every setting. This is a narrower and more defensible
statement than claiming a new safe world dynamics model.

## What This Round Rejects
The added selected-group objective does not improve all-ADE accuracy. The
joint learned controller does not reliably beat the old neural rule and fails
conditional positive-harm constraints even while preserving net easy error.
Do not scale this loss, relax the 2% risk limit, select the best seed or open
confirmation to rescue the result.

## Immediate Repair Target
Retain the promising query-allocation mechanism. Completed posthoc accounting
separates numerator underprediction from denominator inflation and missing
future mass: at the worst locality, the supported easy-harm prediction is only
about 9.9% of actual harm, while unknown rows supply just 1.4% of predicted
easy mass. It does not create a label-aware inference rule.

The next intervention study should target conditional easy-harm occurrence
and severity, keep R/P forecasts and source-role boundaries fixed, and retain
mean/ridge and query-count-matched controls. Event-factorized costs or
importance-corrected rare-event sampling are hypotheses, not validated repairs.
Audit their B-only label frequency and held-source calibration before choosing
one factor to change. Register that choice before a new fit/readout.
Do not repeat already-negative generic
history-percentile rejection or another sweep on the six selection localities.

## Before a Main Paper Claim
Still needed: consistent advantage over strong simple controls under observed
harm constraints; separately frozen development transport; a feasible
independent calibration argument; independent confirmation; public strong
method comparisons and interaction-consistency evidence. Twelve reserved
calibration localities do not automatically certify a 2% unbounded ratio.
No silent clipping, tolerance change or reassignment of reserved roles.

The long-term goal remains active, not submission-ready. Routine engineering,
training and audits proceed under the user's delegation; formal submission
remains a separate action. Historical test-selected results stay exploratory.
This is still detector-derived image-pixel annotation-step work, not metric,
seconds, human-gold, physical safety, true3D or foundation success.
Stage5C and SMC remain off.
