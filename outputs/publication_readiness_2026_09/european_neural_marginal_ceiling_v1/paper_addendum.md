# Working-Paper Addendum: Candidate Opportunity And Causal Value

Following the negative complete-region training comparison,we measured whether
the fixed neural path adds candidate opportunity beyond a per-query physical
oracle. The future-informed minima use the same causal candidate bank and known
evaluation support,but their choices are explicitly not available to inference.
All48source/context groups replay exactly,without controller-seed duplication.

On exposed inner-held SOURCE data,the physical oracle improves known ADE over
the floor by32.396033%,versus33.899940%for the mixed oracle. The paired
marginal opportunity is1.503906percentage points,conditional95%locality interval
[0.951225,2.074855]. The fitting marginal is2.743738points. Hard held aggregation
is not estimable because two required views contain no fitting-defined hard
known queries;they are not removed to create a complete aggregate.

These quantities are future-informed opportunities,not learned performance.
Their nonnegativity is by construction. Moreover,the difference of physical
and mixed oracles does not bound the difference between their optimal causal
policies under uncertainty. A simple equilateral-triangle example has zero
oracle increment but a center forecast with smaller expected Euclidean error
than any available vertex forecast. Thus neither a large nor a small oracle
gap alone settles whether the neural path is useful to a causal decision maker.

The experiment narrows the next question to a matched causal expected-cost
comparison,rather than another loss-weight sweep or a claim of learned success
from an oracle. Existing binary forest negative results remain relevant but
cannot silently stand in for a different seven-action feature/target contract.
