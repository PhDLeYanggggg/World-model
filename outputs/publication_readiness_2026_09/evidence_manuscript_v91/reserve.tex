\section{A Causal One-Edit Reserve}

We make one optional additional slot available from
the start of every recording, without altering incumbent
edits. At the first qualifying query, the slot adds a
quarter-amplitude neural change. The easy rule uses a
frozen easy-probability head. The gain rule requires positive
predicted lower gain. The joint rule also requires a
predicted local support-and-gain event probability of at
least 0.25. The factorized follow-up uses the original easy
and gain thresholds jointly, with no retraining.

The easy rule changes lower utility by \ReserveSlotEasyBase{}
versus the incumbent. The gain rule improves it by
\ReserveSlotGainBase{}, but versus CV gives \ReserveSlotGainCV{}.
The joint rule changes it by \ReserveSlotJointBase{}.
At identical reserve timing, factorization yields
\ReserveSlotFactorEasy{} over the easy recipient and
\ReserveSlotFactorGain{} over the gain recipient. The
full factorized policy yields \ReserveSlotFactorCV{} versus
CV. Post-hoc zero-edit views are not used to trigger the
slot; the easy rule repairs \ReserveSlotZeroEasy{} of them
and factorization \ReserveSlotZeroFactor{}.
Improved support does not establish net utility or a
full-roster pass. The original fixed-budget experiment
and 2\% risk rule remain unchanged.
