## Hypothesis And Single Change

The public deferral study improved mean routing utility without meeting the
original risk contract. A candidate-pool cost model may misestimate the actions
that a different router proposes. Test supervision restricted to those actions,
instead of supervising every nonfloor action. This is a new development test,
not a proven remedy or new general safety guarantee.

For each of18 frozen contexts and three existing inner-C parts, freeze a public
router A, train a point cost critic on a different part B, and evaluate on the
third part V. There are six ordered A/B pairs per context:108 new critics.
Router A's causal logits define exactly one supervised action on each B row
where A proposes a nonfloor switch. All B rows and complete queries remain in
the sampler. A fallback row has no cost supervision, not a zero-harm label.
Unknown selected outcomes retain the original envelope harm bounds. No label
availability, future endpoint or validation outcome can define proposals.

The model, initialization, own-B preprocessing, five moment targets (B,H,R,ER,EH),
RMS, optimizer, sampler draws, and2000-update budget match frozen all-action
point replica0 on B. Query averaging and all-source coverage remain unchanged.
The effective supervised-action support changes by design; it is not matched
label exposure. The control is cached_verified, not newly retrained. Shared48
latent slots stay zero. No new auxiliary features or loss coefficients.

## Frozen Rules

Keep public router A, all forecast amplitudes and original per-action2%positive
harm/easy-harm veto. Both old and new critics can only retain or reject A's
proposal. No threshold search, scene exclusion, identity fallback certification,
support relaxation, new H access, or test-driven model selection. Repeated old
development exposure is disclosed. All108 full-budget models freeze before
readout. No seed, orientation or validation view is selected afterward.

Primary: new-veto versus its original within-query same-count/same-amplitude
matched utility control, retaining empty controls as NA. Report paired direct
new-veto minus old-veto known effect and direct missing-outcome bounds; do not
subtract marginal bound intervals. Keep full original risk, easy-net and support
requirements on all696 required recording views. The older24group primary is
still not estimable, not passed. Physical floor is not analytically certified.

Secondary: source-macro available-label ADE, available endpoint FDE diagnostic,
hard/easy, intervention, old/new critic cost reliability on identical proposed
actions and on each admitted subset. Keep23source units and all276 repeated
source/context/orientation views. Use the inherited3000source bootstrap after
seed/context/orientation averaging. This is not IID independent test evidence.
