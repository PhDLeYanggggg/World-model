\paragraph{Larger menus and missing-label debits.}
Appending fixed OLS4-centered causal blends, then learned blends,
yields \BlendCausal{} and \BlendNeural{} verified views, respectively.
To separate the effect of the two missing-label debits in the
native bank, we omit the utility debit only, the positive-harm
debit only, or both, while retaining the reference support,
signed easy check, available-step errors and exact query counts.
These counterfactuals verify \NoUtilityDebit{}, \NoRiskDebit{}
and \NoBothDebit{} views, respectively. All views are resolved,
and \StillFailNoDebits{} still fail with both debits omitted.
Setting a debit to zero does not recover ground truth; these
counts are diagnostic and do not replace the original rule.
