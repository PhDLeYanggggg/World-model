# Better Fitting Did Not Establish Safe Held-Scene Utility

## What The Experiment Establishes

This is a complete real Torch experiment, not a quick probe. All 36 heads
finish 116,160 primary optimizer updates, with 4,000 first full-refit updates
and a 1,200-update recovery pilot. The frozen feature cache took about two hours
to build and another two hours to re-extract exactly. Every new prediction pair
and all 72 metric groups reproduce exactly. The two engineering failures and
their corrections remain in the record; current fitted IDs and normalizers
are verified to use critic G only.

The interaction feature bundle improves mean full-bound fitting objective from
1.498058 to 1.326343, about 11.5%. The known-only fitting objective also falls
from 1.008933 to 0.904304. This is real fitting improvement, not proof of useful
held-scene intervention or physical world-modeling ability.

## The Primary Remains Unsupported

Interaction64 minus Zero64 gives known-label gain +0.4800 pp, with locality
bootstrap CI [-0.1007, +1.1025]. The registered paired completion-lower effect
is -0.0213 pp [-0.6148, +0.6482]. Both intervals cross zero. This is inconclusive,
not evidence that the latent is certainly useless or significantly harmful.
The positive upper effect cannot replace the unsupported lower primary.

Against the older 444-input direct control, the paired lower effect is
-0.6425 pp [-1.1074, -0.0951], while its separate known-label effect remains
inconclusive. This is not an isolated capacity-matched encoding contrast.
Zero64 itself loses known utility to that older head; a weak new control must
not be used to announce an upgrade to the previous best.

## Safety Is A Larger Failure Than The Mean

The feature policy has +3.3501% mean known gain over its producer-fit-selected
floor and +4.2613% mean easy gain. However, only 110/444 repeated recording/pipeline
views support the complete original contract. Known all/easy violations are
109/229; completion all/easy violations are 151/330, with four undefined easy
views. Worst signed easy-net degradation is 39.3712%, far above 2%.

The old direct control passes 108 views and Zero64 passes 93. Moving these counts
slightly does not repair the failed complete policy. The 444 views repeat only
74 recordings across producer/critic/seed pipelines, not 444 independent scenes.
Mean easy improvement must not hide a severe worst-recording loss.

## Reference Choice And Missing Endpoints Matter

Under the registered equal-locality mean of CV-relative ratios, Interaction64
is -2.1850% versus constant velocity, with a wide interval crossing zero. Its
lower mean pixel ADE alone does not establish a win under that ratio metric;
an average of ratios differs from a ratio of averages. The producer-fit-selected
floor is not retrospectively replaced using held outcomes. A future fixed-CV
reference experiment would need a separate prospective definition, not a
relabeling of this failed original contract.

The unchanged whitelist is highly uneven. Localities 008 and 074 contribute
137,731 of 149,510 windows, while 082 has only 78 windows, including 16 wholly
unknown futures, no complete 12-step future and no valid final endpoint. The
all-locality endpoint interval is therefore not estimable. These support counts
are posthoc diagnostics; no locality is removed, future is filled, or horizon
changed to improve the result. Other recordings also fail risk, so 082 alone
does not explain the complete failure.

## Causes Still To Distinguish

The previous frozen-head diagnosis found selected easy-harm underprediction on
both fitting and held queries. That measurement belongs to the older heads;
it must be recomputed on the new ones before attributing the current failure
to the same mechanism. The immediate decisive diagnostic is fit-versus-held
selected H/EH error and R/ER support on identical selected sets, for all current
heads and seeds, with no optimizer updates or new threshold choices.

A second hypothesis comes from the input code, not an outcome experiment:
`pack_scene` centers positions at the current agent, and detector64 contains
box sizes, confidences, differences and overlaps, not absolute viewport position.
The current interaction encoder shares that relative-input limitation. Camera
boundary or annotation-visibility context could matter to future label support,
but its omission is not yet demonstrated to cause these errors. Any such test
needs past-only, independently verified viewport metadata; future trajectory
extrema must never become scene bounds or inference features.

The h64 bundle also differs in raw neighbor coverage from the current 444 fields.
Neither nonconstant features nor better fitting proves an isolated encoding
contribution. If a later result becomes positive, a raw-original-context control
is still needed. No additional hidden width, generic loss factorization or
global alpha sweep is justified merely by the current negative primary.

## Decision

Do not deploy the new head, change the original risk threshold, or open reserved
confirmation data for repair. SOURCE remains exposed development. These results
do not establish a new scene-joint contribution, JEPA lift, neural dynamics
upgrade, independent confirmation, or CCF-A submission readiness. They identify
a more precise next experiment while preserving all earlier negative results.
Stage5C execution and SMC remain disabled.
