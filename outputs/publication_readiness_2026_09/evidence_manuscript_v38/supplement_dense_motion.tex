\section{Broader Motion and Predicted Risk Feasibility}
On the same opened calibration roster, we fix both CV and OLS4 paths
and learn normalized gain, positive harm and reference mass for four
nonidentity interpolation levels. The heads use only eight observed
positions and internal backtests. Three models fit on the original
nine fitting groups, never on the calibration outcomes. An independent
gain arm has no intervention count cap. A recording-level price ladder
chooses a predicted feasible nonempty subset, or retains CV.

The price ladder selects no changes, although every view contains
an individually feasible positive-gain action under the estimated
moments. To repair the search without refitting, we select one such
action per eligible row. Adding the per-row predicted constraints
preserves the two ratios, with denominators still positive. All
120 views now intervene, but only \DenseUnionPass{} pass on actual
outcomes. The past-hindcast control assigns the same amplitude
multiset in each query, but uses its own causal recipient order.
The repaired learned choice is worse than that control
(\DenseUnionMatched{} pp, 95\% source interval \DenseUnionCI).
This isolates estimated feasibility from achieved safety and
does not establish a deployable broad motion rule.
