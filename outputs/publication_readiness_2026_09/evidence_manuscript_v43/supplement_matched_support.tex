Conditional versus marginal record allocation gives
\MatchSupportDirect{} pp (95\% paired source interval
\MatchSupportInterval). It overestimates selected easy reference
in \MatchSupportConditionalOver{} rather than
\MatchSupportMarginalOver{} views. Keeping actions fixed and
substituting actual reference mass for the estimate yields no
ratio passes. Neither is a causal counterfactual policy.

At fixed recipients, minimum observed ADE and, on wholly missing
rows, minimum maximum disagreement minimize both CV-relative risk
numerators. Denominators do not change. This hindsight risk optimum
passes \MatchSupportFixedRecipient/72 for the conditional record
arm; it is not a dual-reference utility optimum.
