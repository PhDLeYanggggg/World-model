\section{Training Against Missing-Step Disagreement}

Unlike imputing missing history before prediction~\cite{trajimpute},
we keep complete histories and address missing \emph{future} labels.
For candidate $p$, reference $b$ and observed set $V$, let
\[
 R_b(p)=\sum_{t\in V}(\|p_t-y_t\|-\|b_t-y_t\|)
       +\sum_{t\notin V}\|p_t-b_t\|.
\]
The second term is the supremum of excess Euclidean error over each
unobserved target; the triangle inequality supplies the upper bound,
and setting that target to $b_t$ attains it. It is not a pseudo-label,
a bound on physical risk, or a distribution-free calibration
guarantee~\cite{crc}.

We fit the same ego-only comoving EqMotion adaptation under three
objectives: observed error alone, mean CV completion regret, and the
larger of the mean CV and OLS4 completion regrets within each batch.
Regrets use a past-only scale. All arms see the same six training
sites per held OpenDD location, batch draws (including rows without
future labels), initialization, and updates. This gives 63 fits for
seven exposed locations and three paired seeds. No labels or future
masks are passed to the predictor at inference.
