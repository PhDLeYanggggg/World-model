\section{Two-Role Causal Stopping}

Both role clocks can change. The fixed neural bank proposes
up to two recipients per role from the current query. A
past-only head estimates the value of a current action, its
best two-query completion, and its advantage over waiting.
Training-only labels score feasible plans under the original
recording rule against CV and OLS4. The nomination heads
exclude both the fitting donor and evaluation sites. The
matched earliest control retains exactly the realized roles
in each recording, but not the realized radii.

At \DualClockWaitEdits{} matched edits, learned waiting gives
\DualClockWaitEffect{} and passes \DualClockWaitJoint{} views
versus \DualClockWaitMatchJoint{}. A no-refit follow-up uses
an expiring two-query credit for each role. This raises
coverage to \DualClockDeadlineEdits{} edits, but its matched
contrast is \DualClockDeadlineEffect{}, with
\DualClockDeadlineJoint{} versus \DualClockDeadlineMatchJoint{}
joint passes. There is no end-of-record forcing in either arm.
The exact outcome-aware search on the same nominations and
the original role counts verifies \DualClockCapacity{}/606
views. These results separate capacity from the ability to
learn where and when to intervene.
