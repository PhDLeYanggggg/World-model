\section{Matched-Radius Dual-Reference Direction Selection}

At the same 943 interventions, time, recipient and peak
displacement are fixed. The neural shape is compared with
OLS4 and an observed-prefix analytic shape. Two matched
heads learn shape contrasts against the original neural edit:
one uses CV alone, the other uses CV and OLS4 and maximizes
the smaller predicted improvement. Both see double-excluded
fitting forecasts, with no held-site threshold selection.

The dual-reference lower contrast against the CV-only head is
\DualDirPrimary{}, but against the frozen neural edit it is
\DualDirFrozen{}. Removing the highest-contributing site gives
\DualDirOmit{} for the primary contrast; a site sign-flip
diagnostic gives $p=\DualDirSignP{}$ under symmetry.
The CV-only and dual heads pass \DualDirCVPass{} and
\DualDirDualPass{} views, respectively, versus
\DualDirNeuralPass{} for the frozen neural rule.
Predicted-risk guarding passes \DualDirGuardPass{}; joint
choice at the \DualDirCoview{} co-observed role pairs yields
the same predictions. The fixed-recipient hindsight shape
menu admits \DualDirOraclePass{} witnesses. No shape can
recover selected easy reference when the selected rows are
held fixed. These are development results, not deployment evidence.
