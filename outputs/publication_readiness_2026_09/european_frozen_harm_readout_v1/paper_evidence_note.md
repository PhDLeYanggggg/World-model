# Paper Evidence Note

## Defensible Development Finding

Under matched frozen-representation readout training, fractional supervision
leaves detectable information about harm ranking but does not establish
better conditional expected easy-harm magnitude. The experiment includes
all six source assignments, three seeds, locality-excluded preprocessing and
original controls. It is a negative mechanism/control result suitable for
the research record or an eventual limitation/ablation discussion.

## Claims Not Supported

This is not a new trajectory-dynamics gain, independent cross-dataset
confirmation, calibrated intervention guarantee, novel JEPA/Transformer
architecture or evidence of publication readiness. Better AUROC and average
harm coverage cannot stand in for the failed primary expected-cost endpoint.
Four-locality bootstrap intervals are exploratory; repeated assignments are
not six independent experiments.

## References and Reading Scope

The registration cites Alain and Bengio's linear-probe diagnostic at the
original-abstract level. This experiment uses a bounded regression readout,
not their classification setting. No claim about novelty or a theoretical
safety guarantee is derived from that citation.
