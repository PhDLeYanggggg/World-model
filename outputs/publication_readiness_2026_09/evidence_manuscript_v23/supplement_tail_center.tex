\paragraph{Tail-center stress test.}
We hold out contiguous released positions once per eligible track and
length in the unique recording-agent-step ledger. For \GapProbeTailGaps\ paired
twelve-step terminal gaps, we keep the same radius and hide the same
positions, but change the center from constant extension to a last-four
OLS velocity extrapolation anchored at the last retained position.
Whole-gap coverage rises from \GapProbeTailConstant\% to \GapProbeTailOLS\% (difference
$\GapProbeTailDelta$ points; source-bootstrap interval $[\GapProbeTailDeltaCI]$).
The pseudo-gaps are not a model of actual missingness; the coverage
checks a pointwise corridor, not the shared-direction witness family.
Re-solving the witness problem with the new center on \GapProbeTailMoved\ of
\GapProbeTailSteps\ requested missing terminal positions, the reference
gives $\GapProbeTailReference$ pixels and the two feasible $1\times$ completions
give $\GapProbeTailLow$ and $\GapProbeTailHigh$ pixels. The reversal is therefore not
specific to fixed-position terminal extrapolation. No releases,
forecasts, intervention counts or official thresholds change.
