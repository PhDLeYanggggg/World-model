## Question

The nine fully trained EqMotion forecasts improve observed SOURCE accuracy even
against current history-PCT. The previous cost critics already underestimate
selected harm on fitting data, and width alone does not repair them. Before
training another critic, ask whether bounded amplitude alone can retain useful
neural gain while satisfying the original recording-risk contract.

Use the unchanged 149,510-window controller SOURCE whitelist, all its recordings,
all twelve SOURCE localities, two excluded producer folds per locality and actual
seeds17/29/43. Compare both frozen EqMotion and actual current history-PCT with
the same producer-fitting-selected causal floor. No new role split or primary
final-evaluation definition is introduced. SOURCE is exposed development.

For every row and fixed alpha, predict floor+alpha*(forecast-floor). Alpha grid:
0,.001,.005,.01,.02,.05,.1,.25,.5,1. No row is removed for history quality, future
availability, zero errors, unfavorable scene or unknown future. Exact trajectory
aliases remain no intervention. For positive amplitudes the intended active
support stays fixed; numerical aliases are counted, not silently forced active.
No clipping, label-dependent per-row amplitude or learned decision is used.

The fixed quarter-amplitude comparison is the focal mechanism check: report
its known and completion-lower gains versus floor, its paired EqMotion-minus-PCT
effects and complete recording risk. All other amplitudes are retained as a
descriptive curve. This is not a grid search selecting a deployment threshold;
even a favorable curve point is not promoted to an independent result.

Preserve the original 2% positive-harm/reference, easy-harm/easy-reference and
signed easy-net requirements in every recording, including unknown-row upper
bounds and finite support. Zero intervention has no added harm but undefined
selected reference is not a supported learned-policy success. Explicitly count
known-harm failures, additional unknown-radius failures and undefined support.
Keep full-population and selected-set denominators distinct. Current controls,
easy/hard cutoffs and floor choice stay fixed from their original fitting sites.

Compute available-label ADE, observed endpoint-only FDE diagnostic, tails,
easy/hard scores, active rate, disagreement mass and per-record risk for both
families at every amplitude. Primary uncertainty uses 3,000 locality bootstrap
resamples after averaging excluded folds and actual seeds. Overlapping windows
are not independent pedestrians. Bounds cover wholly unknown rows only and do
not identify missing steps inside partial futures or full12-step ADE.

outcome reductions. Resume completed group files only with exact bindings. A
