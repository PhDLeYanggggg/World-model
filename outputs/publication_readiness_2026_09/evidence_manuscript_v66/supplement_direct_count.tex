\paragraph{Learning action moments instead of one plan.}
We use the same frozen nested nominations and chronological credits as
the count experiment. Both models predict two incremental conservative
gains, signed all/easy excess, easy mass and net easy change, with
additional binary event targets. The motion arm zeroes the added
channels; the peer arm observes the two nearest histories per nominee
in heading-aligned relative coordinates. The same architecture and
predeclared score are used, with no held-outcome threshold selection.
Removing the risk penalties gives \DirectCountMotionGain{} joint
passes for motion and \DirectCountPeerGain{} for peers, versus
\DirectCountMotion{} and \DirectCountPeer{} with penalties. All-risk
excess AUROCs are \DirectCountMotionRisk{} and \DirectCountPeerRisk{};
easy-excess AUROC is undefined in three location--seed cells, so no
whole-roster average is given. Both exact-count retrospective policies
make \DirectCountMatchedEdits{} edits; the paired peer minus motion
effect is \DirectCountGap{} pp [\DirectCountLow{}, \DirectCountHigh{}].
These exposed-location results do not establish a transferable benefit.
