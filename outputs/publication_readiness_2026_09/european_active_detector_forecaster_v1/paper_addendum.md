## Question And Controlled Design

cost learner, rather than an earlier related forecasting backbone? Code and

partial-neighbor, dimensionless producer. The preceding flat complete-neighbor

## Partial Labels And Claim Boundary

There are91,609complete,54,738partial and3,163wholly unknown unique futures.
A separate exact raw audit found no importer omission or recoverable exact
labels. Completion-lower history-minus-disabled is -18.258pp,

The observations support neither a stable added-input contribution on the
actual active backbone nor a safe deployable neural upgrade. A better mean
proposal is different from a learned policy meeting every recording's2%
all/easy positive-harm budget. No new controller is trained by this producer
experiment, and empty selected risks remain not_estimable, not safe zero.
