## 1. The Original Backbone Mismatch Is Now Repaired

The previous 72-model input study used a flat complete-neighbor SOURCE
forecaster. The actual routing bank uses the later grouped, partial-neighbor,
dimensionless forecaster. The two studies cannot establish the same matched
input contribution by sharing fold names and parameter counts.

The present experiment trains all nine actual parents with four input arms,
preserving original masks, loss, normalization, sampling and 4,000-step budget.
All nine disabled full scientific states and fresh predictions reproduce the
actual parents. The first history model also has an exact 4,000-step refit.
The result is therefore not explained by an untested substitute backbone,
a shortened probe, a NumPy training fallback or a healthy process mistaken
for completed model training.

## 2. A Positive Mean Is Not A Stable Added-Input Effect

Known masked-ADE gain versus the own-fitting selected reference is 9.326%
without detector input and 10.486% with history. The increment is +1.160pp,
95% locality-bootstrap interval [-0.355,+2.540]. History-minus-noise is
+1.483pp, interval [-0.149,+2.934]. Neither supports a stable positive
increment over its respective input control across this locality roster.

History does beat current-only by +2.052pp, interval [+0.863,+3.129]. However,
the current-only point estimate is below the disabled parent; beating that
control alone does not establish an improvement over the actual original model.
The primary all-control proposal gate is retained and fails, with 3/8 checks
passing. No more favorable secondary control replaces the primary.

Hard-subset history-minus-disabled is +1.547pp, interval [-0.055,+2.967].
This is also not a stable positive increment under the fixed locality resamples.
These intervals are conditional development evidence, not independent-test
coverage after extensive historical model development.

## 3. Spatial Variation Is Visible Despite Positive Seed Averages

The three seed-averaged known increments are +0.981pp, +0.996pp and +1.504pp.
Nine of twelve locality increments are positive, but locality007 is -3.314pp,
082 is -4.176pp and 124 is -0.385pp. The average seed sign therefore does not
eliminate between-locality failure. No locality is removed to tighten the CI.

This observation does not prove a causal explanation such as a particular
detector shift or inadequate capacity. Those remain hypotheses requiring
controlled input/target or policy experiments, not conclusions inferred from
one aggregate contrast.

## 4. Easy-Case Damage Remains An Obstacle

Raw history known-easy ADE gain is -12.222%, interval [-40.664,+5.842]. This
is a point-estimated 12.222% degradation relative to the causal reference,
before any newly learned safety policy. The disabled parent is already weak
on this subset (-9.003%), and adding history changes it by -3.219pp,
interval [-11.130,+1.650]. It does not solve the inherited easy-case problem.

Raw history easy gain at locality082 is -157.308% and at124 is -21.423%.
At124, history-minus-disabled easy change is -40.981pp. Percentage changes
depend on their reference-error mass and are not physical displacement units.
The small/partial-label locality082 stays in the roster with its limited
support. Large percentages are not evidence of metres, seconds or physical
collision risk.

The trajectory objective averages fitting-weighted ADE. It does not directly
optimize the approved positive-harm ratio on every required recording. A motion
bound does not imply that selected easy-reference harm stays below 2%.
These results reject direct deployment of the raw proposal; they do not claim
that a new learned fallback policy has been tested and failed in this run.

Across444repeated recording/producer views,6known-record mean changes and13
known-record p95 changes are worse than the reference by over2%. The worst
mean gain is -12.954% and worst p95 gain -25.042%. These are descriptive net
error/tail comparisons, not the approved positive-harm risk test; repeated
views are not444independent recordings. Exactly stationary ego histories
are absent, so the retained stationary-motion architectural bound cannot
explain these observed failures.

## 5. The Strong Fixed-Baseline Comparison Also Limits The Claim

The fixed damping0.97 candidate gains 6.436% against the original fitting
reference. The paired history-minus-damping0.97 contrast is +4.050pp,
interval [-1.214,+8.322], on that same original reference-mass denominator.
Stable superiority over all six strong causal alternatives is not established.
The six physical comparisons were specified before scoring, not added as a
way to choose a favorable method after seeing the result.

## 6. Missing Labels Are An Independent Evidence Limit

The same 149,510 allowed queries include 91,609 complete, 54,738 partial
and 3,163 wholly unknown futures. The exact raw audit found no importer
omission or recoverable exact labels. Most missing requested points follow
an ID's last supplied detection, not the entire recording's last detection.
These are export-support descriptions, not verified physical exits.

History-minus-disabled completion-lower change is -18.258pp, interval
[-45.072,-2.865]. This is a conservative unknown-outcome bound, not an
observed 18.258% deterioration. Masked ADE on partial rows still does not
identify full-grid trajectory accuracy. Locality082 has no final-step labels,
so required all-locality FDE and complete-future summaries stay not_estimable.
No interpolation, label imputation or post-readout horizon change is made.

## 7. Lower Training Loss Is Not The Missing Deployment Proof

Median first minibatch loss is 1.065716 for all four arms. Median final loss
is 0.966489 disabled, 0.946820 current, 0.966346 noise and 0.940728 history.
These are actual fitting minibatches, not validation/independent-test losses.
Their decrease verifies optimization, not selected-risk calibration or a
statistically stable input effect. All 36 loss traces remain available.
