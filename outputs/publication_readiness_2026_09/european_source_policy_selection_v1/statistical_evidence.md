# Interpretation and Reproduction Boundaries

## Material Passport

Fresh source-policy selection and frozen-action evaluation of cached_verified
heads. No new neural training. Twelve exposed development localities, not an
independent confirmation population. The source-validation recordings already
selected an MSE checkpoint; reusing them to choose a policy is model development,
not independent risk calibration.

Primary: same-query-count ADE improvement over MSE selection. Aggregate each
paired contrast within locality before a 3,000-draw locality bootstrap. Report
three seed summaries without calling them independent datasets. No multiplicity
correction or adaptive-research correction is claimed. Zero matched coverage
means no policy comparison headroom was exercised, not proof of equivalence.

## Eleven Checks

1. Simpson: report locality/seed contrasts and independent versus count-matched
   coverage. Do not infer uniform improvement from the overall average.
2. Ecological: average source behavior does not certify an individual agent.
3. Selection: source-validation screening and exposed locality reuse remain
   explicit; selected heads are not representative random samples.
4. Collider: easy, selected and validation-supported subgroups are descriptive,
   not causal explanations of subgroup membership.
5. Base rates: preserve unknown selected labels, reference denominators,
   fallback counts and undefined-risk views; no future-validity inference gate.
6. Regression to the mean: preserve the frozen MSE comparator and all 72 source
   choices, not just the few passing validation heads.
7. Survivorship: require all 216 directions and full replay. A rejected source
   remains in evaluation using fallback, not removed from the denominator.
8. Look elsewhere: fixed three-candidate set and fixed budget before readout;
   no transfer-threshold search or favorable subset promotion.
9. Forking paths: registration, source choices and transfer decision hashes are
   committed sequentially. Prior adaptive development exposure remains.
10. Causation: this changes the computational policy-selection rule, not the
    data-generating process. A pass on validation is not a transport theorem.
11. Reverse causality: source labels choose a model offline; all deployment row
    decisions remain past-only and cannot see label availability or outcomes.

All 11 interpretation risks are considered, not declared empirically absent.
Selected all/easy positive-harm budgets remain 2% with original selected-reference
denominators. Overall easy ADE degradation is separate. Empty intervention is
unsupported fallback and cannot establish neural usefulness or a risk pass.

Independent calibration/confirmation and the full legacy test suite are not_run.
No metric, seconds, human-gold, true3D, foundation or physical-safety claim.
Stage5C and SMC remain off. Repeated source views and overlapping rows are not
independent samples.
