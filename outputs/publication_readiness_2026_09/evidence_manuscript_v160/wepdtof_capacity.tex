\section{Can camera-based edits catch OLS4 at the frozen radius?}
This diagnostic uses only the exposed Convenience Store camera.
It keeps all \WepCapHistories{} online histories, per-query counts,
eligibility and seed-specific radii of the fixed-count comparison.
It does not re-use the thirteen external sources. Each seed has
\WepCapEdits{} slots; the seeds are not independent cameras.

For a selected row with radius $r_i$, the reduction in error from
the CV path $c$ at a known step is at most
$\min(r_i,\|c-y\|)$, by the triangle inequality and nonnegative
error. At an unknown step the lower-bound gain versus CV is
nonpositive. The increase in lower-bound gain versus OLS4 path $o$,
relative to leaving the row at CV, is at most
$\min(r_i,\|o-c\|)$ on that unknown step. For each reference we
sum these bounds over steps, take the largest eligible weighted
row gains up to each query's original count, add the unedited CV
gain against that reference, and divide by the common known CV
error mass. Each reference may favor different rows; this is an
optimistic bound that does not enforce the original risk checks.

Even the largest OLS4 upper bound across seeds is
\mbox{\WepCapObserved{} pp} on known steps and \mbox{\WepCapConservative{} pp}
after conservative scoring. This rules out ranker-only repair
under these particular limits. At the same budget, we add four
causal paths crossing OLS versus median pair slopes with raw
versus fitted final position. A ranker using only rolling errors
inside the prefix satisfies risk on \WepCapPrefixPass{}/3
seed views but risk and both utility comparisons on
\WepCapPrefixDual{}/3. The expanded finite menu has negative
joint upper bounds in \WepCapExpandedExclusion{}/3.
Without the small-edit constraint, a full median-slope
forecast gains \WepCapMedianVsOLS{} pp against OLS4 but its
easy risk upper bound is \WepCapMedianEasy\%, above 2\%.
The constraint on action magnitude and the constraint on harm
are distinct; neither result supports deployment.
