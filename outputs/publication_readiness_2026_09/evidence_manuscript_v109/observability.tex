\subsection*{Pedestrian observability at matched query budgets}
I model conditional cost and future label missingness separately.
The geometry head adds relative position, neighbor distance, crowd
size, and outward motion to motion features. Predicted missingness
weights the action radius; predicted availability weights conditional
cost. Future masks are labels only. Except for the free-budget arm,
each policy matches the entangled screen's edit count at each query.
