```text
(selected_known_easy_harm + selected_unknown_envelope)
```

condition for this finite SOURCE/evaluator,not a population-safety theorem.

predicted-positive mask in both training and inference. It therefore could not
repair this obstruction. The current2%definition is not being relaxed.
