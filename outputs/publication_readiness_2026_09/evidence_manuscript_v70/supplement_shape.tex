correction, two nonlinear timings with the same endpoint, a damped-speed
forecast, a longer velocity fit, and a tapered turn. All alternatives
share the exact peak CV-relative radius for that row. Observed-prefix

future path with missing labels masked, then selects the closest legal
shape without emitting that internal path. The latter yields direct

\RadiusShapeTangent{} against an earlier frozen neural shape rescaled

\RadiusShapePrefix{}. Joint passes are \RadiusShapeLinearPass{} for
