# Secondary Label-Only Pair Accuracy

Declared after the main experiment started,before inspecting its complete
policy results. This is a post-hoc secondary diagnostic,not a new registered
primary endpoint,model-selection criterion or replacement for any original gate.

After all main actions are frozen,compare their predicted pair distances with
the actual future pair distances on valid labels. Restore paths to a common
local image frame;use complete contemporaneous query-target pairs and the
twelve original requested steps. Normalize by geometric-mean frozen past scale.

Absolute pair-distance-error difference between two policies is bounded by
their predicted-distance disagreement. Sum known error differences and bound
missing pair steps by that disagreement. Divide both by all requested pair
steps. Keep every original group;zero pair support is not_estimable. Report
pooled points and all12 sources with support,not independent-pair confidence
intervals. The original ADE paired-locality bootstrap remains the main interval.

Future coordinates/validity masks are evaluation labels only. They cannot
change selected actions,features,normalizers,graph construction or thresholds.
This is not a collision,body-geometry or physical-safety endpoint. Coordinates
remain unverified detector-silver local-image pixels,not meters or seconds.
No primary/gate rescue,independent confirmation,new training,Stage5C or SMC.

Pre-execution clarification:all distance/error/bound arithmetic explicitly
casts cached predictions and labels tofloat64,so a float32 subtraction cannot
artificially tighten the missing-label disagreement bound. The first generated
registration is preserved as registration_before_float64_clarification.json.
No real diagnostic labels/results had been processed;the main running decoder
and its frozen registration are unchanged.
