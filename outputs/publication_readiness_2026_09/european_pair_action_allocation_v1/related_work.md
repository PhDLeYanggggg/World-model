# Prior Work And Claim Boundary

Primary sources reopened on1November2026. This focused check does not certify

## Joint Forecasting

[JFP,CoRL2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08710),Section3.3,combines unary and
learned pairwise trajectory energies. AppendixA/Table5 also compares heuristic
overlap penalties with and without retraining. Its no-training heuristic
reduces overlap but worsens pairwise trajectory error against the backbone.
Thus geometric compatibility and forecast accuracy are distinct endpoints.
Our seven-action geometric penalty is a controlled comparator,not a new
interaction-learning architecture or JFP reproduction. The graph,coordinate
frame,risk constraints,forecast bank and single-choice task differ.

## Regression Deferral

[Mao,Mohri and Zhong,ICML2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html)
studies regression with multiple experts,including pretrained predictors and
learned routing. The published abstract covers bounded regression losses and
instance/label-dependent costs. A baseline-relative selector alone is not a
new problem. Their consistency results do not automatically apply to our
unbounded native ADE,selected-risk ratios or scene-coupled assignment.

## Risk Calibration

[Learn then Test,Section2](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01052v5) separates a
pretrained predictor from independent calibration and valid multiple testing.
Its risk/error tolerances are fixed before calibration. Current SOURCE fits
and conditional locality bootstrap intervals are not that independent stage.
Successful MILP constraints on predictions do not prove realized risk control.
No LTT guarantee,physical safety or new risk theorem is claimed here.

## What This Experiment Can Establish

The single registered question is whether the fixed pair term improves this
frozen seven-action decoder on the identical original population and counts.
It cannot establish a novel world model from lower predicted contraction.
Only genuine utility and original all/easy risk results justify advancement;
independent evidence and full-pipeline forecasting seeds remain separate work.
