\paragraph{Capacity against both motion references.}
Risk feasibility alone does not establish useful capacity.
At the same query counts and with the same CV risk reference,
we maximize the smaller conservative gain against CV and
OLS4. For wholly unobserved rows we retain every legal path:
small CV disagreement need not imply small OLS4 disagreement.
The CV-maximizing solutions already beat both references in
\FixedCVWitness{} and \FullCVWitness{} views with one path per
person and the full bank, respectively. Direct maximin search verifies
\FixedDual{} and \FullDual{} views. The full bank has
\FullRiskInfeasible{} risk/count infeasibilities and
\FullNegativeBound{} negative maximin upper bounds, with
\FullUnresolved{} unresolved views. These are hindsight
counts, not evidence that a past-only selector can recover
the solutions or that independent calibration will succeed.
