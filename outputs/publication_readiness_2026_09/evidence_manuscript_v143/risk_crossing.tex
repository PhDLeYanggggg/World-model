\section{Supervision at the recording risk boundary}
Fitting-only feasible plans are reverified in the common bank.
A substitution changes one trajectory or exchanges one recipient
in its query, preserving all query counts. We retain pairs whose
alternative keeps both lower gains positive and easy support
defined but violates an original risk inequality.
Of \RiskCrossingPairs{} training pairs, utility alone prefers the
risk-violating action in \RiskCrossingDisagree{}\%. Two action
rankers use the same samples and \RiskCrossingUpdates{} total
updates, differing only in whether the label prefers utility or
feasibility. Both act on past features and add a bounded bonus to
the same frozen path scores.

At \RiskCrossingEdits{} count-matched edits, the conservative
contrast for risk vs utility is \RiskCrossingUtility{} pp, and
risk vs independent selection is \RiskCrossingIndependent{} pp.
With free counts, risk vs utility is \RiskCrossingFree{} pp
(3,000 paired source resamples). At matched counts utility/risk
pass \RiskCrossingPasses{} original checks out of 72, with worst
known signed easy changes of \RiskCrossingWorst{}\%.
Calibration qualifies \RiskCrossingQual{}/9 cells.
