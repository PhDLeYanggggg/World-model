{\Large\bfseries When the Forecast Bank Cannot Supply Easy Reference}

\textbf{Setting.} Consider the exposed full-recipient oracle in
which each query may emit any distinct legal actions up to its fixed
cap. Let $b_i$ be the observed CV error of an easy target $i$, and
$h_{ia}=\max(e_{ia}-b_i,0)$ the positive harm of alternative $a$.
Only actions with a path different from CV count as switches.
Easy reference requires $b_i>0$ by the original scoring rule.

\textbf{Criterion.} The risk constraints admit a solution if and
only if there is at least one legal easy action at a query with
nonzero cap such that $h_{ia}\leq 0.02b_i$.

\textit{Sufficiency.} Emit only that action. The selected all and
easy harm ratios are identical and at most 2\%. Its signed easy
change is no greater than its positive harm, which is bounded by
2\% of the entire easy reference. Both selected denominators are
positive and no unknown-outcome radius is incurred.

\textit{Necessity.} A passing policy must have positive selected
easy reference. If every eligible easy action has positive
$h_{ia}-0.02 b_i$, any set with an easy action has positive easy
excess. Other known actions add no easy credit. Wholly unobserved
actions can only increase that numerator. Thus the easy cap fails.

\textbf{Empirical case.} The criterion agrees with all 102 previous
solves, including 99 feasible views and three infeasible views.
The failures repeat one recording over three forecast seeds.
Each seed has three easy rows with 27 distinct eligible row--path
alternatives, and the minimum positive-harm ratio is 23.925\%
for each seed. Ranking or allocating those same paths cannot create
a passing subset in this case.

\textbf{Scope.} This is an elementary consequence of the risk rule,
not a statistical guarantee or a claim of method novelty. Risk uses
the original unweighted selected-row masses; the separate OLS4
utility objective uses equal query weights. The criterion need not
hold for intact multi-agent proposals or a mandatory switch count.
Feasible risk alone does not establish positive gain over OLS4.
Labels are used for this diagnostic, not for past-only inference.
The data are exposed Moving A recordings, eight observed and
twelve predicted annotation steps in image pixels. No independent
data or policy change is involved.
