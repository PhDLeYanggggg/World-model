\section{Controlled Setup}

We keep the forecast bank, direct policy, and original 2\% recording risk and reference-support rules fixed on exposed Moving A. Each of 42 cells uses seven fitting sources, six calibration sources and one held source. The 102 views repeat 34 recordings over three forecaster seeds. The task uses eight observed and twelve forecast annotation steps in image coordinates.

\section{Choice At Fixed Coverage}

The fixed shortlist puts the highest original-score candidate from each family first, then fills the remaining slots up to eight. A ridge model trained on donor-excluded fitting shortlists estimates utility, risk costs and the useful-action event. The gain and risk choices see the same shortlist and predicted outputs. In the unthresholded first-query control, both make 6,998 switches at identical queries. Risk minus gain changes paired completion-lower utility by +0.00156 percentage points (95\% source interval [-0.00688, +0.01348]). Both have 5/102 complete passes in this diagnostic. With donor-only threshold selection, the gain and risk arms have 2 and 2 complete passes, versus 2 for direct. These do not support improved calibrated intervention.


\section{Relation To Deferral}

\href{https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html}{Mao et al. (ICML 2024)} already study multi-expert regression deferral with fixed predictors. Here we isolate candidate availability, an eight-slot shortlist, and final choice under the original trajectory-risk contract. We do not implement their surrogate or claim their consistency guarantees. The gap between a useful action being available and being chosen remains the central empirical limitation. No independent confirmation or deployment follows from these results.
