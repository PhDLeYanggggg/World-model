{\Large\bfseries Forecast Geometry at Fixed Intervention Recipients}

\textbf{Question.} Is the absence of a reliable learned improvement
explained only by deciding when and whom to switch, or does it also
reflect the trajectories available for those agents? We measure a
hindsight capacity limit for the latter. Our evaluation uses eight
observed and twelve predicted annotation steps in native image pixels.
The 102 views repeat 34 exposed recordings across three forecaster
seeds, covering 14 sources; they are not independent trials.

\textbf{Matched geometry.} Keep the four frozen whole-query choices.
For each choice, add two alternatives on its same recipients: OLS4
and a shifted OLS4. For query $q$ and forecast step $t$, the shift is
\[
  \Delta_{q,t} =
  (\bar p^{\mathrm{CV}}_{q,t} + \bar p^{\mathrm{self}}_{q,t}
    - 2\bar p^{\mathrm{OLS4}}_{q,t})/3 ,
\]
where each bar is over the original query roster. The shift preserves
the OLS4 relative paths between recipients, not their paths relative
to agents left at CV. An alternative with a no-op recipient is dropped
to retain its exact intervention count.

\textbf{Diagnostic objective.} In each recording, choose abstention
or one intact option per query to maximize conservative ADE utility
over fitting-selected OLS4. CV remains the reference and fallback.
The original 2\% all/easy positive-harm caps, signed easy cap and
positive easy reference support remain in force. Utility weights each
query equally and charges path disagreement for wholly unknown
outcomes. Every feasible solution is rechecked with the original risk
scorer; all optimizations terminate with a verified optimum or proved
infeasibility.

\textbf{Interpretation.} Geometry alone changes the feasible set: the
combined slate adds ten feasible views and six positive views across
three sources. Among the 61 originally feasible views, the median
increase in optimum lower gain is 0.236 percentage points, normalized
by the same observed CV reference mass. The expanded bank still leaves
31 views infeasible and 38 feasible with no positive lower gain. These
are exact development-slate findings, not performance intervals, proof
that an online policy can find the solutions, or independent evidence.
The original policy and external calibration protocol are unchanged.
