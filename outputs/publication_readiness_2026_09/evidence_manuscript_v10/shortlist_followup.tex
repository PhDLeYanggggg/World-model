The exposed Moving A development task uses eight observed and twelve forecast annotation steps in native image coordinates. We keep the bank, direct actions, eight candidate slots, and the original 2\% risk and reference-support rules fixed. Each of 42 source/seed cells uses seven fitting, six calibration, and one held source. The views are 34 recordings repeated across three forecaster seeds.


\section{Retrieval And Choice Are Separate Bottlenecks}

In the first two eligible queries of 18 original zero-direct views, the full bank has a positive supported action in 15. The family-diverse list retains one in 6. The new cost portfolio keeps the old top action and cycles through support, gain, risk-adjusted gain, and low excess, preferring different agents. It retains one in 3: 1 shared with the old list, 2 new and 5 lost. Thus diversifying cost rankings is not a replacement for family diversity.


\section{Refit Results}

Early hits are final choices in the first two eligible queries, out of 18 original zero-direct views, without thresholding. Pass and easy columns are complete calibrated policy counts out of 102 views. The unchanged selector has zero early hits, two passes and eight signed easy violations. Matched effects compare exactly 6,998 switches on identical queries to that selector, in percentage points of completion-lower utility. Intervals resample 14 sources 3,000 times, averaging the three seeds within source.

No tested model adds a complete observed pass. The short-history backtest improves matched utility without solving early choice. Already-resolved feedback is absent in those early queries. Neighbor summaries do not recover a useful choice; rollout proximity adds a calibrated easy violation. The portfolio event model finds one early choice but loses matched utility and complete passes at fixed coverage. None provides independent confirmation or changes deployment. The units are not seconds, meters or physical safety.
