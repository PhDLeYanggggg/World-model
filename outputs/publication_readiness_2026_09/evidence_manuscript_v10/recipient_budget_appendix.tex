{\Large\bfseries Recipient Access at a Fixed Query Budget}

\textbf{Design.} Starting from twelve intact whole-query options,
we relax the allowed switches in three steps: combine already
offered recipient--path pairs, offer the full path bank to the same
union of recipients, and offer that bank to every eligible query
recipient. The original intervention limit $k_q$ stays fixed for
each query $q$, but realized counts may be lower. The result diagnoses
capacity on 34 exposed recordings in 14 source groups across three
forecaster seeds (102 dependent views), with eight observed and
twelve predicted annotation steps in native image pixels.

\textbf{Optimization.} Let $z_{ia}$ indicate that recipient $i$ gets
path $a$ rather than CV. Require $\sum_a z_{ia}\leq 1$ and
$\sum_{i\in q,a}z_{ia}\leq k_q$. The recording's all/easy positive-harm
constraints, signed easy cap and positive easy reference support are
unchanged. We maximize the conservative difference from OLS4 with
equal query weights. The lower bound for a wholly unobserved row
charges maximum path disagreement from OLS4, and both risk caps
charge disagreement from CV. These charges are distinct. Every
feasible witness is independently checked by the original scorer.
The solver sees development labels and is not a prediction method.

\textbf{Interpretation.} Expanding recipients creates twelve positive
views across five sources, more than recombining or changing paths
on the original recipients. Yet 48 of the 99 feasible views have no
positive conservative OLS4 optimum, and three remain infeasible
despite available easy candidates. The matched-budget diagnosis
supports investigating retrieval but does not demonstrate a
deployable improvement, identify physical interaction benefits, or
change the independent calibration protocol.

\textbf{Solver follow-up.} The initial four-second cap leaves nine
unresolved full-bank solves. On those nine, a longer cap and exact
dominance pruning of unobserved actions recover proved optima.
Discard an unobserved alternative if abstention has at least its
utility, or another alternative on the same recipient weakly
improves both CV radius and OLS4 radius. No view is unresolved in
the final table.
