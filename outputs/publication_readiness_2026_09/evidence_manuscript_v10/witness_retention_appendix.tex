{\Large\bfseries From Recording Witnesses to Causal Decisions}

\textbf{Matched comparison.}
Both rankers retrieve eight recipients per query and propose the same
9,675 interventions across three forecaster seeds. The gain-only
control keeps its full shortlist. The witness ranker keeps the first
four gain slots and fills the rest from a source-excluded predictor of
membership in a risk-feasible recording solution with positive
conservative gain over OLS4. The teacher uses exposed labels and can
only intervene on labeled rows; it retains the original uncertainty
charge on wholly unobserved rows. Both rankers see the same past-only
inputs and use the same path bank, action costs, decoder and gate.

\textbf{Retrieval and operation.}
On 34 exposed recordings across 14 sources and three forecaster
seeds, the number of views with positive hindsight OLS4 utility is
45 with gain-only retrieval and 48 with witness retrieval. The
three gains incur no losses, but the source-bootstrap contrast is
$+3.571$ percentage points [$0.000,+10.714$]. At matched counts the
paired conservative utility difference is $-0.361$ [$-0.828,-0.023$]
points on the CV denominator. The witness policy passes 4/102 views
after gating, compared with 3/102 for the original unshortlisted
controller, and remains below OLS4.

\textbf{Attribution.}
All three nested optimizations allow abstention and keep the same
recording risk and reference-support conditions. This isolates two
losses after retrieval: collapsing the candidate paths to one joint
choice, and rejecting that choice before accumulating recording
support. A feasible query subset with labels is not a certificate
for the full operating policy, which must act without those labels.
The unchanged 2\% limits, original query caps, CV risk reference,
and OLS4 accuracy comparison apply throughout. There are no unresolved
solves. Distances are in pixels over eight observed and twelve
predicted annotation steps. No independent test is used here.
