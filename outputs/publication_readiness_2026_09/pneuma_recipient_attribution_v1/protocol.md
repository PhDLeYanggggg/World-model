# Secondary attribution for the frozen Athens test

This addendum does not choose the scientific date roles. It binds the
previously frozen 3-by-3 recipient/path bank to the same primary
confirmation run, once the author chooses the primary protocol.
It has no role in calibration, score selection or release thresholds.

Compare joint with independent, and joint with additive, on the exact
original roster and budget. For each comparison use four cells: both
control choices (00), control recipients with joint paths (01), joint
recipients with control paths (10), and both joint choices (11).
The path contribution averages the two path-only changes, while the
recipient contribution averages the two recipient-only changes.
Their sum equals the observed diagonal contrast.

At a missing point use a common unknown target for the four cells.
Each component is half a signed sum of four distances, two positive
and two negative. Pair the positive paths with the negative paths in
either of the two possible ways. The triangle inequality bounds the
absolute contribution by half the sum of the two paired distances;
take the smaller of the bounds. This can be narrower than adding
two separately bounded contrasts. Component intervals are marginal
envelopes and must not be added to replace the diagonal interval.
Report these separately from the date bootstrap intervals. Retain
the observed CV mass and query weights from the primary contrast.

Read each confirmation member's future only once, share the labels
in memory, and write only scalar outputs. The primary diagonal must
agree at every seed. Replay from scalar member receipts must not
reopen futures. No new baseline, cutoffs, metric unit or deployment
claim is introduced. The original 2% risk rule stays unchanged.
