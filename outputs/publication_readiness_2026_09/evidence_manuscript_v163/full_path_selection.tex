\section{Can a past-only head recover the full-path gain?}
For each of seven OpenDD locations and three seeds we train a clean
and a noisy spline excluding that location (\FullPathFits{} fits).
The value head uses these held-site trajectories and donor outcomes
to estimate conservative gain against CV and OLS4, positive harm,
and the corresponding reference masses. The three value heads and
donor fits take \FullPathUpdates{} updates. The camera uses the
unchanged, previously frozen all-donor forecasters.

The raw-path rule admits at most two edits per query and abstains
unless the predicted two-reference gain is positive and predicted
all/easy excesses are nonpositive. This predicted screen is not a
certificate. On the exposed camera, the expanded raw bank has a
verified hindsight plan in \FullPathFullCapacity{}/3 seed views;
the past-only raw policy passes original risk in \FullPathRawRisk{}/3
and risk and both utility checks in \FullPathRawDual{}/3.
All actions have positive predicted easy excess in
\FullPathEasyRejected{}/3 views. We keep the gains and candidate paths fixed
and replace the risk factorization with a head that estimates signed
excess directly. It makes \FullPathSignedEdits{} edits by seed but
passes both checks in \FullPathSignedDual{}/3; its easy upper risk
is \FullPathSignedRisk\%. Changing the prediction target does not
change the 2\% evaluation rule.

The equal-peak diagnostic uses a common radius from the analytic
paths in each query, shortening but never enlarging a candidate.
The number of edits and sum of peak changes are identical across
the independent, common-family and joint controls within this bank.
Its expanded hindsight capacity is \FullPathEqualCapacity{}/3
and the joint rule passes both checks in \FullPathEqualDual{}/3.
