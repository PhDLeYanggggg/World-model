\paragraph{Observed peers and the cost of an expired wait.}
On the same released slots we compare self history, two aligned peers,
and peers described in a different focal frame from the same query.
Of \PeerOffers{} offers, \PeerPos{} can be moved without dropping an edit,
improve paired utility and meet the full joint check. Only
\PeerLocations{} locations have estimable within-location AP.
Aligned-minus-self AP is \PeerSelfAP{}, and aligned-minus-wrong-focal
AP is \PeerWrongAP{}. The aligned controller clears \PeerJoint{} joint
checks versus \PeerAnchor{} for the frozen anchor. Its direct gain is
\PeerFull{} versus the anchor and \PeerMatch{} versus the diagnostic
replay of the same emitted slots at their original times. At most one
change per recording implies an exact split into moved and expired
slots. Of the \PeerExpired{} expiries, \PeerProxy{} have no later
positive-prefix candidate and \PeerBlocked{} are blocked by the actual
scheduler. This ablation does not establish interaction-aware deferral
lift. The emitted-slot diagnostic does not establish full-policy safety.
