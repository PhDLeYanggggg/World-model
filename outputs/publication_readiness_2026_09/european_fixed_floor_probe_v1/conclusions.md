# Neural Opportunity Exists; the Conditional Risk Prediction Still Fails

Fresh fitting and scoring on2026-09-27. Registration `3b09d834` and decision
freeze `18c0b38d` preceded this readout. This experiment finishes the next action
from the source-calibration study without another threshold sweep. It is not an
independent confirmation result or a new deployable model.

## What Changed

Nine coordinate-unit-repaired neural forecast banks are held fixed. For every
producer/seed/controller assignment, the same protected-damping floor predicts
both the new probe's training and readout rows. Four sources train the neural
forecaster, four train the floor controller, two train the probe and two evaluate
it. All six fitting/held pairs are retained. No probe row is in its upstream
forecaster or floor fitting sources. This removes the inner/outer floor shift
that limited the older234-head target-reference experiment.

The new models are216 linear cost heads from108 closed-form ridge fits, not new
neural dynamics training. The matched target comparison changes only whether
benefit/harm/reference labels are relative to CV or the frozen protected floor.
Both use the same380 causal features, training-only preprocessing, regularizer,
source weights and fixed movement/risk/support screens. No winner is selected.

## Failures Retained

The registered target-reference hypothesis fails again. Floor-target screened
selection is-0.01686% [-0.02177%,-0.01152%] worse than its matched CV-target
control. Every locality-average point favors the CV-target control. That
control gains+0.540% [0.380%,0.707%] over the floor. Simply changing the target
reference is not the demonstrated source of improvement.

More seriously, the floor-target policy's selected positive incremental harm
ratio is4.910% [3.889%,5.975%], above the unchanged2% budget.171/216 dependent
held-source views violate it, and all twelve locality-average ratios exceed2%.
The CV-target control violates169 views. The name `safe` identifies a predicted
screen in the code, NOT proven or observed safety. Net easy preservation and
positive-harm control are different properties.

The positive-gain-only policy achieves+9.108% [5.565%,12.300%] over floor, but
easy error degrades3.563% on average and38.314% in the worst held view. One
locality-average all-ADE result is-6.279%. Releasing the risk screen is not a
solution. Raw neural forecasts are still worse on easy error, with a worst
held-view degradation67.732% and observed zero-CV harms.

## Why This Is a Controller Problem, Not Only a Forecast Problem

The floor-target screened policy captures0.705 percentage points of possible
benefit and incurs0.182 points of harm, leaving20.541 of21.246 oracle points
unused. Its intervention fraction is5.63%. The positive-only policy captures
16.966 points, but pays7.857 in harm. This identifies the benefit/risk bottleneck
without pretending oracle choices are causal inputs.

Held gain AUROC is only0.535 [0.511,0.558]. Benefit and harm MSE skill intervals
cross zero; easy-harm MSE skill is negative. The fixed two-source linear probe
therefore gives weak ordering and unreliable selected moments. This cannot
prove that all nonlinear causal representations fail. It also cannot assign
the small observed lift specifically to scene, interaction or JEPA features.

The post-readout moment audit makes the false confidence more concrete. For
the floor-target screen,65.36% of selected predictions have harm clipped to
zero, but23.89% of those selected zero-harm predictions incur realized harm.
The predicted reference cost is about2.03 times its realized counterpart on
average over sources. These errors make the predicted risk ratio look smaller.
Undefined component ratios remain undefined where the actual denominator is
zero; those source/views are not silently dropped to create a favorable mean.

## Decision

No deployment or independent-role opening. Retain the strong protected damping
comparison and all negatives. Do not enlarge the forecaster or sweep more
cutoffs on these outputs. The next controlled training should target conditional
harm underestimation with a matched nonnegative/envelope-constrained cost head
and fit-only tail weighting, keeping the same producer chain, target reference,
source weights and threshold budget. Include an unchanged moment-loss control;
do not credit a new loss merely for reducing coverage. Calibration must be
source-separated before any future deployment evaluation.

These are twelve already-opened development localities, with overlapping rows
and repeated producer/seed views.3,000 locality-bootstrap draws are conditional
development uncertainty, not independent confirmation. Image-local detector
silver, obs8/pred12 rawstride12; not historical Stage37t50, seconds, metric,
human gold, physical safety, true3D or foundation evidence. Stage5C and SMC stay
disabled. The research goal and submission package remain incomplete.
