# Causal-Descriptor Risk-Head Card

## Material Passport

Fresh training of a risk estimator, not a new neural forecaster. Parent
signed-excess models, forecasting banks, protected fallback, utility and
source caches are cached_verified. Final outcomes are in results.md and
gates.json. No independent confirmation, calibration certificate or deployment
upgrade follows merely from successful training.

## Model

The existing380-dimensional input enters a64-unit GELU hidden layer with four
nonnegative score outputs. A zero-initialized6x64branch adds causal motion and
rollout descriptors to that preactivation. Total parameters25,028,384more
than the control. Initial shared weights and outputs are identical; all
source-balanced draws, optimizer settings, steps and old preprocessing match.

The added descriptors describe past motion magnitude relative to observed
extent, trajectory nonlinearity, mean turn, neighbor-history availability and
neural/floor prediction disagreement. They are not scene semantics, physical
velocity in meters, or future goals. No new visual or generative modality is
introduced. This comparison does not isolate extra capacity from the meaning
of each individual descriptor.

Supervision uses all/easy positive neural harm minus2%of protected-floor error.
Future coordinates and validity masks are used only for fitting/evaluation
labels. Output components are not identified cost moments or calibrated
failure probabilities. The policy keeps stationary, utility and source-support
guards plus the fixed2%risk screens. When not admitted, the protected floor
remains. No residual correction or stochastic rollout is trained here.

## Data and Roles

Twelve already-exposed European source-training localities,318,969queries.
For each of108groups: four forecast-training sources, four floor/utility
sources, two risk-fitting sources and two held-development sources. All four
roles are disjoint within each group. Three forecasting seeds17/29/43.
Independent selection/calibration/confirmation stay closed; nested held
development results cannot be promoted to final tests.

Observed8steps, requested12steps, raw-frame stride12. Positions and labels
are image-local detector silver. Partial future validity affects supervision,
never inference features. Future-unknown examples remain in the inference
population but do not generate training losses or known evaluation outcomes.
Overlapping query windows and repeated role/seed views are not independent;
uncertainty resamples locality summaries.

## Reproduction and Limits

Native arm64 CPU4/interop1, workers0; fixed2,000updates, checkpoints every500,
atomic lossless gzip states, compressed decision masks and frozen prediction
hashes. The initial100-update pilot is part of the total budget. Prediction
hashes are checked against checkpoint replay before readout. Raw data, large
caches and checkpoint weights remain private; public evidence is aggregate.

No metric, seconds, human-gold, physical-safety, true3D or foundation claim.
Stage5C and SMC remain disabled. Full legacy suite and cold raw reconstruction
are not_run; scoped checks and actual replay evidence are separately listed
in verification.json.
