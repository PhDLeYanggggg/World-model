# Frozen Occurrence Versus Matched Trainable Occurrence

## Material Passport

Prospective controlled development experiment following the sealed fitting
component diagnostic. No new result yet. That diagnostic found worse occurrence
prediction and cancellation of cost errors after auxiliary-gradient capping.
It does not establish a causal mechanism of held failure. This experiment tests
one specified response, without sweeping weights or selecting factor mixtures.

## Treatment and Control

Every group starts from its SAME original uncapped supervised checkpoint. Clone
its network into separate occurrence and cost branches for BOTH arms. The
initial predictions must exactly equal the warm-start teacher. Both arms reset
AdamW; neither continues the old optimizer. Both retain the original normalized
causal inputs, causal envelope, source roles, easy label, cost scale and loss:
marginal risk loss + occurrence BCE + conditional reference/harm MSE.

The trainable control updates both branches. The fixed treatment freezes every
occurrence parameter; only its cost branch updates. This is the sole difference
between the paired arms. Both have the same split architecture, initial values,
sampler/query sequence and2000-update budget. This comparison isolates freezing
within the split architecture, not the effect of splitting versus a shared trunk.
Compare marginal and individual losses, not total loss as an efficacy ranking:
the frozen occurrence BCE remains a constant in its scalar reporting sum.

Retain all108 groups /216 heads and the original three seeds. No checkpoint
selection: use the prescribed final update. Freeze probability parameters
exactly, not approximately. Check full versus interrupted-resumed training and
paired query hashes. CPU4/interOp1/workers0, no DataLoader multiprocessing.

## Data and Fitting Diagnostics

Keep original4producer/4controller/2fitting/2held-development roles per context.
Only the two fitting sources enter training or the new diagnostics. Predictors
receive the original causal whitelist; future labels are loss/evaluation only.
The signed-gain sidecar must match frozen row IDs, old target hashes and source
identity. Unknowns remain unknown and are excluded from fitting queries.

Report complete source/query-balanced risk objective, occurrence Brier,
conditional errors and benefit/harm retained by the fixed q_easy<=0 sign screen.
That one-constraint screen is explicitly NOT a deployment policy: it omits
overall risk, original eligibility, utility allocation and independent calibration.
It can diagnose lost benefit but cannot certify risk or forecast improvement.

## Subsequent Registered Development Comparison

After all training and checkpoint hashes are frozen, produce causal held actions
before reading outcomes. Keep the original all-risk estimate, utility, eligibility,
nominal2% all/easy budget and joint allocator. Compare frozen-occurrence versus
trainable-occurrence at identical per-query intervention counts using their
common eligible independent-admission anchor together with the raw control.
Retain raw control, independent admission, own-count joint allocation and floor.
The primary developmental contrast is fixed_matched versus trainable_matched;
report raw control comparisons, lost benefit, positive harm, easy, tails,
undefined/unsupported views and scene-level3000-draw uncertainty as well.

Register the action implementation before actions; commit all action hashes
before a new outcome readout. Do not drop views or alter the selected-risk
denominator. The existing unsupported views remain a structural gap. No
independent selection/calibration/confirmation source is opened in this study.
No deployment decision follows from fitting loss alone.
