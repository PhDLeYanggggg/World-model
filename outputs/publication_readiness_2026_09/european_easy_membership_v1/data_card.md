# Easy-Membership Data Card

## Material Passport

Cached_verified source-development lineage from european_frozen_harm_readout_v1.
New targets use the existing fitting-only easy definition, not a revised
forecasting endpoint or risk tolerance. Current independent confirmation is
not_run and stays closed.

Each source-A chain excludes B. Three B localities fit the classifier and
all normalizers, prevalence and easy cut; a fourth B locality is held out.
Six source assignments, three seeds and full/motion-only forecast pairs are
retained. Source-development localities are historically exposed, not newly
independent test domains. Six selection localities are unused this round;
12 reserved calibration and 6 confirmation localities remain closed.

Only current/past observations and causal producer predictions are features.
The baseline's realized error and resulting easy membership are supervision
or evaluation only. Unknown labels are excluded from fitting, not converted
to negatives. The inherited context loader can load cached held arrays but
never uses held targets in fitting or selection. Future endpoints do not
construct features/goals. No central velocity or held normalization.

The easy cut is a positive-CV-error 25th percentile from the three fitting
localities. It varies by fold; exact-zero CV error is not a positive E label.
Reports distinguish all rows from positive causal disagreement, and show
training/held prevalence shift rather than recalibrating on held labels.

Three seeds are averaged within locality before 3,000 bootstrap resamples
of four localities. Overlapping rows and repeated role assignments are not
independent samples. CIs are exploratory and not multiplicity-adjusted.
Detector-derived image-pixel labels do not establish human gold or physical
safety. Annotation-step horizons do not establish seconds. No true3D or
foundation claim; Stage5C/SMC off. Raw data, caches and weights stay private.
