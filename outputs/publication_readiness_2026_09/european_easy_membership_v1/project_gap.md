# Project Gap After Direct Membership Learning

## Current Position

The registered full-input MLP diagnostic is positive, including the stronger
pre-readout conditional-prevalence sensitivity. This is a useful component
result: existing causal inputs predict which rows satisfy the inherited easy
event. It does not repair the previously failed expected-harm model or prove
that a scene-level controller can safely exploit neural forecasts.

Fresh_run: membership fitting and evaluation. Cached_verified: source data,
causal producer forecasts and parent lineage. Not_run: the conditional-severity
repair, new policy evaluation, independent calibration and final confirmation.

## Shortest Defensible Next Step

1. Register one matched, source-only cost-factorization experiment. Compare
   direct expected easy harm with membership times conditional harm severity,
   retaining zero-harm easy rows. Any learned second-stage input must use
   fitting-safe/out-of-fold predictions; no ground-truth membership at inference.
2. Test expected-cost MSE, tail errors, locality transport and coverage against
   both equally trained and original controls. Keep all six assignments and
   three seeds. Motion-only weakness and bad-locality calibration stay visible.
3. Only after an expected-cost gain, evaluate the unchanged scene-level policy
   under the approved risk budget and role separation. Independent calibration
   and confirmation cannot be used to tune a failed source method.

These are next actions, not completed experiments. Passing membership is not
authorization to change the main endpoint or 2% risk tolerance. It does not
open independent data or permit Stage5C/SMC execution.

## Remaining Publication Evidence

- A causal/matched contribution beyond a conventional binary classifier.
- Reliable expected harm where interventions occur, not just AUROC.
- Improvement from scene-level allocation under the actual risk constraints.
- Comparisons on the same population and budget; full versus motion-only
  positive-disagreement subsets are not an isolated modality ablation.
- Independent calibration and confirmation with locality-level uncertainty.
- Updated paper claims and reproducibility materials tied to those experiments.

Current evidence is detector-derived image-pixel source development with
eight observed/twelve predicted annotation steps. No metric/seconds, human
gold, true 3D, foundation, physical-safety or submission-ready claim. The
long-term goal remains active, and the deployment policy remains unchanged.
