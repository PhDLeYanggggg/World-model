\paragraph{Separating gain, harm, and easy support at exact totals.}
All matched arms use the online gain-only controller's exact realized
totals (\FactorRiskEdits{} edits per arm), identical chronological
credit constraints and the same fixed nominations. Replacing only
gain, harm terms, or easy support by future-label values gives
\FactorRiskGain{}, \FactorRiskRisk{} and \FactorRiskSupport{} joint
passes versus \FactorRiskBase{}/\FactorRiskViews{} for learned motion.
All true local terms give \FactorRiskAll{}; exact constrained hindsight
verifies \FactorRiskGlobal{}. The matching sees the recording's query
scores and is retrospective. A separate causal prefix-risk heuristic
uses only the observed window and donor cutoffs; its online joint count
is \FactorRiskPrefixRisk{}, versus \FactorRiskPrefix{} for prefix gain.

\paragraph{Factorizing signed risk.}
We train 21 new Torch heads to estimate occurrence and magnitude of
nonnegative harm, reference mass, missing-label debit and easy benefit,
then reconstruct signed risk under the original rule. Replacing only
the risk part of the frozen score increases matched joint passes from
\FactorRiskBase{} to \FactorRiskFactor{}, while direct conservative gain
is \FactorRiskPoint{} pp (95\% location interval
[\FactorRiskLow{}, \FactorRiskHigh{}]). The factorization has lower
positive-tail magnitude error than the signed head, but its easy-risk
sign false-positive rate is \FactorRiskFalsePositive{}\%, and a zero
prediction has lower pooled absolute error. The benefit in recording
pass counts does not establish usable risk calibration or improved
forecast utility. These are exposed OpenDD data; confirmation remains
sealed and the risk criterion is unchanged.
