\section{Training a New Forecast Instead of Reranking}
On the same exposed OpenDD development cohort, we change the
forecast itself. A heading-aligned network predicts three bounded
velocity knots, interpolates them over twelve steps, and integrates
from the last observed position. It is trained with observed trajectory
error and a positive excess-error term relative to CV. The matched
ablation adds up to two observed pedestrian histories and then up to
two nearby motor-vehicle histories. Vehicle positions come only from
the eight requested past timestamps, with no interpolation of missing
input. There are \VelocityTrafficCount{} targets with observed local
vehicle context. All arms have the same architecture and draws.

The fixed blend of self-only forecasts with CV improves conservative
gain at all seven held locations. In contrast, the direct traffic
ablation is negative even when nearby vehicles are observed. The
self-only blend increases the worst descriptive easy ADE by
\VelocityWorstEasy\%, so this is a candidate forecast, not a
qualified intervention policy. OpenDD has informed development;
the result does not change the original risk rule, open the independent
confirmation split, or demonstrate multimodal generalization.
