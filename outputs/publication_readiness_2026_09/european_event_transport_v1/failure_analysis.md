# Why The Common-Event Repair Is Not Promoted

## Supported Findings

1. Response-side event alignment alone does not improve full-input OOF repair
   consistently:zero positive,one negative,five overlapping MSE intervals
   versus both the old OOF repair and the original outer estimator.
2. This is not solely a weak-comparator issue. All six point estimates versus
   prior three-locality context and both common-event cyclic controls are
   negative. Four intervals against prior context are below zero.
3. Motion-only lacks evidence of recovery:all six point estimates versus
   original remain negative,from-19.9452% to-1.6391%.
4. Gate failure is numerical/scientific,not a crash or changed endpoint.
   All144 views are included; held target/row hashes and D/H/D_E predictions
   are unchanged.864 algebra checks isolate the registered label change.

## What Is Not Established

The result does not prove that easy-event drift is irrelevant. It disproves
the sufficiency of this one response relabeling, for these frozen models and
this fixed additive probe. Inner teachers retain their own event definitions.
Their two-locality training distribution and prediction scale still differ
from the three-locality outer estimator receiving the correction.

Nor does this prove that OOF methods generally fail, that more epochs help,
that the seven causal features are useless for every task, or that a particular
scene causes the failure. Four held localities per assignment and sparse harm
support limit inference. Overlapping assignments/seeds cannot be counted as
independent scene replications. No held-optimal coefficient was fitted.

The exact linear factor accounting holds before clipping. After clipping to
[0,original H], event and remaining shifts do not add independently. The
observed MSE difference is the complete implemented repair effect, not an
additive causal share attributable to labels, model size or data composition.

## Next Falsifiable Repair Boundary

Stop the event-cut/threshold sweep. The next unresolved transport is the
prediction producer:OOF errors from a two-locality head are being applied to
a distinct three-locality head. Measure their fitting-only cost/residual
location and scale using the common event, preserving all views and the
original strong control. Distinguish changed producer calibration from sparse
feature support before registering one producer-matched residual design.

Merely replacing the inner prediction by the three-locality head's in-sample
prediction reduces to the prior context control, which already failed its
primary gate. Likewise, adding their prediction difference algebraically
cancels back to that same failed residual. Check this equivalence and existing
support-aware experiments before proposing any new model. A renamed replay
of that control is not another repair or a contribution.

Do not choose a shrinkage, feature subset, site or risk budget from the held
outcomes. Do not open independent calibration to compensate for a failed
development method. Keep deployment unchanged and retain negative evidence.

This remains risk-estimation evidence,not improved trajectories,calibrated
intervention utility or world-model success. Detector pixels/annotation steps
only; no metric/seconds,physical-safety,human-gold,true3D or foundation claims.
Stage5C and SMC stay off.
