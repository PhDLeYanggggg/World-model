# Remaining Research Gap

The common-event response repair is fully specified and tested, but its
scientific gate fails. This does not advance M3W to an independent cross-domain
or submission-ready result. The original estimator and deployment remain.

The most immediate unresolved issue is residual transport between different
prediction producers, not whether the local Torch environment works. A source
trained on two localities and a deployed estimator trained on three can have
different errors even under the same event. Response relabeling has now been
tested and is insufficient. A fitting-only producer-mismatch diagnostic is the
next step; it must lead to one controlled repair or a justified rejection, not
another unrestricted architecture/threshold search.

A producer-matching proposal must first be checked against the old in-sample
control:subtracting the outer head's fitting predictions simply reproduces
that already-tested method. The next design must address an identified
support/transport limitation without this equivalence or held-guided selection.

Even a successful risk-head repair would still need demonstrated trajectory
utility against strong causal controls, scene-joint intervention/conflict
evaluation at matched budgets, and genuinely independent calibration and
confirmation. Historical Stage35/37/43/44 development-exposed evidence cannot
be restored as independent by relabeling. Fixed ridge/OOF corrections are not
novel contributions by themselves.

The completed experiment and negative contrasts are usable for reproducibility
and failure analysis, not as the paper's positive main claim. There is no new
neural dynamics,metric calibration,seconds-level evaluation,human gold,physical
safety,true3D or foundation evidence. Main protocol remains8 observed/12
predicted annotation steps in detector image pixels. Stage5C and SMC remain
off. Formal paper submission still requires the user's final confirmation.
