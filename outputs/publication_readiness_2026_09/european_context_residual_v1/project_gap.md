# Research Gap After the Context Probe

## Progress This Round

The complete registered experiment tests, rather than assumes, that simple
past-only context can repair transported cost estimates. It adds 864 fresh
closed-form fits and 36 fresh readout groups while preserving all frozen
neural estimators, source exclusions and independent roles. It identifies
some recurring bias directions but does not pass the fixed magnitude gate.

## What Is Still Missing

- Stable expected gain/harm estimation across source localities, with the
  original strong estimator retained as a control.
- Evidence that such cost estimates improve scene-level joint intervention,
  including easy, tail and worst-scene behavior at matched intervention rates.
- Independent risk calibration and final confirmation, followed by strong
  public-method comparisons and a supported method contribution.

Engineering replay, source-development AUROC gains and repeated signs do not
fill these scientific gaps. Historical Stage35/37/43/44 exposed lineage cannot
supply independent confirmation or an untouched deployment validation.

without changing the seven features, primary endpoint or comparator? First
enumerate the inner producer/target/preprocessing dependencies; then register
one matched OOF-versus-in-sample experiment. Preserve the outer-held exclusion
and audit support before training. This avoids treating learned in-sample
errors as an independent calibration set. It is a proposed next experiment,
not a result, and it may fail.

Do not promote the observed closing-speed counts into a feature-selection
rule, sweep thresholds on held outcomes, or spend the reserved independent
roles to debug this estimator. The goal remains active; no runtime or access
hard blocker was encountered in this local experiment.
