# What class-specific labels add to the SDD camera evaluation

I used the same 92 SDD development windows in 23 recordings and
five scenes. No detector, history, forecast or threshold changed.
I kept the original per-frame pedestrian associations, then matched
the remaining detections to other visible annotation classes.
A match still requires the same published identity in all eight
observed frames. The class label is used for scoring, not for
prediction or for deciding which forecasts to emit.

| Frozen input | Online histories | Complete pedestrian | Complete cyclist | Complete skater | No scored future | Clips / scenes with adequate pooled support |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original threshold | 126 | 46 | 30 | 6 | 43 | 4 / 2 |
| Lower threshold | 423 | 109 | 68 | 8 | 234 | 9 / 4 |
| Additive bridge | 237 | 82 | 57 | 8 | 88 | 6 / 3 |

The existing support rule is eight complete histories in at least
three windows within a recording. When pooled across these classes,
the bridge clears it in six clips. With the classes kept separate,
three clips have enough cyclist histories, two have enough
pedestrian histories and none has enough skater histories.
These are support counts, not validation or deployment gates.

## Joint decisions need more than two labeled agents

| Frozen input | Windows with 2+ online agents | 2+ complete futures | Complete futures in 2+ classes | All online futures complete, with 2+ agents |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original threshold | 28 | 18 | 7 | 7 |
| Lower threshold | 68 | 42 | 24 | 7 |
| Additive bridge | 44 | 32 | 21 | 9 |

Lowering the threshold increases the multi-agent window count from
28 to 68, but the number of fully labeled joint windows stays at
seven. A test on only
the labeled agents would omit other forecasts made in the same scene.
I keep those other forecasts in the denominator; the original 2%
CV-relative risk rule is unchanged and has not been evaluated here.

## Forecast error and missing outcomes

For the bridge, the same complete targets give the following
12-step ADE under four-frame least-squares velocity (OLS4):

| Class | From camera histories (px) | From publisher histories (px) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pedestrian | 8.31 | 1.81 |
| Cyclist | 7.62 | 2.59 |
| Skater | 7.99 | 2.97 |

This comparison holds the target fixed. The gap includes detection,
association, and differences in the observed box centers; it does not
isolate any one cause. Of the bridge's 1,775 scored future positions,
38 are not flagged as generated by the publisher.

On the full bridge roster, OLS8 gains **4.24 pp** against OLS4 on
known steps. Its conservative bound after debiting missing outcomes
is **-8.48 pp**, compared with **-22.10 pp** when only pedestrian
outcomes were attached. The normalizer changes when additional
outcomes are observed, so these are not a paired gain from switching
policies. The new bound is still negative. None of the fixed
motion substitutions evaluated here establishes a positive
full-roster lower bound. No model was fitted and no deployment changed.

I would prioritize more recordings with near-complete joint labels
over still lower detection thresholds. This study does not establish
an advantage for joint intervention, safety in new scenes, or
readiness for submission. All distances are video pixels; the
eight observed and twelve predicted steps are raw annotation frames.
Reserved DroneCrowd and WEPDTOF outcomes were not accessed.
