probe worsens:row0.000144110 to0.000600934,set0.000153624 to0.000477941.

## What not to repeat as a new fix

The preceding unit experiment already improved fixed-set score fit and known
utility while failing robust utility/risk. The exact selected-score audit
already separates harm underprediction from reference bias. Earlier reference
sharing,completion-endpoint supervision,shared TRAIN risks,aggregate mass
correction and asymmetric original-budget objectives also exist and failed
their respective advancement checks. Those studies have different controls
and populations;they are not pooled into this trial or renamed as new methods.

In particular,the earlier completion-endpoint experiment trained all five costs
and changed the sampler jointly with its paired control. A proposed risk-only
or separate-risk-bound head would need to isolate its distinction and directly
test useful final-policy risk,not merely improve proxy fit or latent distance.
This is a possible design question,not a trained or registered new model.
