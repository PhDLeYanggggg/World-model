\section{Direct Signed Risk for Analytic Bridges}
We keep the gain head, neural-arm risks, and candidate menu fixed.
For each analytic bridge, a new head predicts the all and easy signed
excess (positive harm minus 2\% of reference mass). The calibration
offset uses a fixed residual quantile, averaged across donor locations.
Each donor residual comes from a fit and easy threshold excluding both
that donor and the outer scored location. This is an empirical screen,
not a formal guarantee. There are 147 native Torch fits.

Direct risk raises the original 4/606 dual-positive risk passes to
\SignedBridgeDirect/606. Donor calibration makes \SignedBridgeEdits{}
edits but reaches \SignedBridgeCal/606 versus \SignedBridgeControl/606
for independent utility at the same query counts. Among gain-positive
bridge candidates, \SignedBridgeFalseAdmits{} that pass the individual
predicted excess test fail their outcome-derived test. Substituting
outcome-derived risk only for analytic bridges yields
\SignedBridgeOracle/606 dual-positive passes. This is a diagnostic
intervention on risk, not an inference feature. Opening more edits
does not establish better safe-action selection or deployment.
