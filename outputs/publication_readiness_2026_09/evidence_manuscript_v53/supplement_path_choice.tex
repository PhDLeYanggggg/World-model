\section{Learning to Choose Among the Matched Paths}
Three seeds fit each of two shared utility heads on the same six
OpenDD path families, with donor predictions from location-excluded
models. The target is signed full-grid lower utility and positive
observed easy harm per unit radius, with a pairwise cost term.
The action-only head removes explicit history and CV displacement
but retains the normalization of the path. The frozen agreement
policy uses a candidate only when its utility and harm-penalized
rankings agree and the latter score exceeds hindcast. It otherwise
keeps hindcast at the same radius and recipient set. It is not
a calibrated certificate.

On the same exposed TUMDOT campaign at half radius, full agreement
loses \PathChoiceCV{} points against CV, \PathChoiceHindcast{}
against matched hindcast, and \PathChoiceAblation{} directly against
action-only agreement. The original risk passes are \PathChoiceFull/27,
\PathChoiceSimple/27 and \PathChoiceHand/27 for full, action-only and
hindcast. The failures of the full head have defined easy risk and
are concentrated at one intersection. No policy has both positive
mean CV utility and all risk passes. Donor predictions use individual models while
the target uses a frozen ensemble, so domain and ensemble effects
are not isolated. This is a negative development transport result,
not a new independent test.
