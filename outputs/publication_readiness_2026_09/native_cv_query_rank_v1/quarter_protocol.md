# Compare selection methods at the same quarter amplitude

The fixed-quarter follow-up gave a small positive lower interval for
CV ranking but did not protect easy cases. Apply that exact quarter to
all other registered nomination methods as well, without reranking,
except that the diagnostic oracle sees quarter-amplitude outcomes.
Compare the direct paired lower gain at equal counts; do not use
differences of separate lower bounds as paired evidence. No further
amplitudes are evaluated. This is a same-source explanatory readout.
