\section{Certified edit budgets and minimum-edit supervision}
We bound how many edits are needed, rather than treating the edit
count of one feasible solution as a requirement. Write $A x\geq b$
for the two conservative accuracy requirements, three risk limits
and the easy-support requirement, with at most one action per row.
For any nonnegative multiplier $\lambda$, a lower bound on the
minimum edit count is
\[
 b^\top\lambda +
 \sum_i \min\bigl(0,\min_a[1-A_{ia}^\top\lambda]\bigr).
\]
Deleting edits from a known feasible plan produces a verified upper
bound only after rescoring the remaining plan. Among the 53 views
with a full-bank witness, the median minimum lies between
\EditCertMedLow\% and \EditCertMedHigh\% of rows.

Budgets in this table apply to each entire recording. They do not
imply that a query-local controller can allocate edits equally well.

Separately, integer search reduces \EditCertTeachers{} verified
analytic training-role plans from \EditCertOldTeacher{} to
\EditCertNewTeacher{} edits. We train identical cost scorers on
dual gain and risk targets, with or without an auxiliary action
imitation loss from those plans. Both share the sampled inputs and
optimizer budgets (\EditCertUpdates{} updates in total). The model
uses only causal histories, query summaries, and forecast differences.
At \EditCertMatches{} exactly matched query edits, adding imitation
changes the conservative trajectory comparison by \EditCertMatch{}
pp, the known-step comparison by \EditCertMatchKnown{} pp, and
the conservative pair-distance contrast by \EditCertPair{} pixels
(95\% paired source-bootstrap intervals).
Risk pass counts (frozen, cost, teacher, matched teacher, guarded
teacher) are \EditCertPasses{} out of \EditCertViews{}. Calibration
qualifies \EditCertQual{} of nine cells. These are held development
comparisons, not independent confirmation.

Changing recipients accounts for \EditCertRecipientShare\% of the
missing-step disagreement debit. Fixing recipients to the cost-only
choices narrows the conservative deficit to \EditCertFixed{} pp.
Clipping each replacement to its reference displacement narrows it
further to \EditCertClipped{} pp, but it remains negative and does
not qualify a policy. These two interventions are retrospective
diagnostics, not validation-selected strategies.
