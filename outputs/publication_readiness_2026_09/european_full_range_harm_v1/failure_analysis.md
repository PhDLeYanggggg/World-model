# Wider Harm Range Did Not Repair Safe Generalization

All18 matched continuations and144 readout views completed. The registered

test demonstrates. It retains causal bounds and exactly protected benefit/
reference outputs. All18 initial real objective values match their old controls,
and initial weights, sampling, parameter counts and update budgets match.
It changes both reachable values and gradient geometry, not range alone.

## Why a Favorable Number Is Not Enough

The new policy selects31,270 held row occurrences versus24,204 before. Its
same-count contrast versus joint risk is+0.006613[-0.290089,0.314868], and it
does not have a positive lower confidence bound against ANY of the six controls.
All controls and sources remain in the report. Unknown outcomes receive the
original completion bounds, not optimistic labels. The13 failed conditions
are twelve utility comparisons plus full-policy support.

All intervals are3,000 locality-bootstrap draws over12 already exposed source
localities with repeated context/seed views. They are nominal and not independent
confirmation or simultaneous inference. Utility bounds are not ADE/FDE and
pair-specific bounds cannot be treated as transitive improvements.

## Next Discriminating Test

Before another fit, use only the existing inner-fit packets to measure which
examples have zero derivative from hard clipping, how the source scales affect
gradient magnitudes, and whether frozen benefit caps themselves exclude needed
harm moments. Compare against the odds branch on identical samples. Do not
repeat the previous broad gradient-conflict audit: this question is specifically
about the new bounded parameterization's saturation and conditioning.

Only after that check should one matched smooth, full-range parameterization
be registered, retaining the same risk target and six controls. Alternatively,
if fitted causal support is inadequate, stop treating parameterization as the
explanation and design a producer-excluded policy-conditional calibration study.
Neither direction is a demonstrated fix; do not tune against these held results.

Current blockers to a paper claim remain a reliable method effect, independent
scene evidence, safe selected-tail estimates and complete fair benchmarks.
More architecture names or longer training cannot substitute for those.
