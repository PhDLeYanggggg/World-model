\section{Pooling the Moving budget over an entire recording}
We ask whether the single-query limitation is merely temporal.
The experiment holds fixed the half-anchor count and sum of
individual CV-relative peak displacements, either within each
query or across the entire recording. These are ceilings, not
requirements to spend. The base bank includes the old half-anchor,
OLS4, OLS8, completion spline, EqMotion and PCT; the expanded
bank adds the forecasts from the preceding section. Each non-anchor
path is also interpolated from CV at fractions 0.10, 0.35 and 0.70.
The same \RecordPeakRecords{} development recordings,
\RecordPeakSources{} sources and \RecordPeakViews{} recording--seed
views are used. No new predictors are fitted.

\paragraph{An arbitrary-path upper bound.}
Relative to starting at CV, an edit with peak radius $r_i$
can improve any reference's observed-step gain by at most
$\min(r_i,\|y_{it}-\mathrm{CV}_{it}\|_2)$. At a missing step,
the reduction in disagreement debit is at most
$\min(r_i,\|\mathrm{Ref}_{it}-\mathrm{CV}_{it}\|_2)$.
For each reference separately these caps form a concave
piecewise-linear function in $r_i$. The relaxed optimum under
the sum-of-peaks constraint fills the segments in descending
query-weighted slope. The two maxima need not be attained by
the same paths. Neither count, trajectory shape nor recording
risk is imposed in this upper bound. Even so, both timing
budgets exclude positive OLS4 gain in
\RecordPeakExcluded{}/\RecordPeakViews{} views. The median OLS4
upper gain moves from \RecordPeakQueryMedian{} to
\RecordPeakRecordMedian{} CV-normalized pp with pooling.

On the \RecordPeakPositiveBudget{} views with nonzero original
budgets, the median minimum peak mass for an OLS4 tie is
\RecordPeakKnownMultiple{} times the old budget on available labels
and \RecordPeakDebitedMultiple{} times after missing-step debits.
This is a lower limit on the budget needed, not a proposal
to use that budget. It does not show what the errors would
have been if the missing positions were observed.

\paragraph{Finite-menu witnesses.}
We maximize the smaller of the two conservative gains with one
path per row, both budget ceilings, and the unchanged all,
easy and signed 2\% risk limits with nonempty reference support.
The initial search uses a linear relaxation and an integer search
with time limits. We recheck all \RecordPeakRechecked{} unresolved
views by freeing fractional rows and nearby rows ranked by
relaxation reduced costs. This restricted follow-up cannot prove
infeasibility. Every listed witness is replayed through the original
evaluator and the count and peak checks.
