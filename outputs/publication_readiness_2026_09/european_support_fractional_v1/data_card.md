# Support-Fractional Data Card

## Source and Roles

The study reuses the verified European source-development data and forecast
lineage from european_harm_tail_crossfit_v1. Its original mean controls,
normalization definitions, splits and causal schema are cached_verified.
The new fractional heads and their paired diagnostics are fresh_run when
completed. No independent confirmation or deployed policy result is implied.

Source A supplies the previously trained prediction/causal feature chain.
Within four B localities, train on three and hold one out. Refit normalization,
cost scale and the positive-CV-error25th-percentile easy cutoff on those three
only. This does not open selection/calibration/confirmation data. The twelve
source localities have previously been used for method development.

No source-C policy is evaluated in this round. Twelve reserved calibration
and six confirmation localities remain closed, as do the six already opened
selection localities for this particular experiment. Their historical roles
are not silently reset. Historical contaminated/test-selected Stage35/37/43/44
scores remain exploratory, not recertified by this new loss.

## Targets and Support

For known future labels, H=max(candidate ADE-reference ADE,0). The causal
envelope e is mean disagreement of the two forecast trajectories, so H<=e.
The auxiliary targets H/e and easy-event H/e where e>0. e=0 rows remain in
the base moment objective but have no fractional loss. Future labels never
enter the feature matrix or determine inference-time support.

Missing labels remain missing, not zero; they are not sampled. Weak event
support and unestimable metrics are reported. No observed high-harm tail is
discarded. Each fold's easy target differs with its train-only cut, so pooled
calibration or matched-target claims across different folds are prohibited.

Three-seed averages are formed within locality, followed by3,000 resamples of
four localities per ordered source assignment. Row overlap and repeated roles
do not supply independent sample size. Bootstrap comparisons are exploratory
and not multiplicity-adjusted. Tail capture, event ranking and mean-cost
accuracy answer different questions.

Coordinates are detector-derived image pixels; observation/prediction lengths
are8/12 annotation steps at raw stride12. This is not verified meters/seconds,
human gold, physical safety, true3D or foundation training. Stage5C and SMC
remain off. Raw tracks, arrays, histories, images and checkpoints stay private.
