# Progress Without Model Promotion

The proposed loss repair was implemented and actually trained, not left as a
plan. Its primary neural generalization hypothesis failed. The improvement on
damping strengthens the causal control and makes the neural claim harder, which
is useful evidence but not success of the requested world-model objective.

## Next Priority

Register source-separated calibration/support-aware fallback for the fixed
moment and signed-score families. Keep the2% budget, static safety guards,
forecaster bank and damping control. A held source must be absent from the
forecaster, head, preprocessing and calibration producer chain. Use only opened
training sources for this development test; the independent roles stay closed.

Avoid another arbitrary loss-weight or readout-threshold sweep. First establish
whether a genuinely excluded calibration source predicts the risk on another
held source and whether coverage remains useful. Zero interventions is fallback,
not a learned positive result. If calibration consumes the neural gain, inspect
source support/candidate-error variability before spending on a larger model.
