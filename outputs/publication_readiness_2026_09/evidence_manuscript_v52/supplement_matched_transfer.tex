\section{Matched-Radius Transport on TUMDOT-ING}
Frozen OpenDD models replay the original transfer exactly on nine
previously exposed recordings from one campaign, over three training
seeds. No TUMDOT fitting or threshold selection is performed. The
incumbent, earlier shape, tangent, finite and curvature variants,
and a causal hindcast share edit recipients and per-row maximum
displacements. At half the realized peak of the original blend,
tangent gains \MatchedTransferIncumbent{} percentage points directly
over the incumbent, but loses to matched hindcast
(\MatchedTransferHindcast{}) and to CV (\MatchedTransferCV{}).
Only \MatchedTransferRisk/27 tangent views pass the original risk
test. Quarter-radius edits pass all 27 views for every arm, but
none improves conservative utility over CV. Against CV, the
tangent's observed gain of \MatchedTransferObserved{} points
is offset by missing-step debits of \MatchedTransferPartial{}
and \MatchedTransferMissing{} on partially and wholly missing rows.

At half radius the label-aware minimum-risk policy on the same
six paths meets both the risk rule and positive conservative CV
utility in \MatchedTransferWitness/27 views, with mean lower utility
\MatchedTransferWitnessUtility{} points. Maximizing the CV lower
score instead gives \MatchedTransferOptUtility{} points and
\MatchedTransferOptRisk/27 risk passes. This separates the existence
of useful matched paths from the ability to choose them causally.
Neither hindsight diagnostic is deployable, and none of these
recordings provides independent confirmation.
