## What Has Already Been Tried

The older `source_visual_start_v1` trains small RGB-vs-coverage CNN probes on
SDD/ETH stationary-history queries. It is a separate source/task. Its
equal-agent effects are negative and its main cohorts are small. It neither
establishes an image-feature lift for this European SOURCE experiment nor
rules out stronger pretrained visual representations. No such current SOURCE
pixel encoder has been evaluated in the cost-bank or amplitude controls.

## Actionable Requirement

A genuine current-cohort visual-feature experiment needs original recording
identity, camera/view correspondence, frame timing, permitted use and a
past-only extraction rule. First seek a matching publisher-provided resource
or an already admitted training source with actual frames. Do not silently
substitute a new webcam view, open DroneCrowd/independent roles or change the
primary split to make an image experiment possible. New input acquisition
and causal alignment must precede claims of multimodal predictive lift.

The present task remains SOURCE detector-track midpoint pixels, obs8/pred12,
stride12 raw frames. Publisher georeferencing metadata is not validation of
our pixel adapter as metric, seconds-level or human gold. No deployment,
Stage5C, SMC, true-3D, foundation or submission-readiness claim follows.
