# Statistical Interpretation

The prospective primary is selected-context full-joint minus zero-actor full-joint
**full completion-lower ADE utility**,not the known-label difference or whichever
context arm performs best. Own floor-relative lower utility and complete original
recording risk are additional requirements. All three are needed for SOURCE fitting
advancement;even a pass would not establish independent confirmation.

Intervals use3,000paired SOURCE-locality bootstrap draws with seed20261101.
Repeated contexts and critic/actor seeds are averaged within locality before
resampling. There are12exposed localities,not72independent groups. These are
conditional,nominal development intervals after a long adaptive research history.
They do not address all method-selection uncertainty or provide a simultaneous
familywise guarantee over secondary policies,components and slices.

Known-label and completion-bound contrasts have different meanings. A positive
known gain can coexist with a negative lower bound because policies disagree on
unobserved outcomes. Completion-upper values are uncertainty bounds,not observed
errors. Wholly-unknown envelopes do not solve partially observed interiors.
FDE with absent requested endpoints stays not_estimable. None of these quantities
licenses substituting a pooled mean for each original recording's safety criterion.

The three seeds vary critic/actor learning while sharing forecast producer43;
they are not three independent trajectory training pipelines. Self-context and
matched-count policies are registered component controls. Empty matched comparisons
have no estimable conditional risk;they do not make the full policy safe.

The complete readout retains all149,510targets and74recordings. Per-record risk,
undefined support,known easy tails,unknown selected mass and all11policies must
remain visible. A lower confidence limit crossing zero is inconclusive,not proof
of a negative population effect. Source-only significance is not cross-scene
confirmation. No independent calibration/confirmation role is opened by this study.
