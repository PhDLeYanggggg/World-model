# Remaining Gap To The Research Objective

The original objective remains a credible, independently evaluated research
method and complete submission materials, not a passing runtime demo. This
experiment is complete but the project objective is not achieved.

1. **Useful admission is not established.** The source-disjoint cost model
   underestimates selected harm, loses to its matched control and fails the
   original recording-level contract. Test a better harm-estimation model,
   not a relaxed metric or another uncalibrated mean-cost veto.
2. **Reference support remains a data limitation.** Model training cannot
   create missing observed easy-reference mass. Under the retained rules,
   those views remain unavailable; do not promote identity fallback to a new
   pass or force interventions only to fill an efficacy denominator.
3. **Independent confirmation is still absent.** Current source groups are
   exposed development and not verified independent physical scenes.
   Reserved data stay closed under their existing role restrictions. No
   cached or repeatedly tuned result is renamed as a new independent test.
4. **Method contribution needs evidence.** Source splitting, multiple risk
   constraints and generic calibration are established ideas. A contribution
   must show useful joint multi-agent admission beyond strong matched
   controls, with the required risk preservation and uncertainty accounting.
5. **World-dynamics contribution is not proved by this study.** No new
   trajectory dynamics, scene/goal contribution, JEPA lift, cross-dataset
   success, verified time or geometry follows from these cost-head fits.
6. **The paper is not ready for submission.** The existing draft and this
   negative addendum do not establish a successful main claim. Independent
   primary evidence, contribution validation, complete final figures,
   anonymous reproducibility material and final manuscript checking remain.

event/conditional-magnitude head against the frozen point model, with own-fit
preprocessing and a real pilot. Keep the same required views and original
2% contract. Register the comparison before new scores. This hypothesis has
not been trained or validated and is not presented as an achieved result.

No new deployment, Stage5C execution, SMC, true 3D, foundation, metric,
seconds-level or human-gold claim. Formal submission still requires the
