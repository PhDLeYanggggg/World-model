violations without a supported utility gain. Before another objective rewrite,
test whether the original frozen forest's harm-bearing source rows concentrate
in incomplete or unstable detector-derived trajectories. This is a descriptive
diagnostic, not a new model or an independent confirmation experiment.

## Registered Readout

reference mass, easy harm/reference and unknown disagreement envelope. Undefined
ratios remain undefined. Primary descriptive summary: fraction of selected harm
mass arising on all12-label rows, averaged over heads/controllers within each
locality, then over localities. Also report pooled occurrence counts separately
from distinct row, recording-query and tracker counts.

For known harmful selections, calculate whether removing any single available
time point reverses/ties the mean signed error, and whether early/late halves
disagree when both are observed. Singleton leave-one-out is undefined, not stable.
These are temporal label-sensitivity diagnostics, not independent label draws or
proof of annotation noise. Do not recalculate the official metric after removal.

Compare harmful-minus-nonharmful selected means for the seven fixed past proxies
and three offline future proxies in the config. Include same-recording/frame
paired means when both cohorts exist. Use 3,000 locality-cluster bootstrap draws;
aggregate repeated heads/controllers within locality first. All intervals are
nominal exposed-development associations, with no multiple-comparison correction.
No proxy is promoted as a causal explanation or a selected feature from these CIs.
