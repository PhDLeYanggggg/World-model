# Causal Recipient and Amplitude Allocation

Keep the hash-verified OpenDD incumbent direction, seven development folds
and three seeds. Train on pair-excluded donor forecasts. Do not alter the
2% readout, open external outcomes, or admit a deployment policy.

Each query can edit up to two distinct pedestrians (but not everyone),
at 1/4, 1/2 or all of the incumbent displacement. An arm may abstain.
Predict observed gain and positive all/easy harm, reference mass, and
missing support. The direct comparator regresses debited utility and
harm. The factorized model instead predicts wholly missing probability
and displacement-weighted missing fraction separately, then multiplies
by the exact proposed displacement. Compare factorized predictions
with and without rolling past-support geometry. The direct comparator
has the same prefix geometry; use fixed tree settings, no held selection.
The prefix includes only current histories and eight previous queries
from that recording, never label availability or future query count.

All allocation variants carry only predicted reservations forward.
Both predicted all/easy reference masses must be positive and predicted
risks at most 2%. The joint arm penalizes near-pedestrian compression,
with fixed weight 0.25; the independent arm has no pair penalty.
Record all three risk terms, including signed easy risk, on observed
outcomes afterwards using the unchanged original scorer. Empty
reference remains undefined. Predicted risk is not a guarantee.

For the primary utility comparison, replay independent and additive
geometry choices at exactly the joint arm's number of edits and
**actual per-target radius multiset within each query**. Reassign a radius
only to a target whose incumbent supports it, using linear interpolation
of its predicted utility between registered levels. These matched
controls do not enforce the original joint policy's predicted ledger.
Compare the joint forecast to each with pair-specific missing-step
debits, then compare geometry. A required seed/site view with no edits
is unestimable, not a tie or safety pass. Report direct, factorized,
prefix and joint operating arms plus CV and full incumbent.

Fixed roster, 8 observations / 12 predictions in release coordinates.
Three seeds averaged before 3,000 paired location bootstrap draws.
No new fit on held locations, no policy selection from results, no
DroneCrowd access, deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
