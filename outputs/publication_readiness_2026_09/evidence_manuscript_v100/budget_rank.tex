\section{Ranking at a Fixed Intervention Budget}

The original causal clock allocates both roles. At each
released query, the model can change the recipients and
directions, but not the number of edits or the per-role peak.
Training regresses direct gain and missing-step debit relative
to the original slate, plus recording-risk labels. The
ranking term weights a preference only when its completion
interval excludes zero. This uses the decision-focused
ranking idea~\cite{mandi2022rank}, not a new general ranking loss.
The geometry control retrains with four relative-path
features masked.

The direct lower contrast against point regression is
\BudgetRankPrimaryEffect{}; against the original policy it is
\BudgetRankOriginalEffect{}. Geometry improves the matched
ablation contrast by \BudgetRankGeometryEffect{}. The original,
point, rank, masked and guarded policies pass
\BudgetRankOriginalJoint{}, \BudgetRankPointJoint{},
\BudgetRankRankJoint{}, \BudgetRankMaskedJoint{} and
\BudgetRankGuardJoint{} views, respectively. A frozen
policy replay attributes all \BudgetRankLost{} lost and
\BudgetRankNew{} new easy references to changed
recipients, not shape-only changes. The risk rule is unchanged
and no ranker qualifies for deployment.
