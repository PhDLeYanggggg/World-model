## Question And Controlled Comparison

Can separately supervising observed costs and label support avoid the target
interference of composed-bound regression, and can joint decisions use the
original action bank without breaking the retained per-recording contract?
The older generic observation-support model failed and is retained. The
decomposition and existing allocator are not themselves novelty claims.

## Interpretation And Limits

Observed conditional/composed five-moment MSE and support calibration improve
within the matched model family, while bound composed MSE worsens. The frozen
known-only head still has lower conditional signed MSE on its matched proposal
cohort. Therefore the result is not a universal regression-quality improvement.
Joint optimization exposes selected-reference/harm optimism:61/72easyreferences
are overpredicted,35/72easyharmsunderpredicted, and seven easy violations select
no unknown rows. The source-held selected-risk problem remains unresolved.
