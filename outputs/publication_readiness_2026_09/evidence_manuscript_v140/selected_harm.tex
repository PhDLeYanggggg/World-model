\section{Harm prediction for nominated edits}
Each eligible row gets one fixed path: its frozen parent action or
its best independent alternative. Two harm heads use identical
gains and \SelectedHarmUpdates{} total fitting updates but different
pinball quantiles (0.50 or 0.85). Both rerank \SelectedHarmEdits{}
edits at matched query counts, not matched displacement.

All-harm exceedance on parent nominations averages
\SelectedHarmExceed{}\% by source. Risk passes for parent/median/tail are
\SelectedHarmPasses{} out of 72; their worst known signed easy
changes are \SelectedHarmWorst{}\%. The tail head loses
\SelectedHarmParent{} conservative pp against the parent and
\SelectedHarmMedian{} against the median head (3,000 paired source
resamples). Calibration qualifies \SelectedHarmQual{}/9 cells.

Ignoring risk, the exact lower-score maximum for one comparator
adds the best $k$ row increments to the fallback intercept.
At-most counts use positive increments only. Nonpositive OLS4
bounds number \SelectedHarmCapacity{} out of 72: one path, analytic
menu, and full menu (all at exact counts), then the full menu at
at-most and free counts. These hindsight maxima do not establish
joint feasibility against both comparators under the risk rule.
