# Primary Reading And Method Scope

Material Passport:fresh primary-source reading on30October2026;local prior
experiments are cached_verified.Cited work was inspected for the stated
scope only.This is not a completed literature survey or a novelty certificate.

[Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.02814v4)
controls expected loss using an exchangeable calibration construction and a
bounded monotone loss family.This fitting-only asymmetric training objective
does not implement that procedure.It cannot turn predicted cost scores into
a conformal guarantee or replace the required independent calibration role.
Our selected-harm/reference ratios also need their own justified loss and
dependence treatment;the paper's guarantee is not inherited by naming a head
conservative.

[Selective Regression under Fairness Criteria](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/shah22a.html)
shows that reducing coverage can worsen subgroup regression performance.
This supports checking every required recording and the easy subset rather
than claiming safety from a pooled average or fewer interventions alone.
Its fairness setting and criteria are not identical to our frozen relative-
baseline positive-harm contract.

Local predecessors already tested shared reference moments,signed-budget-
score regression alone,selected means and teacher-fixed region moments.
The present narrow contrast keeps identifiable five-moment regression while
adding a coupled signed-budget score penalty,with a symmetric counterpart.
Asymmetry and multitask losses are not novel by themselves.A paper-worthy
contribution still needs effective neural intervention,scene-joint decisions,
independent risk evidence and stronger external comparisons.This experiment
only tests whether this specific learning mismatch is repairable.
