\section{Learning the query count and the joint edit}
A shared analytic bank produces whole-query alternatives at zero,
25, 50, 75, and 100 percent of eligible rows and at the prior count.
Two query scorers learn paired conservative utility, risk and
relative-geometry targets for the same options
(\JointCountUpdates{} updates); only pair inputs differ.
At \JointCountEdits{} matched edits, the conservative contrast
against independent selection is \JointCountInd{} pp, and
against the no-pair scorer it is \JointCountPair{} pp.
Free versus fixed counts gives \JointCountCountKnown{} pp on
observed steps but \JointCountCount{} pp under missing-step bounds
(95\% paired source-bootstrap intervals). Original risk passes
\JointCountPasses{}/72 and worst signed easy change is
\JointCountWorst{}\%; calibration qualifies \JointCountQual{}/9.

With outcomes, a recording LP relaxes discrete query alternatives
to mixtures, keeping both gain comparisons, the three original risk
inequalities, and easy support. Its dual yields an upper bound; a
rounded plan is credited only after the original scorer verifies it.
Verified fixed/variable-count views are \JointCountVerified{}.
The variable-count menu has \JointCountFailed{} nonpositive
upper bounds, infeasible relaxations, and unsupported views,
respectively. Broader counts help in hindsight, but this menu
and learned rule do not establish a usable policy.
