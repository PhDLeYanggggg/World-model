\section{Trajectory Shape at Fixed Intervention Slots}

We test whether a new trajectory shape can help without changing the
intervention clock, recipient, or radius. We retrieve the eight nearest
velocity histories from other OpenDD sites in a heading-aligned, past
RMS-step normalized frame. We average their complete future deviations
from constant velocity (CV), map the result into the recipient frame,
and rescale it to the original peak displacement from CV. This is a
candidate generator, not a novelty claim about forecast memory; for
example, \href{https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Marchetti_MANTRA_Memory_Augmented_Networks_for_Multiple_Trajectory_Prediction_CVPR_2020_paper.html}{MANTRA}
retrieves trajectory embeddings for multimodal prediction.

The prefix control decides whether to use the new candidate by scoring
forecast errors on suffixes of the eight observed steps. We fit
\ShapeFits{} heads to predict the direct conservative gain over the
original shape and changes in the three risk numerators. Inner fits
exclude both the outer site and their inner validation site, and the
retrieval bank and cuts for each fit row also exclude its source site.
The gain rule and the risk-penalized rule select thresholds on these
out-of-fold donor predictions. The latter requires nonpositive changes in
all three donor-macro risk numerators on legal row/amplitude options;
these proxy checks are not recording-level certification.

The direct conservative gains over the prefix rule are
\ShapeGainEffect{} for gain only and \ShapeRiskEffect{} for risk aware
(3,000 site bootstrap draws after seed averaging). Neither improves
utility reliably. The latter reverts to the original shape for all but
\ShapeRiskChoices{} slots. The outcome-aware utility chooser reaches
\ShapeOracleEffect{}, without optimizing the risk gate.

Optimizing the lesser of the two conservative gains under the original
risk gate, with outcomes known and the same two choices at each slot,
qualifies at most \ShapeCapacity{} views. We check each witness with the
original scorer. \ShapeUndefined{} views lack selected easy support
(\ShapeNoEdit{} with no edit and \ShapeNoEasy{} with edited rows but no
positive easy reference), \ShapeUtilityMissing{} cannot improve on both
references even without risk constraints, and \ShapeRiskBlocks{} would
improve without the risk constraints but cannot qualify. Neither a
different shape nor a threshold repairs missing selected reference
support at these fixed slots. This is a limit of this menu and
assignment, not a bound on other recipients or trajectory families.
No deployment decision or independent confirmation follows from it.
