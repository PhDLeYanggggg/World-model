# Local Conditional Means Do Not Repair Selection Risk

We test whether recent cost-head failures are principally parametric by
using coupled other-recording k32 estimates on the same seven forecasts.
Fixed controls compare known-only, completion endpoints, global means and
within-recording target shuffling. All 288 comparison groups and a separate
full retrieval/readout replay complete. The policy is deterministic; three
seeds index frozen MLP comparators, not independently trained retrieval models.

Local completion costs gain 0.830% on known fitting ADE versus 0.581% for the
fresh known-only MLP. Yet complete risk support is 30/444 versus 77/444, and
completion-lower change is +0.006pp, 95% fitting CI [-0.722,+0.635]. Count-matched
improvement is also unsupported. A global mean gains 0.004% and leaves 123 easy
risk views undefined. No secondary is promoted after the primary fails.

The mixed local bank has a small positive completion-lower increment over
physical-only,+0.0409pp[+0.0012,+0.0866]. This limited fitting signal remains
visible, but does not establish a deployable neural contribution. A separate
posthoc control permutes targets within recording and action-eligibility
strata. Real matching gains +0.140pp on known utility, CI [-0.227,+0.496], while
completion-lower change is -0.122pp, CI [-0.482,+0.230]. Fine matching therefore
does not show a stable advantage under this specified representation.

Necessary consistency inequalities and a nonparametric learner do not suffice
for reliable selected risk. This is not an impossibility theorem or unique
causal attribution to record priors. Source weights are fixed on the full
causal fitting pool, not rebalanced after each recording exclusion. Forecasts
and normalizers were exposed to fitting data. Twelve-locality intervals are
nominal development evidence, not independent generalization, whole-pipeline
OOF or repeated-selection-corrected inference.

The evidence concerns oblique detector-silver image pixels and raw-frame
obs8/pred12, not metric/seconds, human gold, true3D, foundation or a newly
successful world-dynamics model. Independent roles stay closed. Stage5C/SMC
remain off. This is a negative/limited-result paper section, not proof of
submission readiness.
