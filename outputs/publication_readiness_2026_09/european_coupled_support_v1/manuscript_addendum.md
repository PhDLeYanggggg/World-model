# Exploratory Negative-Result Addendum

## Material Passport

Internal source-development evidence; not a confirmatory main-table result.
No independent scene or fully out-of-fold upstream evaluation is claimed.

We constrained locally estimated total and easy harm jointly and evaluated a
donor-record-excluded convex blend with a frozen original risk estimator. Across
72 exposed-training views, joint constraints eliminated9,019 order violations.
The blend reduced easy signed-risk mean-squared error by26.09% relative to the
previous independent local-cost fit, but remained5.69% worse than the original
estimator. Only1/18 context/head-seed pairs beat both controls. The learned
per-query blend also underperformed a recording-mean blend control, so the
experiment does not establish a conditional-reliability contribution.

The result separates moment consistency from risk predictability. Nominal
32-neighbor fits often had much lower recording-level effective support, but
low support did not fully explain the inconsistent or inaccurate estimates.
All fixed-roster source/context/seed summaries and negative outcomes are
retained. These are source-normalized risk-estimation errors, not trajectory
ADE/FDE improvements. No deployment or statistical safety certificate follows.

Remaining methods work must test easy-specific objective conflict and the
transferability of causal risk features without tuning future query outcomes.
Independent calibration and confirmation must follow a genuinely disjoint
whole-pipeline protocol before any formal generalization claim.
