# Leakage Check and Evidence Boundary

The new experiment stays on twelve already opened source-training localities.
It does not open independent selection, calibration or confirmation. Source
readout is a held fitting-chain role, not an untouched scientific test set.

The source loader verifies the prior forecaster seal and every bound artifact.
Each of three producer groups has four localities. Controller and readout are
the two other groups, giving six ordered assignments. The learned forecaster is
absent from its controller and readout localities; head preprocessing, targets,
sampling and optimization use only the controller group. The readout belongs to
neither. Existing producer-training easy/hard cutoffs are unchanged.

The 355-feature inference function accepts only causal geometry, CV rollout and
candidate rollout. Training futures form cost labels; future masks control loss
and scoring support, never inference. The pointwise and joint decision functions
accept predicted cost moments and observed geometry, not labels. Hash-selected
scene queries use only locality, recording and frame keys. Matched intervention
counts are selected without labels. Numerical failures explicitly revert to CV.

Registration commit7194f8a3 precedes training. Decision freeze06f93eeb precedes
the comparative readout. The full108-head score replay and all36decision groups
reproduce exactly. Zero unknown-label rows were sampled for training. A separate
reporting-boundary regression test verifies the frozen float64 threshold, rather
than adjusting the policy to a reporting mismatch.

This passes the scoped producer/controller/readout exclusion and causal-input
checks. It does not undo prior source-development exposure, establish source
independence, certify calibration under arbitrary shift, or rehabilitate historic
Stage35/37/43/44 deployment claims. No metric/seconds or physical-safety inference
follows from the audit. No new image/goal construction, Stage5C or SMC occurs.
