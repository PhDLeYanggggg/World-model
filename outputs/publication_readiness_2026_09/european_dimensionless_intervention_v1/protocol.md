# Frozen Predictor, Matched Intervention Study

## Question and Scope

Does the already improved dimensionless forecaster retain a useful advantage
once an equally trained causal intervention controller protects both it and
fixed damping? This is source-development evidence, not independent confirmation.
The prior predictor benefit is not a safety certificate. The preceding turn
only acknowledged delegated authorization and made no experimental progress.

Only the twelve already opened European source-training localities are used.
The three four-locality rosters are inherited. Each of six ordered producer /
controller / readout assignments excludes the readout from the whole fitting
chain. Seeds 17/29/43 give eighteen role-seed views per candidate. Repeated
producer contexts and overlapping windows are not independent samples.
Independent selection, risk calibration and confirmation remain closed.

## Fixed Experiment

Compare the frozen dimensionless neural bank with fixed damping (existing
baseline index3). Both are candidates relative to constant velocity (index1),
not an oracle-selected fallback. The previously training-selected strongest
causal reference is also reported. No forecaster is retrained.

For each candidate and role-seed view train three existing envelope-bounded
64-wide Torch heads: positive benefit/harm, total reference-cost/harm moments,
and positive-easy reference-cost/harm moments. Exactly108 heads,2000updates
each,batch256,AdamW lr0.0003,clip5. A100-update first-head pilot resumes inside
the fixed budget. Every checkpoint has an identity, sampler/RNG and optimizer
state. Means, scales and sampling are fitted on controller localities only.
The causal355-feature schema contains observed target/neighbor histories,
full CV/candidate rollouts and observed scale. No target or future mask enters
inference. Labels use controller futures for training only. All-risk labels
include zero-CV-error cases; positive-easy labels retain the existing definition.
The easy/hard cutoffs remain those fixed from each producer's source training.

Fixed pointwise policy: moving last observed step, predicted benefit greater
than harm, and both all/easy predicted harm moments at most2% of their predicted
reference-cost moments. Also report training-constant moment selection and raw
unprotected candidates. No held outcome tunes thresholds, epochs or models.
This predicted constraint is not conformal certification or a guarantee on
observed relative error. Undefined zero-reference ratios remain absolute costs.

Scene controls use96hash-selected recording/frame queries per locality with
the existing query salt, all indexed agents in each selected query. Compare
pointwise, whole-scene uniform, half-cap independent, unary-geometry and full
pairwise joint decisions. Independent optimization determines the label-free
exact count used by unary and joint controls. Pair weight0.1, radius3median
current bbox widths, proximity threshold0.5width, solver2seconds, fraction0.5
are inherited diagnostics, not fitted thresholds or physical collision units.
Solver failures and zero/missing matches stay explicit. These query-subset
metrics cannot be represented as full-bank joint evaluation. Geometry proxies
do not certify physical validity. Joint inference reads no labels.

## Frozen Readout and Decision Rule

Commit registration before training and decision artifact hashes before reading
comparative outcomes. Primary contrast is full-bank pointwise neural vs equally
protected damping ADE. Average producer contexts and seeds inside each locality,
then equal-weight the fixed12localities. Paired locality bootstrap3000draws,
seed71431. Also report all/hard/easy/zero-reference, FDE, intervention, tail error,
worst locality, native positive harm, and matched joint comparisons on the fixed
query subset. Report all three seeds, not a selected seed. Missing-support CIs
stay undefined rather than silently dropping sites.

Exploratory useful-safe screen requires positive lower primaryCI, positive
neural gain over CV, no locality positive-easy degradation beyond2%, no added
zero-reference harm, and nonnegative hard gain. Joint benefit is a separate
matched-count contrast with its own support diagnostics; raw forecast improvement
does not satisfy either screen. No outcome authorizes deployment. Calibration
and final confirmation still need independent-source evidence.
