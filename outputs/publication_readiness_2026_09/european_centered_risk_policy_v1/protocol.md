# Fitting-Only Centered Risk: Equal-Count Policy Experiment

## Material Passport

Development follow-up to a verified frozen-action diagnosis and exact
fitting-only bias probe. These outcomes informed the hypothesis, so this is
not independent confirmation. The entire inference/action pipeline is committed
before new held-development policy readout. Independent roles remain closed.

## One Changed Factor

Use the already fitted two nonnegative signed-score offsets from each of216
frozen risk heads. No further fit or threshold search. Add the offsets to the
normalized all/easy signed-risk scores; leave the forecast, protected floor,
utility, eligibility, features, time units, labels and4/4/2/2roles unchanged.
Keep both pointwise and aggregate parent families; no post-readout winner.

At each current recording/frame query, the centered independent set contains
eligible rows whose two centered scores are nonpositive. It is a subset of the
old independent set. Its count is fixed for each joint solve. Compare:

1. Frozen original independent/joint actions (cached verified).
2. Centered independent and centered joint actions.
3. Original-score joint optimization at exactly the centered count, using the
   centered independent set as a feasible anchor. Utility is unchanged.
4. Same-count utility top-k without a risk constraint, diagnostic only.
5. Whole-query uniform centered admission, diagnostic and not rate matched.
6. Protected floor and ungated neural predictions.

Use the same checked MILP solver/node budget256. Fall back to the common
feasible anchor if a solve or numerical check fails. No labels, future fields,
validity masks or test statistics enter these decisions. Unknown rows remain
in inference and selection counts; they are excluded only from outcome costs.

## Evaluation and Interpretation

Evaluate all108groups and216locality views per policy on the same twelve opened
development localities, three forecasting seeds. Main *development contrasts*
are centered-joint versus matched-raw-joint for each parent arm. Secondary
contrasts compare centered joint with its original joint policy and independent
anchor. No family is selected on these results.

Report ADE/FDE, hard/failure and easy error, positive harm, captured and lost
benefit, selected-harm ratio, intervention/unknown rates, tail and worst-source
error. Attribute aggregate changes to added/removed/shared action sets. Report
the original2%selected-risk screen unchanged; empty denominators remain
undefined, not zero-risk certificates. Full-floor-denominator harm is a
descriptive secondary diagnostic, never a substitute risk tolerance.

First average dependent views within locality; then3,000paired locality
bootstrap draws, seed101531. No CI when a full-roster metric is incomplete.
Intervals are nominal development evidence, not multiplicity-adjusted formal
claims. Matched-count benefit and harm improvements must be distinguished from
benefits of abstaining more. Retain all negative results and all localities.

The original structurally incomplete selected-risk primary is not replaced or
retroactively repaired. No new independent calibration claim or deployment can
follow from this development experiment alone, even if some contrasts improve.
There is no new collision label or verified physical-validity model; joint
selection here constrains statistical cost, not physical safety.
