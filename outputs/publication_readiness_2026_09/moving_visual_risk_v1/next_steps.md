# Next Model Repair, Not A New Risk Rule

## Established In This Experiment

Past images are available and aligned well enough to build and replay causal
features on the complete permitted cohort. Four matched real neural models,
three risk-head seeds and all complete refits have actually run. No-image,
bounds, quality and motion are separated; the controller's original recording
checks fail for every arm. The motion component's primary interval crosses
zero. No promotion, new outer readout or threshold search is justified.

## Most Informative Remaining Failure

Better observability Brier coexists with worse held-source conditional gain,
harm and reference errors. A fully labelled tail case has an accurate support
prediction but extreme harm underprediction. Simply adding more confidence to
the support head or counting identity fallback cannot repair this error.
Training loss alone is also not enough: every motion/quality pair fits better.

## Shortest Model-Side Route

1. Use producer-A source-held diagnostics to distinguish conditional harm
   transfer from retained forecast error; keep own-B as exposed development.
   Compare predicted versus actual harm/reference in common causal scale,
   including high-disagreement and partial-label strata. Do not select a
   deployment threshold from the current own-B failures.
2. Before another large controller search, test whether a past-image-conditioned
   trajectory predictor produces better whole12-step candidates on source-held
   A donors. Keep the old actor as a matched control and preserve its negative
   prefix-balanced result. Motion compensation is standard prior art, not a
   novelty claim. This experiment has not trained such an actor.
3. If candidates improve, register a new source-held harm/reference model and
   regenerate only legal OOF supervised costs. Confirm the complete original
   2%/support/NA contract before reopening any independent confirmation. A
   positive average or a smaller optical-flow distance is not the pass criterion.

## Remaining Paper Gaps

Independent source confirmation, three forecaster training seeds, a method
contribution beyond standard motion processing and a successful full recording
risk contract remain missing. The current bootstrap is exploratory after past
exposure, not a new test. Pixel8/12results do not establish meters, seconds,
true3D or foundation modeling. Stage5C execution and SMC remain off.
