# Forecasting Improves, Admission Remains Unresolved

## What Is Positive

The complete fixed-budget SOURCE experiment produces a stronger observed-label
forecast. EqMotion gains 17.2138% available-label ADE relative to CV on the
original population. Its registered difference versus original PCT is +14.5236
pp [9.5506, 22.1861]. Pre-readout secondary controls prevent crediting only a weak
earlier PCT: differences are +8.7522 pp versus dimensionless PCT on that same
population and +4.6779 pp [1.3917, 7.4120] versus current history-PCT on the
unchanged controller whitelist. The two populations are reported separately.

These are actual three-seed Torch fits, not metadata-only selection, a NumPy
fallback or oracle performance. All primary and stronger-control metric groups
and the prescribed full forecasts replay. The published backbone remains an
existing method; this improvement is not a novel architecture contribution.

## What Does Not Pass

The lower completion contrasts are +5.7879, +2.2097 and -21.9971 pp, with all
three intervals crossing zero. The current-history comparison has a known
negative locality, 082 (-9.6668 pp), and especially large unknown-row prediction
disagreement there. Uncertainty about missing future outcomes is not measured
catastrophic error. No favorable locality or complete-case subset replaces the
full registered roster.

Positive average easy gain (+0.9153% relative to CV on the original population)
does not establish every-record positive-harm or finite easy-reference support.
No new admission policy has been trained or passed the original 2% contract.
The old cost-loss/forest/width negatives remain negative and are not repaired
merely by presenting this better forecasting backbone.

The fit-selected physical baseline averages -0.8388% versus CV on excluded
SOURCE groups, while fixed damped velocity 0.97 averages +3.9755%. Thus fitting
selection itself need not transport. All fixed candidates are retained; the
observed ranking does not retroactively change the floor or prove a unique
cause of the earlier cost-head failure.

## What Is Still Unidentified

The comparisons do not isolate geometric equivariance from architecture,
capacity, input conditioning, output parameterization or neighbor processing.
They do not prove JEPA lift, a new Transformer contribution, scene-image use,
goal reasoning, globally consistent interactions or true physical dynamics.
SOURCE exclusion is useful development evidence but not a new untouched test;
all SOURCE sites have informed research. More favorable observed error alone
does not establish calibrated selected-cost predictions or missing-label safety.
