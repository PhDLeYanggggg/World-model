# What Improved, What Still Failed

These are completed, source-excluded development comparisons on already exposed
MovingDroneCrowd++ SOURCE. They are not an independent test or a deployment claim.
All results use the original rules, including the 2% risk limit, easy-net limit
and support requirements. No identity fallback certification was added.

## 1. Mean Routing Utility Is Learnable

The public cost-sensitive router gains 12.295% available-label ADE over the
physical floor (95% source-bootstrap CI 6.937 to 17.878). Its hard-subset gain is
14.800% (9.250 to 21.089). The known-label contrast against the original point
proposer is +5.249 percentage points (1.307 to 9.551), using the common floor
denominator within each source. This is not a5.249%relative error reduction
against the point proposer, and not a raw-frame t50 result.

The conservative floor-comparison completion lower is +4.209% (0.165 to 8.290).
However, the **direct** public-minus-point completion lower is -2.410pp
(-6.139 to 1.249). Subtracting two marginal lower bounds is not a valid substitute
for the direct paired bound. Missing outcomes still prevent the stronger
comparative claim. Partial-label ADE is not fully observed twelve-step ADE.

This is a useful public-method control, not an original M3W loss or a new
trajectory model. Parameterization and effective supervised support differ:
the original point head used envelope supervision for wholly unknown outcomes,
whereas the public surrogate excludes them. The result cannot identify the
algebraic loss change alone as the cause.

## 2. Average Error Is Not Selected Positive Harm

The unguarded public router switches on65.828%of rows, source-macro, versus
56.689%for the point proposer. It still has514known all-risk violations and
582known easy-risk violations among696repeated recording views. Only14views
satisfy the entire original contract. Gains elsewhere cannot cancel positive
harm on an easy agent in this contract.

The public objective optimizes predictive cost, not the selected harm/reference
ratio. Its positive mean result therefore does not demonstrate safe admission.
Its worst defined signed recording easy-net degradation is579.881%, not an
overall dataset mean. Thirty required recording views have no defined full
known easy-net reference; none is replaced by a favorable zero.

## 3. The Frozen Risk Veto Does Not Resolve The Problem

With the same independent frozen point critic, public-minus-point known effect
falls to +0.817pp (-1.047 to 2.931); the direct completion lower is -0.780pp
(-2.679 to 0.594). These full policies have different coverage and do not isolate
a ranking effect. Public guarded coverage is29.346%, versus26.961%for guarded
point routing.

The public guarded policy supports38/696recording views, with454known all-risk
violations,483known easy-risk violations and144undefined selected easy-risk
views. Worst defined signed recording easy-net degradation is469.929%.
The point guarded control supports44/696views. A marginal mean gain is not
evidence that this fixed critic is reliable on the new selected distribution.

At the same query-level count and amplitude histogram, the public veto's
full-strategy **secondary** contrast against its own utility-ranked control is
-0.807pp known (-1.747 to -0.245), and -2.176pp direct lower
(-3.628 to -1.184). The required nonempty matched comparison remains NA because
8/276source views are empty. The original24group primary also remains NA.
The matched control itself fails risk, so this is not evidence that simply
removing the veto produces a safe method. Risk/support diagnostics are mixed,
not uniformly ordered, and counts overlap.

## 4. Scene Heterogeneity And Evidence Limits Remain

There are both positive and negative source contrasts. For example, direct
public-minus-point known effect is +30.907pp on source055 and -12.572pp on
source078. These are descriptions, not a basis for dropping scenes or choosing
domain-specific thresholds. All23source units,108orientations and696recording
views remain in the report. They are not696independent samples. Bootstrap uses
3000resamples after within-source seed/context/orientation averaging; verified
IID sites and independent confirmation are not established.

No history, source role, threshold, predictor or risk rule was changed after
readout. The old point control reproduces108prediction/action blocks and828
source-policy readouts exactly, making silent control drift an unlikely
explanation for this comparison. No generative execution or SMC was enabled.

## Next Model Experiment

The next justified model change is **proposal-conditioned risk learning**:
fit the critic on actions proposed by a separate source-excluded router, retain
the original unknown-outcome bounds, and test whether calibration improves on
the actions actually admitted. Its router, critic and held validation sources
must remain separate; using a proposer trained on the held source to create
critic training targets would invalidate that experiment. Compare against the
same all-action critic with unchanged forecasts, risk rules and update budget.

This next experiment is not_run here. It is a model hypothesis, not a promised
fix. It cannot manufacture missing easy-reference labels. Those support gaps
remain a separate evidence limitation even if mean prediction improves.

**Decision:** no deployment upgrade; not submission-ready. Keep the original
rules, the existing deployment policy and every prior negative result.
