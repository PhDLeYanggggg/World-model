# Missing Labels Amplify, But Do Not Explain Away, Selected-Harm Failure

## Evidence Scope

The fresh diagnosis uses all18 original G-role frozen model pairs, all three
actual seeds and all three completed duration-probe decision sets. There are
no optimizer updates, H scores, changed thresholds or new source roles.
Every unpartitioned predicted/target mass and selected row count matches the
completed duration probe exactly. Complete, partial and wholly-unknown support
partitions exhaust those same selected rows. All18 diagnostics are reproduced
in a separate execution; see [readout.md](readout.md) and its receipts.

These are444 repeated recording/pipeline views, not444 independent scenes.
Ratios below are medians of defined view ratios, not pooled population claims.
Unknown EH is a conservative contribution, not measured forecast error.

## Quantitative Findings

On extended own selection,7,751 of573,770 repeated selected row instances
have no released future label (1.35%). Despite that small row fraction, their
median share of the evaluated easy-harm numerator is64.26%. The corresponding
share on the original decisions is63.55%. Thus missing-label uncertainty can
make a substantial contribution to the original conservative risk contract.

It is not the only contribution. Of307 extended own-selection views violating
whole-set easy risk,223 also violate when evaluated only on available labels;
84 do not. One own-selection observed denominator remains undefined. The
original decision set has324whole-set violations,252also observed and72only
after adding the conservative unknown contribution. These explanatory subsets
are not alternative safety gates and do not authorize filtering unknown rows.

On the extended model's own selected rows, median EH prediction/target ratio is:

| Label support | Ratio | Defined views |
|---|---:|---:|
| All twelve released steps | 0.008336 | 416 |
| One to eleven released steps | 0.010831 | 434 |
| No released future step | 0.000001420 | 416 |

The first ratio is only0.83% of its evaluated target. This is direct descriptive
evidence of selected easy-harm underfitting even without a missing future step.
It does not identify whether unobserved causal information, conditional noise,
parameterization or objective aggregation is the dominant cause.

On the unchanged original decisions, fourfold training improves complete-label
EH ratio0.008077->0.015161 and partial-label ratio0.011744->0.030384. The
own-selection ratios remain much lower. This agrees with the preceding finding:
better average fitting does not remove optimism on newly selected decisions.

## Research Consequence

Do not claim that removing unknown labels repairs the policy, or that labels
with twelve valid steps establish human-gold/sensor-time/metric truth. Do not
deploy the extended head or open another duration sweep. A missingness head
alone is not an evidenced cure; earlier availability and conditional-membership
controls are already negative and should not be relabeled as new proposals.

The next useful research branch must change the information or validate a
distinct mechanism, rather than retune thresholds on exposed H results. The
existing DroneCrowd source audit supplies imagery and more complete annotation
support, but clips remain unassigned/quarantined under the frozen intake rules.
The concrete next data task is a hash-bound, conservative scene-group role
manifest and source-admission integration, before any predictive readout.
This is preparation for an offline8/12 image-space study, not permission to
reuse reserved confirmation outcomes for fitting or selection. Preserve the
original2% contract, historical failed calibration and untouched reserved
outcomes. If independent-unit support is inadequate, report it rather than
manufacture a risk certificate. No routine author reapproval is required for
the already delegated technical source work.

The current SOURCE method still lacks safe held intervention and independent
confirmation. Main v9 PDF remains unchanged; this is separate new evidence.
The overall research goal is active and unmet, not CCF-A submission-ready.
Pixels/raw frames/silver remain distinct from metric/seconds/human gold.
Stage5C execution and SMC remain off.
