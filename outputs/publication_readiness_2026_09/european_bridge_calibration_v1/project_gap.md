# Current Project Gap

## Supported
- Fixed forecast selection has useful opened-development ADE gains over source-
  selected motion baselines. The current study preserves every source/seed view.
- The reference/event implementation is corrected and inference is past-only.
- Source-held calibration and independent-coordinate evaluation are executable;
  failures are reproducible, not hidden behind a successful process exit.
- Calibration can reduce empirical positive-harm violations, but sacrifices
  accuracy and still fails transfer. This is a useful negative comparison.

## Not Established
- A neural ranking/dynamics advantage over matched simple controls.
- A useful policy satisfying the 2% all/easy positive-harm constraints across
  all opened evaluation settings. Net easy preservation alone is insufficient.
- Independent finite-sample risk certification or independent confirmation.
- Scene-level joint intervention gains under matched budgets and reliable
  interaction-consistency evaluation.
- Full independent-domain and public strong-method comparisons supporting a
  paper's main method claim, rather than development-only selection gains.

## Shortest Evidence-Producing Next Step
Keep the full and motion-only candidate pairs fixed. On source-only data,
measure feature-support overlap and selected-harm residuals by held locality;
separate unconditional moment bias from the selected tail and easy-event bias.
Implement a small support-aware fallback or scene-query risk controller only
if that diagnosis predicts a testable repair. Include ridge, matched coverage,
all seeds and all source-role assignments. Predeclare the repair, freeze its
decisions, and never choose locality thresholds on the six readout outcomes.

Before opening the 12 reserved calibration localities, the inferential target
must be feasible: the present ratios are not known [0,1]-bounded, and locality
count does not support a casual distribution-free 2% claim. Additional data or
justified assumptions may be needed. Do not silently clip the loss, relax 2%,
reassign confirmation scenes or call repeated windows independent observations.
