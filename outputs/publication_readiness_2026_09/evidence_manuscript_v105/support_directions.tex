\subsection{Expanded support directions at fixed radius}
We augment the early support bank with OLS4 and an analytic family
selected on observed prefixes. For each row, both must reach the
native neural support radius without amplification. Gain is fixed.
A donor-trained advantage and reference-loss head can release one
candidate before or at gain, while the original support trigger
has priority. The shape-only control replays exactly the learned
support recipient, query, radius and count with the neural direction.

Conservative utility for expanded versus matched neural is
\SupportDirMatchedEffect{}; versus the original it is
\SupportDirOriginalEffect{}. Oracle rows preserve defined references,
but may add support, so their counts do not establish an
equal-budget causal gain. Of \SupportDirOpportunities{} previously
failing views with an exact feasible action, \SupportDirNoRelease{}
have no learned release (all feasible scores below the advantage
gate), \SupportDirWrongQuery{} miss the query,
\SupportDirWrongDirection{} select the wrong direction at a feasible
recipient, and \SupportDirRealized{} is recovered. There are no
recipient-only misses. The model also destroys \SupportDirDestroyed{}
original joint passes. The observed menu helps in hindsight;
the learned release policy does not pass the original rule.
