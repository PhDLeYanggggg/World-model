# Data Card: Completion-Cost Fitting Cohort

## Provenance And Role

Existing EuropeanSquares SOURCE-development packets, cached_verified against
the registered lineage. These are oblique webcam detector-track midpoints in
image pixels, not SDD official human annotations or verified world positions.
The observation protocol is obs8/pred12/stride12 raw frames. Seconds, homography
and metric scale are not established for this experiment.

Only original fitting roles are read. Six contexts and three controller seeds
generate dependent recording views; 444 per supervision mode/bank is not an
independent recording count. Inner-held, independent selection, calibration,
confirmation and DroneCrowd remain closed. No new source data are downloaded.

## Labels And Missingness

Observed masked-ADE-derived costs retain their original support. Wholly
unknown labels remain NaN for evaluation. Separate training endpoint proxies
are derived from a causal finite candidate-disagreement envelope. They do
not identify missing future errors, easy events or reference errors. Known
partial trajectories are not reclassified as fully observed human gold.

## Causal Inputs

History, contemporaneous context, fixed region proxies and candidate rollouts.
Support masks define supervised losses only, not inference inputs or action
eligibility. All learned normalization is fitting-only. No future endpoint,
central velocity, test goal construction or test statistics are admitted.

## Distribution And Storage

Each 36-head mode draws 348,388 repeated unknown rows and 8,867,612 known
supervised visits. These are visits, not new trajectories. Extra full refit
and pilot counts are reported separately. Raw data, feature packets, weights,
private logs and raster previews are excluded from Git. Public outputs are
aggregate statistical receipts, diagnostic summaries and generated SVGs.

This release does not grant third-party data redistribution rights or make
the private assets available from a clean public clone. See the Chinese
reproduction guide for the verified local execution and asset boundary.
