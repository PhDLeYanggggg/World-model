# OpenDD: observed interaction and map context for one-edit ranking

Preserve the 606 development views, nested candidate paths, realized edit
counts, per-recording movement caps, chronological credits and the original
2% rule. Retain the same guarded one-exchange decision and donor sampling
from the earlier study. All configurations train for 400 steps with shared
seeds, losses, architecture width and sampling. Held locations stay out of
fitting and donor label preparation, with pair-excluded donor producers.

Add nominee-level features from two sources. The publisher's static vehicle
area raster supplies signed-distance trend residuals for CV and the frozen
forecast. The closest simultaneously observed peer supplies separation,
closing motion, and relative-path descriptors using that peer's causal CV.
The raster is not pedestrian ground truth; relative path distances are
proxies, not physical-safety outcomes. No future coordinates, labels,
location IDs or raster pixels from future video frames are input features.

The arms are motion-only, map-only in addition to motion, peer-only in
addition to motion, both aligned, and both misaligned. The misalignment
rotates both descriptor blocks together between eligible queries in the
same recording and with identical per-slot modality support. Singleton
strata get zeros in both versions. The exact same candidate paths, contexts
and action budgets are used across arms. Primary: both aligned versus
motion only and versus misaligned, measured in paired conservative gain
and dual-pass rate, with 3,000 paired location draws after seed averaging.
Report modality support, risk pass counts and rescues versus destructions.
The full sequence of past-derived proposals and the frozen spend are
available offline; none of these is a streaming deployment or external
confirmation. No thresholds are chosen on held outcomes.
