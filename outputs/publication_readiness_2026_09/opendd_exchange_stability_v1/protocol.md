# OpenDD: are one-exchange rescues stable within a recording?

Reuse all 606 development recording-seed views and their frozen 780
edits. Enumerate the exact same 7,198 legal exchanges. Among views
where the incumbent fails, take all alternatives that pass the original
three-part 2% risk rule and beat both CV and OLS4 on the full roster.
Compare the existence of such an alternative with the existence of
one that survives a pre-specified within-recording stress.

Partition the chronologically ordered query windows into three
contiguous groups of similar size. Omit one group at a time, keeping
both exchange endpoints even when they lie in the omitted group.
Compare the same incumbent and alternative on the same retained rows,
with equal edited-row counts. Recompute the original risk and utility
checks; do not refit or recalculate cutoffs. Require actual removal
in all three groups for the complete diagnostic. Otherwise report
insufficient deletion support, never a pass. The counterfactual keeps
the frozen action; it is not an online policy or independent replication.

Also report full-roster reserve beneath the worst of the three risk
ratios (0.25, 0.50, and 1.00 percentage points), utility above 0.10 pp
against both references, and availability of selected easy references
in at least two distinct queries. These describe the witnesses; no
threshold or official outcome changes. Count existential witnesses
per view and report candidate counts separately. Only OpenDD
development labels are accessed.
