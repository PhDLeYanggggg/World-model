# Reproducibility Checklist

- Protocol, configuration, scientific implementation and targeted tests hash-bound before fitting.
- Parent seal and local detailed metrics freshly rehashed.
- Cached inner/outer neural producers and references exclude the held fitting locality correctly.
- Forecast producers and controller fitting sites remain disjoint.
- Equal-locality fitting weights use only supported fitting OOF records.
- No held outcome enters readout fitting, score edges, prediction or fallback policy.
- Fixed two slopes, [0,8] bounds and 80 bisection steps; no hyperparameter selection.
- Zero targets and infeasible bounds remain visible, not silently removed.
- Prediction freeze committed before held-source scoring.
- Three seeds averaged within locality; 3,000 paired four-locality resamples per assignment.
- Overlapping assignments are development views, not independent datasets.
- Final fitting, metric, report and figure replay: pending.
- Full legacy suite not_run; final verification records the scoped dependency tests.
- No fresh neural training, new forecasting or new deployment policy in this study.
- Independent selection, reserved calibration and confirmation remain closed.
- Public artifacts omit data, row-level metrics, caches and model files.
- Obs8/pred12 native steps, detector pixels; no metric, seconds or physical-safety claim.
- Stage5C not executed; SMC disabled.

Private parent data, checkpoints and prediction arrays are required for exact
replay. This public evidence package alone is not a portable training dataset.
