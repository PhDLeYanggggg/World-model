# Remaining Gap and Next Discriminating Step

## What Changed

The registered mass readout was implemented and run, not merely proposed.
It rejects a simple global magnitude repair under the fixed constraints:
matching training means is insufficient for held-locality expected costs.
The failure is already visible in fitting OOF tradeoffs and becomes
scene-dependent under transport. This does not establish that useful causal
features or a better conditional estimator cannot exist.

## Do Not Repeat

Do not enlarge slope bounds after these held outcomes, select a favorable
assignment, promote the AUROC result in place of the MSE primary, or launch
another undifferentiated architecture or threshold sweep. Generic hurdle,
membership-factorization, sampling, prior, gradient and crossed-cut repairs
already have negative or mixed records in the results ledger. A proposed
repair must differ in a testable way from those experiments.

## Shortest Next Question

Before more neural training, determine on fitting OOF data whether the present
causal score can support simultaneous mean preservation and squared-cost
improvement at all. A constrained readout with an intercept or shape freedom
could distinguish the origin-only restriction from poor risk allocation, but
must be registered separately, use honest inner fitting, retain identity/L2
and shuffled controls, and preserve the same primary and guards. This is an
unexecuted next experiment, not a claim of improvement. No new independent
roles may be opened to choose its shape, regularization or threshold.

If that controlled feasibility test also fails, stop cycling through global
readouts. Prioritize additional causal context and effective rare-event scene
support over more coefficients, while checking raw-image/interaction label
availability without inspecting reserved outcomes. The contribution must be
useful scene-level intervention under matched forecasters, not module count.

## Main Goal Still Open

This turn adds reproducible source-development evidence, not a new deployed
trajectory gain. Independently confirmed neural dynamics contribution,
stable expected costs with all guards, meaningful multimodal ablations and
an independently evaluated joint-scene controller are still not established
by this study. No submission-ready or foundation claim is justified.
