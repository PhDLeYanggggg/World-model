# Data and Readout Card

## Intended Scope

This is a source-development cost-estimation diagnostic for M3W, not a new
trajectory model, deployment policy or independently validated safety system.
It uses the established European locality protocol with8 observed and12
predicted native annotation steps and detector-pixel coordinates. It does not
establish verified metres, seconds,3D or foundation-model capability.

Three forecast seeds and six producer/controller assignments yield144 outer
views across full and motion-only forecast families. Four localities are the
paired resampling units in each assignment. The assignments overlap, and their
historical exposure cannot be undone by the present prediction freeze. Full
versus motion is not a matched feature ablation: event populations differ.

## Inputs, Outputs and Labels

Each readout consumes four frozen expected-cost components and a causal
intervention envelope. It changes only H_all and H_easy using two slopes,
then applies the existing nested envelope projection. There is no future
outcome argument at prediction time. Harm labels are used solely for fitting
on honest nested OOF records or for subsequent source-held scoring.
The outer locality is excluded from every fitting predictor, reference,
preprocessing transform, target definition and score-bin fit. The forecast
producer roster is disjoint from controller fitting sites.

The fixed projected mass fit first matches mean H_all, then mean H_easy under
the fitted H_all cap, with equal mass per supported fitting locality. Bounds
remain[0,8];80 bisection steps are deterministic. Unreachable means retain the
bound and a failure flag. Matching a training moment is not a conditional,
cross-domain or conformal guarantee. Raw and origin-L2 controls remain intact.

## Evidence and Limitations

Fresh work:432 two-parameter fits, loss decomposition, source-held measurement
and locality bootstrap. Cached_verified:forecasters, causal features, nested
neural cost heads, raw/L2 predictions and source lineage. Not_run:new neural
training, any deployment comparison, independent-role access or new datasets.

The primary is expected easy-harm MSE on positive-envelope records. Top10 harm
capture, coverage-log error and all-envelope H_all MSE remain guards. These
are cost-estimation outcomes, not ADE/FDE gains or measured easy degradation.
The public aggregate package omits raw records, private caches and models.
Exact replay requires the local parent asset chain. No human-gold or physical
safety claim is supported. Stage5C and SMC remain disabled.
