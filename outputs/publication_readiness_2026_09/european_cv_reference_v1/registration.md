# European CV-Reference Contract Repair V1

## Material Passport

Source-only code experiment, registered after the completed European source
forecast/intervention studies exposed failed safety. No new independent result
is implied by this version. Routine execution is delegated by the project owner.

## Hypothesis and Priority

The selected motion baseline has 15.48% positive-easy degradation against CV.
The former policy learns harm relative to that baseline, not CV. Re-anchoring
both the decision reference and its cost supervision to causal CV may remove
this mismatch without removing useful neural intervention. It may instead lose
utility or still harm easy/zero-CV cases. Both are falsifying outcomes, not
grounds to retune this readout.

Priority1: resolve this reference contract before more model scaling or joint
geometry complexity. Priority2: if reference repair is insufficient, separately
test event-conditional risk prediction. Priority3: only after a defensible source
candidate, freeze independent selection/calibration/confirmation. No data role
is opened in this study.

and the fixed strong controls, not just the now-safer CV reference. Report
all and hard gains, positive-easy mean/worst-locality degradation against CV,
exact-zero-CV harmed rows and absolute harm, p95/p99, intervention rate,
complete-future sensitivity and matched-count contrasts. Preserve the original
training-only easy/hard cutoffs; no definition changes. Bootstrap3,000locality
resamples with seed39271, three model seeds; conditional source-development
intervals, never independent confirmation or window-iid uncertainty.

The unchanged actual limits are positive-easy degradation<=2% and exact-zero-CV
added harm0. Check worst locality as well as the mean. A zero-switch CV outcome
is only safe fallback, not neural contribution. A positive mean with a failed
safety gate does not pass. A successful engineering run does not establish a
paper-ready method. No claim is selected after looking at this readout.
