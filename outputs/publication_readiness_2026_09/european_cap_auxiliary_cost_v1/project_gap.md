   comparable intervention/risk budgets. This study evaluated no new policy.
3. Independent selection, calibration and confirmation with adequate scene
   support. The four-locality bootstrap and repeated source development cannot
   substitute for untouched data roles or formal risk control.
4. A demonstrable methodological contribution and matched public strong
   forecasting baselines. Auxiliary BCE/shared encoders are not novel alone.
5. A complete paper and reproducibility package grounded in the missing
   independent main results. Current status: not submission-ready.
