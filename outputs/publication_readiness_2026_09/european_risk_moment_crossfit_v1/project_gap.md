# Research Progress and Remaining Gap

This turn adds a completed144-head,288000-update source-only diagnostic, not a
new deployable dynamics model. It falsifies the simple explanation that risk
heads merely failed to learn or that only unseen-source shift caused the problem.
They beat constants on mean prediction while remaining miscalibrated on their
own accepted subset, including inside fitting sources. The next bottleneck is
decision-conditional risk learning, not missing candidate-rollout inputs.

evidence. Prior protected-neural-vs-damping failure is unchanged. The historical
Stage37 score is not recertified by these new European source experiments.
