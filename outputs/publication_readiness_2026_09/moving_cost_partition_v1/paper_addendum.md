# Partition Learning Does Not Establish Safe Admission

With fixed causal120-dimensional descriptors and a two-channel expected-risk
readout, we compare squared-error tree partitions with unweighted and
cost-weighted sign partitions. Classification changes the fitted partition;
all final leaf means use the original source/query measure. Classifier
probabilities do not determine acceptance and the decision threshold is fixed.

Sixteen new models and eight verified prior controls cover eight source-held
fitting folds. All24cells reproduce by full refit. The registered cost-partition
contrast reduces risk MSE10.744%,1.042%,35.804% across three contexts, but changes
joint decision cost-1.830%,+0.041%,+0.284%. Absolute harmful-admission cost
increases in all three. The internal expansion criterion fails0/3; no extra
development or reserved evaluation is opened. The sign-only secondary is not
substituted for the failed primary.

Target diagnostics distinguish label prevalence from cost magnitude: weighted
jointly nonpositive labels account for90.15%,72.24%,63.04%, yet positive easy-
channel excess mass is6.39,56.49,52.21times negative mass. These are known-label
fitting quantities, not population safety ratios. A same-partition occurrence/
severity decomposition reconstructs the original mean to numerical precision
with no decision changes; that identity is not a new forecasting contribution.

The result motivates auditing the candidate forecasts' harmful tails rather
than treating further risk-head complexity as sufficient. It does not prove
irreducible uncertainty or make a separately trained hurdle model equivalent
to direct regression. Independent confirmation, original full-risk/support,
three-seed primary success and a submission-ready method remain unestablished.
Original2%rules, prior failures and closed reserved sets remain. This addendum
is separate from the unchanged compiled v9 paper; no deployment is promoted.
