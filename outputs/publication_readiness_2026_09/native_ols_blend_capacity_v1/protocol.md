# OLS4-Centered Menus Under The Same Recording And Count Rules

Test whether a fixed, past-only interpolation around the stronger OLS4
reference expands the conservative dual-reference capacity established
by the prior exact-count experiment. This is a diagnostic on the same
exposed Moving A roster (34 recordings, 14 source groups, three frozen
forecasting seeds). Do not refit a forecast model or change the current
external policy, fallback, 2% risk checks, counts, labels, or units.

Starting with the eight frozen alternatives, append three fixed convex
blends of OLS4 with each non-OLS4 causal forecast. Then append the same
three blends with each learned forecast. Fractions toward the endpoint
are .25, .50 and .75. The menus are nested and preserve every original
option. New blends within one millionth of an image pixel of CV at all
steps are treated as CV, not as extra genuine switches. Do not equate
matched counts with matched replacement magnitudes or menu sizes.

Use the prior maximin conservative objective against CV and OLS4 and
the exact same genuine replacement count per query. On wholly unknown
rows a legal candidate can be removed only if another nonidentity path
for that person is no worse in distance to both references. Abstention
does not dominate a legal option in an exact-count problem. Use the
original independent scoring of the witness and signed tolerance.
Re-score and carry forward valid witnesses from a nested smaller menu;
do not carry forward its negative bounds. For unresolved positives
or negatives use the same 3-second solve and 12-second refinement
as the prior audit. Keep unresolved outcomes explicit.

Primary diagnostic: changes in verified both-reference pass counts
between native, causal interpolation and causal-plus-learned
interpolation. Report newly positive recordings and source groups and
a paired 3,000-draw source-bootstrap positive-rate difference, averaging
seeds first. Do not publish row-level actions or label-aware assignments.
If the menu does not expand capacity, do not spend effort fitting a new
selector for it. A positive oracle effect is not a result for a deployable
policy, independent evidence, a physical claim, or a reason to change
the external calibration. No Stage5C or SMC.
