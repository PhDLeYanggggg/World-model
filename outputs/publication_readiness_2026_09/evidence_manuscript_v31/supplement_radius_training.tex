\paragraph{Training under equal realized departure.}
In a follow-up OpenDD development study, we constrain the decoder to
the incumbent's per-target radius during training. The producer supplying
training shapes excludes both the donor and the outer held location.
Across 84 fresh fits and three seeds, the self-only easy-harm arm gains
\RadiusGain{} points vs.\ the incumbent at the same recipients and radii
(location-bootstrap 95\% interval \RadiusInterval{}).
The peer-history arm does not improve the matched contrast or the
observed relative-vector error between pedestrians.

This shape gain does not imply safety. The incumbent passes
\RadiusOldPass{} of 606 recording--seed views, compared with
\RadiusNewPass{} for the new arm. Since the recipients, labels, and
maximum departures match, the selected CV error mass and debit for
wholly unobserved targets are invariant between arms. All
\RadiusLost{} lost passes violate the upper easy-risk test; only
\RadiusGained{} passes are recovered. The 30 undefined views are unchanged.
Hence this gap comes from harm on observed trajectories, not from
a changed support denominator or a larger missing-label debit.
This is development evidence, not a replacement policy.
