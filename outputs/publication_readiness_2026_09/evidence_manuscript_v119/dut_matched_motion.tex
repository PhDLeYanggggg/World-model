\section{DUT: Forecast Quality At Matched Displacement}

The raw-annotation DUT diagnostic~\cite{dut} asks whether a neural
forecast advantage persists after the frozen intervention decision.
We keep the previously exposed 27 recordings at two physical sites,
including the original quarantine of one other recording. A deterministic
time-only schedule selects \DutMotionQueries{} disjoint eight-observation,
twelve-prediction windows. Of \DutMotionTargets{} agent targets,
\DutMotionComplete{} have complete futures. Future labels affect
neither the roster nor the predictions. All three source-trained
EqMotion seeds and bounded-fraction heads stay frozen.

The raw comparison uses the full forecast. The strict policy uses
the registered past-only guard. A separate comparison keeps its
recipient and query times, and gives the damped and OLS4 directions
the exact same peak deviation from CV as the neural path at each
selected row. All three matched arms abstain together if either
analytic direction is zero. The original guard nominates
\DutMotionStrictCount{} edits and the comparison retains
\DutMotionMatchedCount{}, across repeated seeds.

Complete contrasts are relative complete-path ADE improvements.
Lower and upper contrasts retain missing steps and use the common
observed CV error mass. The matched damped lower score has a
descriptive two-site interval of \DutMotionMatchedInterval{} pp;
three seeds do not increase the independent site count. The worst
site/seed known easy changes for raw and strict predictions are
\DutMotionRawEasy\% and \DutMotionStrictEasy\%. For the matched
neural, damped and OLS4 paths they are \DutMotionMatchedEasy\%,
\DutMotionDampEasy\% and \DutMotionOLSEasy\%. Selected easy
reference is undefined in all 81 matched recording--seed views,
so zero signed easy change is not a supported risk pass. These
diagnostics neither calibrate a new policy nor certify safety. No
thresholds or models are selected on DUT.

To rule out the disjoint-query schedule as an explanation, we
recompute OLS4 on the historical \DutMotionFullTargets{}-target
roster. Per-recording target counts, complete counts, known steps
and native CV error sums agree with the frozen readout. On its
\DutMotionFullComplete{} complete targets, OLS4 gives ADE/FDE
\DutMotionFullOLSADE{}/\DutMotionFullOLSFDE{}, versus
\DutMotionFullEqADE{}/\DutMotionFullEqFDE{} for the cached raw EqMotion
scores. OLS4 wins \DutMotionFullWins{}/6 site--seed ADE views.
This is a same-cohort complete-label comparison; no full-roster
paired missing-label score is reconstructed from the old aggregates.
