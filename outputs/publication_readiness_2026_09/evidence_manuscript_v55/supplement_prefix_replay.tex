\section{Observed-Window Replay as a Path-Choice Signal}
The frozen models forecast from prefixes of length 4, 5, 6 and 7,
padded only before the prefix. Outcomes used to score each replay
are already observed at the final origin. The final forecast uses
the unchanged 8-to-12 path bank, recipients and radii. A second
rule requires agreement between linear and stationary padding;
disagreement returns matched hindcast at the same radius.

At half radius on exposed OpenDD, linear replay improves over
matched hindcast by \PrefixReplayHind{} percentage points, but its gain
relative to fixed tangent is \PrefixReplayTangent{} points.
Mean location-level pairwise concordance is \PrefixReplayRank\%.
At the same counts per action within each recording and seed, exact
permutation expectations give direct gains of \PrefixReplayShuffleLinear{}
and \PrefixReplayShuffleAgree{} points for linear replay and padding
agreement. Intervals resample locations after averaging seeds. The
permutation comparison is about assignment utility, not a risk test.
On the single exposed TUMDOT campaign both lose to CV
(\PrefixReplayTumLinear{} and \PrefixReplayTumAgree{} points), and
neither beats matched hindcast. The apparent OpenDD improvement over
the weaker comparator is not evidence of useful individualized choice.
