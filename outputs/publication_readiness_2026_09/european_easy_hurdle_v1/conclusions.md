# Easy-Risk Supervision: Decision and Limits

## Decision

Do not deploy or promote either new arm. The registered exploratory screen
fails. These are fresh risk-head training and held-development results with
cached_verified forecasts, floor, utility and all-risk scores. Independent
selection, calibration and confirmation are not_run and remain closed.

The action freeze was committed as `96b82ac3` before this readout. There was no
held-label checkpoint selection or threshold search. The original incomplete
primary is not replaced by this experiment or by a full-floor denominator.

## What Worked

- All 216 actual Torch heads completed 2,000 updates each. All own fitting
  objectives decreased. Training hashes and paired sample chains were checked;
  the first two heads reproduce exactly on the same CREATE runtime except time.
- Easy occurrence and conditional costs now have explicit supervision rather
  than unidentifiable component labels. Brier decreases from 0.262075 to
  0.158131; log loss from 1.09921 to 0.499397. Conditional moment errors improve.
- The prescribed common-query intervention counts match. Easy error and zero-CV
  protection pass their developmental screens. This is not a risk certificate.

## What Failed

- Primary supervised versus marginal matched ADE gain is -0.0204671%, with
  nominal 95% locality interval [-0.0320605%, -0.00956935%]. Eleven of twelve
  locality point contrasts are negative. The raw matched control also wins.
- At the same query counts, 0.00648741 percentage points of positive harm are
  avoided, but 0.0269217 points of selected benefit are lost, using a common
  full-floor denominator. Lower harm alone is not better allocation.
- Composed signed-risk MSE increases from 0.00418997 to 0.00444302. Its bias
  rises from 0.00165742 to 0.00891619. Better component fits do not guarantee a
  better product near the admission boundary.
- Supervised matched has 51 realized selected-risk violations and 24 undefined
  views out of 216 dependent views. Abstention is not a defined zero selected
  risk. These failures remain even though every predicted query constraint is
  constructed to be feasible.
- Marginal own-count selection improves ADE but has 152 violating views. It
  cannot be promoted by reporting only its favorable mean accuracy.

## Interpretation

The repaired component semantics are useful for diagnosis, but not a sufficient
method contribution or a deployable improvement. The paired design identifies
the effect of the entire auxiliary-supervision package, not separate effects of
occurrence, conditional reference and conditional harm losses. The marginal arm
does not directly supervise probability, so its poor Brier score is not evidence
of a broken probability-training implementation.

The occurrence probability cancels from an individual signed-risk test when
strictly positive. Its contribution is query weighting. All-risk and utility
remain frozen and imperfect. Fixed predicted feasibility therefore cannot be
equated with a guarantee about future costs, especially under source shift.

Loss-scale competition and shared-representation conflict are plausible but
unproved causes. The next minimal diagnostic should measure each component's
gradient magnitude and alignment using fitting-only sources and the frozen
checkpoints. A controlled remedy must be registered before another policy
readout. Do not choose a new weight, threshold or budget from these outcomes.

## Paper and Model Gap

This experiment can support a transparent negative-results or methods-diagnosis
section: same architecture, same queries, 432,000 updates, count-matched actions,
all locality and seed results retained. It cannot support safer deployment,
independent generalization, successful world dynamics or submission readiness.

The independent source stages are still unopened. A useful allocation method,
valid selected-risk assessment under source exclusion, independent evidence,
complete comparative experiments and the final paper package remain necessary.
No algorithmic novelty is claimed for the elementary expectation factorization.

All observations are image-local detector silver; obs8/pred12 with raw-frame
stride12. Repeated windows and source-role views are not independent samples.
The 3,000 locality-bootstrap draws are nominal exploratory intervals, not
simultaneous bounds. No metric, seconds, human-gold, physical-safety, true-3D or
foundation claim. Historical exposed results remain exploratory. Stage5C
execution and SMC remain disabled.
