\subsection{Earlier support with a fixed gain edit}
The gain recipient, path and time remain frozen. The original support
trigger has priority. A learned head can fill the unspent support slot
before or at gain. A local feasible-reference head (incidence and
conditional reference mass) makes no changes. A second head learns the
recording-level consequence of replacing original support, including
transitions in reference availability, risk and joint pass. Its score
cutoff is set by the 90th percentile of training-recording maxima,
not by held outcomes. This is not independent risk calibration.

Recording advantage adds \EarlyRefAdded{} supports and advances
\EarlyRefAdvanced{}, losing \EarlyRefLost{} old references, all
from advances because an addition cannot remove an existing edit.
Conservative utility changes by \EarlyRefPrimaryEffect{} versus the
original (paired location bootstrap), but the risk result deteriorates.
Easy ranking at the exact learned times, radii and counts changes
\EarlyRefMatchedChanged{} recipients, with a matched effect of
\EarlyRefMatchedEffect{} in favor of the head. The outcome-aware
menu adds \EarlyRefOracle{} joint passes while retaining defined
references, but can also add edits and is not a fixed-budget gain.
Neither learned policy qualifies under the original 2\% recording rule.
