# What the Frozen Decisions Reveal

## Material Passport

Fresh diagnostic calculations on cached, hash-verified models and actions.
Already-opened development localities only; no training, policy change or
independent confirmation in this diagnostic. No deployment upgrade.

## Supported Observations

1. **Risk is underpredicted on selected rows.** The aggregate joint policy's
   signed all-risk residual is +0.011099 in fitting-cost-scale units, with
   positive locality means in all twelve localities. This is not a percent.
   Sixteen empty views remain undefined; the reported mean is descriptive.
2. **Selection does not show greater average optimism than nonselection.**
   The paired selected-minus-eligible-unselected residual is -0.007172 for
   aggregate joint actions. The proposed explanation that the optimizer simply
   chooses the most underestimated rows is not supported by this contrast.
   This does not rule out decision-induced bias relative to another reference.
3. **The inspected support proxy is not the whole explanation.** About0.945%
   of aggregate-joint selected rows are outside both fitting-source descriptor
   radii, versus2.436% of eligible rows. Even inside those radii the selected
   residual remains positive (+0.011174). These means average nonempty dependent
   views within locality. Six descriptors cannot certify full-feature support;
   this does not establish that broader distribution shift is absent.
4. **The old controller proxy is incomplete, but not wholly irrelevant.**
   It covers64.008% of aggregate-joint selected rows. Risk remains underpredicted
   both inside and outside that proxy. Expanding proxy coverage alone has not
   been demonstrated to repair the problem.
5. **Added harm offsets added useful intervention.** At matched rank counts,
   aggregate supervision adds0.047020pp benefit and0.013598pp harm, while removing
   0.044038pp benefit and0.011319pp harm. Net gain is only0.000703pp relative to the
   complete known floor-error denominator; CI[-0.008459,0.010327]. That denominator
   differs from relative ADE improvement and from the selected-harm screen.
6. **A possible fitting issue remains.** Aggregate in-sample all-risk residual
   averages+0.006685 over rows. But the actual objective balances sources,
   queries and subsets, not rows. It would be incorrect to conclude undertraining
   from this number alone. An exact loss-weighted intercept probe is the next
   falsifiable check, before a new policy experiment.

## What Is Not Established

There is no causal decomposition of scene shift, stochastic uncertainty,
capacity limitation and optimization bias. The nearest-descriptor diagnostic
is not predictive uncertainty. In-sample fitting errors are not out-of-source
calibration errors. Detector missingness need not be random. The selected-query
counts repeat contexts across seeds/heads and cannot be treated as independent
samples. Undefined risk remains undefined; no risk tolerance or roster changed.

The original aggregate-joint method still has84/216 observed screen violations,
16 undefined views and no deployment permission from this evidence. A narrative
about why it failed does not turn the method into a successful contribution.
