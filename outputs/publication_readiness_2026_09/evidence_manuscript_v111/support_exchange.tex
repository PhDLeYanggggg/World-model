\subsection*{Same-query support exchange}
I keep the exact incumbent query counts. A replacement must edit a
known easy row in the same query and move at most as far from CV as
the removed edit. The outcome-aware program checks the original risk
rule and, for a joint witness, positive paired lower gain against
the incumbent, CV, and OLS4. All witnesses are rescored.

Of \ExchangeUndefined{} undefined views, 12 have no original
edits and five more have no admissible exchange. There are
\ExchangeStrong{} strong-OLS4 views. Joint witnesses at one
exchange per query yield \ExchangeEffect{} on the full roster
with incumbent fallback, using 3,000 source resamples.

\textbf{Support is not gain.} Dropping a wholly unlabeled edit
with radius $r$ incurs debit $r$. Adding a known edit of radius
$s$ gains at most $s$ by the reverse triangle inequality.
With equal query weights and $s\le r$, its paired lower gain is
nonpositive. This is a restriction on this exchange, not on
other intervention menus or budgets.
