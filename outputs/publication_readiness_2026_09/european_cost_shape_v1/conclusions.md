## Why This Is Useful but Not a Model Upgrade

In full-input cost-only fitting,shape mass improves on origin mass in66/72
easy-harm views. Only35 improve on both fitting and held data;31 improve only
on fitting data. Against origin L2,only15/72 improve fitting MSE and only4
improve both fitting and held MSE. Thus adding shape freedom makes the means
feasible and often fits better than the previous mass readout,but does not
solve held-locality cost estimation. These are dependent descriptive counts.

The matched shape control is important:all three full-input arms have five
negative and one overlapping primary interval when mean constraints are added.
Neither simply adding a fitting mean nor dropping it while keeping these
global shapes yields a reliable advance over the strong origin-L2 control.
This rejects the tested repair;it does not prove that all conditional risk
estimators,causal features or neural world dynamics must fail.

## Decision

Fitting support passes. Primary,guard,auxiliary-information and development
advance screens fail. No deployment change,no new ADE/FDE or t+50 gain claim,
and no independent confirmation. Complete comparisons,motion-only failures,
absolute costs and fitting/held discrepancies remain in the companion files.

Honor the registered stop rule:do not continue sweeping global readout shapes,
slopes or thresholds. The next study must test additional causal context or
effective rare-event support with matched forecasters and honest inner fitting.
Do not open independent outcomes to design that study.
