\subsection{Sparse intervention on pedestrian development}
We retain the Moving A bank, 14 source groups and 34 recordings
over three seeds (102 repeated views). The support head uses only
the seven training sources of each frozen split. From CV, each
learned arm and the old gain rank make one edit at the first
legal query. The unchanged per-recording rule is applied; beating
CV and beating both CV and OLS4 are distinct outcomes.

The CV-support head gives \SparseSupportMatchedEffect{} in paired
conservative utility versus gain ranking and \SparseSupportStrongEffect{}
versus OLS4, with source-bootstrap intervals. The oracle uses
at most one edit and may abstain, so its edit counts differ.
The all-query safe class with at most one individually improving
easy edit per query reaches only \SparseSupportEasyAll{} dual passes.
Requiring one easy reference but also allowing other known
CV-improving edits raises capacity to \SparseSupportBridgeEight{}
at eight edits and \SparseSupportBridgeAll{} with no recording
cap. It reaches \SparseSupportStrongBridge{} of the
\SparseSupportStrong{} views where OLS4 is certified better
than CV by the paired completion interval. These are exact bounds
within the stated zero-positive-harm action classes, not bounds
for every policy under 2\% risk. They do not establish a transferable
intervention or use independent confirmation.
