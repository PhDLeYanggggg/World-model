\section{Fixed Shapes and the Reserve Alone}

We freeze recipients and times. Neural, OLS4 and the
past-prefix analytic path share their smallest peak radius,
without amplification. At zero radius all retain the original
edit. This matches \ShapeSlotGainMatched{}/\ShapeSlotGainSlots{} gain slots,
\ShapeSlotEasyMatched{}/\ShapeSlotEasySlots{} easy slots, and
\ShapeSlotIncumbentMatched{}/\ShapeSlotIncumbentSlots{} original slots.
OLS4 against neural yields \ShapeSlotGainForward{} on gain
slots, but the reverse lower contrast is \ShapeSlotGainReverse{}.
Missing-step debits are symmetric and do not change sign
when reversing the comparison. The reverse effects on the
easy and original slots are \ShapeSlotEasyReverse{} and
\ShapeSlotIncumbentReverse{}, respectively.

Removing incumbent edits but retaining their eligibility trace
gives \ShapeSlotSingle{} over CV; without the trace it gives
\ShapeSlotFree{} (error-mass weighted \ShapeSlotWeighted{}).
The combined result is \ShapeSlotCombined{}. The sparse
rule improves aggregate utility but fails full-roster risk
on the exposed sites. The confirmation protocol is unchanged.
