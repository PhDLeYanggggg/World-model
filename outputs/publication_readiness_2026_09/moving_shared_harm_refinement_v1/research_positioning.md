# Research positioning and primary sources

This is a cost-estimation feature test, not a claim to invent crowd pooling.
[Social LSTM (CVPR2016)](https://www.cv-foundation.org/openaccess/content_cvpr_2016/papers/Alahi_Social_LSTM_Human_CVPR_2016_paper.pdf)
already shares nearby agents' recurrent states through social pooling.
[Social GAN (CVPR2018)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Gupta_Social_GAN_Socially_CVPR_2018_paper.html)
also aggregates information across people for trajectory prediction. These
primary sources establish an obvious prior for interaction aggregation; merely
adding48crowd moments cannot be presented as an architectural contribution.

The present narrower question is whether already-admitted past/current crowd
statistics help estimate upward intervention harm after a local tree cost
estimator has repaired fitting magnitudes. Forecasts and trajectory candidates
stay fixed. Fresh equal435width local-zero/shared controls distinguish this
information question from the earlier387wide loss-region study. No public
Social-LSTM or Social-GAN reproduction is claimed by citing the papers.

Potential evidence concerns selection-conditioned gain/harm learning, preserving
explicit missing-outcome uncertainty and controlling joint intervention at a
fixed risk budget. A positive development contrast alone would not establish
novelty, finite-sample risk control, independent generalization or a successful
world model. The full support/risk contract and independent evidence remain
separate requirements. No generative experiment is conducted here.
