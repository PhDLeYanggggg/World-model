# OpenDD: can a different direction improve a correction of the same size?

At every future step fix the incoming forecast's distance from CV
to the original value. Point that same amount of displacement
toward a causal short-velocity fit, long-velocity fit, bounded
acceleration, or decaying turn. Keep the original direction when
an alternative displacement is effectively zero. The intervention
count, recipients, available labels, cumulative norm, maximum radius,
and 2% rule cannot change. Unlike the earlier phase-only change,
endpoint direction may change.

First enumerate the original 7,198 legal exchanges and check
exact risk-plus-dual-utility capacity with and without new directions.
Repeat the existing protected-endpoint background deletion check.
Then replay the same 101 chosen exchanges. Compare each direction,
the exact uniform family expectation, and an observed-suffix rule
using prefixes of length 4, 5, and 6 of the eight observed steps.
The cautious variant requires all three suffix scores to improve
over the original. No post-origin coordinates enter any choice.
Report native available errors, original risk, paired conservative
utility, and 3,000 paired location draws after averaging seeds.
OpenDD development only, no training, external scoring or release.
