# What Is Still Missing

## Evidence Added
This round falsifies a concrete proposed repair on its registered development
population. Incumbent actions were fixed; all matched variants used identical
forecasts, addition counts and predicted-harm caps. Independent, unary, joint and
outcome-independent hash controls separate ranking from pairwise effects. The
negative result and easy violations remain in the public record.

## Why This Is Not The Main Method Yet
The proximity objective changes only three unique queries relative to unary
geometry. It does not produce a supported accuracy lift over strong independent
selection. Restricting the intervention count loses useful additions and can
violate easy preservation. Optimizing a proximity proxy is not learning new
dynamics or establishing physical safety. A learned2% risk threshold is not a
calibration theorem. Earlier incumbent-relative gains remain development
evidence, not independent confirmation.

## Next Evidence Step
1. Stop treating a fixed intervention-count reduction as a safety guarantee.
   Preserve the frozen incumbent and study prediction of positive incremental
   harm and benefit with source-held-out reliability, including zero-cost and
   low-support cases. Do not refit thresholds on this readout.
2. Before opening reserved data, freeze one controller family and a source-level
   calibration rule with an explicit estimand. Evaluate strong simple risk/rank
   and deferral controls with the same predictors. If only four calibration
   sources cannot support a useful bound, report insufficient independent
   calibration support rather than inventing IID windows.
3. Use the existing independent-scene protocol for model selection, calibration
   and final confirmation only after the method and comparison budget are fixed.
   The full opened population and the small joint-query subset must not be
   relabeled confirmation. Cross-domain replication, a defensible method
   contribution and the full paper/reproduction package remain incomplete.
