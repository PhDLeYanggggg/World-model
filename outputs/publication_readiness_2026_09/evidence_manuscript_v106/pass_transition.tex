\subsection{Direct supervision of recording pass transitions}
The frozen support menu is unchanged. We sample donor sites,
recordings, and their candidate actions uniformly in that order.
A head learns the unconditional probabilities of rescuing a
previously failing recording, destroying an existing pass, and
losing a defined reference. Net pass probability scores a
candidate by the difference of the first two probabilities; the
rescue-only readout omits destruction from ranking.

The count-matched control retrospectively selects by the frozen
composite scores, matching additions and replacements separately
per site and seed. Net pass versus this control gives
\PassTransMatchedEffect{} in conservative utility; versus the
original it gives \PassTransOriginalEffect{}. It never releases on
\PassTransNever{} of the \PassTransOpportunities{} feasible new
views. One location contains \PassTransConcentrated{} of these
opportunities and none is recovered there under held-location
scoring. This concentration limits what can be inferred about
generalization. No policy passes the original risk rule.
