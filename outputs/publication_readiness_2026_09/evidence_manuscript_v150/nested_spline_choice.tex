\section{Nested risk-aware spline choice on OpenDD}
We retain \NestedSplineEdits{} fixed slots and peak displacements and
learn to choose between the analytic and spline shapes. In contrast to
pointwise expert deferral~\cite{mao}, the joint decision prices predicted
risk changes across up to two same-query slots. The head predicts
conservative utility and changes in all, easy, and signed easy risk
masses. Thresholds use only nested donor predictions from a deterministic
two-recipient proxy roster. The outer controller's realized slots are
unchanged. Proxy calibration constrains aggregate relative risk; it
does not guarantee the original recording-level rule.

Outer and calibration sites are excluded from fitting the corresponding
selector and forecast generator. Candidate forecasts used to fit a
selector also exclude the candidate's source site. The study trains
\NestedSplineProducer{} nested generators and \NestedSplineSelector{}
selector heads (\NestedSplineUpdates{} updates), and freezes outer
predictions before scoring. The joint calibration chooses a nontrivial
rule for \NestedSplineJointRules{} of seven outer folds.

Risk and dual-reference pass counts are out of \NestedSplineViews{}
recording--seed views; all arms have \NestedSplineUndefined{}
undefined easy-reference views. The guarded policies both select
only \NestedSplineJointUses{} spline paths and make identical choices;
the shared-rule control therefore provides no evidence of a grouping
advantage. Gain-only selection intervenes more often, but passes fewer
risk and dual-reference checks than the prefix rule. The registered conservative contrast
against past prefix is \NestedSplinePrimary{} pp. Joint against the
original is \NestedSplineAgainstAnchor{} pp, and grouping alone at the
independent rule is \NestedSplineGrouping{} pp. Intervals are paired
location bootstraps, not repeated-window intervals. This is exposed
development evidence; no deployment or confirmation is implied.
