# Current Evidence Manuscript

This revision connects the completed SDD cost-ranking study with the later
EuropeanSquares cost-support, extrapolation and temporal-target experiments.
The two cohorts remain separate. It preserves negative primary comparisons,
strong controls, unknown outcomes and selected-risk failures.

- [Chinese reproduction guide](reproduction_zh.md)

The numerical evidence is `cached_verified`. Assembly and source consistency
checks are fresh work; no model, prediction, bootstrap or deployment is newly
produced here. All intervals are nominal development evidence. Neural temporal
auxiliary fitting and its policy evaluation remain `not_run` at this revision.

The most important distinction is between learning temporal error structure and
improving the cases a policy actually selects. Current temporal probes support
the former, not the latter. The registered matched experiment is needed to test
that link. Better manuscript organization is not a scientific gate pass.

The [v2 manuscript](../evidence_manuscript_v2/manuscript.md) is a frozen historical
study, not superseded numerical evidence. The new paper preserves its primary
failures and secondary results. This draft is neither anonymous nor formatted
for submission, and has not received independent scientific peer review.
