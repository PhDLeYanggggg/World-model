# Bank-Dependent Cost Estimates And Neural Gate Suppression

Working evidence addendum, 29 October 2026. Fitting-only mechanistic evidence;
not an independent main result or a submission-readiness claim.

We separate two reasons why a frozen neural candidate is not selected:
failure of its predicted benefit/risk gate, and competition with a physical
candidate after that same gate. All decisions use causal availability and
predicted costs, not realized errors. Same-estimator neural restriction
isolates competition; combining dedicated physical and neural estimators is
a separate control with twice the estimator capacity, not a pure ablation.

Across 144 SOURCE fitting groups, the mixed forest admits 437 neural query
views and selects 271. Its dedicated neural estimator admits 85,544; 85,117
of these fail the mixed estimator's gate. Thus the forest's neural suppression
primarily concerns learned conditional costs, not physical competition alone.
The neural cost-head family also has 13,487 mixed-gate competition losses,
so the proportions are estimator-dependent.

| Family | Factorized minus mixed known gain (pp), fitting 95% CI | Matched-count known gain (pp), fitting 95% CI | Mixed to factorized recording support |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Forest | +0.071403 [+0.018321,+0.137631] | +0.003750 [+0.001153,+0.007120] | 330 to 285/444 |
| Neural cost head | +0.444742 [+0.147436,+0.817331] | +0.164349 [+0.038002,+0.354595] | 77 to 57/444 |

Positive fitting utility is accompanied by worse complete 2% recording risk
support. Count matching evaluates utility only, not safety. Empty selected
risk remains undefined and selected unknown outcomes remain in completion
bounds. Intervals resample localities after averaging repeated context and
head-seed views. They are descriptive fitting-resubstitution intervals, not
held generalization, whole-pipeline OOF or independent confirmation.

The result rejects permissive expert substitution as a safety repair. It
motivates checking candidate-output geometry and identifiable conditional
support, not choosing thresholds from held labels. No new forecast, new
Torch training or additional tree fitting is claimed. Separate inference
replays every group and summary exactly; mixed-with-itself recomposition
reproduces the original policies exactly.

The experiment retains eight observed and twelve predicted steps, stride
twelve raw frames, on EuropeanSquares oblique detector-track midpoint pixels.
It provides no metric/seconds, human-gold, true-3D, foundation or deployment
claim. Independent roles and DroneCrowd remain closed. Stage5C and SMC are off.
