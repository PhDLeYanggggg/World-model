# Why Expert Separation Is Not A Safety Repair

## Measured Mechanisms

For the same mixed forest, only 437 of 894,975 causally available neural
query views pass its fixed gate. Of those, 166 lose to a physical action and
271 are selected. The dedicated neural forest passes 85,544 views, of which
85,117 fail the mixed gate. All-harm rejection occurs on 82,224 of that subset
and easy-harm rejection on 85,112; these counts overlap. Competition alone
cannot explain this forest's low neural intervention rate.

The neural cost-head family is different: 35,733 mixed-gate passes, 22,246
neural choices and 13,487 competition losses. Dedicated-expert passes rejected
by the mixed estimator number 13,953. Nonpositive estimated gain is common
in this subset. It would be incorrect to generalize the forest proportions
to every estimator or identify one universal architectural cause.

## Why The Apparent Repair Fails

Replacing the mixed estimator with physical and neural experts raises known
fitting ADE gain from 0.000111% to 0.071514% for forests and from 0.678064%
to 1.122806% for neural cost heads. The paired increases have positive
fitting locality-bootstrap intervals. Nevertheless, required recording risk
support declines from 330 to 285/444 and from 77 to 57/444, respectively.
Unknown selected outcomes increase from 80 to 883 and from 853 to 1,076.
Undefined easy views remain 104 and 42, respectively, in the new policies.

The old estimator can reject useful actions while a more permissive expert
also admits harmful or insufficiently observed ones. Recovering utility is
therefore not evidence that the learned cost gate controls selected risk.
This agrees with the earlier fixed-region diagnosis: aggregate cost agreement
does not establish calibration inside the chosen region. Prior failed global
and selected-set recalibrations are not reset by this result.

## What Is And Is Not Identified

The same-mixed restriction isolates physical competition exactly, using only
predicted costs. Expert substitution changes training allocation, parameters
and inference work: two 128-tree forests or two 26,309-parameter heads replace
one. It does not isolate class imbalance, loss, architecture or information
content. No new trajectory model or optimizer step is involved.

The 897,060 query views per family repeat contexts, head seeds and windows;
they are not independent people or recordings. There are 74 unique fitting
recordings and 444 required recording/context/seed views. The 3,000 locality
resamples are fitting-resubstitution intervals, not held generalization.
FDE aggregation is not_estimable because a required view lacks an estimable
endpoint denominator; it is not silently dropped or filled with zero.

## Next Action

Do not deploy expert separation or use held outcomes to loosen its gate.
Check whether the frozen output paths themselves admit meaningful lower-risk
interventions, treating physical and neural candidates symmetrically. A fixed
amplitude control must retain all required recordings, unknown outcomes and
undefined easy support. Tiny or zero interventions cannot be presented as
neural dynamics success. Earlier future-informed segment-oracle results use
a different candidate pool and cannot substitute for this current check.

The local SOURCE resource archive contains polygons and georeferencing
metadata, not matched training video frames. Actual pixel features have not
been established for this current SOURCE cost task. An older SDD/ETH RGB
onset probe is a separate negative study, not an appearance ablation here.
