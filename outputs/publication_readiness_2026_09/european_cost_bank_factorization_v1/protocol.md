# Frozen Fitting-Only Cost-Bank Factorization

Previous goal turn is progress: 54 full ExtraTrees fits / 6,912 primary trees,
exact 144-group readout and 1,332 paired fitting-cost views. Utility and full
risk gates fail. On identical neural selection regions, forest EH overestimates
while neural EH underestimates. Neither global aggregate agreement nor oracle
opportunity is learned safe success. Local assets are available; no hard blocker.

## Question And Fixed Controls

Does the shared mixed-bank estimator reject the neural candidate, or does that
candidate pass its cost gate and lose to a physical candidate? Does composing
the independently fitted physical and neural experts change that operating
point? This is a diagnostic comparison of fixed estimators, not new training.

Run six original contexts x three controller seeds x four SOURCE fitting
localities x two estimator families = 144 fitting source/head/family groups.
Replay all 54 forests and 54 static-scene neural cost heads. No inner-held,
independent selection/calibration/confirmation or DroneCrowd readout. The
frozen producer seed remains 43, not three fresh forecasting pipelines.

For each family retain original physical, mixed and neural-only policies.
Add the same-mixed-estimator neural-only restriction; a two-expert composition
using physical-expert costs for actions 0-5 and neural-expert costs for action6;
and neutral composition of the mixed estimator with itself. Neutral arrays,
choices and ranks must be exact. Original choices and complete policy metrics
must reproduce the verified parent fitting groups.

Two causal diagnostic policies isolate cases where the dedicated neural expert
passes but the mixed estimator rejects, and where the mixed neural gate passes
but a physical action wins. Neither subset uses observed benefit, harm,
easiness, labels or future availability. All other queries retain the floor.
All original eligibility, no-op removal and 2% predicted risk rules remain.

## Attribution And Accounting

Record independent and overlapping gain/reference/easy-reference/all-harm/
easy-harm gate failures, their fixed-order exclusive counts and bit patterns.
The ordering is explicitly diagnostic, not a causal attribution of correlated
failures. Neural gate pass versus actual neural choice separates competition
from estimated gate rejection exactly for a fixed mixed head.

Expert composition changes learned costs, supervision allocation, capacity and
inference work: two 128-tree forests instead of one, or two 26,309-parameter
heads instead of one. It is not matched compute or a pure architecture effect.
All expert preprocessors and cost units must match exactly. No weighting,
interpolation, thresholds, tree settings or best seed are learned here.

Known ADE/FDE, completion-aware paired utility and full per-record all/easy
2% risk remain separate. Full and count-matched utility contrasts compare each
fixed control with its mixed policy. Empty risk stays not_estimable; unknown
outcomes remain in bounds. Fitting-defined hard/easy known-error summaries
are supplementary, with empty groups retained. No reduced-risk subset is
allowed to replace the complete roster.

Three thousand locality resamples are descriptive fitting-resubstitution
intervals, not generalization, independent confirmation or whole-pipeline OOF.
All controls and negative results are reported. A promising fitting result
only motivates a new registered trial; it cannot promote deployment.

## Execution And Scope

Freeze code/config/protocol before execution, run all 144 groups locally, then
repeat inference in a separate process and require exact groups and summary.
Use native arm64 CPU4, interop1, workers0, no resource probing. Save immutable
groups and private heartbeat/logs with resume and an exclusive process lock.
Preserve 10GiB disk reserve. No estimator or trajectory checkpoint is trained.

EuropeanSquares SOURCE is oblique detector-track box-midpoint pixel data,
obs8/pred12/stride12 raw frames. It is not SDD human gold, metric/seconds,
true3D or foundation evidence. Future endpoints, central velocity, test goals,
future masks and test normalization are not inference inputs. Stage5C and SMC
remain disabled. This study changes no prior failures or protected data roles.
