# Joint Decisions: Utility Improves, Safety Does Not

## Decision

Keep the original rules and deployment unchanged. The joint-loss model is
not deployable under the original 2% all/easy positive-harm, signed easy-net
and reference/support contract. No identity-fallback certification was added.
Empty controls and missing support remain not estimable. Old failures remain
failures; the original 24-group primary is still NA, not passed.

## What Was Actually Changed

A fresh fitting audit rejected the earlier all-action-averaging explanation:
each supported row already supervises one frozen router proposal. No opposing
all/easy target signs occur among the observed proposed actions. I corrected
that explanation without changing the old result numbers.

A second audit found a narrower problem. Exactly zero targets receive no
signed-cost gradient, yet a positive prediction in that channel vetoes the
entire intervention. Zero-only blockers account for about 39%, 42% and 49%
of normalized false-rejection cost in the three fitting contexts. The new
auxiliary loss trains the maximum across both heads and both channels; it
does not waive any inference check. Architecture, warm starts, source roles,
sampling, unknown-label coefficients and 2,000-step budgets stay fixed.

All six real neural fits finished. The three per-channel controls exactly
reproduce their predecessors. Both resume pilots, one full treatment refit,
all six fitting attributions and the complete readout replay exactly.

## What Worked On Fitting Data

| Context | Joint cost reduction | Zero-only rejection cost reduction | False rejections, control to joint | False-safe decisions, control to joint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| critic0 | 13.903% | 45.325% | 2,987 to 2,655 | 16 to 18 |
| critic1 | 17.585% | 52.548% | 2,073 to 1,924 | 20 to 35 |
| critic2 | 30.113% | 67.356% | 23,981 to 22,704 | 22 to 162 |

The measured zero-channel problem is reduced, not eliminated. The fitting
benefit is not uniform: SOURCE073's joint cost rises by 0.00000471, despite
the aggregate critic2 improvement. Matched all-channel decision costs worsen
by 12.567%, 7.216% and 2.265%; matched easy-channel costs worsen in critic1
and critic2. The internal 3/3 screen compares with warm initialization, so it
does not imply superiority to the matched fitted control or scientific safety.

False-safe cost rises from 0.00004070 to 0.00009716 in critic1 and from
0.00003831 to 0.00053751 in critic2. These are normalized training costs, not
trajectory-degradation percentages. The aggregate loss can reward recovering
many useful proposals while tolerating extra costly harmful admissions.

## Failure Taxonomy

1. **Zero-channel loss coverage: partly repaired.** The fixed loss change
   reduces the attributed error in every fitting context. It also changes
   gradient scale/routing, so these effects are not independently identified.
2. **Benefit-harm discrimination: unresolved.** More useful proposals are
   admitted, but harmful admissions also rise. Reducing a pooled joint cost
   is not equivalent to bounding every recording's harm under the old rules.
3. **Source transfer: unresolved.** Fitting gains do not translate into safe
   held-development decisions. The results do not uniquely identify model
   capacity, features or label incompleteness as the cause.
4. **Removing the veto is not a repair.** The risk-only diagnostic improves
   utility but worsens easy harm to 40.38%. It cannot be promoted post hoc.
5. **Reference support: independently unresolved.** Model weights cannot
   create missing reference events. A loss repair cannot turn NA into pass.
6. **Independent evidence: not run.** Reserved20 Moving, DUT, DroneCrowd and
   H remain closed. No repeatedly inspected source is relabeled independent.

## Next Model Work, Not Yet Run

The next useful contrast is preventing harmful admissions while retaining
the joint loss's reduced rejection cost, not another threshold sweep. First
use own-B fitting roles to measure the severity and source concentration of
the newly admitted harmful proposals. Then preregister one harm-sensitive
training change, for example a source-balanced positive-excess objective,
against the current matched control. Keep the original inference gates,
unknown-label bounds and thresholds; freeze all models before any permitted
development readout. Do not choose weights from SOURCE075 or the worst held
view. The proposed change has not been trained and its benefit is unknown.

A deployable result still needs the original complete-strategy checks,
supported independent confirmation, and the planned multi-seed/strong-method
comparisons. This experiment is neural intervention-risk learning, not new
forecasting dynamics or JEPA training. Native obs8/pred12 image coordinates
only; no metric, seconds, true 3D, foundation or human-gold claim. Stage5C and
SMC remain disabled. The project is not submission-ready.
