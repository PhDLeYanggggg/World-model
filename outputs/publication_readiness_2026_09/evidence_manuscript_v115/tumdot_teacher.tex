An exact outcome-aware program chooses one path per edited row,
maximizing the minimum paired lower gain against CV, OLS4 and
hindcast under the unchanged risk contract. At half radius it
passes on \TumTeacherPass/27 views; mean minimum gain is
\TumTeacherOracle{} pp. A donor-only joint teacher uses these
assignments as preference labels. Against local row-wise labels
with the same network and training, the effect is
\TumTeacherEffect{} (guarded: \TumTeacherGuard). Inference is
still causal row-wise choice, not online oracle allocation. This
development study has three intersections from one campaign.
