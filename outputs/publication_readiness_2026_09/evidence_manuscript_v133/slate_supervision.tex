\section{Recording-feasible supervision for whole queries}
In each training role, we construct CV-anchored recording plans using
analytic forecasts only. The original scorer verifies positive lower
gain over CV and OLS4, the original positive-harm and signed-easy
limits, and nonempty easy support. The three folds provide
\SlateWitnessWitnesses{} verified recordings. Replacing exactly one
query while keeping all other edits fixed creates counterfactual
preferences: favor a feasible replacement, then maximize the smaller
of the two conservative gains. A gain-only preference with the same
additive architecture isolates the change in supervision. A nonlinear
composition head additionally uses feature dispersion and causal
neighbor geometry, so this is not a parameter-count-matched ablation.
Teacher actions and future labels are not inference inputs.

Across three seeds and three folds, the 27 fits use
\SlateWitnessUpdates{} updates. The frozen control and the learned arms
make \SlateWitnessEdits{} edits each, matched in every query. Their
original risk pass counts (frozen, gain-only, feasible-additive,
feasible-joint) are \SlateWitnessPasses{} out of \SlateWitnessViews{}.
The paired full-grid lower contrast is \SlateWitnessFeasible{} pp for
feasibility supervision and \SlateWitnessJoint{} pp for adding the
joint composition head (95\% source-bootstrap intervals). The contrast
is conservative under missing outcomes, not a point estimate of
unobserved errors. No setting qualifies on calibration.

A separate hindsight check uses a bounded mixed-integer solve,
retaining one of the same six slates at
each active query. It independently verifies \SlateWitnessFixedVerified{}
recording--seed witnesses; \SlateWitnessFixedExcluded{} are excluded by
the constraints or an upper bound, \SlateWitnessFixedUnknown{} lack
support, and \SlateWitnessFixedUnresolved{} remain unresolved. The
witnesses diagnose the frozen query budgets and menus and never choose a
model or threshold. These are development experiments; no independent
confirmation outcomes were opened.
