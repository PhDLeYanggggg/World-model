# Shape Correctness Did Not Establish Safe Admission

## Material Passport

Fresh_run: 18 real Torch constrained heads, 36,000 primary updates, two
separate complete verification refits, full held constrained allocations.
Cached_verified: 18 original unconstrained amplitude controls and their saved
allocations, with fresh exact control fitting/held predictions and scalar
checks. The trajectory backbone is not retrained. Separate replay receipts
accompany the complete results. This is exposed-source development evidence,
not independent calibration or final confirmation.

is negative; this is a negative robust-bound criterion, not proof that the
unobserved true effect is negative. The observed-label comparison is close
to zero with an interval spanning zero. Neither establishes superiority.
These are nominal intervals after source/producer/seed averaging, not an
independent test or correction for the wider search. Original 24-group
primary remains not estimable, not passed; all 23 supported groups remain.

The constrained policy's known floor-relative gain is 11.705786%, versus
11.727646% for the control. The new method cannot claim that shared advantage
over the floor as its contribution. Its separate floor-relative completion
lower is 5.491804%, versus 4.568233%. Subtracting those separately bounded
scores is not the registered paired completion bound: the latter accounts
for direct policy disagreement on wholly unknown futures. A favorable
difference between separate lower bounds must not replace the fixed contrast.

## What The Structural Repair Actually Achieved

The preceding fitting audit found H convexity violations in 822,588/1,171,716
repeated supervised-amplitude fitting rows and EH violations in 542,904 rows.
The new decoder's H/EH curve checks are zero in every one of its 18 fitting
views. Its mathematical nonnegative hinge structure is supported by focused
tests, finite gradients, original-control equality and exact resume. Both
complete first-job refits agree, and all 18 initial parameters, draw streams,
normalizers, IDs and budgets match the cached controls. This is a working
structural implementation, not a new convex-network invention or improved
real-world dynamics merely because it enforces a property.

Selected magnitudes remain wrong. On the constrained head's own independent
fitting choices, pooled known H/predicted-H is 40.662441 and EH/predicted-EH
245.059453. The original amplitude head's own EH ratio was 202.249747.
These are different policy-selected regions and pooled cost masses, not a
same-sample calibration contrast or proof of a unique cause. They show that
shape correction alone did not remove a reliability deficit already visible
on fitting sources. A globally reasonable fitting objective can coexist
with severe underprediction precisely where decisions intervene.

## Diagnosis And Limits Of Attribution

1. Cross-amplitude inconsistency was real, but eliminating it was insufficient
   under this fixed model and budget. Do not infer that all convex predictors
   fail or that further training could never help.
2. Absolute selected-cost reliability, reference support and policy composition
   remain distinct from curve validity and action ranking. The evidence does
   not establish which one alone causes the remaining error.
3. Unknown-policy disagreement weakens the paired lower comparison; known
   easy violations independently rule out the complete deployment claim.
4. Stable hinge arithmetic is disclosed together with the decoder change.
   This is not a separate precision ablation, so effects are not attributed
   exclusively to convexity. No original training loss or risk threshold
   was changed to improve the outcome.
5. Joint allocation pools predicted budgets rather than geometric pair costs.
   Solver fallbacks remain in the full result. They do not certify actual
   risk or prove an exact optimum; see the complete solver ledger before
   attributing failure to optimization timeouts.

## What Changes Next

Do not repeat amplitude/threshold sweeps or increase this constrained head's
width solely because its form is correct. First use the completed fitting
and policy receipts to distinguish selected-magnitude fitting error from
input/support limitations, checking prior conditional-reliability, published
decision-loss, region-weighting and calibration controls before proposing a
distinct comparison. Those prior methods must not be renamed as a new idea.
Any further fit needs a fixed hypothesis and matched control before its own
held readout; this analysis does not claim that another repair was executed.

Independent model-selection/calibration/confirmation roles remain separate.
The pending unused-source allocation is not implemented without its scientific
decision; existing DUT/DroneCrowd reservations remain closed. Adding a scene
or visual modality also requires its actual source/admission evidence, not
a causal claim inferred from failure. No new image or goal input is used here.

The project remains short of the requested submission candidate: there is no
new robust positive method comparison or passed complete risk contract, and
no independent confirmation of this repair. No deployment promotion, true3D,
foundation, metric/time or human-gold claim. Current units are pixels/native
8/12 annotation steps; partial-label errors do not identify missing steps.
Stage5C execution and SMC remain disabled.
