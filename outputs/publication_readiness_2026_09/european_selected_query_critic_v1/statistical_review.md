# Statistical interpretation checklist

These are interpretation safeguards, not eleven newly passed scientific gates.
The current experiment uses exposed source-fitting material. Nominal intervals
are conditional descriptive evidence and do not establish independent efficacy.

|Issue|Relevance and treatment|
|---|---|
|Simpson-type aggregation|Report locality and head-seed effects, not only a pooled mean. The primary known context contrast is negative in all12 localities; other contrasts may differ.|
|Ecological inference|Locality-average gains do not establish benefit for every agent, recording or intervention. Retain per-recording risk and subset results.|
|Berkson-type selection|Labels are available on a selected support. Keep all causal-eligible rows in inference and report unknowns; do not claim complete-case fitting represents missing futures.|
|Collider conditioning|Original/new selected sets depend on learned predictions; selecting only good interventions can bias an assessment. Evaluate full policies and keep selection-specific diagnostics explicitly descriptive.|
|Base-rate effects|Changing selected population/reference mass changes easy-risk support. Equal counts do not equalize reference error mass or certify the ratio.|
|Regression to the mean|Do not choose unusually bad recordings or head seeds and claim a subsequent repair from their rebound. Use the frozen complete72-group roster and fixed final step.|
|Survivorship|Unknown, empty and failed-risk records remain reported. Empty denominators are not safe-zero outcomes, and no hard recording is dropped to restore support.|
|Look-elsewhere effects|Many earlier development repairs informed this research. The3,000-draw intervals are not search-adjusted confirmatory p-values or family-wise guarantees.|
|Forking paths|Freeze each experiment before its own fits/outcomes. Retain the negative primary control even if a secondary parent comparison looks positive.|
|Correlation versus causation|Measured weak query gradients and low risk MSE do not by themselves identify why the final policy fails. The separately registered one-factor loss-unit comparison tests one explanation.|
|Reverse causality|Forecast-error arithmetic and changing prediction choices do not intervene on pedestrian behavior. No causal interaction-dynamics or physical-safety claim is inferred.|

Three critic seeds share one forecast producer. Reused rotations/windows do not
create independent samples. Locality bootstrap follows context/seed averaging,
with12 exposed locality units; it is not evidence from independent new scenes.
Unknown-outcome completion bounds are bounds for their stated missing support,
not confidence intervals for latent unobserved labels or missing interior points.

No deployment or submission-readiness promotion. Independent selection,
calibration and confirmation remain unopened by this experiment. DUT's earlier
predictive exposure is retained; reserved does not mean pristine. No metric,
seconds-level, human-gold, true3D, foundation, Stage5C execution or SMC claim.
