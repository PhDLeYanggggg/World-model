# What Amplitude Learning Did And Did Not Fix

## Fixed Question

The preceding label-aware action-class diagnosis found feasible witnesses in
330/348 repeated recording views after adding intermediate amplitudes, versus
299/348 for the binary proposal. That was an opportunity result, not learned
performance. This study tests whether matched causal cost learning realizes
some of it. All sources, incomplete labels and original 2% requirements remain.

There are 36 fresh real Torch heads and 72,000 primary updates, with two
additional complete first-job refits. Both arms have identical 428-column
inputs, capacity, initialization, normalization, query draws and budgets.
The treatment changes supervised action availability as well as deployment
availability; the same-weight binary deployment is a separate secondary.
The trajectory forecasts and legacy-head metric receipts are cached_verified.
They are not newly trained or newly inferred legacy results in this study.

## Registered Primary Is Not Supported

For joint-free amplitude-bank minus newly trained binary-only control:

| Fixed contrast | Point (pp) | Source-group 95% development CI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Known available-label utility | +0.457936 | [-0.480517, +1.493011] |
| Paired completion lower | -1.158427 | [-2.906426, +0.218829] |

The primary fixed supported-group completion-lower contrast does not pass.
The known contrast also does not establish a stable advantage over the
matched training control. This does not prove the two methods equivalent,
nor prove that amplitude learning can never help. The original 24-group
primary remains not estimable, not passed. These 23 supported source groups
are exposed development data, not independent final confirmation.

## Small Secondary Action Benefit Is Not Safety

With the same amplitude-head weights, permitting intermediate actions rather
than only the full neural action changes joint-free known utility by
+0.594669 pp [0.298759, 0.919206]. The paired completion lower is
+0.101009 pp [-0.427861, 0.543615]. Thus there is a small observed-label
secondary action-availability signal, but its missing-outcome interval does
not establish a positive effect. It cannot replace the failed primary.

The amplitude head's known gain over its frozen physical floor is 11.727646%
[5.120161, 18.658069]. Its floor-relative completion lower is 4.568233%
[-4.481983, 12.032217]. Those floor-relative scores are different estimands
from the paired head-to-head effect above; their lower differences must not
be obtained by subtracting two separately bounded scores.

## Complete Recording Risk Still Fails

| Joint-free deployment | Full original support | Known all-risk violations | Known easy-risk violations | Undefined selected easy risk | Worst known easy-net degradation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Newly trained binary-only | 6/348 | 279 | 281 | 55 | 428.913029% |
| Amplitude bank | 10/348 | 273 | 281 | 46 | 376.331142% |
| Amplitude weights, binary deployment | 7/348 | 283 | 275 | 59 | 428.913029% |

These are repeated recording/producer/seed views, not 348 independent sites.
Every deployment fails the full original contract. A decrease in violations
or a few additional supported views is not a deployment pass. Undefined easy
selected-reference denominators are not safe zeros; positive observed harm
violations are a separate substantive failure. The 15 views without any
observed easy event are retained. Further undefined views can also arise
because a policy selects no qualifying easy reference mass.

The floor makes no path changes. Its lack of selected-risk support is not
evidence that it causes harm. Analytical zero change is not an empirical
finite-selected-support certificate.

## How The Richer Menu Is Used

The richer menu is not used as simple global shrinkage. Its joint-free
source-macro switch rate rises from 63.237067% to 70.925824%, and its mean
amplitude over all rows rises from 0.632371 to 0.658174. Among its 990,780
repeated selected row views, 918,751 (92.730071%) still use the full neural
action; 72,029 use an intermediate fraction. These histogram counts weight
repeated rows, not independent sources. They show what the policy does,
not a causal proof of why the risk gate fails. The same-weight binary control
has 66.189912% source-macro switching and mean amplitude 0.661899, so the
secondary availability contrast has a different intervention-size tradeoff.

There are 3/138 empty same-count source/pipeline comparisons for the amplitude
head versus 4/138 for binary. Full-scope same-count utility is consequently
not estimable for either, not a safe zero. The amplitude arm has 175/16,734
nonoptimal free solves under the fixed 0.25-second budget; the solver's
predicted-feasible parent fallback is retained. This is not evidence that
timeouts explain the observed safety failure; optimal predicted decisions
can still be wrong under realized outcomes.

## Failure Was Already Visible On Fitting Sources

Before new held scoring, all 144 fixed-region fitting checks were evaluated
and separately replayed. In the amplitude head's own independent action
region, pooled known EH / predicted EH is 202.249747; the binary head's own
region is 731.853309. Yet scoring all legal full-amplitude actions gives
ratios 0.703540 and 0.639841 respectively. Globally overpredicting a cost
therefore does not imply reliability on the actions a policy actually selects.

These are resubstitution cost-mass ratios, not average per-row ratios,
independent calibration, or a causal decomposition. Their direction supports
a policy-conditional fitting deficit already before held-domain shift. It
does not identify a unique cause: learned features, saturation, tail-weighted
optimization, ambiguous outcomes and selection can all interact. A smaller
ratio for the amplitude arm is not calibration success.

## What This Changes Next

Do not add another held-threshold sweep or choose the best uniform fraction.
First check the frozen heads on fitting sources for violations of the known
amplitude-cost shape: true available-label positive harm is convex,
nondecreasing and zero at amplitude zero; H/alpha is nondecreasing for alpha>0.
The current decoder bounds costs and shares reference moments, but does not
enforce those cross-action shapes. A shape diagnostic or constrained model
would be a distinct hypothesis, not an already measured improvement. No such
new fit or calibration is claimed here. Check prior controls before registering
it, and fix its comparison before reading its own held outcomes.

Independent selection, risk calibration and confirmation remain separate
data-role requirements. The pending role allocation for unused sources is
not silently implemented. Do not reopen DUT or DroneCrowd confirmation, remove
unsupported recordings, or change the original risk contract to obtain a pass.

## Claim Boundary

This experiment learns forecast-intervention costs, not a new neural trajectory
backbone, latent rollout, JEPA improvement or world dynamics improvement.
No new model is deployed. Pixels/native 8-observation/12-prediction annotation
steps are not verified metres, seconds or SDD raw t50. Partial-label ADE and
whole-missing-future bounds do not identify missing steps inside partial
trajectories. Results do not establish physical safety, true 3D, foundation
success or submission readiness. Stage5C execution and SMC remain disabled.

The complete scalar readout, all policies, solver reasons, usage histograms,
fitting checks and verification receipt accompany this analysis. Exact replay
supports engineering reproducibility, not independent scientific confirmation.
