# Does A Useful Continuous Pair Correction Exist At The Original Recipients?

The learned missing-label-aware blend shrank toward the individual
forecast. Before fitting another gate, test the available frozen
path family with a label-aware query-local bound. Use the same
native Moving A roster, frozen individual and pair-trained
predictions, CV risk reference and 9,675 original recipients.
Only the blend weights of those recipients may change. Keep all
other paths fixed, including rows without future labels.

The loss increase relative to zero blending is convex: add the
change in observed relative-path norms and the disagreement
norms on unobserved pair steps, divided by the full requested
edge-step count. On the observed steps with nonzero reference
error its directional derivative is linear; all remaining
terms contribute norms. A negative minimum derivative on the
nonnegative unit simplex is necessary and sufficient for a
strict query-local improvement, without the recording-risk
constraints. Compare a common coefficient for the two switched
people with coefficients chosen separately in the same query.

Use the exact one-coordinate derivative or a bracketed
one-dimensional convex support bound. Report no-gain at
numerical tolerance only when the lower slope bound is at
least -1e-9 pixels per unit blend sum. At most two recipients
can switch; the resulting maximum undetected gain is 2e-9
pixels per query in that classification. Negative slope alone
is not counted as a witness: search along the direction, then
re-score a concrete nonidentity path with the original pair
evaluator. Retain unresolved queries separately. Reuse a
checked shared witness in the separate arm if better. Ray
search is not a global maximizer of gain magnitude.

Assemble the query-local witnesses by recording and score
the original 2% risk/support rule, CV and OLS4 comparisons.
This step is descriptive, not joint optimization of the
recording gate. Compare the native fixed-recipient forecast,
shared and separate hindsight witnesses. Use 3,000 paired
source resamples after averaging seeds. Report counts only
over active queries separately from whole-roster scores.

This diagnostic sees development labels; it supplies no
causal policy, independent evidence, new training or external
access. Native image pixels and annotation steps only.
No deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
