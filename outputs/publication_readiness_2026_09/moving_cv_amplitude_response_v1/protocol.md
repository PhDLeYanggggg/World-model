# Learn the response to edit magnitude

Keep the v138 source roles, native 8/12 trajectory windows, CV
risk reference, OLS4 utility comparator, and original 2% rule.
Do not open A or any independent confirmation outcomes.

The path bank contains analytic directions on train/calibration,
and adds the frozen source-excluded directions on held development.
Use fractions .05/.15/.35/.65/1 of each complete candidate ray.
Train matched direct and shape-constrained heads. Both estimate
two conservative gain increments, two nonnegative harm masses,
signed easy change, two reference masses and annotation support.
The shape head enforces concave gain and convex harm/signed cost;
the risk allowance is subtracted after decoding the harm masses.
Both heads use the same train-only action-ray sampling and
training budget. No observed target or observed mask enters inference.

Compare independent ranking, shape versus direct at matched counts,
and query portfolios obtained by pricing predicted risk. The
portfolio screen permits slack on predictions only, never in the
original scorer. Calibrate family, coverage cap and predicted slack
on the assigned calibration sources. Otherwise retain CV. Include
fixed enlargements of the old causal policy as calibration controls.

On held development compare the main portfolio with an independent
control at exactly the same query counts. Compare adaptive shape
amplitudes with uniform amplitudes on the same recipients and ray
directions, matching the sum of peak displacement in each query.
Report actual count, peak displacement, original risk, dual gains,
pair geometry, and source-bootstrap intervals.
