\section{Source-Disjoint Recipient Calibration}
Each development fold uses six source groups for fitting, two
for inner calibration, and one for outer scoring. A fresh ridge
path nominator is shared by a gain ranking and a neural ranking
of beneficial, harmful or unsupported, and safe-easy events.
There are \NestedRecipientFits{} Torch fits across three seeds.
The query-local quota is unchanged. Deployment requires
the original risk rule in every calibration recording and
positive conservative utility against both CV and OLS4.

Neither score qualifies in any fold-seed. Both calibrated
policies make zero changes, with undefined easy risk in all
\NestedRecipientViews{} outer recording-seed views. The
same-count efficacy comparison at zero switches is not
estimable. When both rankers are forced to use the original
quota, each makes \NestedRecipientActions{} changes. The
direct conservative support-minus-gain contrast is
\NestedRecipientEffect{} pp (95\% source bootstrap
\NestedRecipientInterval); neither arm passes risk.

A diagnostic sweep of all \NestedRecipientLevels{} distinct
inner score levels still finds no threshold satisfying risk.
In \NestedRecipientUnsupported{} of nine folds an inner
recording has no easy reference even on the full roster.
In the other \NestedRecipientSupported{}, a hindsight
allocation with the same nominees can satisfy risk alone.
This allocation uses evaluation labels and is not a
deployable rule or evidence of improvement against both
references. This study uses exposed development sources;
the separate DroneCrowd confirmation was not accessed.
