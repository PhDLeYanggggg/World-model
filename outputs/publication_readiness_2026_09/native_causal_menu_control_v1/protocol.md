# Matched Past-Only Causal Menu Control

On the fixed exposed Moving A development roster, compare a selector
over the five causal motion alternatives to the same selector over
those five plus the three frozen learned alternatives. CV remains
the fallback and risk reference; OLS4 is the accuracy comparator.
The shared-motion transform is not offered to either selector.

Reuse the original source-excluded per-candidate regression heads,
past-only inputs, stable action order, and independent/joint ranking
rules. Each causal head is estimated independently in the frozen
fit, so slicing its coefficients does not change its fitting
problem. Freeze the choices for all three forecaster seeds before
opening score labels in this runner. The development labels were
previously exposed and are not an independent result.

The primary contrast is mixed joint versus causal joint at exactly
the same number of real switches per query. On any query with
unequal attainable counts, both primary arms retain CV and that
query is counted as a mismatch. Independent choices are matched
by the same rule. Separately apply the original predicted screen
to each natural joint proposal; these can operate at different
counts. The matched screened comparison uses the intersection
of queries both screens accept with equal counts. This intersection
uses both banks and is an analytic control, not a causal-only
deployment policy. Empty matched utility is not estimable.

Keep eight observed and twelve predicted annotation steps in
pixels, the original recording risk and positive-reference
rules, and the fixed 2% budget. Report paired row-support lower
utility versus each other, CV and OLS4, available ADE and FDE
with support, hard/easy errors, all recording risk outcomes and
the original query-level relative-path contrast with missing pair
steps. Use 3,000 paired source-group draws after averaging seeds.
No external input or outcome access, no post-score policy or
threshold selection, no deployment, Stage5C or SMC.
