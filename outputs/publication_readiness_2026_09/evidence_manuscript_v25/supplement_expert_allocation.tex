\section{Expert Allocation Under Fixed Intervention Counts}
We tested whether a different forecast family remedies the failure
against OLS4. These are fitting-source development experiments, not
new calibration or confirmation. Inputs are eight observed annotation
steps; outcomes span the next twelve. The same nine held fitting
sources and three seeds are used for all comparisons.

The first experiment fits a bounded mixture of five motion forecasts.
The replay arm uses errors on one- and two-step forecasts inside the
observed history. The matched history arm keeps the trajectories,
architecture and training draws, but removes those replay errors.
Across \XAllocMotionFits\ fits, neither the OLS4 nor the matched
history comparison establishes a full-grid gain from replay.

The second experiment keeps those experts and their equal-weight
pair averages fixed. We train \XAllocAllocFits\ gain and harm heads,
excluding each evaluation source during fitting. All active controls
make \XAllocSwitches\ changes per seed across \XAllocQueries\ queries.
The joint choice uses the same one-action nomination per agent as
its additive control, and substitutes the two-agent contraction for
their separate contractions. It changes only \XAllocCountLow--\XAllocCountHigh\
queries per seed relative to that control.

The hindsight margin decomposes into \XAllocRecipient\ points lost
by choosing the wrong recipients and \XAllocExpert\ lost by choosing
the wrong experts for those recipients. Hindsight is not a model
result. The joint penalty reduces its distance-contraction proxy
without establishing forecast lift or physical safety. Equal counts
control the number of changes, not their amplitudes. The original
2\% recording rule is unchanged and not certified here. The held
fitting sources do not replace the sealed external groups.
