\section{Conserving Recipients as Well as Motion}
Starting at fixed half amplitude, we transfer radius between at most
two targets per query. Neither becomes an identity edit. This keeps
the selected reference denominators and query radius fixed, but may
change the missing-label debit. We compare summing predicted marginal
effects against predicting the exact pair action. Both predict and
then optimize; neither implements the decision loss of \cite{spo}.
Each uses 21 new heads and pair-excluded donor forecasts.

Both risk guards preserve every fixed-half pass but neither establishes
a positive conservative gain. The marginal matched-action comparison is
undefined in four required views. Direct prediction does not resolve the
choice problem: only \ExchangeActualGood{} of \ExchangeGuardActions{}
selected actions are both beneficial and nonincreasing in all observed
risk numerators. A fixed event threshold reduces exchange coverage to
\ExchangeEventActions{} queries without proving gain. The hindsight
envelope finds a beneficial risk-nonincreasing exchange in
\ExchangeOracleActions{} queries, showing room in the menu rather
than the ability to find useful actions from the past. Even hindsight
cannot pass all 606 views. No new independent policy qualifies.
