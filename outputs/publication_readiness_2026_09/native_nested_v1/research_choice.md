# Research Route and Zero-Reference Decision

## Material Passport

- Date: 2026-09-21.
- Scope: prospective research-route decision following the author's delegation.
- Evidence: current native-forecast reports and nested-producer registration.
- Status: a decision, not a new performance result or safety certificate.

## Chosen Route

The author delegated the choice to the route most likely to yield a substantial,
credible paper. I prioritize native-coordinate forecasting with learned
baseline-relative benefit and harm, followed by conservative intervention.
The main task remains eight observed to twelve predicted annotation steps;
raw-frame t+50 remains supplementary. The previous primary-metric amendment is
already resolved and is not reopened here.

This is a research judgment, not a guarantee of a large effect or acceptance.
The practical reason is the completed matched experiment: native-loss training
has a positive source-exclusion forecasting contrast, but easy protection is
still unresolved. Addressing that concrete failure is a more falsifiable next
contribution than expanding the architecture into an unverified general world
model. JEPA, larger models, and scene-level coupling must earn their inclusion
through matched evidence; they are not presumed contributions.

## Strict Protection, Not a New Pixel Allowance

Under this delegated choice, retain the strict option. For an evaluation group
whose reference ADE is exactly zero, report native absolute error and require
no added absolute error for an empirical protection pass. Percentage degradation
is undefined. Do not add an epsilon to the denominator, delete these rows, or
choose a positive pixel tolerance after inspecting results. Other defined easy
groups retain the existing <=2% relative-degradation requirement; their precise
native-scale group construction must be frozen in the follow-up registration,
not copied from the old normalized metric without review.

Zero-reference status uses future labels for evaluation only. It must never be
used to route an inference query. This decision therefore does not claim that
the system can identify all such queries from history. Finite calibration data
cannot establish population-wide zero harm under arbitrary shift. Failure to
meet the empirical rule remains a failure, even when overall ADE improves.

The earlier pending-question statements in frozen registrations are historical
records. This document resolves the choice prospectively without editing those
hash-bound files. No thresholds are fitted or relaxed by this document.

## Evidence Before Claims

The current nested experiment trains 18 two-site-excluded forecasters and
reuses 12 previously bound outer-held models. It supplies honest exploratory
cost-supervision views, not an already successful risk head. The four source
sites have influenced design and cannot be relabeled independent confirmation.
Next, a separately fixed cost-head comparison must establish conditional harm
reliability and useful intervention, with independent calibration/confirmation
kept distinct from this developmental readout.

Selective prediction itself is established, including integrated rejection in
[SelectiveNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html).
[Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814) controls expected
monotone losses under its stated assumptions; its guarantee must not be silently
transferred to subgroup-relative trajectory harm or arbitrary domain shift.
These primary sources motivate comparators and caution, not a novelty claim for
our current implementation.

Success requires a useful accuracy-versus-harm tradeoff beyond those controls,
clean producer lineage, matched ablations, and genuinely independent evidence.
Large-looking numbers on reused source scenes alone are insufficient.
Pixel/raw-frame only; Stage5C and SMC remain disabled.
