candidates. Share those estimates across available candidates,with R_easy<=R.

no-op anchor,manual risk-reference inflation or future-known eligibility is added.
The shared estimates are arithmetic means,not maxima or target-based quantities.
Anchored summation preserves exact fixed points when the references are already
shared. There is no fitted pooling coefficient,temperature,margin or threshold.

These are cost-moment invariants,not proof of trajectory safety or physical validity.

Reference MSE can improve by the averaging property alone;this is not evidence
that decisions improve. Full policy effects and actual recording risk decide that.
Projection changes inference costs and choices. It is neither exact old-model
prediction reproduction nor neural trajectory/cost training.
