\section{Source Candidate Averaging}
We repeat the three-seed fit of both heads with averaged OpenDD
candidate paths. Each donor uses one location-excluded producer and
six pair-excluded producers, sharing one location-excluded refiner
per path family. The donor recipients, peak radii, training draws and
loss are fixed. Averaging changes the hindsight best arm on
\SourceEnsembleDonorChanged{} of \SourceEnsembleDonorStrict{} strict
source row-seed cases. The frozen TUMDOT bank is unchanged.

At half radius the full policy changes \SourceEnsembleChanged{} of
\SourceEnsembleRows{} target choices. Relative to training on individual
donor forecasts, direct conservative utility is \SourceEnsembleFull{}
percentage points and risk passes are \SourceEnsembleFullEns/27 versus
\SourceEnsembleFullSingle/27. The action-only difference is
\SourceEnsembleSmall{} points with \SourceEnsembleSmallEns/27 versus
\SourceEnsembleSmallSingle/27 passes. Neither policy beats CV or matched
hindcast. Matching producer averaging this way does not fix transport.
Source and target still differ in how refiners and producer training
sets are combined, so this does not isolate domain shift on its own.
