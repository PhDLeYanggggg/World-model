# Upward harm cost refinement: fixed experiment

This is an exposed SOURCE development experiment, not independent confirmation.
The original24group primary remains not estimable; the supported23group matrix
is retained as a development secondary. No reserved source, DUT or DroneCrowd
role is opened. No deployment, Stage5C or SMC execution is authorized here.

## Diagnosis And Hypothesis

The complete preceding convex-head experiment fixed its numerical shape but
failed both the paired completion-lower criterion and the complete risk rule.
On its own selected known fitting rows, pooled actual/predicted H was40.66 and
EH245.06, versus R1.012 and ER1.097. Those are pooled fitting masses, not macro
risk or independent estimates; they do not prove references are correct in
every query. This motivates a direct harm numerator repair, not another shape
change or a claim that squared error is intrinsically decision-inconsistent.

Question: does refreshing the region of costly actions during a fixed-budget
harm-only refinement improve on weighting the original frozen selected region?
Compare three arms: global, fixed_region and adaptive_region. The last two use
4x selected-action weight, normalized within each query. Source and query mass
are equalized. Queries with no legal action keep their unsupported roster;
they are not turned into measured-safe zero observations.

## Fixed Computation

All arms reuse the same18 verified original unconstrained amplitude_bank neural
heads, original C-fitting preprocessing, seven fixed trajectory amplitudes,
three seeds17/29/43 and unchanged data roles. This is NOT54new neural models.
Each of54new refiners fits20 multi-output regression trees: depth4, random
splitter,64candidate features,128minimum rows per leaf, rate0.5. Total primary
tree fits1080. Three first-job full refits and the recovery pilot are additional
verification, not primary training. All fits finish before any new held score.

Inputs: original380 clipped causal fields, five current predicted costs divided
by original causal scale and fitting RMS (clipped0..20), action amplitude, and
log1p causal query size:387columns. No source ID, future-validity or target input.
Per-round targets are signed residual H/EH divided by that original scale/RMS.
Unknown outcome rows use the original whole-unknown envelope H/EH bounds, not
invented observed labels. Train only on each original C-fitting partition.

Apply only the positive part of each tree's predicted correction. Keep R/ER
fixed, enforce EH<=H<=causal disagreement envelope, and reduce B only as needed
so B<=R and B+H<=envelope. Individual predicted feasibility cannot expand.
This does NOT guarantee realized risk, monotone joint memberships or useful
nonempty decisions. Ordinary and region-weighted controls share the tree count,
parameters, seed schedule and data; learned tree node counts may differ.

## Prior Work And Scope

Post-processing and subgroup residual auditing are established ideas, not our
novelty. We read the abstract/introduction of Kim, Ghorbani and Zou's
[Multiaccuracy](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~mpkim/pubs/multiaccuracy.pdf), and the
original [Multicalibration abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/hebert-johnson18a.html).
Their fairness/classification guarantees are not imported to this upward-only,
continuous-cost, reused-fitting, adaptive trajectory selector. This is not a
proved multiaccurate estimator or independent calibration procedure. Older
adaptive-region NN weighting and static score-cell calibration already failed
in this repository. The distinct tested change is frozen-forecaster, candidate-
specific sequential tree harm refinement, with a matched fixed-region control.

All coordinates remain dataset-local pixel coordinates and native8/12steps;
no verified metric scale, raw-video FPS or effective-seconds claim. No new
scene images, trajectory residual model, JEPA or generative rollout is trained.
