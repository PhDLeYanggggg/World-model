# Harm-refinement component and data card

## Intended Use

Exposed SOURCE research into reliable substitution of a frozen neural trajectory
for a strong causal motion baseline. This component estimates intervention
costs; it is not a new trajectory forecaster, physical safety controller,
generative world model or certified deployment policy. Final results, runtime
and gates belong in the complete readout, not the existence of this card.

## Component

18original, hash-verified amplitude neural cost heads stay frozen. Three matched
tree-refinement arms per head give54refiners with20trees each. The global arm
weights all legal actions; fixed_region emphasizes the initial independent
chosen action; adaptive_region updates that region from its own current causal
cost predictions. The fixed4x multiplier is normalized within each query.
All share budgets, seeds and depth/leaf settings, not necessarily learned node
counts. Trees change predicted H/EH upward only; R/ER stay fixed, B may shrink
to retain causal-bound coherence. Trajectory candidates are never retrained.

387inputs are past-only original380local descriptors, current predicted cost5,
amplitude and causal query size. Original C-fitting mean/std/RMS are reused.
Neither future endpoint, future validity/easy membership, source identity,
central velocity nor test-derived goals is an inference input. Future outcomes
are supervised targets and evaluation labels only. Whole-unknown targets use
the existing disagreement envelope, not fabricated observed outcomes.

## Runtime And Reproducibility

Actual arm64 local CPU execution, Torch4threads/interop1, workers0, single-process
sklearn trees. Checkpoints contain trees, exact schema/binding hashes and round
traces. Resume reconstructs fitting predictions and compares their hash.
The9real2+2versus4round pilot cells and three first-job full refits check exact
scientific states, not only successful exit. These are tree fits over frozen
Torch forecasts, not fresh neural optimizer updates. MPS/CUDA performance is
not_run. Public metrics/report code are separate from private weights/caches.

## Limits And Deployment

Upward-only corrections can remove useful interventions and need not be
multiaccurate, calibrated or useful. Less predicted risk is not observed safety;
fewer switches are not utility lift. Empty selected easy support and empty
same-count controls are not estimable, not measured-safe zero. An unchanged
floor has zero path change analytically, which is different from estimating
selected-event risk with absent support. The full original2%contract and all
negative source/recording views remain visible. Training loss has arm-specific
weights and is not a matched performance score. No deployment is authorized by
this experiment alone. Stage5C/SMC remain off; not a foundation or true3D model.
