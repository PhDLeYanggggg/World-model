These checks are not combined into a misleading total pass score. No-path-change
is an exact trajectory identity, not an estimated selected-event risk. The old
gate remains unchanged. Ratios and intervals use the registered available-label
native 8/12-step protocol and observed-floor normalization, not verified raw
video seconds, complete-trajectory ADE or metric coordinates. No true-3D or
foundation claim. The full legacy pytest suite is not run: it contains historical
report-mutating integrations. The focused current suite passes 36 tests.

The four solver families each retain 16,734 queries at 0.25 s/query. Joint-free
nonoptimal counts are 175 frozen, 67 global, 82 fixed and 67 adaptive; matched
counts are 323/107/115/123. This does not establish bitwise MILP reoptimization
or prove that finite solve budgets have no effect. Recorded feasibility is not
observed outcome safety.
