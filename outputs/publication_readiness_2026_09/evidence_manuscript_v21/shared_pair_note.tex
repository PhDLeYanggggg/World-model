\paragraph{Shared-target sensitivity.}
Overlapping queries may predict the same pair at the same
annotation step. Within one recording and seed, couple the
two forecast multisets at that target using their original
query weights as both marginals~\cite{peyre}. The reverse
triangle inequality bounds the contrast by the coupling
cost; the diagonal coupling is the original bound. A
verified lower-cost coupling tightens the bounds without
imputing a location or changing the observed term.
The joint-minus-additive point envelope contracts from
\OverlapOld{} to \OverlapNew{} pixels. Its lower bound
improves by \OverlapGain{} pixels and remains negative.
We retain the original primary metric and recording rule.
