# TRAIN-Identity Leaf-Quality Extension Control

The prior frozen diagnostic found improving TRAIN error for all72 cost heads,
but worse projected recording-held error in43. Error concentrates outside TRAIN
leaf-quality boxes, with sparse nonzero easy-harm support and positive-link tails.
This motivates one frozen feature-extension contrast, not a threshold search.
It is post-hoc, exposed-development research, not independent confirmation.

Within each immutable tree leaf, estimate componentwise minima and maxima of
the seven standardized past-quality coordinates on the exact known-label TRAIN
fitting rows. Before the fixed positive harm link, replace each query quality
coordinate by its projection into that leaf's interval. Reuse the original
coefficients, TRAIN means and log normalizer. All known TRAIN rows are inside
their leaf intervals, so their raw predictions must be bit-identical. Validate
this for every head. Unknown-label TRAIN rows do not define fitted bounds, but
unknown validation rows remain eligible and are never removed by future status.

No quantile, radius, multiplier, threshold, refit, new penalty or validation-
estimated support rule is introduced. Tree routing, raw B/R/ER, forecasts,
feature schema, TRAIN normalization, feasibility projection, disagreement
envelopes and the2% selected-positive-harm budget stay unchanged. Inference
accepts no future labels. Boxes are marginal bounds, not a joint support model
or a calibrated risk guarantee. Clipping may increase or decrease harm scores.

Compare five arms: original forest, two-harm additive, Poisson positive,
squared-cost positive and leaf-quality-extended squared-cost positive. Rehash
all owned weights and replay four control predictions, actions and readouts
against the completed cost experiment. New extension inference repeats exactly.
Evaluate full actions and same-query/count-matched actions versus all controls.
Undefined denominators and unavailable outcomes are not treated as zero risk.

Preserve the previous stringent exploration screen: signed MSE must improve
with nominal95% CI upper below0 against every control; full and matched lower
utility gains must each have CI lower above0 against every control. Complete
support, known/upper violation counts and worst upper risk must not worsen
versus original. Report an additional absolute all-head supported2% risk check.
Passing a relative exploration screen is not certification or deployment.
Do not choose a favorable alternative screen after seeing results.

Record raw and projected score errors, changed-row counts, selected/unselected
error attribution, training invariance, full and matched utility, known risk
and unknown completion. Mean/CIs use12 locality blocks and3000 resamples,
seed20261003, not overlapping windows or72 nominally independent heads. Three
cost-head seeds are not three new end-to-end neural trainings. Report locality
breakdowns and all failures, including empty/unknown support.

Run a first-head engineering pilot then all72 if execution/invariance passes;
do not select or stop based on pilot scientific performance. Use native arm64,
four threads and zero workers, existing data and read-only owned CREATE streams
with bounded reads/SSH keepalive. No new numeric cache, no new weights or HPC
jobs; aggregate output cap20MiB,12h per invocation. Resumes replay/hash-check
existing groups. Freeze registration before real execution.

All12 localities are already development-exposed. Independent selection,
calibration and confirmation stay closed. No deployment change, Stage5C or SMC.
Detector-silver, image-local obs8/pred12 at rawstride12 only: no seconds, metric,
physical-safety, true3D, foundation or submission-ready claim. This standard
extension control is a mechanism test, not an architectural novelty claim.
