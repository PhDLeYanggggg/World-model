# External Frozen-Chain Experiment: Methods Addendum

This extends the September22 development manuscript; it does not replace its
negative results or treat a new source refit as a new method contribution.
Results belong in a separate table generated by the registered DUT readout.
No positive result is asserted by this methods addendum.

## Source Fitting and Transport

We fit fixed-budget Transformer and deterministic K=1 EqMotion predictors on
the four development-exposed SDD sites, retaining seeds17,29,43. Each predictor
completes4000 updates of64 samples. Source eligibility uses past observations;
future masks affect supervised loss, not inference membership. These six final
fits are not outer-site validation predictors. Their checkpoints and source
exclusions are preserved in the predictor-refit manifest.

For each family and seed, cost supervision uses existing predictions produced
without the target row's source site. A width64 neural cost head and128-tree
ExtraTrees comparator are fitted to the same complete-supervision population;
tree weights reproduce neural sampling counts. Each neural head completes3000
updates of256 rows. Both estimate nonnegative benefit/harm fractions of causal
candidate-to-CV disagreement. The denominator bounds the two costs by the reverse
triangle inequality; it does not establish their conditional calibration.

This setup differs from the prior development manuscript's width128 temporal-
ramp head experiment. Its figures must not be copied into the external table.
The present chain uses the full fixed forecast action and newly registered
width64 source heads. It also has an unresolved producer shift: cost labels use
three-site source predictors, whereas final external candidates use four-site
source predictors. All source sites have influenced research design.

## Input and Decision Boundaries

We form each target's local coordinates using only its observed origin, heading
and path/speed scale, and restore predictions to a common recording frame before
joint decisions. Neighbors include all currently visible context with explicit
history/CV support. An agent with incomplete target history can still be visible
context; unknown velocity is not treated as known stationary motion. Future
label availability cannot create, remove or reorder inference targets.

For each frozen candidate, the strict guard requires observed motion, supported
finite outputs, positive expected benefit over harm, and estimated harm no more
than one tenth of benefit. At full and half of the eligible intervention count,
independent, unary-geometry and joint choices share the reference count and its
predicted harm budget. Their realized harms need not match. The separate uniform-
scene arm can fall back entirely and is not an equal-count contrast. Unknown
context edges are unpriced and reported, not certified physically safe.

## External Population and Interpretation

DUT is calibration-reserved, not a final confirmation set. The current producer
chain excludes it, but a universal assertion that no historical project process
ever encountered related data is not made. We admit27 source-audited recordings,
exclude one duplicate-annotation recording, and retain two author-described
physical sites. Pedestrians and vehicles are both evaluated and separately
reported; the SDD fitting population was pedestrian-only.

Every observed query frame after seven preceding frame slots is considered.
Targets require all eight historical positions; neither a complete future nor a
minimum eventual track length is required. Requested predictions cover twelve
native frames. External stride1 and SDD source stride12 are not matched physical
durations. Raw author annotations support an offline annotated-history study,
not proof of real-time observation availability or verified metric dynamics.

Complete-path native ADE is the preregistered descriptive DUT readout, with
equal weight per physical site. It must not be pooled with the older SDD
available-point ADE headline. Relative change in mean site errors and mean
site-relative percentage change are distinct statistics; both are labelled.
Easy/hard cuts are inherited source-only normalized-CV-error cutoffs, not DUT
quantiles. Every seed's site-specific easy degradation is retained.

If future labels are absent, the missing absolute error is unidentified. However,
at each missing step the CV-minus-candidate error difference lies between plus
and minus their prediction disagreement. Adding these bounds to known-step
differences produces a full-population relative-gain interval without imputation.
These deterministic bounds are different from a bootstrap interval and do not
identify absolute trajectory error or physical safety.

We retain all models and arms without external winner selection. A paired3000-
resample bootstrap uses only the two physical sites. Its coarse support is
descriptive; recordings, overlapping windows and random seeds do not create
additional independent sites. This evidence cannot certify a tight2% risk
tolerance or the transport of risk to DroneCrowd. The latter collection remains
reserved for a separately specified one-shot confirmation, not opened here.

## Remaining Contribution Test

A useful result must show more than successful execution or bounded scores:
the learned intervention must improve a strong causal reference, preserve easy
trajectories, and demonstrate a joint decision effect beyond matched unary
geometry. Fitting loss, source-only QA and a statistical bound implemented in
code do not supply that evidence. If DUT contradicts the hypothesis, preserve
the negative result and diagnose support/transport using source development
experiments rather than repeatedly selecting against its outcome.

This remains a2.5D trajectory/world-state study. No true3D, foundation, metric,
seconds-level, independent safety, Stage5C execution or SMC claim is made.
