\section*{Learning gain under fixed predicted risk and intervention counts}
We retain the low-coverage component-risk joint policy as the incumbent. For
each query we fix its exact edit count, keep each of its three predicted
risk sums as an upper bound, and use its two predicted selected reference
masses and frozen CV gain as lower bounds. We optimize OLS4-relative utility
over the same motion paths with a linear relaxation and feasibility-checked
rounding. The incumbent remains an option. The constraints are on predictions
and do not certify actual risk.

We compare direct gain regression with a factorized head that predicts
masked observed benefit and adds a geometric missing-step debit weighted by
estimated per-lead missingness. A no-fit control uses the OLS4 geometric term
under complete missingness. The two learned arms use identical fitting
samples and \FixedGainUpdates\ updates in total. The outcome-aware diagnostic
uses the same solver only after causal choices are frozen. It measures
attained capacity, not an oracle optimum. All arms in the table use
\FixedGainEdits\ edits, matched exactly per query.
