\section{Signed excess and within-query gain retraining}
The previous pooled policy could not detect most true easy seed actions.
We refit two matched risk heads that directly supervise signed all/easy
excess, rather than combining predicted positive harm and reference
masses. The conditional version predicts a signed value only within
the relevant observed subset, then adds the exact edit radius weighted
by the predicted probability of wholly missing outcomes. A separate
gain head regresses the two CV-anchored bounds and ranks proposed edits
on different agents in the same query. A swap label is used only when
the observed margin exceeds the joint missing-step displacement.
The design uses \SignedCoverUpdates{} updates on fitting sources only.

At the fixed predicted screen, frozen, direct, and conditional risk
heads recover a true seed on the same action in \SignedCoverOldDonor{},
\SignedCoverDirectDonor{}, and \SignedCoverConditionalDonor{} of
\SignedCoverTrueDonor{} true-seed query views. With frozen gain the
direct and conditional heads choose \SignedCoverDirectEdits{} and
\SignedCoverConditionalEdits{} edits, passing \SignedCoverDirectPass{}
and \SignedCoverConditionalPass{}/\SignedCoverViews{} recording views.
Their lower contrasts against per-query count-matched gain controls
are \SignedCoverDirectMatched{} and \SignedCoverConditionalMatched{}
percentage points, respectively. Adding the swap gain head gives
\SignedCoverDirectSwapPass{} and \SignedCoverConditionalSwapPass{} passes.
Its conservative contrasts at the fixed risk readouts are
\SignedCoverSwapOnDirect{} and \SignedCoverSwapOnConditional{} points
(paired 95\% source bootstrap intervals). Those contrasts can change
coverage and do not isolate the ranking loss from a new gain fit.
Calibration qualifies \SignedCoverQual{}/9 cells.
This is fitting-source evidence, not independent confirmation.

In a separate matched replay, both conditional-risk policies make
\SignedCoverSwapMatchEdits{} edits with identical counts in every query.
The swap ranker gives \SignedCoverSwapMatchKnown{} on known labels
but \SignedCoverSwapMatchLower{} as its conservative contrast.
Under the uncapped conditional-risk/frozen-gain policy,
\SignedCoverKnownEasyOver{} recording views already exceed the easy
positive-harm limit on known outcomes; the wholly unobserved debit
rejects \SignedCoverMissingFlip{} additional views that otherwise pass
all observed checks. The original recording rule retains both failures.
