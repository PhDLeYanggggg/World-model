# Frozen external WEPDTOF test

Convenience Store is development-only. The other 13 source groups
are used once, with no model or threshold choice from their geometry
or score. Retain all planned camera windows and all online tracks
with eight consecutive detections. An unmatched camera track has
unavailable outcomes; it is not removed from intervention counts
or the original missing-label risk debit. Match one-to-one on past
positions only and require stable identity across all eight frames.

CV and OLS4 use the same past camera centers. Use the centered OLS4
spline chosen from exposed OpenDD development (positive conservative
gain versus CV at five of seven sites). Average its seven site-excluded
checkpoints within each of three original seeds. Use past RMS to map
each nonstationary trajectory to a dimensionless unit before forecast
and invert the mapping; it is not a pixel-to-distance conversion.
Zero motion retains CV. No new forecaster fitting.

Fit one action-value head per seed on the seven OpenDD held-site
forecast caches, each OOF forecast excluding its row's original site.
The bank contains the OLS4 and neural directions. The common query
radius is 25% of the median of the smaller two available peak radii;
skip rows that cannot reach it without amplification in both families.
Retain at least one CV agent and at most two edited agents per window.
The joint policy chooses nonempty actions only if both predicted
positive-harm excesses are nonpositive and the smaller predicted
utility increment, penalized for nearby relative-path displacement,
is positive. Compare matched independent, common-family and
zero pair-penalty policies at exactly the joint counts and total peak.
The pair penalty is a trajectory consistency proxy, not collision risk.

Use the original 2% risk function for all/easy positive harm and
signed easy degradation. Define cutoffs by the existing positive
complete CV ADE quartiles, computed in pixels only on the pilot's
stable matched camera histories, not imported across units. Do not
treat zero selected reference or absent easy support as passing.
Deployment remains CV if the pilot joint proposal fails the original
rule; proposal outcomes are still reported as the fixed research
comparison, never as a certified deployment. Freeze every path and
cutoff before reading the reserved groups' geometry. Report ADE/FDE,
lower full-grid gains against both CV and OLS4, matched contrasts,
tail errors, interventions, relative-path disagreement and source
bootstrap. Include all recordings, including unsupported ones.
DroneCrowd remains sealed. No metric, time in seconds, Stage5C or SMC.
