# Statistical Interpretation Boundaries

## Material Passport

- Mode: statistical interpretation and reproducibility validation for a registered development experiment.
- Inputs: fixed decision receipts, original coordinate errors, locality-level paired metrics and complete result manifest.
- Computational status: the authoritative pass/fail counts are in `completion_checks.json`, produced only after both evaluation banks complete.
- Scientific status: conditional development evidence; no independent confirmation or deployment promotion.

## Estimand And Uncertainty

ADE averages observed future-step errors within an indexed row. FDE requires the actual endpoint label. Paired comparisons require identical valid-label support, and unknown outcomes remain unknown. The primary summary first forms model/reference error ratios within each source locality, then gives the fixed roster of eight localities equal weight. The denominator therefore differs from a pooled-row percentage and from a percentage-point removal ledger.

Each paired interval uses 3,000 source-locality resamples with the registered seed. This retains within-locality window overlap, but eight localities provide limited support for tail generalization. Three existing training seeds are retained; the 36 views per policy share models, localities and examples and must not be pooled as independent replication. The two normalization settings and event-target arms are complete sensitivity comparisons, not extra test cohorts.

All 936 views and their locality breakdowns are retained. This is an exploratory family of dependent comparisons without a multiplicity-corrected acceptance claim. A count of positive or negative intervals is descriptive, not a binomial success rate. There are no p-values to apply a retrospective false-discovery filter to, and no policy is selected from this readout.

## Eleven Interpretation Checks

| Check | Treatment and residual limitation |
|---|---|
| Simpson reversal | Report both source-equal summary and every source; a positive mean cannot stand in for source stability. The generated CSV retains opposite-direction sources and tails. |
| Ecological inference | Locality aggregates support locality-level descriptions only, not individual safety or demographic fairness. |
| Selection/Berkson effects | Cohort requires an eligible complete observed history; it is not every visible pedestrian. Support membership can select distinctive motion patterns. |
| Collider conditioning | Hard/easy strata use future error only for evaluation, never inference. These selected strata are descriptive and not causal subpopulation effects. |
| Base-rate neglect | Retain indexed, known-label, switched and rejected counts. Four zero-CV examples from one locality are not a robust rare-event denominator. |
| Regression to the mean | Fixed forecasts and paired unchanged-stop anchors prevent calling a new forecast draw a repair. Previously opened poor slices remain selected development targets. |
| Survivorship | Report partial/unknown future-label support and complete-window sensitivity; never score missing labels as zero or silently require future track survival at inference. |
| Look-elsewhere | Publish every registered factor and matched control; no best-arm promotion from many intervals. |
| Forking paths | Registered hashes and both-bank barrier freeze decisions before new readout. Earlier results motivated the experiment, so registration does not restore independence. |
| Correlation/causation | Fixed-rule counterfactual error differences identify the effect of algorithmic removals on these predictions, not the causal reason for producer or domain shift. |
| Reverse direction | Input histories precede outcomes and model disagreement is prediction-only. Association of rejected cases with future error does not turn future labels into support features. |

Coverage: 11/11 interpretation categories checked. This is not eleven statistical tests or proof that all assumptions are satisfied.

## No Safety Or Transport Certificate

The 2% positive-easy budget is an empirical criterion relative to constant velocity. It does not cover unknown outcomes, zero-reference cases without adequate support, physical collisions or all future domains. Same-frame count matching controls intervention volume, but a query with no flexible quota cannot demonstrate ranking quality. Per-agent ADE does not test whether a scene's joint trajectories are mutually consistent.

Support-factor gains relative to the joint box must also be compared with the unchanged stop controller. Recovering discarded benefit from a harmful filter is not new neural dynamics lift. New source/producer-matched training and independent calibration/confirmation would remain separate requirements even if a factor has a favorable development interval.
