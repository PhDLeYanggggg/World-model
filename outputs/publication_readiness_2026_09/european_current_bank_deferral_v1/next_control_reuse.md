## Mechanism And Next Falsifiable Comparison

The current fixed guard both admits observed signed-risk-unsafe actions and
rejects useful easy-reference anchors. Twelve repeated views lack any admitted
known easy anchor; six have a singleton-query support obstruction. The original
legal bank retains beneficial safe actions. This distinguishes a faulty learned
cost/eligibility interface from an absence of useful forecasts.

The next control must assess two-sided admission error on the original complete
causal action region, not only mean cost error on choices already retained by a
previous guard. Before another full fit, it must specify how it differs from
the existing symmetric/upper-score objectives and own-choice query loss. Useful
checks are sign discrimination at the actual 2% boundary, recovery of beneficial
easy-reference support, false-low-risk admissions, and selected reference bias.
All known legal targets can supervise these losses; labels may not define
inference availability or choose an oracle-safe subset.

A new fit is **not run or registered here**. Its pre-outcome design still needs
to isolate a real change in representation, output parameterization or learning
signal. If the current causal schema cannot distinguish safe from unsafe actions,
adding another weight sweep is not evidence that it can; lawful additional
history/context or better outcome support may be necessary. The present diagnosis
does not establish irreducible uncertainty or prove such new inputs will work.

Retain the original 2% recording requirements, finite/undefined support handling,
all source recordings, unchanged future-label boundary and the independent-data
lock. Do not manually inflate reference mass, use oracle anchors, select a
favorable seed, drop a small recording or reinterpret unknown outcomes as harm.
No deployment change, Stage5C execution or SMC follows.
