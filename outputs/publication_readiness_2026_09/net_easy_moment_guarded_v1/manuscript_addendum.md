# Manuscript Evidence Addendum: Net Easy-Risk Accounting

We isolate risk accounting while holding the candidate forecasts, source-only
draw counts, preprocessing, feature inputs and estimator budget fixed. Four
bounded conditional moments represent easy positive harm, easy benefit, easy
reference error and easy probability. A query-local policy uses their signed
difference rather than positive harm alone. Both policies share the same fitted
head, and a count-matched arm separates intervention coverage from selection.

On four development-exposed SDD sites with three seeds, net accounting recovers
available-point ADE utility relative to positive accounting. Transformer gain
over CV is2.94%, compared with1.04% for the same forest's positive-population
control. EqMotion gains2.83% versus0.79%. Worst site/seed positive-easy degradation
is0.86% and1.84%, respectively. At matched counts, the gains are substantially
smaller, so a large part of the unrestricted improvement is associated with
broader intervention rather than selection alone.

Relative to the previous strict rule, Transformer's0.50 pp difference has a
paired physical-site CI95 spanning zero. EqMotion's1.22 pp difference has a
positive nominal interval, but worsens easy performance and harms six zero-CV
query/seed instances. Transformer harms three. No new policy is deployed.

These results support an accounting-mechanism hypothesis, not a finite-sample
risk certificate or a new world-model contribution. A signed constraint credits
improvements against harm and is weaker than positive-risk control. Estimator
error, complete-versus-partial outcome support and site shift remain unresolved.
The count-matched Transformer arm contains one reported numerical fallback;
its primary-gain sensitivity is bounded below0.001 pp, without outcome-based
decision replacement. All three numerical fallbacks and all controls are retained.

The protocol is observation8/prediction12 native SDD annotation steps, stride12,
pixel coordinates, equal physical-site weighting and3000 site resamples. It is
not historical raw-frame t+50, metric prediction, seconds-level prediction,
independent confirmation or a physical-safety study. Forecasts are frozen and
the prior negative interaction-ablation finding is unchanged.
