# Statistical Interpretation and Claim Audit

## Material Passport

- Type: descriptive validation of the frozen source experiment.
- Status: analyzed; separate arithmetic completed. Exact replays have their own receipts.
- Sources: `analysis.json`, `postreadout_diagnostics.json`, `separate_checks.json`.
- Scope: four development-exposed SDD physical sites, three seeds, native8/12.
- Assessment: caution for generalization; not independent scientific validation.

The primary estimates are equal-site percentage gains over CV. Three seeds are
averaged before 3000 paired physical-site resamples. This is not a 175756-unit
independent test, and does not integrate every source of model-selection or
training uncertainty. Percentages are not metres, seconds or individual-safety
probabilities. The 27 registered paired comparisons each report three subsets
(81 nominal intervals); no multiplicity correction or new discovery declaration
is made. There are no p-values to interpret as formal significance.

The cutoff-versus-native population contrasts are +0.0300 pp for Transformer
[-0.0260, 0.0996] and +0.0394 pp for EqMotion [-0.0122, 0.0910]. Both span zero.
The EqMotion contrast with old strict is positive descriptively but changes risk
accounting and intervention coverage; it is not a matched-rate causal claim.

## Eleven Scoped Checks

| Check | Observation and limit |
|---|---|
| Aggregate/group reversal | Matched-native contrasts have mixed site signs: Transformer loses in deathCircle; EqMotion loses in hyang. All per-site values are retained. No claim of improvement in every site. |
| Ecological inference | Site-average easy preservation does not guarantee individual protection. The observed zero-CV harms explicitly falsify that stronger interpretation. |
| Selection/Berkson risk | Past eligibility is fixed and all 175756 windows remain. Complete-label fitting and target-MSE subsets can still differ from the prediction population; support counts and bounds remain explicit. |
| Collider conditioning | Hard/easy subsets use future errors only for labels/reporting, not inference. Subset comparisons are descriptive, not estimates of a causal effect in real pedestrian behavior. |
| Base-rate neglect | Each seed has 11566 zero-CV windows; affected unique windows and repeated instances are both reported. Moment MSE is not a calibrated positive predictive value or failure-detection guarantee. |
| Regression to the mean | There is no uncontrolled before/after claim. Predictors, forecasts, targets, draws and three seeds are paired. The follow-up was motivated by exposed prior errors, which remains development exposure. |
| Survivorship | Unknown and partial futures are retained for decisions. Observed-grid means and complete-label MSE are distinguished; unknown selections cannot be counted safe. |
| Look-elsewhere | All 39 control rows and all 81 contrast intervals are published. None is selected as a new deployment winner. Intervals remain nominal. |
| Forking paths | The local hashed registration preceded fitting; the prior variants and failures remain. This is not independent third-party preregistration, and the inspected sites remain design-exposed. |
| Causal overclaim | The fixed representation comparison supports statements about this fitted pipeline. The synthetic information-loss witness is not empirical proof that one feature caused all prior failure or new dynamics ability. |
| Reverse direction/leakage | Scale/disagreement inputs and the cutoff come from past/frozen train sources. Future errors are training/evaluation labels, not causal inference inputs. Existing producer bindings and matched targets were checked. |
