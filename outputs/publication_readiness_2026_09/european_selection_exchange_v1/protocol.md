# Frozen Same-Query Benefit/Harm Exchange Diagnosis

## Material Passport

Post-readout diagnostic of the completed causal-descriptor experiment, not a
new primary test. Registration precedes the new decomposition but does not
make these already opened development sources independent confirmation.
Twelve development localities, three fixed forecasting seeds, 108 frozen
four/four/two/two source-role groups. No model fit or action change.

## Question and Fixed Analysis

The descriptor policy has worse ADE than its same-current-query-count
control, despite a small improvement in overall score MSE. Is this because
the exchanged selections lose benefit, add harm, or both?

For every frozen query, retain the shared eligible pool and the exact same
number of interventions, including future-unknown rows. Partition rows into
common, descriptor-only, matched-control-only, unselected eligible and
excluded. These sets must be disjoint and cover all rows. Do not allocate a
spare intervention to another recording or future frame.

For known evaluation labels, let B=max(floor error-neural error,0) and
H=max(neural error-floor error,0). Then the new-versus-control net change is
(B_new_only-B_control_only)-(H_new_only-H_control_only). Check this identity
against the frozen parent ADE contrast in every held-source view.
Normalize each contribution by the same old-policy total error in that view;
their difference exactly recovers the existing ADE contrast. Scale-normalized
sums use each head's fitting-only cost scale, never held normalization.

Also report the frozen ridge utility's expected-utility contrast on the
exchanged rows, unknown-label counts, useful/harmful/tied counts and source
breakdown. These are diagnostics of an imperfect prior, not calibrated
probabilities. Future labels are used only for outcome accounting.

Average the dependent producer/fit/seed views within each of the same twelve
localities, then use 3,000 paired bootstrap resamples with seed101531. No
source exclusion, subgroup selection, threshold search or new positive
primary claim is allowed. Zero denominators remain undefined.

## Interpretation Rule

If benefits fall more than harms fall, focus the next repair on utility-aware
ordering under the unchanged harm budget. If harms rise, focus it on
conditional harm estimation. If neither is stable, treat the result as
inconclusive and investigate label/support variation before another fit.
The decomposition cannot alone prove the causal root mechanism or deployment
safety. A subsequent training trial requires a separate frozen contract.

decisions and lineage. Save small per-group aggregates, heartbeat and resume;

selection/calibration/confirmation remain closed. Image-local detector silver,

true3D or foundation claims. No Stage5C or SMC.
