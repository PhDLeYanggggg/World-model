# Failure Taxonomy

## Evidence Scope

Fresh registered cost training and held-locality metrics on historically
exposed source development; cached_verified producers/membership. No independent
evaluation, deployment or threshold change. Labels are detector-derived image
coordinates, not human gold or physical safety outcomes. Annotation steps are
not seconds; no metric/true3D/foundation claim. Stage5C/SMC remain off.

## 1. Membership Helps, but the Strong Comparator Still Wins

Full conditional composition improves MSE over fixed training prevalence in
all six assignment intervals (+51.12% to +81.60%). Yet no interval is positive
against original_mean, and four are negative. A useful subcomponent can sit
inside a worse overall estimator. These percentages are not trajectory gains.

For example, producer1/controller0 has MSE gain -88.84% against original
(95% locality CI [-241.81%, -0.44%]). Its eu-locality-082 point is -322.00%.
This slice is retained, not removed to improve the headline. With four
localities per assignment, a large slice can materially influence the result.

## 2. Excess Easy-Harm Remains Outside the Easy Event

Conditional worsens 53/72 full views; outside-easy contributions dominate
50 of these, with 92.90% of positive excess mass. The largest individual
partition is outside-easy/no-all-harm in 43 views, outside-easy/positive-all-harm
in five, easy/no-harm in two and easy/positive-harm in three. The combined
outside-easy count can differ from the largest single-partition count.

On outside-easy rows, the target E*H is zero, while prediction is p_hat*m_E.
The observed excess is therefore a product-estimation problem. It is
consistent with imperfect membership probabilities multiplying conditional
severity extrapolated outside its fitting stratum. This is a hypothesis about
the mechanism, not proof that probability error alone is responsible.

An exact diagnostic identity is:

```
p_hat*m_hat - E*H = (p_hat-E)*m_hat + E*(m_hat-H)
```

Membership error, severity error and their cross term must be separated before
claiming a cause. E and H may be used only in an explicitly label-assisted
offline diagnostic, never deployment features. That finer attribution is
not_run in this experiment and cannot change its failed gate.

## 3. Not Pure Overfitting or a Runtime Failure

Conditional improves the original fitting MSE in only 14/72 full views and
the held MSE in 19/72; 13 fitting improvements fail to transfer. New direct
improves fitting in 23/72 and held in 16/72. Its original comparison has five
negative assignment intervals. Thus more training time or membership alone
is not an evidence-supported solution. The current fitted objectives/readouts
also need attribution. Runtime completed all 576,000 updates without a crash.

The two new heads share capacity, draws and budget, but have different output
priors and loss scales. The original joint moment model is a stronger retained
control, not an identical two-output architecture. Conditional includes an
extra frozen classifier. This is not a clean architectural-superiority test.

## 4. Ranking, Mean Coverage and Tail Costs Disagree

Conditional median full harm-event AUROC is 0.56160, but top10 harm mass capture
is 0.25911 versus original 0.27396. One original-comparison top10 interval is
negative. Mean predicted/actual harm coverage is median 1.13623, but ranges
0.01249 to 6.64146. A median near one is not per-row calibration. All-row
H_all MSE also has a negative interval against each stronger control.

## 5. Support and Repeated Development Limit the Claim

Full views have no below-20 positive/negative event-support flags; lack of
all positive events is not the main full-arm explanation. Motion-only has
30 weak-support views. Repeated source-role exposure prevents an independent
generalization claim. Bootstrap resamples cannot create additional independent
scenes, and absence of a negative CI would not establish non-inferiority.

## Next Repair Decision

First perform a source-only, label-assisted attribution of membership versus
severity error, retaining every role and the original comparator. Inspect
cost-weighted membership reliability and expert support, not another global
threshold sweep. Then register one controlled objective/readout repair only
if this diagnosis identifies a testable mechanism. Any future stacked model
must use nested/out-of-fold membership probabilities during fitting. Keep
independent data closed and preserve the 2% tolerance and forecasting endpoint.
