\paragraph{Whole-recording deferral targets.}
To distinguish a supervision mismatch from unavailable actions, we use
the same release states and model, but train one head on an isolated
first positive destination and the other on the difference between
two full schedules with one release flag toggled. The schedules include
reserved credit, expiry, and competition for destinations. The training
roster producers exclude both the fitting donor and the held location.
Across \LedgerLabels{} dependent donor comparisons, the real scheduler
changes \LedgerChanged{} targets and flips \LedgerSigns{} gain signs.
The risk-gated whole-recording head has paired gain \LedgerEffect{}
against the risk-gated local head, and clears \LedgerJoint{} joint
checks compared with \LedgerLocal{} for local supervision and
\LedgerAnchor{} for the unchanged anchor.

Restricting the exact timing solver to positive-prefix destinations and
original edits leaves \LedgerRestricted{} joint witnesses. With the
actual reserve-and-expiry decoder, an oracle allowed to toggle at most
one release per recording without dropping an edit clears \LedgerToggle{}
views versus \LedgerAnchor{} with no change. The utility in this last
comparison is available to a label-aware selector, not a past-only model.
It is not evidence that further loss tuning will recover it.
