\section{Pooling predicted slack at broader coverage}
We remove the query cap and allow all frozen causal proposals. In the
current query, an action with predicted easy support and nonpositive
all/easy excess starts the pool. Subsequent actions must fit both
predicted budgets; no later queries are used. The observed recording
rule is unchanged. The relaxed independent screen makes
\CoveragePoolMarginalEdits{} edits and passes \CoveragePoolMarginalPass{}/\CoveragePoolViews{}
views. The uncapped pooled rule makes \CoveragePoolEdits{} edits, passes
\CoveragePoolPass{}/\CoveragePoolViews{}, and qualifies \CoveragePoolQual{}/9 calibration
cells. Predicted risk constraints did not deliver broader coverage.

The fitted head identifies a seed action in \CoveragePoolEstimatedDonor{}
of \CoveragePoolQueries{} repeated query views; the corresponding label-aware
count is \CoveragePoolTrueDonor{}. On true donor actions, the probability
screen alone retains \CoveragePoolProbability{} queries, whereas the two
predicted excess signs alone retain \CoveragePoolBothCosts{}.
Replacing only predicted support and risk
with their realized values expands coverage to \CoveragePoolRiskOnlyEdits{}
edits, but its full-grid lower gain versus CV is \CoveragePoolTrueRiskCV{}
percentage points. Replacing both risk and gain gives
\CoveragePoolRiskGainEdits{} edits, \CoveragePoolTruePass{} risk passes,
and conservative gains of \CoveragePoolTrueBothCV{} versus CV and
\CoveragePoolTrueBothOLS{} versus OLS4. These are hindsight interventions
into the decoder, not learned alternatives. All intervals are source
bootstrap intervals after averaging the seeds.

Initial joint hindsight optimization at the recording level verifies
\CoveragePoolCapEight{}, \CoveragePoolCapWide{}, and \CoveragePoolUncappedInitial{} dual-reference
witnesses out of \CoveragePoolViews{} at query caps eight, 32, and no cap,
respectively. For unresolved uncapped views we evaluate a Lagrangian
upper bound. A convex weighting combines the two gains, and nonnegative
multipliers penalize the three risk excesses and the easy support
constraint. The relaxed maximum separates into each agent's best path
or zero for CV, making any dual feasible value an upper bound. After
this continuation, \CoveragePoolUncapped{} views have verified witnesses and
\CoveragePoolUnresolved{} are still unresolved.
These calculations separate the availability of favorable paths from
the ability to identify them without outcomes.
