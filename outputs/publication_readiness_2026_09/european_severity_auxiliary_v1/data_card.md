# Data Card: Frozen Source-Development Cost Supervision

## Material Passport

Cached_verified source forecasts, causal features and source-development labels
from the existing European locality protocol. Fitting support is fresh_run.
No new dataset, image annotation, world trajectory or independent test is
created by this experiment. Outcome status is in the final receipts.

## Roles and Targets

Twelve source-development localities appear in three fixed rosters of four.
Each ordered assignment keeps the source-A producer outside the four B head
localities. Three B localities fit a head; the fourth supplies a source-held
comparison. Six assignments and three seeds reuse localities and overlapping
windows. They are not additional independent scenes.

Six independent selection,12 reserved calibration and6 confirmation localities
remain unused. Historical exposed SDD/Stage35/37 results are not restored to
independent tests. Input and target hashes bind every fit to the frozen source
chain. Preprocessing, easy cut, normalization and severity weights use fitting
rows only. Unknown future labels remain unknown and are never sampled.

E remains1(0<causal-CV error<=positive fitting25th percentile). Exact-zero CV
error remains outside this inherited E definition. H is nonnegative excess
candidate error over the paired reference; H_E=H*E. These future-derived values
are training/evaluation labels only, not inference features or goal inputs.

in both E strata. Full easy-harm tracks minimum165, motion-only minimum23,
