# Remaining M3W Research Gap

## Current Status

Not yet a submission-ready or independently deployable world-model result.
The severity-weighted auxiliary comparison is complete and negative under
its registered cost-estimation endpoint. It changes no deployment policy,
trajectory forecaster, reserved role or historical evidence classification.

## What This Round Adds

A controlled loss intervention with genuine training, matched initialization/
draws/budget, three seeds, all source assignments, prediction freeze before
readout and retained negative intervals. Ordinary event supervision and
magnitude-weighted supervision both fail to give stable easy-harm accuracy.
Useful ranking in some assignments does not fix cost magnitude or tail risk.
This narrows the problem but is not the main method contribution by itself.

## What Still Prevents the Main Claim

- Reliable expected gain/harm under locality shift is not established.
- Scene-level joint intervention has not passed the intended matched-policy
  comparison using this repaired estimator.
- Independent selection, risk calibration and final confirmation remain
  unopened. Previously exposed source comparisons cannot replace them.
- Few localities, overlapping windows and detector-derived labels limit
  statistical and scientific claims even with thousands of bootstrap draws.
- A scoring-rule identity or adding an auxiliary head is not sufficient
  novelty; a transported decision/risk-control contribution remains needed.
- The complete paper, strong matched forecasting/policy comparisons and
  final independent result package are not established by this component run.

## Shortest Evidence-Driven Route

1. Finish frozen recording-level excess-error and fitting influence/support
   attribution; distinguish a few high-mass trajectories from systematic
   missing causal context without opening independent data.
2. Preregister one repair justified by that evidence. Keep the original
   estimator as a strong comparator and preserve easy-harm, all-harm,
   coverage and tail guards. No opportunistic coefficient/threshold sweep.
3. Only after a reproducible source-development signal, advance the approved
   independent selection/calibration/confirmation and scene-joint policy
   comparisons. A failed source estimator does not justify spending those roles.

Ordinary audits and authorized experiments are handled within the project;
formal submission remains user-confirmed. Raw data/caches/checkpoints remain
private and unrelated work stays untouched. Stage5C/SMC remain off. M3W is
still a 2.5D trajectory/world-state research track, not true3D or foundation.
No metric, seconds, human-gold or physical-safety claim.
